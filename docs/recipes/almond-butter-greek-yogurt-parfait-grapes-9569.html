Almond Butter Greek Yogurt Parfait with Grapes

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 7 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (9) $1.39/serving
Ingredients
  • natural almond butter
  • honey
  • 1 ½ cupsplain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ lbred grapes
  • ⅓ cupalmonds, sliced
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Add almonds to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly toasted, 2-3 minutes. Set aside to cool.
    ⅓ cup almonds, sliced
  3. 3
    Combine yogurt, honey, and almond butter in a small bowl and whisk until smooth.
    1 ½ cups plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp honey
    ¼ cup natural almond butter
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and cut grapes into halves.
    ¼ lb red grapes
  5. 5
    To serve, divide yogurt, grapes, and almonds between jars or bowls, alternating ingredients to create layers. Enjoy!

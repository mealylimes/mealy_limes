Almond Dusted Sole with Balsamic-Parmesan Asparagus & Tomatoes

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $6.55/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½lemon
  • almond meal/flour
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ lbsole fillets
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil for easier cleanup (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Snap or cut off woody ends of the asparagus and transfer to the baking sheet.
  4. 4
    Add tomatoes to the baking sheet with the asparagus and spread veggies out in an even layer.
  5. 5
    Cut butter into small pieces and sprinkle over the veggies; drizzle with vinegar, then season with salt and pepper.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place veggies in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until fork-tender, 12-15 minutes.
  7. 7
    While the veggies are roasting, zest lemon into a medium bowl. (Reserve lemon for serving.)
  8. 8
    Add almond meal, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the lemon zest; stir to combine.
    ½ cup almond meal/flour
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Working one by one, dredge fish fillets in the almond meal mixture, pressing lightly to adhere to both sides. Transfer fish to a plate.
    ¾ lb sole fillets
  10. 10
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the fish and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 2-3 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate.
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  13. 13
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  14. 14
    When the veggies are done, remove from the oven and sprinkle with Parmesan and half of the parsley; toss to combine. (Reserve the rest of the parsley for serving.)
  15. 15
    To serve, divide fish and veggies between plates. Juice lemon over fish and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

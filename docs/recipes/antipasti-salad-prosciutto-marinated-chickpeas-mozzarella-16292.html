"Antipasti" Salad with Prosciutto, Marinated Chickpeas & Mozzarella

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 91% (8) $4.60/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 2sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • onion powder
  • ⅓ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 stick celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse chickpeas; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a small bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp white wine vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp onion powder
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot into thin half-moons; place in a medium bowl.
    ½ shallot
  5. 5
    Trim celery and cut crosswise into thin pieces; add to the bowl with the shallot.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Mince sun-dried tomatoes; add to the bowl along with the chickpeas, half the dressing (reserving the rest for serving), salt, and crushed red pepper. Stir to combine and set aside to marinate.
    2 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, halve tomatoes and place on a plate.
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the plate with the tomatoes, keeping them separate.
  10. 10
    Medium dice mozzarella and add to the plate.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  11. 11
    Cut or tear each slice of prosciutto into 3 pieces; roll each piece and add to the plate.
    4 slices prosciutto
  12. 12
    Add artichokes and olives to the plate.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
    ⅓ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  13. 13
    To serve, divide mixed greens between plates or bowls. Place a scoop of marinated chickpeas in the center of the greens, then surround with tomatoes, bell pepper, mozzarella, prosciutto, artichokes, and olives. Drizzle with reserved dressing and enjoy!

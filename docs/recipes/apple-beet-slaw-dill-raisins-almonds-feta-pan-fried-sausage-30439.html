Apple-Beet Slaw with Dill, Raisins, Almonds, Feta & Pan Fried Sausage

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (16) $3.88/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 mediumbeet
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ⅛ cupraisins
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • 1Gala apple
  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 2pork sausages
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium beet
    1 Gala apple
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add almonds and cook, stirring frequently, until lightly toasted, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
  4. 4
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with vinegar, oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    2 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate beet. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Quarter, core, and coarsely grate apple. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Add toasted almonds, broccoli slaw, feta, and raisins to the bowl. Stir to combine the slaw.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ⅛ cup raisins
  10. 10
    Slice the sausages into bite-sized rounds.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the sausage and slaw between plates. Enjoy!

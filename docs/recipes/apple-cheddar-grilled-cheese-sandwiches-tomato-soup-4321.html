Apple-Cheddar Grilled Cheese Sandwiches with Tomato Soup

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (13) $2.35/serving
Ingredients
  • 1Gala apple
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • oregano, dried
  • butter, unsalted
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • black pepper
  • salt
Cookware
can opener chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 Gala apple
  2. 2
    Quarter the apple lengthwise, then cut out the core and stem from each section. Cut 2 of the quarters lengthwise into thin wedges and transfer to a small bowl. Medium dice the other 2 quarters and transfer to a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; small dice the onion (cut into ¼-inch pieces) and add to the bowl with the apple.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add to the bowl with the apple and onion.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once the saucepan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  7. 7
    Add the apple, onion, and garlic to the saucepan; season with oregano, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until the apple and onion are softened, 4 to 6 minutes.
    ½ tsp dried oregano
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Coarsely grate 1 cup of cheddar.
    ½ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    Add the diced tomatoes and broth to the saucepan; season with additional salt and pepper. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 8 to 10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Add butter to a nonstick skillet and heat over medium-low heat.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  11. 11
    Place the bread slices on a clean, dry work surface. Divide the apple slices and cheese between 2 of the bread slices, then top with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  12. 12
    Once melted, spread the butter around the skillet, then season the bottom of the skillet with salt.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  13. 13
    Place the sandwiches in the skillet and cook until golden brown and crispy on the first side, 3 to 4 minutes. Then, add more butter and salt, and carefully flip the sandwiches; cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese has melted, 3 to 4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a cutting board.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
    ⅛ tsp salt
  14. 14
    Using a hand blender, purée the soup until smooth. Alternatively, use a potato masher to mash the tomatoes and apples (the soup will be chunkier).
  15. 15
    Cut the grilled cheese sandwiches in half diagonally.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide the soup between small bowls and serve with the grilled cheese on the side. Enjoy!

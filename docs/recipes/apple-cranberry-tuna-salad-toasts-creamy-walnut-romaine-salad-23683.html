Apple-Cranberry Tuna Salad Toasts with Creamy Walnut Romaine Salad

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (6) $3.17/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupdried cranberries
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • salt
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • onion powder
  • mayonnaise
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 30 mlranch dressing
  • 1 heartromaine hearts
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 sticks celery
    1 Granny Smith apple
    1 heart romaine hearts
  2. 2
    Place yogurt, mayo, onion powder, and salt in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Quarter, core, and small dice apple. Add to the bowl along with the cranberries.
    ⅛ cup dried cranberries
  5. 5
    Drain tuna and flake into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the tuna salad, and set aside.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  6. 6
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  7. 7
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Place in another medium bowl.
  8. 8
    Roughly chop walnuts. Add to the bowl with the lettuce along with additional cranberries, dressing, and salt. Toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    ¼ cup walnuts
    ⅛ cup dried cranberries
    2 tbsp ranch dressing
    1 pinch salt
  9. 9
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    4 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    Divide toast between plates and top with tuna salad. Serve with romaine salad on the side and enjoy!

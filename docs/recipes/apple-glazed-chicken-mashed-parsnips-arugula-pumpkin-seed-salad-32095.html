Apple-Glazed Chicken with Mashed Parsnips & Arugula-Pumpkin Seed Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 61% (9) $5.14/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • all-purpose flour
  • Dijon mustard
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 mediumparsnips
  • ½ small pkgfresh thyme
  • 2Gala apples
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    2 medium parsnips
    ½ small pkg fresh thyme
    2 Gala apples
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Peel and medium dice parsnips. Transfer to a medium saucepan, cover with hot water (from the tap), and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium and cook until parsnips can be easily pierced with a fork, about 15 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, slide thyme leaves off the stems; discard the stems. Place leaves in a medium bowl along with flour and spices; stir to combine.
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Add to the bowl with the flour mixture and turn to coat.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and pan-fry until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use).
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  8. 8
    Quarter, core, and slice apples into ½-inch wedges. Add to the bowl with the onion.
  9. 9
    When the chicken is done, return the skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat. Add onion and apple; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the salad.)
  10. 10
    Add broth, vinegar, Dijon, and maple syrup to the skillet, then stir to combine the sauce. Return chicken (and any accumulated juices) to the skillet and turn to coat. Bring sauce to a simmer.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    1 ½ tsp pure maple syrup
  11. 11
    Once the sauce is simmering, reduce heat to medium. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until apple is soft and sauce has thickened slightly, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Place half in the reserved bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing. (Reserve remaining garlic for later use.)
    2 cloves garlic
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Add arugula and pumpkin seeds to the bowl with the dressing. Toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  14. 14
    When the parsnips are done, drain and return to the pan along with remaining garlic, milk, salt, and pepper. Mash until smooth.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, divide parsnip mash, chicken, and salad between plates. Top chicken with apples and sauce from the skillet. Enjoy!

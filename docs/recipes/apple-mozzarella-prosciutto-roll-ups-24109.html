Apple, Mozzarella & Prosciutto Roll-Ups

Meat Snacks
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (12) $0.79/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • 1Gala apple
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, quarter, and core apple; slice into 8 wedges.
    1 Gala apple
  2. 2
    Thinly slice cheese into 8 pieces.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  3. 3
    Cut or tear prosciutto pieces in half lengthwise into 8 strips.
    4 slices prosciutto
  4. 4
    Place one apple wedge and one slice of cheese at the end of each strip of prosciutto, then roll up.
  5. 5
    To serve, divide roll-ups between plates. Enjoy!

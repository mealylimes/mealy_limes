Apple Slices with Cinnamon-Honey Peanut Butter

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 7 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (19) $1.06/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • 2Gala apples
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place peanut butter, honey, and cinnamon in a small bowl. Mash and combine with the back of a spoon until smooth. Set aside.
    ¼ cup natural peanut butter
    2 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, quarter, and core apples; slice into wedges.
    2 Gala apples
  3. 3
    Divide apples between plates. Serve with cinnamon-honey peanut butter for dipping. Enjoy!

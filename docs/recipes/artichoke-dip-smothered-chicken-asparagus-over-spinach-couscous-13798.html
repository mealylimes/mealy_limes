"Artichoke Dip" Smothered Chicken & Asparagus over Spinach Couscous

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 93% (17) $3.79/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • ½ small bunchasparagus
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts silicone spatula spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking dish with a thin layer of oil.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Wash and dry spinach (skip if it came pre-washed); roughly chop half and transfer to a medium bowl. Set remaining spinach aside.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Drain and coarsely chop artichokes; add to the bowl with the spinach.
    ½ (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  4. 4
    Cut cream cheese into small pieces and add to the bowl along with spices. Use a silicone spatula or spoon to mash and thoroughly combine the artichoke dip.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place chicken on a cutting board. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin cutlets. Place cutlets in the baking dish and season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Spread artichoke dip over the cutlets.
  7. 7
    Wash and dry asparagus. Using a clean cutting board, snap or cut off woody ends; divide and place on top of each cutlet.
    ½ small bunch asparagus
  8. 8
    Coarsely grate mozzarella; sprinkle over the chicken and asparagus.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    Place baking dish in the oven and bake until chicken is cooked through and golden, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, in a medium saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Remove from heat, add couscous, and stir. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  11. 11
    Uncover the couscous and add remaining spinach. Gently stir spinach into couscous until slightly wilted.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide couscous between plates or bowls and top with smothered chicken. Enjoy!

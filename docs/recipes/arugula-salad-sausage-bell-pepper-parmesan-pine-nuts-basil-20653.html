Arugula Salad with Sausage, Bell Pepper, Parmesan, Pine Nuts & Basil

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (4) $6.03/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 4sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Add oil, vinegar, and spices to the bowl with the basil; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, slice the sausages crosswise into ½-inch thick rounds.
    2 pork sausages
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add sausage and cook, turning occasionally, until golden brown and crispy, 5-6 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Thinly slice sun-dried tomatoes and add to the bowl.
    4 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  10. 10
    Finely grate Parmesan and add to the bowl.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Add arugula and pine nuts to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
  12. 12
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and top with crispy sausage. Enjoy!

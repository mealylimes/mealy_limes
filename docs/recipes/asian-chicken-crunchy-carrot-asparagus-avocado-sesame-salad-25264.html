Asian Chicken with Crunchy Carrot, Asparagus, Avocado & Sesame Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (15) $3.66/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • pure maple syrup
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • sesame seeds
  • ½avocado
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • mayonnaise
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 small bunch asparagus
    2 medium carrots
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl along with soy sauce, oil, chili-garlic sauce, and maple syrup. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
  4. 4
    Add chicken to the bowl with the sauce and turn to coat. Transfer to a baking dish and arrange in an even layer.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, flipping halfway though, until chicken is cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place mayo, vinegar, sesame seeds, oil, soy sauce, and chili-garlic sauce in a clean medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp sesame seeds
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
    1 ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  7. 7
    Snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears crosswise into thin coins. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Peel, trim, and slice carrots crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl and gently stir to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide chicken and salad between plates. Enjoy!

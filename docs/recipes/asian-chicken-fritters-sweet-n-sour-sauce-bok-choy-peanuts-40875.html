Asian Chicken Fritters with Sweet 'n' Sour Sauce, Bok Choy & Peanuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (27) $1.23/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • sesame seeds
  • ⅛ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • cornstarch
  • 4baby bok choy
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  2. 2
    Add chicken, bread crumbs, egg, chili-garlic sauce, oil, and spices to the bowl with the green onion. Using your hands, mix well to combine. Use a ¼-cup measure to form the chicken mixture into ½-inch thick fritters and place on a plate.
    ½ lb ground chicken
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    1 tbsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add fritters and pan-fry until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place water, additional chili-garlic sauce, vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, oil, and cornstarch in a small saucepan. Whisk to combine the sweet 'n' sour sauce.
    ¼ cup water
    1 tbsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    2 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp cornstarch
  6. 6
    Set pan over medium-high heat and bring sauce to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
  7. 7
    While the sauce is cooking, wash, dry, trim, and cut bok choy in half lengthwise.
    4 baby bok choy
  8. 8
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bok choy, cut-side down, then drizzle with soy sauce; cover and cook, flipping once, until tender and charred, 3-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tbsp soy sauce
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, roughly chop peanuts.
    ⅛ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  10. 10
    To serve, divide fritters, sauce, and bok choy between plates. Top bok choy with peanuts and sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

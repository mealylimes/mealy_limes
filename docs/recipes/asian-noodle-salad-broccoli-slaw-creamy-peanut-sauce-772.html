Asian Noodle Salad with Broccoli Slaw & Creamy Peanut Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (66) $1.06/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½lime
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 6 ozspaghetti pasta
  • crushed red pepper
  • honey
  • natural peanut butter
  • rice vinegar
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil
  • salt
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    7 cups water
    1 tbsp salt
    6 oz (1 quarter coin = 2 oz) spaghetti pasta
  2. 2
    Peel and mince the garlic. To prepare the sauce, in a small bowl, combine and whisk together the garlic, water, peanut butter, vinegar, soy sauce, honey, oil, and crushed red pepper.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tbsp warm water
    4 tbsp natural peanut butter
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    ½ tbsp honey
    ½ tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  3. 3
    Toss the broccoli slaw into a large bowl. Wash and dry the cilantro. Shave the leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the broccoli slaw.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  4. 4
    Wash and cut the lime into wedges. Roughly chop the peanuts.
    ½ lime
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  5. 5
    Drain the pasta in a colander and rinse under cold water. Shake to drain excess liquid, then add to the bowl with the broccoli slaw.
  6. 6
    Pour the peanut sauce over top and toss to coat.
  7. 7
    To serve, divide the noodles between bowls (or containers) and garnish with peanuts and lime wedges. Enjoy!

Asian Pork Stir-Fry with Pineapple, Bok Choy & Coconut Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $3.49/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • onion powder
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • 4baby bok choy
  • ⅛ cupshredded unsweetened coconut
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and cut bok choy crosswise into 1-inch strips. Place in a medium bowl.
    4 baby bok choy
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, wash, dry, and cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and medium dice. Add to the bowl with the bok choy and set aside.
    ½ small pineapple
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes. Season with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork and stir-fry until golden and almost cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place soy sauce, vinegar, cornstarch, chili-garlic sauce, sugar, and onion powder in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the stir-fry sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp onion powder
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bok choy and pineapple; stir-fry until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    Return pork to the skillet along with the stir-fry sauce. Continue to stir-fry until pork is cooked through and sauce thickens slightly, 2-3 minutes more. Cover and remove from heat.
  11. 11
    Just before serving, fluff rice with a fork. Add coconut and stir to combine.
    ⅛ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Asian-Style Mushroom Frittata with Cilantro & Cucumber-Avocado Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (9) $3.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1avocado
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • ¾ lbcremini mushrooms
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ lime
    ½ English cucumber
    1 avocado
  2. 2
    Thinly slice mushrooms. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add both to the bowl with the mushrooms.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms, ginger, garlic, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms are tender, 6-7 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in the reserved bowl along with eggs and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine and set aside.
    6 eggs
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  8. 8
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces.
  9. 9
    When the mushrooms are tender, add green onions (reserving a small handful for serving), soy sauce, fish sauce, and sugar to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
  10. 10
    Reduce heat to medium-low; pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, 3-5 minutes.
  11. 11
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until top is set, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a clean medium bowl. Add oil, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  14. 14
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    Cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Sprinkle with reserved green onions and serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!

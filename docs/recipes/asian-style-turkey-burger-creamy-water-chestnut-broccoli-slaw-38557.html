Asian-Style Turkey Burger with Creamy Water Chestnut & Broccoli Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (25) $2.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 ozwater chestnuts
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • honey
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • mayonnaise
  • ½green bell pepper
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ green bell pepper
    ½ lime
  2. 2
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the ginger and garlic along with turkey, bread crumbs, soy sauce, and spices; mix with your hands until well combined. Form the turkey mixture into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add burger patties and cook, undisturbed, for 5 minutes; flip patties and cook an additional 5-7 minutes, until cooked through. Once done, transfer to a clean plate.
    1 ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place mayo and chili-garlic sauce in a small bowl. Stir to combine the spicy mayo and set aside.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  7. 7
    Zest and juice lime into a clean medium bowl. Add additional mayo, sesame seeds, honey, salt, and pepper, then stir to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp sesame seeds
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Drain and roughly chop water chestnuts. Add to the bowl with the dressing, along with broccoli slaw, and stir to combine. Set aside.
    2 oz water chestnuts
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  9. 9
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  10. 10
    Spread spicy mayo over the bottom half of each bun; top with burger patties and a small handful of slaw. Close with the top half of each bun. Serve with remaining slaw on the side and enjoy!

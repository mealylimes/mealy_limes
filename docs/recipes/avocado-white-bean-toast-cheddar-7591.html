Avocado White Bean Toast with Cheddar

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $1.60/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ⅛ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1avocado
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½lemon
  • 1tomato
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls potato masher stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    ½ (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 avocado
    ½ lemon
    1 tomato
  3. 3
    Halve and pit the avocado. Cut into chunks while still in the skin, then scoop out into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the avocado.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the same bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Add beans and spices to the bowl with the avocado; mash until well combined but still slightly chunky.
    ¼ tsp thyme
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Medium dice tomato.
  8. 8
    Slice cheese.
    ⅛ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    Toast bread if desired.
    2 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    To serve, divide toast between plates, top with cheese, avocado-bean mix, and tomatoes. Enjoy!

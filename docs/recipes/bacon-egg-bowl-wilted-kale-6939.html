Bacon & Egg Bowl with Wilted Kale

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $1.68/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 4eggs
  • ½ bunchkale
  • 6 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons nonstick skillet spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash kale, fold leaves in half lengthwise, and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and set aside.
    ½ bunch kale
  2. 2
    Preheat a stainless steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Chop bacon into 1-inch pieces; add to the skillet and cook until crispy, 4-5 minutes.
    6 slices bacon
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Once bacon is cooked, tilt the skillet and use a spoon to remove and discard half of the bacon drippings.
  6. 6
    Add kale, crushed red pepper, garlic, salt, and pepper to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionaly, until kale is wilted, 2-3 minutes.
    ½ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Place a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat and add butter.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Once the butter begins to bubble, gently crack the eggs into the skillet. Cook until the tops of the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, 3-4 minutes.
    4 eggs
  9. 9
    To serve, arrange kale, bacon, and eggs in bowls. Season with salt and pepper and enjoy!
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper

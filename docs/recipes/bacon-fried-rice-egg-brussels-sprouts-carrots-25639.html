Bacon Fried Rice with Egg, Brussels Sprouts & Carrots

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (38) $2.24/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • stir-fry sauce
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. When the rice is done, remove from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry Brussels sprouts; using a clean cutting board, trim and thinly slice. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl with the Brussels sprouts along with the carrots.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  8. 8
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat, then add veggies, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until veggies are fork-tender, 4-6 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    When the veggies are done, push to the sides of the skillet and crack eggs into the middle. Let sit for a few seconds and then cook, stirring constantly, until set and no longer runny. Remove from heat.
    2 eggs
  10. 10
    When the rice is done, add to the skillet, along with the bacon, and increase to medium-high heat. Add stir-fry sauce, stir to combine, and cook until warmed through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    ⅓ cup stir-fry sauce
  11. 11
    To serve, divide fried rice between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

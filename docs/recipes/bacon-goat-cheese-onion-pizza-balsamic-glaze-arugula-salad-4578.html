Bacon, Goat Cheese, Onion Pizza with Balsamic Glaze & Arugula Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (12) $2.92/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½lemon
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • brown sugar
  • 2 slicesbacon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls slotted spoon small saucepan spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Place vinegar and sugar in a small saucepan over medium-high heat; stir until sugar is dissolved. Continue to cook, stirring often, until reduced by about half, or until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, 10-15 minutes.
    ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Coarsely chop bacon and transfer to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes.
    2 slices bacon
  5. 5
    While the bacon is cooking, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half rings.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon from the skillet and transfer to a small bowl (leave cooking liquids in the skillet).
  7. 7
    Return skillet to medium-high heat; add onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until caramelized, about 7 minutes. Remove from heat.
  8. 8
    Place flatbreads on a baking sheet pan and drizzle with oil; top each flatbread with crumbled goat cheese, bacon, and onion. Place baking sheet in the oven and bake until flatbread become golden and crispy, about 10 minutes.
    2 flatbreads or naan
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash and dry arugula (skip if it came pre-washed). Transfer arugula to a medium bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper; toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Wash, dry, and slice lemon into wedges.
    ½ lemon
  11. 11
    When pizzas are done, remove from the oven; top each with a small handful of arugula salad and drizzle on balsamic glaze.
  12. 12
    Divide pizzas and remaining arugula salad between plates. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing over the salad. Enjoy!

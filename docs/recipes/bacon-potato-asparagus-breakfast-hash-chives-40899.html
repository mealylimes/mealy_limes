Bacon, Potato & Asparagus Breakfast Hash with Chives

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $2.69/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small bunchasparagus
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate, then set aside. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    ½ small pkg chives
    ½ small bunch asparagus
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and small dice potatoes.
  6. 6
    When the bacon is done, return the skillet (with drippings) to medium heat. Add potato, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 10 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    While the potato is cooking, chop chives into small pieces. Set aside.
  8. 8
    Snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears into 1-inch long pieces.
  9. 9
    When the potatoes are starting to soften, add asparagus, paprika, and additional salt to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until potatoes and asparagus are fork-tender, 5-6 minutes more.
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Return crispy bacon to the skillet, along with chives, and stir to combine the hash. Remove from heat.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide hash between plates and enjoy!

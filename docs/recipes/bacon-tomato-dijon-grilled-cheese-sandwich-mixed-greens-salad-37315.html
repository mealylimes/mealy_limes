Bacon, Tomato & Dijon Grilled Cheese Sandwich with Mixed Greens Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (101) $3.73/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • 1tomato
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
    1 tomato
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    4 slices bacon
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  7. 7
    Coarsely grate cheese.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Slice tomato crosswise into thin rounds.
  9. 9
    Place bread slices on a flat surface. Spread half of the bread with Dijon, then layer with cheddar, tomato, and bacon. Cover sandwiches with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  10. 10
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Place sandwiches in the skillet and cook until the bottoms are golden brown, 2-4 minutes.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Remove the sandwiches to a plate and add more oil to the skillet. Return sandwiches to the skillet, browned-side up, and cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese is melted, 2-4 minutes more. Once done, transfer to a cutting board and slice in half.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Add mixed greens to the bowl with dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad and sandwiches between plates. Enjoy!

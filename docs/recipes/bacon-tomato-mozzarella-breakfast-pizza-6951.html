Bacon, Tomato & Mozzarella Breakfast Pizza

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (6) $1.56/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1tomato
  • 2 slicesbacon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board grater oven mitts slotted spoon spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Chop the bacon into bite-sized pieces. Add to the skillet and cook until crispy, 3-4 minutes. When done, use a slotted spoon to transfer bacon to a plate lined with paper towels and pat dry.
    2 slices bacon
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and slice the tomato.
    1 tomato
  5. 5
    Grate cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  6. 6
    Place flatbread on a baking sheet; arrange tomatoes, cheese, and bacon on the flatbread, leaving the center open for the egg.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  7. 7
    Gently break an egg into the center of each flatbread. Bake until eggs are set, about 8 minutes.
    2 eggs
  8. 8
    To serve, transfer pizzas to plates and season with salt and pepper. Enjoy!
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper

Bacon Wrapped Halibut with Roasted Potatoes & Brussels Sprout Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $5.36/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ¾ lbhalibut fillet
  • ½lemon
  • ½ lbnew potatoes
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater mandoline slicer (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Position racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb new potatoes
    ½ lemon
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
  3. 3
    Quarter potatoes and transfer to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil and season with spices; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet on lower rack of the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until potatoes are fork-tender, 20-25 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the lemon juice, along with oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Pat halibut dry with paper towels, cut into portions (if necessary), and transfer to another baking sheet. Season with spices, then wrap 2 slices of bacon around each portion; drizzle with oil.
    ¾ lb halibut fillet
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    4 slices bacon
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet on upper rack of the oven and roast until the fish is almost cooked through, about 12 minutes.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, thinly slice Brussels sprouts crosswise and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Finely grate Parmesan and add to the bowl.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Add almonds to the bowl, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
  12. 12
    When the fish is almost cooked through, change oven setting to broil on high, and continue to cook until the bacon is crisp and fish is cooked through, about 5 minutes.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide halibut, potatoes, and Brussels sprout salad between plates. Enjoy!

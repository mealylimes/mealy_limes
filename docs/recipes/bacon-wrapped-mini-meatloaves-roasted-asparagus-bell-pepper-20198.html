Bacon-Wrapped Mini Meatloaves with Roasted Asparagus & Bell Pepper

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (8) $4.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Arrange racks in the upper and middle positions.
  2. 2
    Cut bacon in half crosswise and place on a plate.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl along with beef, Dijon, and spices. Using your hands, mix well to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Using 2 generous tablespoons per meatloaf, form the beef mixture into 8 short, sausage-like shapes, approximately 3-inches long. Wrap each meatloaf with bacon and transfer, seam-side down, to a baking sheet pan.
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet on the top rack of the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until meatloaves are just cooked through, 18-20 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; transfer spears to another baking sheet.
    1 small bunch asparagus
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; slice lengthwise into thin strips and add to the baking sheet with the asparagus. Drizzle veggies with oil, then season with salt and pepper; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 red bell pepper
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet on the lower rack of the oven and roast, tossing halfway through, until veggies are tender, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    When the meatloaves are done, change oven to broil on high; broil until bacon is crisp, about 2 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide meatloaves and veggies between plates; enjoy!

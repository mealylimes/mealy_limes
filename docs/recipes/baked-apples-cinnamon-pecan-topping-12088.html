Baked Apples with Cinnamon-Pecan Topping

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $1.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • brown sugar
  • 2Gala apples
  • ⅓ cuppecan halves
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Coat a small baking dish with a thin layer of butter.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and halve each apple lengthwise; remove stem and core.
    2 Gala apples
  3. 3
    Place apples, cut-side down, in the baking dish. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until apples are partially cooked through, about 15 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place flour, sugar, cinnamon, and salt in a medium bowl; stir to combine.
    ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
    3 tbsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
    1 pinch salt
  5. 5
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate cold butter into the bowl with the flour. Using two hands, combine the flour and butter by rubbing the mixture between your thumbs and fingers until it looks crumbly.
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
  6. 6
    Roughly chop pecans and add to the bowl; use your hands to combine the crumble.
    ⅓ cup pecans, dry roasted
  7. 7
    Remove apples from the oven and carefully flip over, so the cut-side is facing up. Divide crumble between apples, forming a mound on top of each half. Return to the oven and bake until crumble is golden and apples are fork-tender, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide warm apples between plates and enjoy!

Baked BBQ Salmon with Cajun-Spiced Broccoli, Bell Pepper & Rice

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (15) $4.05/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • barbecue sauce
  • Cajun seasoning
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, pat salmon dry with paper towels. Place on one half of a baking sheet pan and spread barbecue sauce over top.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    2 tbsp barbecue sauce
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; halve crosswise, then slice into thin strips. Add bell pepper and broccoli to the other half of the baking sheet.
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  6. 6
    Drizzle veggies with oil, then sprinkle with Cajun seasoning and salt. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until veggies are fork-tender and salmon is cooked through, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide rice, salmon, and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

Baked Beef & Zucchini Meatballs with Smoky Bacon-Fried Cabbage

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (18) $3.64/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • onion powder
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking dish with a thin layer of oil.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
  3. 3
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in a medium bowl. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  4. 4
    Trim and coarsely grate zucchini. Add to the bowl with the parsley along with beef, Worcestershire, and spices; mix with your hands until well combined. Using a generous tablespoon measure, form the beef mixture into rounded meatballs. Transfer to the baking dish.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  5. 5
    Place meatballs in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until golden and cooked through, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place in a clean medium bowl and set aside.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  9. 9
    When the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and crispy, 4-5 minutes.
  10. 10
    When the bacon is done, add cabbage and spices to the skillet, then stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until cabbage is tender, 6-8 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    To serve, divide meatballs and bacon-fried cabbage between plates or bowls. Sprinkle with reserved parsley and enjoy!

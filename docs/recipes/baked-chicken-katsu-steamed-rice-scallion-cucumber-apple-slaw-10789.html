Baked Chicken Katsu with Steamed Rice & Scallion-Cucumber-Apple Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (26) $3.43/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • all-purpose flour
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1English cucumber
  • sesame seeds
  • rice vinegar
  • ketchup
  • brown sugar
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan spatula tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, in a small bowl, whisk the egg. Place flour and bread crumbs on two separate plates.
    1 egg
    ¼ cup all-purpose flour
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
  5. 5
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Dredge chicken fillets in flour, shake off excess, then dip into the bowl with the egg. Let the excess drip off, then dredge the chicken fillets in the bread crumbs, pressing to adhere. Transfer to the baking sheet.
  7. 7
    Place in the oven and bake for 12 minutes. Increase oven temperature to 450°F and continue to bake until the crust is golden brown and the chicken is cooked through, 4-6 minutes more. Watch carefully because the bread crumbs can burn quickly.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, peel and mince the garlic; place in a large bowl along with vinegar, oil, honey, and salt. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp honey
    1 pinch salt
  9. 9
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 English cucumber
    1 Granny Smith apple
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; thinly slice into match-like sticks. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Core, quarter, and cut the apple into thin match-like sticks. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Trim off and discard ends of the green onions. Slice onions lengthwise into long, thin strips, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw.
  13. 13
    In a small bowl, prepare the katsu sauce by whisking together ketchup, Worcestershire, sugar, and sesame seeds.
    1 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp sesame seeds
  14. 14
    Slice chicken into strips.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide rice, chicken, and slaw between bowls and drizzle with katsu sauce. Enjoy!

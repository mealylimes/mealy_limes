Baked Chicken with Puttanesca Sauce & Parmesan-Pine Nut Kale Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 56% (4) $4.13/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • ½ bunchkale
  • 3anchovy fillets, packed in oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add pine nuts and cook, stirring often, until golden and fragrant, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside to cool. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
  4. 4
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until chicken is golden and cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and thinly slice garlic; mince the anchovy fillets. Place both in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
    3 anchovy fillets, packed in oil
  7. 7
    Halve olives. Add to bowl with the garlic and anchovies along with capers and crushed red pepper.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    1 tbsp capers
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  8. 8
    Return skillet to medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add olive mixture and cook, stirring frequently, until garlic is fragrant, about 1 minute. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Add tomatoes and salt to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until simmering, about 1 minute. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low, and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 12-15 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, salt, and crushed red pepper in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  11. 11
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  12. 12
    Wash, dry, and fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl, along with cooled pine nuts, and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    ½ bunch kale
  13. 13
    Wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the puttanesca sauce and stir to combine. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving).
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  14. 14
    To serve, divide chicken and salad between plates. Top chicken with puttanesca sauce and reserved parsley. Enjoy!

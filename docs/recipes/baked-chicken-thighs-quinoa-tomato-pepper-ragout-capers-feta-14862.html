Baked Chicken Thighs with Quinoa, Tomato-Pepper Ragout, Capers & Feta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (8) $5.68/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 ½ tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry tomatoes; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, trim, seed, and large dice bell pepper; add to the baking sheet with the tomatoes.
    1 yellow bell pepper
  5. 5
    Peel and large dice onion; add to the baking sheet.
    1 medium red onion
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic; sprinkle over the veggies.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices. Toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides. Nestle chicken into the veggies on the baking sheet.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast until veggies are tender and chicken is cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from the oven.
  10. 10
    Transfer the baked chicken to a clean plate.
  11. 11
    Sprinkle capers over roasted veggies and stir to combine the ragout.
    1 ½ tbsp capers
  12. 12
    To serve, divide quinoa, chicken thighs, and ragout between plates; top with feta and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

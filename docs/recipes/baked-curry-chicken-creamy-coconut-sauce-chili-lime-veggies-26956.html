Baked Curry Chicken with Creamy Coconut Sauce & Chili-Lime Veggies

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (5) $3.37/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • ½red chili pepper
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    2 medium carrots
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ red chili pepper
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel, trim, and slice carrots crosswise into ¼-inch thick rounds. Add to the baking sheet with the cauliflower.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the baking sheet along with oil, salt, and pepper (if coconut oil is solid, melt in a small saucepan over low heat). Stir to coat the veggies, then spread out in an even layer.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and season with spices on both sides. Nestle chicken into the veggies on the baking sheet.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, stirring veggies and flipping chicken halfway through, until veggies are tender and chicken is cooked through, 15-20 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and additional garlic. Place both in a small saucepan along with coconut milk and spices. Stir to combine the sauce.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Set pan over high heat and bring sauce to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until reduced by half, about 8 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    While the sauce is simmering, thinly slice chili pepper into rings; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. (Be careful with chili peppers; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.) Place in a small bowl.
  11. 11
    Zest and juice lime into the bowl with the chili pepper.
  12. 12
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine.
  13. 13
    Once the veggies are tender, remove from oven and transfer chicken to a plate. Sprinkle chili-lime mixture over the veggies and toss to coat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide chicken and veggies between plates; spoon coconut sauce over chicken. Enjoy!

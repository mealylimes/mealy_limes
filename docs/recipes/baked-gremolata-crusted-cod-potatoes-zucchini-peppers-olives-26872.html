Baked Gremolata-Crusted Cod with Potatoes, Zucchini, Peppers & Olives

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 93% (7) $6.31/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • ⅓ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ¾ lbnew potatoes
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb new potatoes
    1 medium zucchini squash
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Quarter potatoes and transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and large dice onion. Add to the baking sheet with the potatoes, then drizzle with oil and season with spices. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until the potatoes start to soften, about 10 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, trim and large dice zucchini. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and large dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the zucchini and set aside.
  8. 8
    Zest lemon into a small bowl. Juice lemon into another small bowl.
  9. 9
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the lemon zest and half to the bowl with the lemon juice. (Set the bowl with the lemon juice aside for later use.)
  10. 10
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lemon zest along with bread crumbs, oil, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the gremolata topping.
    1 clove garlic
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Pat cod dry with paper towels and cut into portions (if necessary). Sprinkle cod with gremolata topping, pressing gently to adhere.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
  12. 12
    Once the potatoes have softened, remove from oven. Add zucchini and bell pepper to the baking sheet, stir to combine, and spread out in an even layer. Add cod to the baking sheet, nestling it into the veggies.
  13. 13
    Continue to bake until veggies are fork-tender, gremolata topping is golden brown, and cod is cooked through, 8-10 minutes more.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, add yogurt, mayo, and salt to the bowl with the lemon juice. Stir to combine the sauce.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp salt
  15. 15
    When the cod is done, remove baking sheet from the oven. Add olives to the veggies and stir to combine.
    ⅓ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  16. 16
    Divide cod and veggies between plates or bowls. Serve with sauce on the side and enjoy!

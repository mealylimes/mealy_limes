Baked Honey Mustard Chicken Thighs with Cauliflower & Snap Peas

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (20) $3.00/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ¼ cuphoney mustard dressing
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
Cookware
measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the chicken and cook until golden brown and almost cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate. (Reserve skillet with cooking juices for the next step.)
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Return skillet to medium heat. Add more butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add cauliflower, snap peas, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies begin to soften, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Nestle chicken into the veggies, then drizzle dressing over the chicken. Place skillet in the oven and bake until chicken is cooked through, 7-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ¼ cup honey mustard dressing
  7. 7
    To serve, divide chicken and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

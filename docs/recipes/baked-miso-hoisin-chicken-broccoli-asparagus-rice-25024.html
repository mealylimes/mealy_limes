Baked Miso-Hoisin Chicken, Broccoli & Asparagus with Rice

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (19) $3.51/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • hoisin sauce
  • 15 mlmiso paste, red
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger. Place in a medium bowl along with hoisin sauce, miso paste, oil, and spices. Stir to combine the sauce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    2 tbsp hoisin sauce
    1 tbsp miso paste, red
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, and snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus. Add to the bowl with the sauce along with the broccoli.
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  6. 6
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and large dice; add to the bowl. Stir chicken and veggies in the sauce to coat, then transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender and chicken is cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide rice, chicken, and veggies between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Baked Pears with Spiced Caramel Sauce, Whipped Cream & Pumpkin Seeds

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $1.56/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • honey
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2pears
  • brown sugar
  • ⅛ cuppumpkin seeds
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • granulated sugar
  • nutmeg, ground
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill. (If space is limited, place in the refrigerator.)
  2. 2
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, quarter, and core pears; medium dice and transfer to a small baking dish.
    2 pears
  4. 4
    Dot pears with small pieces of butter and place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated). Bake until pears are fork-tender, about 20 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, melt additional butter in a small saucepan over low heat.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  6. 6
    Once butter is melted, whisk in cream, sugar, honey, and spices. Increase heat to medium and simmer, whisking frequently, until sauce is slightly thickened and glossy, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool slightly to further thicken the sauce.
    1 fl oz (⅛ cup) heavy cream
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    1 tbsp honey
    1 pinch cinnamon
    1 pinch nutmeg, ground
    1 pinch salt
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, remove bowl from the freezer. Pour cold cream into the bowl; use a whisk to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, about 2 minutes. (For medium-soft peaks, when you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.)
    3 fl oz (⅜ cup) heavy cream
    ½ tsp granulated sugar
  8. 8
    To serve, divide baked pears between bowls; top with caramel sauce, whip cream, and pumpkin seeds. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup pumpkin seeds

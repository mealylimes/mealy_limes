Baked Pesto Tofu over Tomato, Cucumber, Onion, Artichoke & Feta Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (6) $4.64/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • ¼ cupbasil pesto
  • 45 mlFrench vinaigrette
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on another baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  4. 4
    Medium dice tofu and place in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with salt, drizzle with pesto, and gently stir to coat.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ cup basil pesto
  5. 5
    Sprinkle bread crumbs over the tofu and stir once more to coat. Transfer tofu to the baking sheet and spread out in a single layer. (Wipe the bowl clean with paper towels and set aside for the salad.)
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing halfway through, until tofu is golden brown, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion. Place in the reserved bowl, along with vinaigrette, and toss to coat.
    ½ medium red onion
    3 tbsp French vinaigrette
  8. 8
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the onion.
  9. 9
    Trim and medium dice cucumber; add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Drain and thinly slice artichokes; add to the bowl.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  11. 11
    Just before serving, add mixed greens and feta to the bowl with the other veggies; toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  12. 12
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with tofu and enjoy!

Baked Spicy Cajun Chicken with Creamed Corn & Green Beans

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (30) $1.53/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • ¾ lbchicken drumsticks
  • onion powder
  • 1 ½ cupsfrozen corn
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. 2
    Combine flour and spices in a large bowl.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    1 ½ tsp paprika
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp onion powder
    1 tsp thyme
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  3. 3
    Coat chicken with oil. Add drumsticks one by one to the bowl with the spices, rolling and pressing to adhere on all sides. Place chicken on the baking sheet.
    ¾ lb chicken drumsticks
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, heat a small saucepan over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Add corn to the colander and rinse under hot water for several minutes to defrost. Set aside to drain.
    1 ½ cups frozen corn
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Add flour and whisk until smooth. Slowly add cream, whisking to make sure no lumps form. Stir in corn, salt, and pepper. Cover pan; remove from heat and set aside.
    1 tsp all-purpose flour
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy cream
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Wash green beans and trim ends.
    ½ lb green beans
  10. 10
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  12. 12
    Add green beans to the skillet and season with salt and pepper. Cook, tossing frequently, until bright green and tender-crisp, 3-4 minutes. Remove from the heat.
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the chicken, creamed corn, and green beans between plates. Enjoy!

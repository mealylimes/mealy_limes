Baked Tofu Rice Bowl with Edamame, Cilantro & Spicy Peanut Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $2.15/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • natural peanut butter
  • ½shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • rice vinegar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    While the rice is cooking, place oil and spices in a medium bowl; stir to combine the spice paste.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
  6. 6
    Medium dice tofu, add to the bowl with the spice paste, and gently toss to coat. Transfer tofu to a baking sheet and spread out in a single layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing halfway through, until tofu is golden brown, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place peanut butter, soy sauce, water, vinegar, oil, and chili-garlic sauce in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp natural peanut butter
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tbsp water
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  9. 9
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 medium carrot
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; place on a plate.
  11. 11
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot; add to the plate with the bell pepper.
  12. 12
    Peel and thinly slice shallot lengthwise; add to the plate and set aside.
    ½ shallot
  13. 13
    Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain further.
    1 cup edamame, shelled
  14. 14
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide rice between bowls; top with baked tofu, bell pepper, carrot, shallot, edamame, and cilantro. Drizzle with sauce and enjoy!

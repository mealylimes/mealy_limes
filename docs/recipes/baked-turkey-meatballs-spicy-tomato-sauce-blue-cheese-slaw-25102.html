Baked Turkey Meatballs with Spicy Tomato Sauce & Blue Cheese Slaw

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 25% (1) $2.25/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • ¼ cupblue cheese dressing
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  • hot sauce
Cookware
baking dish can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place tomato sauce and hot sauce in a small baking dish. Stir to combine and set aside.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    1 ½ tbsp hot sauce
  3. 3
    Place turkey, bread crumbs, oil, garlic powder, and salt in a medium bowl. Using your hands, mix well to combine.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Using a scant ¼-cup measure, form the turkey mixture into rounded meatballs. Transfer to the baking dish with the sauce and turn to coat.
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring meatballs once, until golden and cooked through, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice green onions crosswise. Place half in a clean medium bowl. (Reserve remaining green onions for serving.)
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice celery crosswise. Add to the bowl with the green onions.
    2 sticks celery
  8. 8
    Add cabbage, dressing, and almonds to the bowl. Stir to combine the slaw.
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    ¼ cup blue cheese dressing
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
  9. 9
    To serve, divide saucy meatballs and slaw between plates. Top meatballs with reserved green onions and enjoy!

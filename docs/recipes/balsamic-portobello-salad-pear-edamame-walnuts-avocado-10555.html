Balsamic Portobello Salad with Pear, Edamame, Walnuts & Avocado

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 60% (5) $4.94/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • 1pear
  • ⅛ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • ½ cupwalnuts
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 pear
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    1 avocado
  2. 2
    Place frozen edamame in a medium bowl, cover with hot water (from the tap), and let sit 5 minutes. Drain in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 cup edamame
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    In a salad bowl, whisk together garlic, nutritional yeast, vinegar, oil, maple syrup, Dijon, salt, and pepper.
    ⅛ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 ½ tbsp balsamic vinegar
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Remove the mushroom stems and carefully scrape out the gills. Transfer to the salad bowl and toss to coat.
  6. 6
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Peel and slice the onion into rings; add to the bowl with the mushrooms and toss to coat.
    ½ medium red onion
  8. 8
    Using tongs, transfer the mushrooms and onions to the grill pan, leaving the excess marinade in the bowl. Cook until vegetables are tender, 3-5 minutes per side. Remove from heat and transfer mushrooms to a cutting board to cool slightly.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, core and chop the pear; add to the salad bowl.
  10. 10
    Add mixed greens, edamame, and walnuts to the salad bowl.
    ½ cup walnuts
  11. 11
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise and lengthwise while still in the skin and scoop out into the salad bowl.
  12. 12
    Add onions to the salad and toss. Slice mushrooms into long strips.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the salad between plates and top with sliced mushrooms. Enjoy!

Balsamic Steak-Tomato Skewers with Steamed Cauliflower & Tzatziki

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $5.16/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 30 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan metal or wooden skewers (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry tomatoes; set aside.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  3. 3
    Pat steak dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  4. 4
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the grill pan heats up, thread the steak and tomatoes onto skewers, alternating ingredients; place on a plate. Drizzle with vinaigrette and season with salt and pepper. (If you don't have skewers, you can cook the steak and tomatoes directly on the grill pan.)
    2 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the grill pan is hot, add the skewers. Cook, turning occasionally, until the steak reaches desired doneness and tomatoes are starting to burst, 6-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan with about 1-inch of hot water (from the tap). Cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the water is boiling, add cauliflower. Re-cover and cook until tender, about 5 minutes; drain and return to the pan. Drizzle with oil, season with spices, and toss to combine; cover to keep warm.
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot.
    ½ shallot
  10. 10
    Divide skewers and cauliflower between plates, then sprinkle with shallots; serve with tzatziki on the side for dipping. Enjoy!
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce

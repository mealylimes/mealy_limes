Balsamic Tofu Veggie Kebabs with Quinoa & Minted Yogurt Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $3.80/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • virgin coconut oil
  • salt
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • ¼ lbwhite mushrooms
  • ¾ cupquinoa
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and water; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ¾ cup quinoa
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) water
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic. Wash and dry mint. Pick the leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. In a large bowl, prepare marinade by whisking together ½ of the garlic and mint (save the rest for the sauce), oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
    3 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Drain and medium dice tofu; place on paper towels and pat dry. Add to bowl with marinade and toss to coat. Set aside.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  4. 4
    Wash and dry bell pepper and mushrooms. Seed and large dice the bell pepper. Halve the mushrooms. Peel and large dice onion. Add vegetables to bowl with tofu and toss.
    1 red bell pepper
    ¼ lb white mushrooms
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Thread tofu and vegetables onto skewers. Alternatively, you can cook the tofu and vegetable pieces directly on the grill/skillet without skewers.
  7. 7
    Coat grill or bottom of skillet with oil. Add tofu and vegetables; cook, turning occasionally, until tofu is browned and vegetables are tender, 8-10 minutes total.
    about 1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    In a small bowl, prepare sauce by whisking together yogurt, salt, pepper, and remaining garlic and mint.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    To serve, divide quinoa and kebabs between dishes. Enjoy with yogurt sauce on the side!

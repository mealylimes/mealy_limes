Banana, Apricot & Almond Oatmeal Protein Bites

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (6) $0.80/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupalmonds
  • cinnamon, ground
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1egg
  • 6dried apricots
  • 1banana
  • oats, quick cooking
  • cardamom, ground
  • vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper potato masher spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Peel banana, break into chunks, and place in a medium bowl. Mash with a fork or potato masher until smooth.
    1 banana
  3. 3
    Add egg and maple syrup to the bowl with the banana. Stir to combine.
    1 egg
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
  4. 4
    Roughly chop apricots and almonds. Add both to the bowl along with oats, protein powder, cinnamon, and cardamom. Stir once more to combine the batter.
    6 dried apricots
    ¼ cup almonds
    ¾ cup oats, quick cooking
    1 serving vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp cardamom, ground
  5. 5
    Using a ¼-cup measure, scoop batter onto the baking sheet, leaving some space between. Use the back of a spoon to spread each "bite" into a 3-inch circle. Place in the oven and bake until just firm to the touch, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Allow to cool for a few minutes before transferring to plates. Enjoy!

Banana-Kiwi Dessert Pizza with Chocolate Tahini Spread & Coconut

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $1.06/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 2kiwifruit
  • ⅛ cupshredded unsweetened coconut
  • 1banana
  • cocoa powder
  • tahini
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place tahini, maple syrup, cocoa powder, cinnamon, and salt in a small bowl. Whisk to combine and set aside.
    3 tbsp tahini
    1 ½ tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tbsp cocoa powder
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    1 pinch salt
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, peel, and trim kiwis; slice crosswise into ½-inch thick rounds.
    2 kiwifruit
  5. 5
    Peel and slice banana crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces.
    1 banana
  6. 6
    Spread tahini mixture over each flatbread, then top with kiwifruit, banana, and coconut.
    ⅛ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
  7. 7
    To serve, slice pizzas into wedges and divide between plates. Enjoy!

Banana Split with Homemade Chocolate Sauce, Walnuts & Cherries

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $0.32/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 fl ozwhole milk
  • ⅛ cupwalnuts
  • 2bananas
  • granulated sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cupdark chocolate chips
  • ½ cupfrozen cherries
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place cherries in a colander and run under cold water to thaw; set aside to drain.
    ½ cup frozen cherries
  2. 2
    Place milk, chocolate, sugar, and vanilla in a small saucepan. Slowly melt chocolate over low heat, stirring constantly, until sauce is combined and thickened, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    ⅓ cup dark chocolate chips
    1 ½ tsp granulated sugar
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
  3. 3
    Roughly chop nuts.
    ⅛ cup walnuts
  4. 4
    Roughly chop cherries.
  5. 5
    Peel bananas and halve lengthwise.
    2 bananas
  6. 6
    To serve, divide bananas and ice cream between bowls. Top with chocolate sauce, walnuts, and cherries. Enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

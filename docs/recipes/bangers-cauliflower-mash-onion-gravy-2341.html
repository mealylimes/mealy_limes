Bangers & Cauliflower Mash with Onion Gravy

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $4.01/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 medium headcauliflower
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • cornstarch
  • salt
  • 4pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand blender or potato masher measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. 2
    Wash cauliflower and cut off the leaves. Separate into small florets and cut the thick stem into smaller pieces. Transfer to a bowl.
    1 medium head cauliflower
  3. 3
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Add sausages to skillet and cook until browned on all sides, 2-3 minutes. Reduce heat to medium, add water, and continue cooking until cooked (or heated) through, 6-8 minutes. Alternatively, cook according to package directions. Transfer to a plate.
    4 pork sausages
    ⅛ cup water
  5. 5
    Add cauliflower to boiling water and cook until very tender, 6-7 minutes.
  6. 6
    Thinly slice onion into half rings.
    1 medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Once sausages have been transferred, add butter and onion to skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until onion is softened and caramelized, about 10 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Drain cauliflower in a colander, then return to saucepan. Add water, garlic, butter, salt, and pepper. Blend using a hand/immersion blender or a potato masher. Cover and set aside.
    ⅛ cup water
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Sprinkle onions with cornstarch and mix until cornstarch is evenly incorporated with the onions.
    1 tsp cornstarch
  11. 11
    Pour in broth and cook until mixture is thickened, 4-5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  12. 12
    Wash and dry parsley. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and chop the leaves.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  13. 13
    Return sausages to skillet on top of the onion gravy and turn until heated through.
  14. 14
    To serve, place cauliflower purée in a shallow bowl. Add sausages and top with onion gravy. Garnish with parsley and enjoy!

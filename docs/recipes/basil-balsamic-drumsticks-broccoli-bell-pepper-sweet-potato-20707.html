Basil-Balsamic Drumsticks with Broccoli, Bell Pepper & Sweet Potato

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $3.16/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • ½lemon
  • 1yellow bell pepper
Cookware
baking dish baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    1 crown broccoli
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ lb sweet potato
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a baking dish.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Juice lemon into the baking dish with the garlic; add vinegar, oil, and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels. Add to the baking dish with the marinade and turn to coat.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
  6. 6
    Place chicken in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  8. 8
    Peel, trim, and small dice sweet potato; add to the baking sheet with the broccoli.
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into ½-inch thick strips; add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Add veggies to the oven and roast, tossing halfway through, until lightly charred and fork-tender, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide drumsticks and veggies between plates; drizzle with sauce from the baking dish and enjoy!

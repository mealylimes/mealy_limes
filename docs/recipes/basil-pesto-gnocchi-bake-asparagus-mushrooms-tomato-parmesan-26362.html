Basil Pesto Gnocchi Bake with Asparagus, Mushrooms, Tomato & Parmesan

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.82/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • ¼ cupbasil pesto
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place pesto, oil, salt, and pepper in a small bowl, then stir to combine. Set aside.
    ¼ cup basil pesto
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, and snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut crosswise into 2-inch pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    ½ small bunch asparagus
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and add tomatoes to the baking sheet with the asparagus.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the baking sheet along with the mushrooms and gnocchi. Drizzle with the pesto oil, stir to combine, and spread out in an even layer.
    1 medium red onion
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are tender and gnocchi is golden, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    To serve, divide gnocchi bake between plates or bowls and top with Parmesan. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

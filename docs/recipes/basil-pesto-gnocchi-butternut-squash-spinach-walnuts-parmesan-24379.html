Basil Pesto Gnocchi with Butternut Squash, Spinach, Walnuts & Parmesan

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $3.08/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • ¼ cupbasil pesto
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 2 cupsfrozen butternut squash, pieces
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, place butternut squash in a colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost.
    2 cup frozen butternut squash, pieces
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add squash and gnocchi to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi is starting to brown, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
  4. 4
    Add broth to the skillet, cover, and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium and continue to cook until squash and gnocchi are tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed, 3-4 minutes more.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, roughly chop walnuts. Set aside.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  6. 6
    Wash and dry spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  7. 7
    When the squash and gnocchi are done, add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  8. 8
    Add walnuts, pesto, and Parmesan to the skillet. Stir to combine and remove from heat.
    ¼ cup basil pesto
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
  9. 9
    To serve, divide gnocchi between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

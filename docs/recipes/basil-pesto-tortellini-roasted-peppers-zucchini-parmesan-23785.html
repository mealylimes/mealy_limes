Basil Pesto Tortellini with Roasted Peppers, Zucchini & Parmesan

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (28) $1.35/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • 80 mlbasil pesto
  • 9 ozcheese tortellini, fresh or frozen
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and medium dice zucchini. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    2 medium zucchini squash
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into thin pieces. Add to the baking sheet with the zucchini.
    1 medium red onion
  4. 4
    Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers, then add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices; stir to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until zucchini are golden and fork-tender, 15-18 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add tortellini, and stir for a few seconds. Cook, stirring occasionally, until just tender, about 5 minutes (or cook according to package instructions).
    9 oz cheese tortellini, fresh or frozen
  7. 7
    When the tortellini is done, drain and return to the pan. Cover and set aside to keep warm.
  8. 8
    When the veggies are done, remove from the oven. Add roasted veggies and pesto to the pan with the tortellini; stir to combine.
    ⅓ cup basil pesto
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and top with Parmesan. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

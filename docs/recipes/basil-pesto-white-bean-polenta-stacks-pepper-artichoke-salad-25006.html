Basil Pesto-White Bean Polenta Stacks with Pepper & Artichoke Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $1.10/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • ¼ cupbasil pesto
  • 30 mlItalian salad dressing
  • 1 (18 oz) tubepolenta, pre-cooked
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Slice polenta crosswise into ½-inch thick rounds. Transfer to the baking sheet, arranging the slices in a single layer.
    1 (18 oz) tube polenta, pre-cooked
  3. 3
    Place polenta in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) while you prepare the topping.
  4. 4
    Drain and rinse beans. Place in a medium bowl along with pesto, salt, and pepper; stir to combine.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
    ¼ cup basil pesto
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Remove baking sheet from the oven. Spoon the bean topping over the polenta slices, then top with cheese. (Wipe bowl clean with paper towels and reserve for later use.)
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  6. 6
    Return baking sheet to the oven and bake until polenta stacks are warmed through and cheese is melted, 6-7 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash and dry spring mix (skip if it came pre-washed). Place in the reserved bowl along with the carrots.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  8. 8
    Drain and roughly chop artichokes and roasted peppers. Add both to the bowl with the mixed greens, along with the dressing, and toss to combine the salad.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    2 tbsp Italian salad dressing
  9. 9
    To serve, divide polenta stacks and salad between plates. Enjoy!

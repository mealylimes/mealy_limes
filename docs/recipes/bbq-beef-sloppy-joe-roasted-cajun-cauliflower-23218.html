BBQ Beef Sloppy Joe with Roasted Cajun Cauliflower

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 74% (45) $2.35/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • barbecue sauce
  • Cajun seasoning
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place cauliflower on a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, then sprinkle with Cajun seasoning and salt. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Cajun seasoning
    1 pinch salt
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until cauliflower is golden and fork-tender, 20-25 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add beef, salt, and pepper. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Set aside.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  7. 7
    When the beef is done, add barbecue sauce and stir to combine. Continue to cook until heated through and slightly thickened, 1-2 minutes more. Cover with a lid and remove from heat.
    ½ cup barbecue sauce
  8. 8
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  9. 9
    When the cauliflower is done, remove from the oven, add parsley, and stir to combine.
  10. 10
    Place bottom half of each bun on a plate. Top with barbecue beef, then close with the top half of the buns. Serve roasted cauliflower on the side. Enjoy!

BBQ Cannellini Beans with Pork Sausage & Creamy Napa Cabbage Coleslaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (12) $2.14/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • Dijon mustard
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • onion powder
  • mayonnaise
  • ketchup
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • brown sugar
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with yogurt, mayo, vinegar, Dijon, sugar, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Arrange sausages on a baking sheet pan and place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated); bake, flipping once, until sausages are golden and cooked through, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 pork sausages
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry cabbage. Trim off and discard the root end, halve lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, peel, and coarsely grate carrot; add to to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw. Set aside.
    1 medium carrot
  6. 6
    Peel and mince additional garlic. Peel and small dice onion.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, drain and rinse beans.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and onion; cook, stirring often, until starting to soften, about 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place water, ketchup, vinegar, sugar, Worcestershire, Dijon, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the BBQ sauce.
    ½ cup water
    2 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp brown sugar
    1 ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch salt
  11. 11
    Add beans and BBQ sauce to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring often, until liquid has reduced to a saucy consistency, about 5 minutes.
  12. 12
    When the sausages are done, transfer to a cutting board and slice crosswise into rounds. Add to the skillet with the beans, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide slaw, beans, and sausage between plates. Enjoy!

BBQ Pork Chop with Corn on the Cob & Creamy Broccoli Slaw

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (37) $2.99/serving
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • 2 earscorn
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • mayonnaise
  • barbecue sauce
  • 22 ½ mlhoney mustard dressing
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet large pot measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, remove husks and silks from corn; set aside.
    2 ears corn
  3. 3
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the grill pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season both sides with salt and pepper, then coat with BBQ sauce.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 ½ tbsp barbecue sauce
  5. 5
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with butter; add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and coat with additional BBQ sauce.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    2 tbsp barbecue sauce
  6. 6
    Once the water is boiling, uncover the pot and reduce heat to medium; use tongs to lower the corn into the water and cook until just tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash and dry parsley, then shave leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl along with dressing and mayo; whisk to combine.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 ½ tbsp honey mustard dressing
    2 tsp mayonnaise
  8. 8
    Add broccoli slaw to the bowl with dressing and stir to combine.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pork chops, corn, and slaw between plates; spread corn with butter and sprinkle with salt. Enjoy!
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    ⅛ tsp salt

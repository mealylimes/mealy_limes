BBQ Pork Chops & Napa Cabbage "Wedge Salad" with Blue Cheese Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (6) $4.34/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • onion powder
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ketchup
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • brown sugar
  • 1Gala apple
Cookware
aluminum foil broiler pan or baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Place ketchup, Worcestershire, sugar, vinegar, Dijon, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the BBQ sauce.
    3 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a broiler pan. Pour ⅔ of the BBQ sauce over the chops and turn to coat. Set remaining sauce aside.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
  4. 4
    Place pork chops in the oven and broil until the tops start to brown and caramelize, about 6 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place yogurt, mayo, vinegar, Dijon, sugar, and spices in another small bowl; whisk until smooth.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    3 pinch salt
  6. 6
    Add blue cheese and stir to combine the dressing; set aside.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  7. 7
    Remove pork chops from the oven, flip, and coat with remaining BBQ sauce. Return to the oven and broil until cooked through, 6-8 minutes more. Remove from oven, loosely cover with foil, and rest for 5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    1 Gala apple
    ½ small pkg chives
  9. 9
    Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage. Cut cabbage in half lengthwise, to make two wedges.
  10. 10
    Quarter, core, and small dice apple.
  11. 11
    Chop chives into small pieces.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pork chops and cabbage wedges between plates; top wedges with dressing, apples, and chives. Enjoy!

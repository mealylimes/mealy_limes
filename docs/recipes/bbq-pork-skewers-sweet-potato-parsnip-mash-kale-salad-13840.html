BBQ Pork Skewers with Sweet Potato-Parsnip Mash & Kale Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $2.75/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2 fl ozwhole milk
  • virgin coconut oil
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • onion powder
  • ½ bunchkale
  • 1 mediumparsnip
  • ketchup
  • brown sugar
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls potato masher tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    1 medium parsnip
    ½ bunch kale
  3. 3
    Peel and medium dice sweet potato and parsnip. Transfer to a medium saucepan, cover with hot water (from the tap), and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium and cook until veggies can be easily pierced with a fork, about 15 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place ketchup, Worcestershire, sugar, vinegar, Dijon, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the BBQ sauce.
    3 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board; cut into 1-inch pieces. Thread pork onto skewers and place on a plate; alternatively, you can cook the pork pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  6. 6
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the grill pan heats up, pour half the BBQ sauce over the skewers and turn to coat. Reserve remaining sauce for serving.
  8. 8
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Add skewers and grill, rotating every 2½-3 minutes, until browned and cooked through, 10-12 minutes. Transfer to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    1 tsp virgin coconut oil
  9. 9
    When the sweet potatoes and parsnips are done, drain, and return to the pan.
  10. 10
    Add milk, oil, salt, and pepper to the sweet potatoes and parsnips; mash until smooth. Cover and set aside.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Peel and mince garlic; transfer to a medium bowl. Add oil and mash with the back of a spoon until soft and combined.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  12. 12
    Add vinegar, Dijon, sugar, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the garlic; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Finely chop leaves and transfer to the bowl with the dressing; toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pork skewers, sweet potato-parsnip mash, and kale salad between plates. Top skewers with reserved BBQ sauce and enjoy!

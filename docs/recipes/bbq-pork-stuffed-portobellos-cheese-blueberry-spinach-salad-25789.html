BBQ Pork Stuffed Portobellos with Cheese & Blueberry-Spinach Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $4.28/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • 4 capsportobello mushrooms
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
  • barbecue sauce
  • 30 mlhoney mustard dressing
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
Cookware
baking sheet pan colander measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    4 caps portobello mushrooms
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  3. 3
    Remove stems from portobello mushrooms and carefully scrape out the gills. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, rub with oil, and season with spices on both sides; arrange cap-side up.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place mushrooms in the oven and broil until starting to soften, about 6 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add pork and cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
  7. 7
    When the pork is done, add barbecue sauce, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    ⅓ cup barbecue sauce
  8. 8
    Using tongs, carefully flip the mushrooms, cap-side down. Fill with BBQ pork and top with cheese.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  9. 9
    Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to broil until the mushrooms are soft and cheese is melted, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place spinach, blueberries, and dressing in a medium bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    2 tbsp honey mustard dressing
  11. 11
    To serve, divide stuffed mushrooms and salad between plates. Enjoy!

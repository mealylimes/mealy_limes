Beef Burger Patties with Garlic Aioli & Tomato-Cucumber Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (8) $3.17/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2tomatoes
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • mayonnaise
  • ½lemon
  • virgin coconut oil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1shallot
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ lemon
  2. 2
    Quarter the tomatoes lengthwise and cut out and discard the stem ends; cut the quarters crosswise into ½-inch-thick chunks and transfer to a medium salad bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; quarter the cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ½-inch-thick pieces and add to the salad bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the ends of the shallot; peel and halve the shallot lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise into half-rounds and add to the salad bowl.
    1 shallot
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    In a medium bowl, combine and mix together the ground beef, salt, and pepper. Form the mixture into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Place the burger patties in the skillet and cook until desired doneness, 2 to 5 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  9. 9
    Juice the lemon; add ½ of the juice to the salad bowl and transfer the other ½ to a small bowl.
  10. 10
    Drizzle the salad with olive oil and season with salt and pepper; toss to combine.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Trim off and discard the root end of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add to the small bowl with the lemon juice.
    1 clove garlic
  12. 12
    To the lemon juice and garlic, add mayonnaise, salt, and pepper; mix together until well combined.
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the salad between plates; place the burger patties on the side and serve with the garlic aioli. Enjoy!

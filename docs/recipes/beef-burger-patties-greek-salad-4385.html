Beef Burger Patties with Greek Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (28) $3.95/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • red wine vinegar
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Transfer ½ of the garlic to a medium bowl (that will hold the ground beef mixture) and transfer the other ½ to a salad bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    To the garlic in the salad bowl, add olive oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon, oregano, salt, and pepper; whisk together.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; small dice ½ of the onion and transfer to the medium bowl with the garlic, then thinly slice the other ½ crosswise and add to the salad bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Add olive oil to a nonstick skillet and preheat over medium heat.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    To the medium bowl, add the ground beef, ¼ pkg of the feta (save the other ¼ pkg for the salad), Dijon, oregano, salt, and pepper; mix until well combined. Form the mixture into 4 patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp oregano
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Place the patties in the skillet and cook until cooked through and golden brown, 4 to 6 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  8. 8
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; quarter the cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch-thick pieces at an angle. Add to the salad bowl.
  9. 9
    Halve the grape tomatoes and add to the salad bowl.
  10. 10
    Add the olives and remaining feta to the salad bowl; toss to combine.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the burger patties and salad between plates. Enjoy!

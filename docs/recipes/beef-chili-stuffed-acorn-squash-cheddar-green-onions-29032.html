Beef Chili Stuffed Acorn Squash with Cheddar & Green Onions

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $3.70/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 stickcelery
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • 1acorn squash
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F and place rack in the center position. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 acorn squash
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    1 stick celery
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Halve acorn squash lengthwise, trim ends, and scrape out the seeds. Rub the inside of each half with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Arrange halves, cut-side down, on the baking sheet.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender, so a knife can easily pierce through the flesh, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Place in a small bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  7. 7
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the pan heats up, trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add half to the bowl. (Reserve remaining green onions for serving.)
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add veggies, beef, and spices. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 5-6 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 tbsp chili powder
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Add tomato sauce and water to the pan. Stir to combine the chili and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 10-12 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ¼ cup water
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, coarsely grate cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  12. 12
    To serve, divide squash halves between plates and fill with the chili; top with cheese and reserved green onions. Enjoy!

Beef, Ginger, Mushroom & Bok Choy Stir-Fry with Jasmine Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (19) $6.23/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • soy sauce
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ¾ lbcremini mushrooms
  • rice vinegar
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 3baby bok choy
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb cremini mushrooms
    3 baby bok choy
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  4. 4
    Thinly slice steak against the grain into strips. Transfer to a small bowl, add soy sauce, and toss to combine; set aside to marinate.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    2 tbsp soy sauce
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, thinly slice mushrooms; transfer to a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak strips to the skillet and stir-fry until just cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, trim ends and separate bok choy into leaves.
  9. 9
    When the steak strips are done, transfer to a plate.
  10. 10
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to brown, about 3 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  11. 11
    While the mushrooms cook, slice white stems of the bok choy crosswise into thin strips; chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces. Transfer leaves to the reserved bowl.
  12. 12
    Add bok choy stems to the skillet and stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are fork-tender, about 5 minutes.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and garlic; add to the skillet and stir-fry until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    2 cloves garlic
  14. 14
    Add bok choy leaves to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the leaves to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  15. 15
    Return steak strips (and any accumulated juices) to the skillet along with soy sauce and vinegar; stir to combine and remove from heat.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tsp rice vinegar
  16. 16
    To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

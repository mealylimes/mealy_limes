Beef Lasagna Noodle Soup with Carrots, Spinach & Basil

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (10) $3.06/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 4no-boil lasagna noodles
  • onion powder
  • 20 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the pan is hot, add beef, carrots, and spices. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until almost cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    1 tsp onion powder
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, break lasagna noodles into bite-sized pieces.
    4 no-boil lasagna noodles
  4. 4
    When the beef is done, add noodles, broth, and tomato sauce to the pan; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer until noodles start to soften, 7-8 minutes.
    20 fl oz (2 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  5. 5
    While the soup is cooking, wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; roll up crosswise and thinly slice into ribbons.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  6. 6
    Add spinach and half of the basil to the pan (reserve remaining basil for serving). Return soup to a simmer and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until spinach is warmed through and noodles are tender, 3-4 minutes more.
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
  7. 7
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and top with reserved basil. Enjoy!

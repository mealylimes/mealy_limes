Beef & Mushroom Stroganoff with Fettuccine Pasta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 45 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (23) $5.58/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • paprika
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • all-purpose flour
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb white mushrooms
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Trim off the discard the stem ends of the mushrooms; thinly slice the mushrooms lengthwise from cap to stem. Transfer to a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the root end of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add to the mushrooms.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; halve the onion lengthwise, then thinly slice into quarter-rounds. Transfer to a small bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    When the water in the saucepan comes to a boil, uncover the pan and season the water with salt, then add the pasta and stir for a few seconds. Cook the pasta, uncovered, for 11 to 12 minutes (or according to package directions). Drain the pasta in a colander.
    1 tbsp salt
    5 oz (about 1-inch diameter bunch) fettuccine pasta
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels, then place on the cutting board and slice crosswise into very thin strips.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  9. 9
    One the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    Add the steak slices to the skillet and season with salt and pepper. Cook, tossing occasionally, until the steak slices have a nice sear, 3 to 4 minutes total. Once done, transfer to a bowl.
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Once the steak has been transferred, add more butter and the onion to the skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until the onion is softened, 3 to 4 minutes.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  12. 12
    Add the mushrooms and garlic to the skillet; season with paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until the mushrooms are softened, 4 to 5 minutes.
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Wash and dry the cutting board. Using a knife, shave the parsley leaves off the stems at a downward angle, working away from your body; discard the stems and finely chop the leaves. Add about ¾ of the parsley to the mushrooms and transfer the rest to a small bowl.
  14. 14
    Sprinkle the flour over the mushroom mixture and stir to combine.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  15. 15
    Add the broth and Worcestershire to the skillet; deglaze the pan by scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Cook, stirring frequently, until the mixture is reduced by about half, 2 to 4 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  16. 16
    Remove the skillet from the heat; stir in the yogurt, then add the steak slices.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  17. 17
    To serve, divide the pasta between bowls or plates, top with the beef and mushroom stroganoff, and sprinkle with the remaining parsley. Enjoy!

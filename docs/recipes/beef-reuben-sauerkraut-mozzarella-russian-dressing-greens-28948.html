Beef "Reuben" with Sauerkraut, Mozzarella, Russian Dressing & Greens

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (16) $1.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • butter, unsalted
  • Dijon mustard
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ketchup
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • smoked paprika
  • ½ cupsauerkraut
  • 8 ozdeli roast beef
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Divide dressing between a small and medium bowl. Set medium bowl aside for the salad.
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
  2. 2
    Add ketchup, Dijon, Worcestershire, and smoked paprika to the small bowl with the ranch. Stir to combine the Russian dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
  3. 3
    Thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  4. 4
    Drain sauerkraut in a colander to remove excess liquid.
    ½ cup sauerkraut
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, place the bread slices on a flat surface. Evenly spread with Russian dressing; layer half of the slices with cheese, roast beef, and sauerkraut. Cover sandwiches with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
    8 oz deli roast beef
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Place sandwiches in the skillet and cook until the bottoms are golden brown, 2-4 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Remove the sandwiches to a plate and add more butter to the skillet. Return sandwiches to the skillet, browned-side up, and cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese is melted, 2-4 minutes more. Transfer to a cutting board and slice in half.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the medium bowl with the ranch dressing and toss to coat.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  10. 10
    To serve, divide sandwiches and salad between plates. Enjoy!

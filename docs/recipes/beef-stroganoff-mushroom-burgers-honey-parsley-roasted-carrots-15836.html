"Beef Stroganoff" Mushroom Burgers with Honey-Parsley Roasted Carrots

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 40% (5) $4.98/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • all-purpose flour
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 4 mediumcarrots
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 lbcremini mushrooms
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 medium carrots
    1 lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and trim carrots. Slice in half crosswise, then lengthwise into ½-inch thick sticks. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, honey, salt, and pepper; toss to coat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Transfer carrots to a baking sheet pan and spread out in a single layer. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, mince half the mushrooms and place in the reserved bowl. Thinly slice remaining mushrooms, place in a small bowl, and set aside.
  6. 6
    Add beef, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the minced mushrooms; mix with your hands until well combined. Form the mixture into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add the burger patties and cook, undisturbed, for 5 minutes. Flip patties and continue to cook until cooked through, 4-5 minutes more. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    Return skillet to medium heat; add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion begins to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Add sliced mushrooms to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until they begin to soften, 3-4 minutes.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  13. 13
    Sprinkle flour into the skillet and stir to combine. Add broth and bring to a simmer; continue to cook, stirring often, until the sauce thickens, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tsp all-purpose flour
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  14. 14
    Once the sauce has thickened, remove from the heat. Add yogurt, Worcestershire, and half the parsley (reserving the rest for the carrots); stir to combine.
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  15. 15
    Once the carrots are done, remove from the oven, sprinkle with reserved parsley, and toss to combine.
  16. 16
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  17. 17
    Place bottom half of each bun on a plate, then top with burgers and mushroom sauce. Close with the top half of the bun and serve with roasted carrots on the side. Enjoy!

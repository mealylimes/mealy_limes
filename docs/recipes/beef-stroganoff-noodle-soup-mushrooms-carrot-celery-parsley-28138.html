"Beef Stroganoff" Noodle Soup with Mushrooms, Carrot, Celery & Parsley

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (6) $1.97/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 20 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 2 ozfettuccine pasta
  • ¾ lbflank steak
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 medium carrot
    1 stick celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Thinly slice mushrooms. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and slice carrot crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the mushrooms.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, use your hands to break the pasta into 2-inch pieces.
    2 oz fettuccine pasta
  8. 8
    When the veggies are soft, add flour to the pan. Cook, stirring frequently, for 1 minute.
    2 tsp all-purpose flour
  9. 9
    Add pasta, broth, milk, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper to the pan; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until noodles are almost tender and soup has thickened, 10-12 minutes.
    20 fl oz (2 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the soup and set remaining parsley aside for serving.
  11. 11
    Pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board. Slice in half lengthwise, then cut crosswise (against the grain) into thin strips.
    ¾ lb flank steak
  12. 12
    When the noodles are almost tender, add beef slices to the soup. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until beef and noodles are cooked through, 3-4 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and top with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

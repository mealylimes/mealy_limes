Beef Taco Bowl with Cauliflower Rice, Tomatoes, Corn, Feta & Cilantro

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (16) $4.75/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • chili powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • brown sugar
  • ¾ cupfrozen corn
  • hot sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater or food processor measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ¾ tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Peel and halve onion. Slice one half crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside. (Reserve remaining onion for later use.)
    1 medium red onion
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 lime
  5. 5
    Cut cauliflower head into quarters; using a box grater, hold the stem end of the cauliflower and coarsely grate. (Alternatively, transfer cauliflower to the bowl of a food processor; pulse until rice-like in consistency.) Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  6. 6
    Drizzle oil over the cauliflower and season with salt. Stir to combine, then spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring cauliflower halfway through, until rice is tender and golden, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice the reserved onion.
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the onion and beef; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  11. 11
    Add whole tomatoes, corn, and spices to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes are starting to brown and burst, 5-7 minutes more.
    ¾ cup frozen corn
    1 tbsp chili powder
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ¾ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves.
  13. 13
    When the tomatoes are starting to burst, juice lime into the skillet, then add water, hot sauce, and half of the cilantro. Cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce is thickened, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
    ⅓ cup water
    2 tsp hot sauce
  14. 14
    To serve, divide cauliflower rice between plates or bowls. Top with beef and veggies, feta, pickled onion, and reserved cilantro. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

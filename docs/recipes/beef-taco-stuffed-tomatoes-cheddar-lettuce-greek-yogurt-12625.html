Beef Taco Stuffed Tomatoes with Cheddar, Lettuce & Greek Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (14) $4.09/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • cumin, ground
  • onion powder
  • 4tomatoes
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add onion and garlic to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
  5. 5
    Add ground beef and spices to the skillet; cook, breaking apart with a spoon, until crumbly and cooked through, 6-8 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    2 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry tomatoes. Slicing from the top down, cut tomatoes into wedges, being careful to not cut completely through to the bottom; sprinkle with salt.
    4 tomatoes
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Coarsely grate cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Wash and dry lettuce. Stack leaves, one on top of the other, halve lengthwise, then chop crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips.
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  9. 9
    To serve, divide tomatoes between plates. Stuff with beef and top with cheese, lettuce, and yogurt. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt

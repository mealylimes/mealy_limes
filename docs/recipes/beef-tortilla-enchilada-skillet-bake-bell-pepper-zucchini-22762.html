Beef Tortilla "Enchilada" Skillet Bake with Bell Pepper & Zucchini

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (130) $2.79/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 2small flour tortillas
  • 1green bell pepper
  • 1 cupsalsa
  • 22 ½ mltaco seasoning
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, wash, dry, and trim zucchini. Small dice and place in a small bowl.
    1 medium zucchini squash
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add beef, taco seasoning, and salt. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until almost cooked through, about 3 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 ½ tbsp taco seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    While the beef is cooking, wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper. Small dice and add to the bowl with the zucchini.
    1 green bell pepper
  6. 6
    Add zucchini and bell pepper to the skillet with the beef. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until beef is cooked through and veggies are softened, 4-5 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, chop or tear tortillas into 2-inch pieces.
    2 small flour tortillas
  8. 8
    When the veggies are done, add tortillas, salsa, and yogurt to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook until heated through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 cup salsa
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  9. 9
    Top skillet with cheese and place in the oven. Bake until cheese is melted and golden, 7-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
  10. 10
    To serve, divide skillet between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

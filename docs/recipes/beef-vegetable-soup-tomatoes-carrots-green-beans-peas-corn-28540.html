Beef Vegetable Soup with Tomatoes, Carrots, Green Beans, Peas & Corn

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (16) $3.33/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 ½ cupsfrozen mixed vegetables (carrots, corn, green beans, peas)
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add onion and beef; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  4. 4
    Add broth, frozen veggies, tomatoes, Worcestershire, and spices to the pan, then stir to combine the soup. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer until the veggies are soft, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 ½ cups frozen mixed vegetables (carrots, corn, green beans, peas)
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ¾ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the soup and stir to combine. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. 6
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

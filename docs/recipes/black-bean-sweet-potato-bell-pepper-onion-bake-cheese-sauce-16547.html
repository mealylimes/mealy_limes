Black Bean, Sweet Potato, Bell Pepper & Onion Bake with Cheese Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 44% (4) $2.62/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2green bell peppers
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 green bell peppers
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers; place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and cut sweet potato into ½-inch cubes; add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and large dice onion. Add to the bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper; toss to combine, transfer to a baking sheet pan, and spread out in an even layer.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are golden brown and fork-tender, about 20 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, coarsely grate mozzarella and set aside.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  8. 8
    Preheat a small saucepan over medium heat. Once the pan is hot, add oil and flour; whisk until combined, about 1 minute.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
  9. 9
    Add broth and spices to the pan; cook, whisking frequently, until thickened, 2-3 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Add mozzarella and yogurt to the pan; whisk until cheese is melted and combined. Cover sauce and set over low heat, stirring occasionally, to keep warm.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  11. 11
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  12. 12
    When the veggies are done, remove from the oven, add beans, and stir to combine. Return baking sheet to the oven and bake until beans are warmed through, 2-3 minutes more; remove from oven.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide roasted veggies and beans between bowls; top with cheese sauce and green onions. Enjoy!

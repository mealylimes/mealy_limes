Black Bean-Sweet Potato Bowl with Mushrooms, Avocado, Feta & Cilantro

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (16) $3.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • white wine vinegar
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • ½avocado
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • brown sugar
  • hot sauce
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Peel and medium dice sweet potato. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Quarter mushrooms and add to the baking sheet with the sweet potato. Drizzle with oil and season with spices. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are browned and fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ¾ tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Peel and halve onion. Slice one half crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside. (Reserve remaining onion for the next step.)
    1 medium red onion
  8. 8
    Small dice the remaining onion. Peel and thinly slice garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the diced onion, garlic, cumin, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally until starting to brown, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves.
  13. 13
    When the onion is done, add beans to the skillet. Cook, mashing with the back of a spoon, until beans are broken up slightly and warmed through, 3-4 minutes. Add water, hot sauce, and half of the cilantro, then stir to combine. Cover and remove from heat. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
    ¼ cup water
    2 tsp hot sauce
  14. 14
    Cut lime into wedges.
  15. 15
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh. Halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin pieces.
  16. 16
    Divide beans and roasted veggies between bowls; top with avocado, pickled onion, feta, and reserved cilantro. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing and additional hot sauce. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    1 tsp hot sauce

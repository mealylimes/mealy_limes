Black Bean & Veggie Quesadillas with Tomato & Avocado Salsa

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (289) $3.53/serving
Ingredients
  • 1avocado
  • ½ (15 oz) canblack beans
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½lime
  • 2tomatoes
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • cumin, ground
  • salt
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry tomatoes, jalapeño, and cilantro.
    2 tomatoes
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Medium dice and transfer tomatoes to a medium bowl. Seed, mince, and add ½ of the jalapeño to bowl (save the rest for the quesadilla filling). Halve and pit avocado; scoop out the flesh, medium dice, and add to bowl. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems, mince the leaves, and add ½ to bowl (save the rest for the quesadilla filling). Peel, small dice onion, and add ½ to bowl (save the rest for the quesadilla filling).
    1 avocado
    ½ medium red onion
  3. 3
    Drizzle ingredients in bowl with olive oil and lime juice; season with salt and pepper. Mix to combine and set aside.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    juice of ½ lime
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Wash, seed, and small dice bell pepper.
    1 red bell pepper
  6. 6
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add remaining onion, jalapeño, and bell pepper. Stirring frequently, cook until vegetables have softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Drain and rinse beans.
    ½ (15 oz) can black beans
  8. 8
    Add beans and remaining cilantro to skillet. Then season with chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Stir and cook until beans are warmed through, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Grate 1 cup of cheddar.
    ½ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  10. 10
    Wash and dry skillet. Heat over medium heat.
  11. 11
    Place tortillas on a flat surface. Layer one half of each tortilla with ¼ of the cheese, ½ of the bean mixture, and another ¼ of the cheese. Fold tortillas over to close.
    2 large (burrito size) flour tortillas
  12. 12
    Coat skillet with oil. Place both quesadillas in the skillet, straight edges next to each other. Cook until golden brown on both sides and cheese is melted, 2-3 minutes per side.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Transfer to a cutting board and cut into wedges.
  14. 14
    To serve, place quesadilla on a plate and serve with salsa. Enjoy!

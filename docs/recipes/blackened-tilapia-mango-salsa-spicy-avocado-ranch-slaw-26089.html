Blackened Tilapia with Mango Salsa & Spicy Avocado-Ranch Slaw

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (6) $3.30/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ¾ lbtilapia fillet
  • ½avocado
  • ½mango
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ mango
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; small dice the flesh. Place in a small bowl along with salsa and salt. Stir to combine and set aside for serving.
    ½ cup salsa
    1 pinch salt
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat tilapia dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with chili powder and salt on both sides.
    ¾ lb tilapia fillet
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tilapia and cook, flipping once, until fish is crispy and easily flakes with a fork, about 6 minutes; remove to a clean plate.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place dressing, chili powder, and salt in a medium bowl. Stir to combine.
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and halve bell pepper crosswise, then slice into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl, along with the shredded cabbage, and gently stir to combine the slaw.
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  9. 9
    To serve, divide slaw and tilapia between plates; top fish with salsa. Enjoy!

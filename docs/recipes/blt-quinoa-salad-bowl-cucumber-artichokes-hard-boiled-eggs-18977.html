"BLT" Quinoa Salad Bowl with Cucumber, Artichokes & Hard-Boiled Eggs

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $4.03/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • 1tomato
  • mayonnaise
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls slotted spoon small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a medium saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain eggs and transfer to a small bowl; add ice or run under cold water to cool. (Reserve saucepan for later use.)
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, place quinoa and broth in a small saucepan, then bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. 3
    While the quinoa cooks, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 tomato
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ avocado
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl. Add mayo, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Medium dice tomato; trim and small dice cucumber. Add both to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Drain and thinly slice artichokes; add to the bowl and set aside.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  7. 7
    When the eggs are done, wipe the saucepan clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the pan heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate.
  10. 10
    Transfer the cooked quinoa to a baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool for 5 minutes.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and roughly chop eggs; add to the plate with the bacon.
  12. 12
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop the flesh out with a spoon and medium dice. Add to the plate.
  13. 13
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-size pieces. Add to the salad bowl, along with cooled quinoa, and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide quinoa salad between plates or bowls; top with bacon, egg, and avocado. Enjoy!

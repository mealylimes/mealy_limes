"BLT" Tacos with Bacon, Lettuce, Tomato, Cucumber & Blue Cheese

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $2.91/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • ketchup
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • 30 mlranch dressing
  • 1 heartromaine hearts
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate; set aside.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 heart romaine hearts
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with dressing and ketchup. Stir to combine.
    ½ shallot
    2 tbsp ranch dressing
    1 tbsp ketchup
  6. 6
    Halve lettuce lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  9. 9
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  10. 10
    Divide tortillas between plates and fill with a small portion of the salad, then top with crispy bacon and blue cheese. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese

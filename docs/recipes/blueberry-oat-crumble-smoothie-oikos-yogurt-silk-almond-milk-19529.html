Blueberry-Oat Crumble Smoothie with OIKOS Yogurt & SILK Almond Milk

Vegetarian Packaged
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (1) $0.18/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • natural almond butter
  • honey
  • 2bananas
  • oats, quick cooking
  • 2 cupsfrozen blueberries
  • ½ cupOikos Triple Zero Greek vanilla yogurt
  • 8 fl ozSilk Original almond milk
Cookware
hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel bananas, break into chunks, and add to blender.
    2 bananas
  2. 2
    Add blueberries, almond milk, yogurt, oats (uncooked), almond butter, cinnamon, and honey to the blender.
    2 cup frozen blueberries
    8 fl oz (1 cup) Silk Original almond milk
    ½ cup Oikos Triple Zero Greek vanilla yogurt
    ½ cup oats, quick cooking
    2 tbsp natural almond butter
    1 tsp cinnamon, ground
    ½ tsp honey
  3. 3
    Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
  4. 4
    Pour the smoothie into glasses and enjoy!

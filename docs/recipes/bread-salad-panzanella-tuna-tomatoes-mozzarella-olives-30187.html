Bread Salad (Panzanella) with Tuna, Tomatoes, Mozzarella & Olives

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (8) $5.51/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 ½ tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 2tomatoes
  • 2 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Cut or tear bread into bite-sized pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Stir to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 slices sourdough bread
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until croutons are golden and toasted, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool, 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Drain tuna, add to the bowl with the dressing, and flake with a fork. Stir to combine.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  7. 7
    Large dice tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  11. 11
    Chop or tear mozzarella into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  12. 12
    Add the cooled croutons and capers to the bowl. Stir once more to combine the salad.
    1 ½ tbsp capers
  13. 13
    To serve, divide panzanella salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

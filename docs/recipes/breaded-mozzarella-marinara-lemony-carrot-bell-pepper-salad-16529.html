Breaded Mozzarella with Marinara & Lemony Carrot-Bell Pepper Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.10/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • honey
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • all-purpose flour
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • ½yellow bell pepper
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    1 medium carrot
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and slice carrot crosswise into thin rounds; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl and set aside.
  5. 5
    Place tomato sauce and spices in a small saucepan, stir to combine, and place over low heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through, about 3 minutes. Cover the marinara and remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, whisk egg in a small bowl. Place bread crumbs on a plate.
    1 egg
    ⅔ cup panko bread crumbs
  7. 7
    Combine flour and spices in another small bowl.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Slice mozzarella into ½-inch thick rounds, pat dry with paper towels, and place on another plate; sprinkle flour mixture over both sides to coat.
    1 (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  10. 10
    Working one slice at a time, dip the floured mozzarella into the bowl with the egg. Let the excess drip off, then dredge in the bread crumbs, gently pressing to adhere; return slices to the plate.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Carefully place breaded mozzarella in the skillet and cook, flipping once, until golden brown, 1-2 minutes per side. Remove to a clean, paper towel-lined plate.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, add mixed greens to the bowl with the veggies and dressing; toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide breaded mozzarella and salad between plates; pour warm marinara over the mozzarella and sprinkle with additional Italian seasoning. Enjoy!
    1 pinch Italian seasoning

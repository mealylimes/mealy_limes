Breakfast Panzanella with Prosciutto, Tomatoes, Mozzarella & Eggs

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $2.63/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • 2eggs
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 3 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan spatula tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic. Cut or tear the bread into bite-sized pieces. Place both in a medium bowl along with oil and toss to coat. Transfer to a baking sheet pan. Reserve bowl for later use.
    1 clove garlic
    3 slices whole grain bread
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Place croutons in the oven and cook, tossing halfway through, until golden and crisp, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, oil, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Peel and thinly slice shallot. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ shallot
  7. 7
    Wash and dry tomatoes; cut in half lengthwise and add to the bowl.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  8. 8
    Wash and dry the basil. Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  9. 9
    Cut mozzarella into bite-sized pieces. Add mozzarella and croutons to the bowl and toss to coat.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  10. 10
    Chop or tear prosciutto into bite-sized pieces. Peel and halve eggs.
    2 slices prosciutto
  11. 11
    To serve, divide panzanella salad, eggs, and prosciutto between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Breakfast Skillet of Greens, Eggs & Ham

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $2.30/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • crushed red pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2eggs
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and finely dice the onion. Peel and mince the garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  3. 3
    Lay prosciutto in the skillet and cook until it browns slightly, about 1 minute per side. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
    4 slices prosciutto
  4. 4
    Add more butter, onion, and garlic to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 3 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and medium dice the zucchini.
    1 medium zucchini squash
  6. 6
    Add the zucchini to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 3-5 minutes.
  7. 7
    Wash and dry the spinach (skip if it came prewashed). Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  8. 8
    Using a spatula, make a well for each egg in the mixture of greens. Crack an egg into each well. Cook until the egg whites are almost cooked through, 2-3 minutes. Then cover the skillet with a lid and cook for 1 minute more.
    2 eggs
  9. 9
    Season with crushed red pepper and serve with prosciutto on the side. Enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper

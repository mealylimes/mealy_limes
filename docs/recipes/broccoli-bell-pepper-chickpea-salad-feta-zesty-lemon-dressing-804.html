Broccoli, Bell Pepper & Chickpea Salad with Feta & Zesty Lemon Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (27) $2.94/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1lemon
  • 1red bell pepper
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • honey
  • salt
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. 2
    Wash broccoli, separate into bite-sized florets, and cut thick stems into smaller pieces.
    1 crown broccoli
  3. 3
    Once water is boiling, add broccoli to saucepan and cook until bright green and tender-crisp, 1-2 minutes. Drain and run under cold water. Set aside to drain excess water.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Wash lemon and grate 1 tsp of zest. In a large bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together garlic, lemon zest and juice, oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper.
    1 clove garlic
    1 lemon
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Rinse and drain chickpeas. Place on paper towels and pat dry. Add to bowl with dressing and toss to coat.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  6. 6
    Wash and dry bell pepper and parsley. Seed and medium dice the pepper. Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to bowl.
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  7. 7
    Add broccoli and feta to bowl. Toss salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  8. 8
    To serve, place salad on a plate or in a bowl. Enjoy!

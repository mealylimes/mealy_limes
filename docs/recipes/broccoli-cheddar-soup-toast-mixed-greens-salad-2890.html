Broccoli Cheddar Soup with Toast & Mixed Greens Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (11) $2.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • thyme, dried
  • all-purpose flour
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • black pepper
  • 1whole grain bun or roll
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • balsamic vinegar
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) stirring spoon toaster (optional) whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and small dice the onion; transfer to a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  2. 2
    Wash the broccoli; cut the head into bite-sized florets and small dice the stems. Add the diced stems to the onion and transfer the florets to a separate bowl.
    1 crown broccoli
  3. 3
    Melt butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Add the diced onion, broccoli stems, and thyme to the saucepan. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion and broccoli is softened, 3 to 4 minutes.
    ¼ tsp thyme
  5. 5
    While the onion cooks, coarsely grate 1 cup of the cheddar; transfer to a small bowl.
    ½ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  6. 6
    Cut the bun in half and toast (if desired).
    1 whole grain bun or roll
  7. 7
    Sprinkle flour over the onion and stir to combine.
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
  8. 8
    Gradually add the broth and milk while whisking constantly to avoid lumps.
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
  9. 9
    Stir in the broccoli and season with salt and pepper.
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a simmer and cook until the broccoli is tender, 6 to 8 minutes.
  11. 11
    While the soup cooks, wash and dry the greens (skip this step if the greens came pre-washed); transfer to a salad bowl.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  12. 12
    Drizzle the greens with olive oil and balsamic vinegar; toss to coat and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp balsamic vinegar
  13. 13
    Add half of the grated cheddar (save the rest for garnishing) to the saucepan and stir until it is completely melted, 1 to 2 minutes. .
  14. 14
    Divide the soup between two bowls (or one bowl and one lunch container) and sprinkle with the remaining cheddar. Serve with the salad and toasted bun on the side. Enjoy!

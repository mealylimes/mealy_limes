Broccoli & Edamame Fried Rice with Sesame Egg Roll

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 74% (18) $1.42/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • ½ crownbroccoli
  • 2eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ¾ cupbasmati rice
  • ¾ cupfrozen peas
  • ¾ cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ¾ cup basmati rice
    1 ½ cup water
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ crown broccoli
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince or grate the ginger. Peel and mince the garlic. Chop the broccoli into small pieces. Transfer all to a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Trim off the ends of the green onions; cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle. Transfer half the onions to the bowl with the other veggies; set the other half aside to use as garnish.
  6. 6
    Preheat a nonstick skillet over high heat. Add oil and swirl to coat bottom.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  7. 7
    Crack the eggs into a medium bowl and beat with a whisk or fork.
    2 eggs
  8. 8
    Add the egg to the skillet and gently swirl the pan so that the egg forms a thin layer coating the bottom of the pan. Cook until the egg is just set and the edges begin to turn golden, about 1 minute. Carefully slide the egg onto a cutting board and allow to cool for a few minutes.
  9. 9
    Return the skillet to the stove and lower the heat to medium. Add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    Add the bowl of veggies to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until broccoli turns bright green, about 3 minutes.
  11. 11
    Add peas and edamame to the skillet and continue cooking for another 2 minutes.
    ¾ cup frozen peas
    ¾ cup edamame
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, roll the egg omelet tightly and cut on a diagonal into ½-inch slices.
  13. 13
    Add rice and soy sauce to the skillet and cook, stirring often, until well combined and heated through, 3-4 minutes.
    3 tbsp soy sauce
  14. 14
    To serve, divide fried rice between plates and top with egg slices and remaining green onion. Enjoy!

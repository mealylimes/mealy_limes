Broiled Paprika Pork Chop with Zucchini, Bell Pepper & Walnut Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (10) $3.90/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ cupwalnuts
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
broiler pan or baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Place pork chops on a broiler pan and season both sides with spices.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place pan in the oven. Broil, flipping halfway through, until chops are no longer pink in the center and are browned at the edges, 12-14 minutes. Remove from oven and let rest, 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and trim zucchini. Halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a medium bowl.
    2 medium zucchini squash
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; medium dice. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
    1 yellow bell pepper
  6. 6
    Roughly chop walnuts. Add to the bowl and set aside.
    ½ cup walnuts
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, peel and thinly slice garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    Add zucchini, bell pepper, walnuts, and spices to the skillet with the garlic. Stir to combine, then cover and cook, undisturbed, until starting to soften, about 4 minutes.
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Uncover skillet and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until zucchini and bell pepper are fork-tender, 3-4 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pork chops and veggie sauté between plates. Enjoy!

Broiled Pork Chops with Sautéed Red Cabbage

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $3.14/serving
Ingredients
  • 1Gala apple
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • apple cider vinegar
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • ½ small headred cabbage
Cookware
broiler pan or baking dish chef's knife cutting board measuring spoons oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat oven on broiler setting and position rack about 6 inches from the top.
  2. 2
    Place pork chops on a broiler pan (or baking dish if you don’t have a broiler pan) and season both sides with chili powder, salt, and pepper.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place dish in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through cooking time, until chops are cooked through and browned at the edges, 10-12 minutes.
  4. 4
    Wash, core, and cut apple into thin bite-sized pieces. Transfer to a large bowl.
    1 Gala apple
  5. 5
    Wash cabbage, halve, and cut off core. Thinly slice crosswise into shreds and add to bowl with apple.
    ½ small head red cabbage
  6. 6
    Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Peel and thinly slice onion into half rings.
    ½ medium red onion
  8. 8
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add onion and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  9. 9
    Add cabbage, apple, water, vinegar, salt, and pepper to skillet; stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until cabbage is tender-crisp, 6-8 minutes.
    2 tbsp water
    2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Serve pork chops with cabbage on the side. Enjoy!

Broiled Teriyaki Chicken Thighs with Bell Pepper-Cilantro Salad & Rice

⏱ 40 min 🍽 4 servings
Ingredients
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 2 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • sesame seeds
  • 2 small bunchescilantro
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • brown sugar
  • 1 cupjasmine rice
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 red bell peppers
    2 yellow bell peppers
    2 small bunches cilantro
  2. 2
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic.
    4 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Place ginger, garlic, broth, soy sauce, sugar, and cinnamon in a medium saucepan; whisk to combine. Place the saucepan over high heat and bring to a boil.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, in a small bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth. When liquid in the saucepan comes to a boil, add slurry in a steady stream while whisking quickly. Let boil for 1 minute, then remove from heat and pour the teriyaki sauce into another small bowl; set aside.
    ¼ cup water
    2 tbsp cornstarch
  5. 5
    Rinse the saucepan and reserve for the next step.
  6. 6
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to the reserved saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup jasmine rice
    2 cup water
  7. 7
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. Line a large baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean up easier (optional) and coat with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  9. 9
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and place on the baking sheet. Pour half of the teriyaki sauce over the chicken (reserving the rest for serving) and turn to coat; spread chicken out in an even layer.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  10. 10
    Place baking sheet in the oven and broil the chicken, flipping once, until golden brown and cooked through, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    While the chicken is cooking, place oil, vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Trim, seed, and cut bell peppers into thin matchsticks; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  13. 13
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the bowl.
  14. 14
    Roughly chop peanuts and add to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  15. 15
    To serve, divide rice, chicken, and salad between plates. Top chicken with reserved teriyaki sauce and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    2 tsp sesame seeds

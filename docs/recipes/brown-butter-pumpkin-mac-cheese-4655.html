Brown Butter Pumpkin Mac & Cheese

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (64) $1.76/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • 6 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 5 fl ozwhole milk
  • ½ small pkgfresh thyme
Cookware
can opener colander grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add pasta and salt, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    6 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
    1 tsp salt
  2. 2
    While pasta is cooking, melt butter in another medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Stir, cooking until butter has browned, 3-4 minutes. Watch it carefully so that it does not burn.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  3. 3
    Remove saucepan from heat, stir in flour, whisking constantly, 1 minute.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  4. 4
    Return saucepan to heat, gradually stir in milk. Bring to a simmer and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly, until thickened.
    5 fl oz whole milk
  5. 5
    Wash and dry thyme. Slide leaves off the stems; discard the stems.
    ½ small pkg fresh thyme
  6. 6
    Stir in pumpkin, salt, pepper, and most of the thyme (reserve some for garnish).
    ½ (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Grate cheese and add to saucepan. Cook, stirring occasionally, until cheese is melted.
    ½ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    When macaroni is done cooking, drain in a colander. Add to the cheese sauce and stir to combine.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide the macaroni and cheese between plates or bowls and sprinkle with fresh thyme. Enjoy!

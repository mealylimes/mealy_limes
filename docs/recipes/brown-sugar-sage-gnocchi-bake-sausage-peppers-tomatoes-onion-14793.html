Brown Sugar-Sage Gnocchi Bake with Sausage, Peppers, Tomatoes & Onion

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (18) $4.52/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • sage, dried
  • salt
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • brown sugar
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Place in a medium bowl.
    1 yellow bell pepper
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the pepper.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  4. 4
    Peel and medium dice onion. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Add gnocchi, oil, sugar, and spices to the bowl; toss to combine.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp sage, dried
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Transfer veggies and gnocchi to a baking sheet pan and spread out in a single layer. Add sausages to the baking sheet, nestling them into the veggies and gnocchi.
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast until sausages are cooked through and gnocchi is golden brown, 20-25 minutes.
    2 pork sausages
  8. 8
    Remove baking sheet from the oven. Transfer sausages to a cutting board and slice crosswise into 1-inch thick pieces.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide gnocchi, veggies, and sausage between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

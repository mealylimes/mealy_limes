Brussels Sprout-Kale Cobb Salad with Bacon, Tomato, Avocado & Egg

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (5) $3.74/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½avocado
  • ½ bunchkale
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 3-4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate; set aside. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
  5. 5
    While the bacon is cooking, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ bunch kale
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
    ½ avocado
  6. 6
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat. Add tomatoes and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to brown and burst, 4-6 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Trim and thinly slice Brussels sprouts. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Using your hands, massage the greens with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes. Set aside.
  11. 11
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and large dice.
  12. 12
    Peel and quarter eggs, then season with salt and pepper.
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide greens between plates or bowls. Top with bacon, eggs, tomatoes, avocado, and blue cheese. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese

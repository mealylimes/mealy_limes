Brussels Sprout Salad with Warm Chickpeas, Beet, Pear & Goat Cheese

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (5) $3.66/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 mediumbeets
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1pear
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium beets
    1 pear
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and small dice beets. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Add to the baking sheet with the beets, drizzle with oil, and season with spices. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until chickpeas are golden and beets are fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, maple syrup, and spices in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Trim and thinly slice Brussels sprouts. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice pear. Add to the bowl and stir to combine.
  9. 9
    Just before serving, add pumpkin seeds to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with warm chickpeas and beets. Crumble goat cheese over top and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese

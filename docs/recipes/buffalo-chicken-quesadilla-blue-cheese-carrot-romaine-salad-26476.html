Buffalo Chicken Quesadilla with Blue Cheese & Carrot-Romaine Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 65% (12) $0.97/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 45 mlFrench vinaigrette
  • 1 heartromaine hearts
  • hot sauce
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  2. 2
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, lay tortillas on a flat surface and divide shredded chicken between them, arranging on one half of each tortilla. Top chicken with cheese and drizzle with hot sauce, then fold in half to close.
    2 large flour tortillas
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
    3 tbsp hot sauce
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add quesadillas and cook, flipping halfway through, until cheese is melted and tortillas are golden on both sides, 5-6 minutes. Remove to a plate.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry lettuce. Using a clean cutting board, chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Place in a medium bowl along with carrots and vinaigrette, then toss to combine the salad.
    1 heart romaine hearts
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    3 tbsp French vinaigrette
  6. 6
    Cut each quesadilla into wedges and divide between plates; serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!

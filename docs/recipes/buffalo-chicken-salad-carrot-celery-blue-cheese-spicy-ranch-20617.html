Buffalo Chicken Salad with Carrot, Celery, Blue Cheese & Spicy Ranch

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $2.84/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 stickcelery
  • ½avocado
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 80 mlranch dressing
  • hot sauce
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 stick celery
    ½ avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Add dressing, hot sauce, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the parsley; stir to combine.
    ⅓ cup ranch dressing
    1 tbsp hot sauce
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Trim and thinly slice celery crosswise; add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and small dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces and add to the bowl.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  7. 7
    Add mixed greens and shredded carrots to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  8. 8
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and sprinkle with blue cheese. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese

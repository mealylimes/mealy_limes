Buffalo Chicken Sloppy Joes with Blue Cheese, Celery & Cucumber Sticks

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $1.98/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 45 mlblue cheese dressing
  • ½ lbground chicken
  • hot sauce
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, chicken, carrots, and salt; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until the carrots are softened and chicken is browned and crumbly, 5-6 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and halve celery lengthwise; cut crosswise into 3-inch long pieces. Place on a plate.
    2 sticks celery
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, trim, and quarter cucumber lengthwise; cut crosswise into 3-inch long pieces. Add to the plate with the celery and set aside.
    ½ English cucumber
  6. 6
    When the chicken is done, add tomato sauce and hot sauce to the skillet. Stir to combine and continue to cook until heated through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    2 tbsp hot sauce
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  8. 8
    Place bottom half of each bun on a plate. Top with saucy chicken and blue cheese, then close with the top half of each bun. Serve with veggies and dressing on the side. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
    3 tbsp blue cheese dressing

Buffalo Shrimp Tacos with Creamy Napa Cabbage-Mango Slaw

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (31) $4.03/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • onion powder
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • 1lime
  • ½mango
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • hot sauce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ mango
  2. 2
    Juice half of the lime into a medium bowl; cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  3. 3
    Add yogurt, mayo, and spices to the bowl with the lime juice, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  5. 5
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the slaw, and set aside.
  6. 6
    Place flour and spices in another medium bowl, then stir to combine.
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, add shrimp to the bowl with the flour mixture and toss to coat.
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp and cook until they are opaque, 1-2 minutes per side.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  11. 11
    Remove skillet from heat, add hot sauce, and stir until sauce coats shrimp.
    ¼ cup hot sauce
  12. 12
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  13. 13
    Divide tortillas between plates, then fill with a small portion of slaw and shrimp. Serve with remaining slaw on the side and lime wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

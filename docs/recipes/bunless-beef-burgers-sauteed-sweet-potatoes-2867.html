Bunless Beef Burgers with Sautéed Sweet Potatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (12) $3.02/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 1tomato
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • garlic powder
  • mayonnaise
  • salt
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) spatula stainless steel sauté pan tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lb sweet potatoes
    1 tomato
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Preheat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, Worcestershire, Dijon, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper; mix well.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Form the beef mixture into 4 patties.
  5. 5
    Once the pan / skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  6. 6
    Add the beef patties to the pan / skillet; cook until they are cooked through, 3 to 5 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  7. 7
    Preheat a sauté pan over medium heat.
  8. 8
    Peel and small dice the potatoes.
  9. 9
    Once the sauté pan is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Add the diced potatoes to the pan and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and lightly browned, 6 to 8 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Slice the tomato into rounds.
  12. 12
    To serve, arrange a bed of spinach on a plate and top with tomato slices, burger patties, and some mayo. Add the sweet potatoes on the side. Enjoy!
    1 tbsp mayonnaise

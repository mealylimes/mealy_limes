Butter Lettuce Salad with Tuna, Potato, Orange, Avocado & Olives

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (5) $7.15/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½shallot
  • 1avocado
  • ½lemon
  • ⅓ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1orange
  • ½ lbnew potatoes
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb new potatoes
    ½ lemon
    1 orange
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 avocado
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    Quarter potatoes and place in a small saucepan; cover with hot water (from the tap) and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and cook until fork-tender, 8-10 minutes. Drain and rinse with cold water to cool, then set aside.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl; add mayo, oil, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
    ½ shallot
  5. 5
    Peel orange, separate into segments, and roughly chop the flesh; add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Roughly chop olives and add to the bowl.
    ⅓ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  8. 8
    Drain tuna, flake with a fork, and add to the bowl.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  9. 9
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces, add to the bowl, along with the potatoes, and toss to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and enjoy!

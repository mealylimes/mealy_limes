Butternut Squash, Prosciutto & Blue Cheese Flatbread with Arugula

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $3.05/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ smallbutternut squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 2 slicesprosciutto
  • ½ small pkgfresh sage
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil and set aside.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small pkg fresh sage
    ½ small butternut squash
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Pick sage leaves off the stems; discard stems and finely chop the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim and peel squash; halve lengthwise, scrape out the seeds, and small dice. Add to the bowl with the sage along with oil, salt, and pepper; stir to coat. Transfer squash to the baking sheet and spread out in a single layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until squash is golden and fork-tender, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add prosciutto slices and cook until crispy, about 1 minute per side. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate and set aside.
    2 slices prosciutto
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom; add onion, water, and salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until onions are soft and golden brown, about 8 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Crumble or chop the crispy prosciutto into bite-sized pieces.
  12. 12
    Carefully remove cooked squash from the baking sheet by lifting the corners of the foil and transferring the whole sheet to a flat surface.
  13. 13
    Arrange flatbreads on the baking sheet; layer with onion, squash, prosciutto, and blue cheese. Place in the oven and bake until flatbreads are golden and cheese is melted, about 5 minutes.
    2 flatbreads or naan
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine.
  15. 15
    When the flatbreads are done, remove from oven, and top each with a small handful of arugula. (Reserve remaining arugula for serving.)
  16. 16
    Divide flatbreads between plates; serve with remaining arugula on the side and enjoy!

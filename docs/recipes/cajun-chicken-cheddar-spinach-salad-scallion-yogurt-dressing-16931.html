Cajun Chicken, Cheddar & Spinach Salad with Scallion-Yogurt Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (11) $4.88/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½avocado
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the grill pan heats up, trim green onions and place on a plate. Drizzle with oil and season with salt.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
  4. 4
    Once the grill pan is hot, add the green onions. Cook, turning once, until tender and lightly charred, about 5 minutes; return to the plate.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place yogurt, oil, water, vinegar, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside. (Add additional water, 1 teaspoon at a time, if necessary, to reach a drizzling consistency.)
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp water
    ½ tsp white wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Place spices in another small bowl and stir to combine the spice mix.
    ¾ tsp garlic powder
    ¾ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  7. 7
    Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and place on another plate; season with the spice mix on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  8. 8
    Once the green onions are done, return grill pan to medium-high heat and coat with oil. Add chicken and grill, flipping once, until charred and cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, mince the grilled onions and add to the bowl with the dressing. Stir to combine and set aside. (Reserve plate for next step.)
  10. 10
    Coarsely grate cheese and place on the reserved plate.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  11. 11
    Halve tomatoes and add to the plate with the cheese.
  12. 12
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the plate.
  13. 13
    Slice chicken into strips.
  14. 14
    Divide spinach between plates or bowls; top with cheese, tomatoes, avocado, and chicken. Serve with dressing on the side and enjoy!

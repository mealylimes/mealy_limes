Cajun Macaroni Salad with Kidney Beans, Corn, Peppers & Green Onion

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (37) $2.36/serving
Ingredients
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cankidney beans
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • 1green bell pepper
  • brown sugar
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • Cajun seasoning
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 red bell pepper
    1 green bell pepper
  3. 3
    Drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can kidney beans
  4. 4
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add yogurt, mayo, sugar, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  5. 5
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Add to the bowl along with the beans.
  7. 7
    Rinse corn under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Add to the bowl.
    ½ cup frozen corn
  8. 8
    Add cooled pasta to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

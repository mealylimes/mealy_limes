Cajun Pork Chop with Pineapple Sauce & Spicy Cauliflower-Carrot Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $3.30/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • cornstarch
  • Cajun seasoning
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board food processor (optional) garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    1 jalapeño pepper
    2 medium carrots
    ½ small pineapple
  3. 3
    Cut cauliflower head into quarters; using a box grater, hold the stem end of the cauliflower and coarsely grate. (Alternatively, transfer cauliflower to the bowl of a food processor; pulse until rice-like in consistency.) Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Small dice and add to the bowl with the cauliflower. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper, then stir to combine. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until cauliflower and carrots are tender and golden, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Sprinkle with Cajun seasoning on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate, cover with aluminum foil, and set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Place in the reserved bowl along with water, soy sauce, maple syrup, cornstarch, Dijon, Cajun seasoning, and salt. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    2 cloves garlic
    ¼ cup water
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and small dice the flesh. Add to the bowl with the sauce.
  12. 12
    When the pork chops are done, return skillet to medium-low heat. Add pineapple and sauce; cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    Thinly slice pork chops.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide cauliflower rice and pineapple sauce between plates. Top sauce with pork and enjoy!

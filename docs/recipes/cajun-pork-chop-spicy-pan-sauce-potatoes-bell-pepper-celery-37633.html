Cajun Pork Chop with Spicy Pan Sauce, Potatoes, Bell Pepper & Celery

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (8) $3.97/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 lbnew potatoes
  • 1green bell pepper
  • Cajun seasoning
  • hot sauce
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lb new potatoes
    1 green bell pepper
    2 sticks celery
  3. 3
    Quarter new potatoes. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the baking sheet with the potatoes.
  5. 5
    Trim and medium dice celery. Add to the baking sheet.
  6. 6
    Peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the baking sheet.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic, then add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices. Toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until potatoes are fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with spices on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    1 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes. (Reserve skillet with drippings for the next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add flour and cook, whisking constantly, for 1 minute.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp all-purpose flour
  13. 13
    Add broth, hot sauce, salt, and pepper to the skillet, then whisk to combine the sauce. Cook, whisking frequently, until sauce is thickened, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tsp hot sauce
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pork and roasted veggies between plates. Spoon pan sauce over chops and enjoy!

Cajun Shrimp-Sausage Sauté with Celery, Zucchini, Bell Pepper & Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (19) $4.72/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 2 stickscelery
  • onion powder
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 2pork sausages
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 sticks celery
    1 orange bell pepper
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  4. 4
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ½ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, cut sausages crosswise into ½-inch thick pieces.
    2 pork sausages
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausage and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy and cooked through, 5-7 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer sausage to a plate, leaving the cooking juices in the skillet.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    While the sausages are cooking, using a clean cutting board, trim and slice celery crosswise into ½-inch thick pieces; place in a medium bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the celery.
  10. 10
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ½-inch thick half-moons; add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    When the sausage is done, return skillet (with cooking juices) to medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add veggies, tomatoes, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, about 8 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ¾ tsp salt
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  12. 12
    When the veggies are tender, add sausage and shrimp to the skillet; stir to combine and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sausage is warm and shrimp are cooked through, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the parsley to the skillet and stir to combine (reserve the other half for serving).
  14. 14
    To serve, divide rice and shrimp-sausage sauté between plates. Sprinkle with reserved parsley and enjoy!

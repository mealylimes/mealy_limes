Cajun-Style Salmon with Saucy Chickpea, Tomato, Pepper & Cilantro Bake

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $4.75/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • Cajun seasoning
Cookware
aluminum foil baking dish can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 green bell pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Place in a baking dish.
  4. 4
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Add to the baking dish with the bell peppers along with marinara sauce, water, and spices; stir to combine.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    3 tbsp water
    2 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until peppers and chickpeas are tender, 15-20 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat salmon dry with paper towels and place, skin-side down, on a plate. Season the top side of the salmon with spices.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    ½ tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add salmon, skin-side down, and cook, flipping once, until skin is crispy and flesh is opaque, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    While the salmon is cooking, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  10. 10
    When the peppers and chickpeas are done, remove from the oven. Add cilantro and stir to combine.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide saucy chickpeas and salmon between plates. Enjoy!

Caprese Frittata with Spinach, Mozzarella, Prosciutto & Balsamic Glaze

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (26) $4.10/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • 2 fl ozwhole milk
  • 6eggs
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • brown sugar
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place vinegar and sugar in a small saucepan; bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until reduced by half, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
    ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
    2 tsp brown sugar
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add prosciutto in a single layer and cook until crispy, 1-2 minutes per side. Remove to a plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    4 slices prosciutto
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place eggs, milk, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl, then whisk to combine. Chop or tear mozzarella into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl, stir to combine, and set aside.
    6 eggs
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  6. 6
    Halve grape tomatoes. Divide between a small and a medium bowl, then set the small bowl aside.
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the medium bowl with the tomatoes.
    ½ shallot
  8. 8
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, then add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tomatoes, shallot, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  10. 10
    Roughly chop spinach. When the tomatoes are tender, add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for it to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Continue to cook until spinach is wilted, then spread veggie-spinach mixture out evenly in the skillet.
  11. 11
    Reduce heat to medium-low; pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, 3-5 minutes.
  12. 12
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until top of the frittata is set, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, remove basil leaves from stems; discard stems. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the small bowl with the tomatoes along with oil, salt, and pepper, then stir to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    Crumble or chop prosciutto into bite-sized pieces.
  15. 15
    To serve, cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Drizzle with balsamic glaze, then top with tomato-basil mixture and crispy prosciutto. Enjoy!

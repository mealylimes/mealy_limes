Caprese-Style Spinach Salad with Grilled Apricots, Tempeh & Mozzarella

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $3.72/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 3apricots
  • 1 (8 oz) packagetempeh
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Slice tempeh crosswise into ½-inch thick pieces and place in a medium bowl. Add soy sauce, vinegar, and Italian seasoning. Gently stir to coat and set aside to marinate.
    1 (8 oz) package tempeh
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    3 apricots
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, halve apricots lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Season the cut-side with salt and pepper.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Add apricot halves, cut-side down, and grill until charred and soft, 1-2 minutes per side. Remove from heat and set aside to cool slightly.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and slice shallot crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a medium bowl.
    1 shallot
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the shallot along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tempeh in a single layer (leaving any excess marinade in the bowl) and cook, flipping once, until browned and warmed through, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    While the tempeh is cooking, halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Cut or tear mozzarella into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  13. 13
    When the apricots are cool enough to handle, thinly slice. Add to the bowl, along with spinach, and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and top with seared tempeh. Enjoy!

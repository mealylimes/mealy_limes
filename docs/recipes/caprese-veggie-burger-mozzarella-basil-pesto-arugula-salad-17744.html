Caprese Veggie Burger with Mozzarella, Basil Pesto & Arugula Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.01/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 1tomato
  • 20 mlbasil pesto
  • 22 ½ mlItalian salad dressing
  • 2veggie burger patties, plant-based
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet spatula toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook, flipping once, until golden brown and cooked through, 8-10 minutes (or cook patties according to package instructions). Remove to a plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 veggie burger patties, plant-based
  3. 3
    While the patties are cooking, slice mozzarella into rounds.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and slice tomato into rounds.
    1 tomato
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the arugula. (Skip this step if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  6. 6
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  7. 7
    Place the bottom half of each bun on a flat surface, then top with burger patties, mozzarella, tomato, and pesto. Close with the top half of each bun.
    4 tsp basil pesto
  8. 8
    To serve, divide caprese burgers and arugula between plates. Drizzle dressing over the arugula, sprinkle with pine nuts, and enjoy!
    1 ½ tbsp italian salad dressing
    ⅛ cup pine nuts

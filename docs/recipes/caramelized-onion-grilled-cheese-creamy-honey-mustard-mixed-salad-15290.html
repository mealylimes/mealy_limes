Caramelized Onion Grilled Cheese with Creamy Honey-Mustard Mixed Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (16) $3.73/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • salt
  • honey
  • butter, unsalted
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½red bell pepper
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • ½English cucumber
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a small saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons.
    1 medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and caramelized, 15-20 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place mayo, honey, water, and Dijon in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp honey
    1 tbsp water
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ red bell pepper
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  6. 6
    Trim ends of the cucumber; quarter lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl and set aside.
  8. 8
    Grate mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    When the onions are done, add vinegar and Worcestershire to the pan, stir to combine, then remove from the heat.
    1 ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
  10. 10
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  11. 11
    While the skillet heats up, place the bread slices on a flat surface. Evenly top half of the slices with the onions and mozzarella. Cover the sandwiches with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Place sandwiches in the skillet and cook until the bottoms are golden brown, 2-4 minutes.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  13. 13
    While the sandwiches are cooking, chop the lettuce into bite-sized pieces and transfer to the bowl with the dressing and veggies. Toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    Remove the sandwiches to a plate and add more butter to the skillet; return sandwiches to the skillet, browned-side-up, and cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese is melted, 2-4 minutes more. Once done, transfer to a cutting board and slice in half.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  15. 15
    To serve, divide the salad and sandwiches between plates. Enjoy!

Carrot Ginger-Turmeric Soup with Bacon, Onions & Parsley-Buttered Pita

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (7) $2.82/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 4 mediumcarrots
  • cumin, ground
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) hand or regular blender ladle measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel, quarter, and thinly slice onion. Place in a medium bowl and set aside.
    1 medium yellow onion
  2. 2
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the pan heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  4. 4
    Once the pan is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until the bacon is starting to brown, 2-3 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    4 medium carrots
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. 6
    When the bacon is starting to brown, add half of the onion to the pan. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until bacon and onions are crispy and golden, 2-3 minutes more. (Reserve bowl with remaining onion for later use.)
  7. 7
    Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon and onions to a paper towel-lined plate; set aside. (Reserve pan with drippings for later use.)
  8. 8
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add both to the bowl with the remaining onion.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Peel, trim, and large dice carrots. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Trim and large dice zucchini. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Return pan (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add butter, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies begin to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 tsp turmeric, ground
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half of the leaves in a small bowl along with additional butter and salt. Mash with the back of a spoon to combine the parsley butter. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ⅛ tsp salt
  13. 13
    When the veggies are done, add broth to the pan; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer until veggies are tender, 6-8 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  14. 14
    When the soup is done, using a hand blender, purée until smooth. (If using a regular blender, work in batches as needed, then return soup to the pan.)
  15. 15
    Spread pita with parsley butter and cut into wedges.
    2 whole grain pitas
  16. 16
    Divide soup between bowls, then top with crispy bacon, onions, and reserved parsley. Serve with pita on the side and enjoy!

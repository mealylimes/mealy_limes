Carrot-Pear Soup with Cheddar & Nutty Strawberry-Orange Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (2) $3.34/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½lime
  • 1orange
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 1pear
  • ⅓ cupwalnuts
  • ⅛ cupmedjool dates, pitted
  • neutral vegetable oil
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 pear
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    ½ lime
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 orange
  2. 2
    Quarter, core, and small dice pear. Place in a medium bowl. (Reserve a small handful of pear for serving.)
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrots. Trim and small dice celery. Add both to the bowl with the pear.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pear, veggies, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    1 tsp smoked paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a clean medium bowl. Add honey and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing. Set aside.
    1 tsp honey
    1 pinch salt
  8. 8
    When the veggies have softened, add broth to the pan; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer; cook, stirring occasionally, until pear and veggies are tender, about 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  9. 9
    While the soup is cooking, trim and quarter strawberries lengthwise. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Peel orange, separate into segments, and roughly chop the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Roughly chop walnuts and dates. Add both to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
    ⅓ cup walnuts
    ⅛ cup medjool dates, pitted
  12. 12
    Coarsely grate cheese. Set aside for serving.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  13. 13
    When the pear and veggies are tender, using a hand blender, purée the soup until smooth. (If using a regular blender, work in batches as needed, then return soup to the pan.)
  14. 14
    Divide soup between bowls, then top with cheese and reserved pear. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

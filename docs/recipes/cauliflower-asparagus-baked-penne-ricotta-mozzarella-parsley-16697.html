Cauliflower-Asparagus Baked Penne with Ricotta, Mozzarella & Parsley

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (11) $2.90/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small bunchasparagus
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
Cookware
baking dish can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ small bunch asparagus
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  4. 4
    Cut cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Roughly chop into ½-inch pieces.
  5. 5
    Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, uncover, add cauliflower, pasta, and salt; stir for a few seconds. Cook until pasta is almost tender, about 7 minutes.
    5 oz penne pasta
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, snap or cut off the woody ends of the asparagus and cut into 1-inch pieces. Set aside.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the garlic (reserving the rest for garnish).
  9. 9
    When the pasta is almost tender, add asparagus to the pan; continue to cook until the pasta and veggies are tender, 3-4 minutes more.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, add tomato sauce, ricotta, and spices to the bowl with the garlic and parsley; whisk to combine the sauce.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Coarsely grate mozzarella.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  12. 12
    When the pasta and veggies are done, drain in a colander. Add pasta and veggies to the bowl with the sauce; stir to combine.
  13. 13
    Transfer the saucy pasta to a baking dish, spread out in an even layer, and sprinkle with cheese. Place baking dish in the oven and bake until cheese is melted and bubbly, 10-12 minutes.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta bake between bowls and top with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

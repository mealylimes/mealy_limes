Cauliflower-Bacon "Mac & Cheese" Gratin with Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (11) $2.56/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • onion powder
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
    ½ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and cut the cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Cut into bite-sized pieces.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
  3. 3
    Once the water is boiling, add cauliflower and cook until tender-crisp, 6-8 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
  4. 4
    While the cauliflower is cooking, preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate lined with paper towels, crumble or chop into small pieces, and set aside.
    4 slices bacon
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  7. 7
    Cut cream cheese into small pieces. Coarsely grate cheddar; set both aside.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Return skillet (with bacon fat) to medium heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add milk and spices; whisk to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, add cream cheese, a few pieces at a time, whisking until melted and combined, 2-3 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp paprika
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Add half the cheddar to the skillet and whisk until melted.
  11. 11
    Add cooked cauliflower to the skillet and stir to combine. Sprinkle with remaining cheddar and place in the oven; broil until the top is golden and bubbly, 2-3 minutes. Remove from the oven.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  13. 13
    Wash and dry the spring mix (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  14. 14
    Divide cauliflower between plates and sprinkle with bacon. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

Cauliflower Fritters with Tzatziki, Smoked Salmon, Greens & Pine Nuts

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $2.64/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • dill, dried
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2eggs
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) bagfrozen, riced cauliflower
  • 45 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add riced cauliflower and cook, stirring often, until lightly golden and cooked through, 5-7 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (12 oz) bag frozen, riced cauliflower
  3. 3
    Transfer cooked cauliflower rice to a medium bowl. Add eggs, flour, and spices, then stir to combine the fritter mixture. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
    2 eggs
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    ¼ tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Return skillet to medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Using a ¼-cup measure, scoop the cauliflower mixture into the skillet, spacing each scoop a few inches apart; press down to form round fritters. Cook, flipping once, until fritters are golden brown and cooked through, 3-5 minutes. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the mixed greens (skip if they came pre-washed). Place in a clean medium bowl along with pine nuts and vinaigrette. Toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
    3 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
  7. 7
    Chop or tear salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  8. 8
    To serve, divide salad and fritters between plates; top fritters with tzatziki and salmon. Enjoy!
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce

Cauliflower Macaroni & Cheese with Lemon-Parsley Butter Lettuce Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (12) $3.16/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½lemon
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater or food processor measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon strainer tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil.
  3. 3
    While the water is heating, cut the cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Using a box grater or food processor, coarsely grate the cauliflower.
  4. 4
    Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, uncover, and add cauliflower, pasta, and salt; stir for a few seconds. Cook until pasta reaches desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
    1 tsp salt
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the parsley to the bowl with lemon juice (reserving the rest for the pasta).
  7. 7
    Add oil, honey, salt, and pepper to the bowl; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Coarsely grate cheddar. Cut cream cheese into small pieces.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
    ¼ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  9. 9
    Preheat a small saucepan over medium heat.
  10. 10
    Once the pan is hot, add milk, cheddar, cream cheese, and spices. Cook, whisking frequently, until cheeses melt and combine, 2-3 minutes; remove cheese sauce from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    When the cauliflower and pasta are done, drain in a strainer, and return to the pan. Add cheese sauce and stir to combine the cauliflower macaroni & cheese. Cover and set aside.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl with the dressing; toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide cauliflower macaroni & cheese and salad between plates. Top pasta with reserved parsley and crushed red pepper. Enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper

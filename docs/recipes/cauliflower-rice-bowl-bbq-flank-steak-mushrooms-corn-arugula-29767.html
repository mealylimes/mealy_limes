Cauliflower Rice Bowl with BBQ Flank Steak, Mushrooms, Corn & Arugula

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $2.78/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½lime
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ketchup
  • brown sugar
  • ⅔ cupfrozen corn
  • ¾ lbflank steak
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater or food processor measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ lime
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Cut cauliflower head into quarters; using a box grater, hold the stem end of the cauliflower and coarsely grate. (Alternatively, transfer cauliflower to the bowl of a food processor; pulse until rice-like in consistency.) Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Halve mushrooms and add to the baking sheet with the cauliflower. Drizzle with oil and season with salt. Stir to combine, then spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until cauliflower rice and mushrooms are tender and golden, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place ketchup, vinegar, sugar, Dijon, Worcestershire, chili powder, and salt in a small bowl. Stir to combine the BBQ sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp ketchup
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board; slice crosswise into thin strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb flank steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and stir-fry until just cooked through, 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, cut half of the lime into wedges and set aside for serving. (Reserve remaining lime for the next step.)
  11. 11
    Juice remaining lime into a medium bowl, then add oil, Dijon, and salt. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 pinch salt
  12. 12
    Add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
  13. 13
    Rinse corn under hot water (from the tap) to thaw.
    ⅔ cup frozen corn
  14. 14
    Divide cauliflower rice, steak, corn, and arugula between bowls; drizzle steak with BBQ sauce. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing and enjoy!

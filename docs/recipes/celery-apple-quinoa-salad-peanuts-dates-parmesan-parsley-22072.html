Celery-Apple Quinoa Salad with Peanuts, Dates, Parmesan & Parsley

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (6) $3.19/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2Granny Smith apples
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • 4 stickscelery
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • ¼ cupmedjool dates, pitted
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 sticks celery
    2 Granny Smith apples
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Place oil, vinegar, maple syrup, and spices in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp white wine vinegar
    1 ½ tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  4. 4
    Peel and slice shallot crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 shallot
  5. 5
    Trim and thinly slice celery crosswise. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apples. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Roughly chop peanuts and dates. Add both to the bowl and set aside.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
    ¼ cup medjool dates, pitted
  9. 9
    Transfer the cooked quinoa to a baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool for 5 minutes.
  10. 10
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add to the bowl, along with the cooled quinoa, and stir to combine the salad.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

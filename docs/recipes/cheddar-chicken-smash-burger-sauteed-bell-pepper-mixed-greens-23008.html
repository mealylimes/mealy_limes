Cheddar Chicken Smash Burger with Sautéed Bell Pepper & Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $3.03/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 stickcelery
  • cumin, ground
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 green bell pepper
    1 stick celery
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Peel and mince shallot and garlic. Divide the parsley, shallot, and garlic between two medium bowls. Set one bowl aside for the salad.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Add chicken, Dijon, and spices to the remaining bowl. Use your hands to combine the chicken mixture. Form into 2 equal-sized "meatballs" and place on a plate. Set aside.
    ½ lb ground chicken
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper and salt; sauté until charred and tender, 2-3 minutes. Transfer peppers to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
  7. 7
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add meatballs to the skillet, spacing them out; press down with the bottom of a spatula to form ½-inch thick patties. Cook, undisturbed, until golden on one side, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    When the burgers are done on one side, flip and top with cheese. Continue to cook until cheese melts and burgers are cooked through, 3-4 minutes more. Add burgers to the plate with the bell pepper and re-cover with foil.
  10. 10
    While the burgers are cooking, add additional oil, mayo, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the remaining parsley, shallot, and garlic. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Trim and slice celery crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Halve tomatoes. Add to the bowl, along with the mixed greens, and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  14. 14
    Spread additional mayo over the bottom half of each bun; top with a cheesy chicken patty and sautéed bell peppers. Close with the top half of each bun.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
  15. 15
    To serve, divide smash burgers and salad between plates. Enjoy!

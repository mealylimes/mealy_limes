Cheddar Halibut Fish Sticks with Lemony Dill, Cabbage & Kale Coleslaw

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $4.96/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • all-purpose flour
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ¾ lbhalibut fillet
  • 1 ½lemons
  • onion powder
  • ½ bunchkale
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of olive oil; set aside.
    ¼ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Finely grate the cheese into a medium bowl; add bread crumbs and salt. Toss together with your hands until well combined, breaking up any cheese lumps. Transfer to the baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. Reserve bowl.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Place pan in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until golden, about 8 minutes. Watch carefully so the crumbs don't burn. Remove from the oven and let cool. Leave the oven on.
  4. 4
    If using halibut with skin, place the fillet, skin-side down, on a cutting board. Holding the tail end firmly, use a knife to cut between the flesh and skin; continue to cut through the length of the fillet (take care not to cut through the skin); discard the skin.
    ¾ lb halibut fillet
  5. 5
    Pat the fish dry with paper towel, then cut crosswise into 1-inch strips; transfer to a plate.
  6. 6
    Combine flour, salt, and pepper in a small bowl; stir together. In another small bowl, whisk the egg.
    ¼ cup all-purpose flour
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 egg
  7. 7
    Scrape the cheese mixture back into the reserved bowl, then break it up into fine crumbs with a fork or by hand. Reserve the baking sheet pan for the next step.
  8. 8
    Dredge fish strips in flour, shake off excess, then dip into the bowl with the egg. Let the egg drip off, then dredge fish strips in the cheese crumbs, pressing to adhere. Place on the baking sheet pan, spacing them 1-inch apart.
  9. 9
    Place fish sticks in the oven and bake until cooked through, 5-7 minutes. Remove from the oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 ½ lemons
    ½ bunch kale
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  11. 11
    Juice 1 lemon into a medium bowl. Cut remaining lemon into wedges and set aside.
  12. 12
    Add mayonnaise, oil, Dijon, maple syrup, and spices to the bowl with the lemon juice; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  13. 13
    Fold the kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Finely chop leaves and transfer to the bowl with the dressing.
  14. 14
    Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; halve the cabbage lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  15. 15
    Using a knife, shave the dill leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing. Using your hands, massage the shredded greens with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide fish sticks and coleslaw between plates. Garnish with lemon wedges and enjoy!

Cheesy Asparagus & Pea Frittata with Strawberry Balsamic Green Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $3.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 45 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears into bite-sized pieces.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add asparagus, peas, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until asparagus softens, about 5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 cup frozen peas
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place eggs in a medium bowl; whisk to combine and set aside.
    6 eggs
  6. 6
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. 7
    When the asparagus is soft, reduce heat to medium-low; pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, 3-5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Sprinkle cheese over the frittata. Place skillet in the oven and broil until top is set and cheese is melted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, trim and thinly slice strawberries.
  10. 10
    To serve, cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Add mixed greens to the plate, top with strawberries and almonds, then drizzle with vinaigrette. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
    3 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette

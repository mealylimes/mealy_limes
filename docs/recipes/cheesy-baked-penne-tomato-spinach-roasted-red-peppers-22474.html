Cheesy Baked Penne with Tomato, Spinach & Roasted Red Peppers

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (184) $0.68/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • salt
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, medium dice peppers and set aside.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  4. 4
    Place spinach in a colander.
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
  5. 5
    When the pasta is done, drain in the colander over the spinach; return pasta and spinach to the pan.
  6. 6
    Add diced peppers, marinara sauce, and ricotta to the pan with the pasta and spinach; stir to combine.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  7. 7
    Place pasta over medium heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until just warmed through, 1-2 minutes. Transfer pasta to a baking dish and top with cheese.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  8. 8
    Place baking dish in the oven and bake until cheese is melted and sauce is bubbly, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pasta bake between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

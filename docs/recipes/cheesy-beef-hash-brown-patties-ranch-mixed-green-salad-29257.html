Cheesy Beef & Hash Brown Patties with Ranch Mixed Green Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (2) $3.26/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1egg
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ketchup
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 30 mlranch dressing
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 1 cupfrozen shredded hash brown potatoes
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place hash browns in a colander; rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Using your hands, squeeze as much water from the hash browns as you can.
    1 cup frozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  2. 2
    Place hash browns in a medium bowl along with beef, cheese, egg, salt, and pepper. Mix with your hands to combine, then form into 6 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
    1 egg
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add patties and cook, undisturbed, until golden underneath, about 5 minutes; flip patties and cook an additional 5-6 minutes, until cooked through. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and halve grape tomatoes. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the tomatoes, along with dressing, and toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    2 tbsp ranch dressing
  7. 7
    To serve, divide salad and patties between plates. Drizzle patties with ketchup and enjoy!
    2 tbsp ketchup

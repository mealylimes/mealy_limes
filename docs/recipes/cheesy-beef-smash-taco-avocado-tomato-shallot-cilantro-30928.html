Cheesy Beef Smash Taco with Avocado, Tomato, Shallot & Cilantro

Meat Snacks
⏱ 12 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (3) $1.37/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • ¼ lblean ground beef
  • ½avocado
  • ¼ small bunchcilantro
  • ½tomato
  • 2small corn tortillas
  • ⅓ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ tomato
    ½ avocado
    ¼ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Small dice tomato. Place on a plate.
  3. 3
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice lengthwise into thin half-moons. Add to the plate with the tomato.
  4. 4
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the plate and set aside.
  5. 5
    Place beef, chili powder, and salt in a small bowl. Mix with your hands until well combined.
    ¼ lb lean ground beef
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, arrange tortillas on a flat surface, then divide the beef mixture between them. Using the back of a spoon (or your fingers), spread the beef mixture over the surface of each tortilla in an even layer.
    2 small corn tortillas
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tacos, meat-side down; cook, pressing down once with the bottom of a spatula halfway through cooking, until the beef is cooked through, about 3 minutes.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Flip tacos, beef-side up, and sprinkle with cheese. Continue to cook until cheese is melted, 1 minute more. Remove from heat.
    ⅓ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, peel and small dice shallot. Add to the plate with the other veggies.
    ½ shallot
  11. 11
    To serve, divide smash tacos between plates. Top with tomato, cilantro, shallot, and avocado. Enjoy!

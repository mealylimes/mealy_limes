Cheesy Beef-Stuffed Jalapeño Peppers with Avocado & Lime

Meat Snacks
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 68% (7) $1.66/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1shallot
  • 5jalapeño peppers
  • ¼ lblean ground beef
  • sesame seeds
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ¼ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • ⅓ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean up easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    5 jalapeño peppers
    ¼ small bunch cilantro
    ½ avocado
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Halve jalapeño peppers lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Transfer to the baking sheet and set aside. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  4. 4
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the cilantro along with beef, cheese, sesame seeds, and spices; mix with your hands until well combined.
    1 shallot
    ¼ lb lean ground beef
    ⅓ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
    1 tbsp sesame seeds
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Stuff jalapeño halves with the beef mixture, pressing firmly into each pepper. Arrange stuffed peppers on the baking sheet, about 1-inch apart.
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven and bake until jalapeños are tender and beef is cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice lengthwise into 10 thin wedges. Cut lime into wedges.
  9. 9
    Divide stuffed jalapeños between plates; top each with a slice of avocado. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing and enjoy!

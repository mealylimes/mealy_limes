Cheesy Broccoli-Bacon Soup with Bell Pepper, Cucumber & Arugula Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (1) $2.98/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½English cucumber
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • 30 mlItalian salad dressing
  • ½ (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and crispy, 4-5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 orange bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  5. 5
    When the bacon is done, add broth, Alfredo sauce, broccoli, and spices to the pan. Stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer until broccoli is tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    While the soup is cooking, using a clean cutting board, trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  8. 8
    Add arugula and dressing to the bowl, then toss to combine the salad.
    2 tbsp Italian salad dressing
  9. 9
    Divide soup between bowls and top with cheese; serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

Cheesy Broiled Pork Chops with Pineapple Salsa & Roasted Asparagus

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (7) $3.96/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and cook until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side (they will not be cooked all the way through).
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  5. 5
    When the chops are done, transfer to one half of a baking sheet pan, sprinkle with cheese, and set aside.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
  6. 6
    Wash and dry asparagus. Snap (or cut) off woody ends and arrange on the other half of the baking sheet. Drizzle with oil and season with salt and pepper.
    1 small bunch asparagus
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven and broil until the chops are cooked through, cheese is melted, and asparagus softens slightly, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and small dice the flesh. Place in a small bowl, along with the salsa, and stir to combine.
    ½ small pineapple
    ½ cup salsa
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pork chops, asparagus, and pineapple salsa between plates. Enjoy!

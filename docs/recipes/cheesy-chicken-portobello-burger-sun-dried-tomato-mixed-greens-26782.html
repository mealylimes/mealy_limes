Cheesy Chicken & Portobello Burger with Sun-Dried Tomato Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $4.40/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • red wine vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 6sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with the chicken, bread crumbs, mayo, Worcestershire, and spices. Mix with your hands until well combined; set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ lb ground chicken
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    2 tsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Wash and dry portobello mushrooms. Remove stems and carefully scrape out the gills. Rub with oil, then season with salt and pepper; place cap-side down on a plate.
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Using your hands, divide chicken mixture between the insides of mushrooms, spreading evenly to the edges. Gently press to adhere.
  4. 4
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add burger patties, chicken-side down and cook, undisturbed, until the chicken is golden brown and cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Flip patties, top with cheese, and cook until mushrooms are golden brown and fork-tender, 6-8 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and slice shallot crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    1 shallot
  8. 8
    Roughly chop sun-dried tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the shallot along with oil, vinegar, honey, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    6 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  10. 10
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  11. 11
    Spread mayo over the bottom half of each bun; top with cheesy burger patties and a small handful of salad. Close with the top half of each bun. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!
    1 tbsp mayonnaise

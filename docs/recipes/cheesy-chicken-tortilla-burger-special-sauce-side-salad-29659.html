Cheesy Chicken Tortilla Burger with Special Sauce & Side Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (8) $2.85/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½English cucumber
  • 4small flour tortillas
  • 2tomatoes
  • mayonnaise
  • ketchup
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place chicken, bread crumbs, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; mix with your hands until well combined. Divide the chicken mixture into 2 portions and form into ½-inch thick patties; place on a plate and set aside.
    ½ lb ground chicken
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats, up thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook, undisturbed, until golden underneath, 3-4 minutes; flip patties, top with cheese, and cook an additional 3-4 minutes, until cooked through. Remove to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
    ½ English cucumber
  6. 6
    Place oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, and spices in a clean medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Slice one tomato crosswise into thin rounds; medium dice remaining tomato. Place slices on another plate and add diced tomato to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Separate lettuce into leaves. Set aside a few leaves per burger and chop (or tear) remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces. Add whole leaves to the plate and chopped lettuce to the bowl. Set the plate aside.
  9. 9
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
  10. 10
    Place mayo, ketchup, Dijon, and cayenne in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the special sauce and set aside.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp ketchup
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  11. 11
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    4 small flour tortillas
  12. 12
    To assemble the burgers, place half of the tortillas on a flat surface, then top with cheesy burger patties, lettuce leaves, tomato slices, and special sauce. Close with the remaining tortillas and cut in half.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide chicken burgers and salad between plates. Enjoy!

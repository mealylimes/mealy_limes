Cheesy Egg Toast with Lemony Dill, Spinach, Radish, & Mixed Pea Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.38/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 3eggs
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ½shallot
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    3 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    ½ bunch radish
    2 sticks celery
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Place peas in a colander and run under cold water to thaw; set aside to drain.
    ½ cup frozen peas
  4. 4
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic and shallot. Add both to the bowl with the lemon, along with oil, honey, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ shallot
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Trim and thinly slice radishes and celery; add both to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Cut sugar snap peas crosswise into ½-inch pieces; add to the bowl, toss to combine, and set aside.
  8. 8
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince additional garlic; place in a small bowl along with mayo, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Peel and coarsely grate eggs. Coarsely grate cheese; add both to the bowl with the mayo and stir to combine.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  11. 11
    Split rolls in half and arrange, cut-side up, on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil until lightly toasted, 1-2 minutes.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  12. 12
    Remove toasted rolls from the oven and spread with cheesy egg mixture. Return to the oven and broil until warmed through and cheese is melted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the veggies, along with thawed peas and spinach; toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide cheesy egg toasts and salad between plates. Enjoy!

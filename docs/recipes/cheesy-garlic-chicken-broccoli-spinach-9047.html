Cheesy Garlic Chicken with Broccoli & Spinach

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (62) $4.78/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • butter, unsalted
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and place over high heat.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the spinach. (Skip this step if it came pre-washed.)
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Wash and separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
    1 crown broccoli
  4. 4
    Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, add the spinach, and stir. Cook 30-60 seconds, then remove to a colander with a slotted spoon. Rinse spinach under cold water, then press out excess water with the back of a spoon or your hands; transfer to a small bowl.
  5. 5
    When the water in the saucepan returns to a boil, add the broccoli, cover, and reduce heat to medium. Cook until broccoli is fork-tender, about 4 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  6. 6
    Coarsely grate the mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch pieces.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    Add chicken and spices to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, peel and mince the garlic. When the chicken is done, add the garlic to the skillet and cook 1 minute more.
    3 cloves garlic
  12. 12
    Add spinach, broccoli, mozzarella, and ricotta to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring frequently, until cheese is melted and starting to bubble.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide chicken sauté between plates, sprinkle with crushed red pepper, and enjoy!
    1 pinch crushed red pepper

Cheesy Indian Spiced Frittata with Tomatoes & Cashew-Spinach Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $3.37/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • curry powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ¼ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • 6eggs
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • garam masala
  • ¾ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Halve tomatoes crosswise. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tomatoes, green onions, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies start to soften, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the later use.)
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp garam masala
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. 7
    Place eggs and salt in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine.
    6 eggs
    ¼ tsp salt
  8. 8
    When the veggies are softened, add half of the spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for it to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. (Reserve remaining spinach for serving.)
  9. 9
    Reduce heat to medium-low; pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, 3-5 minutes.
  10. 10
    Sprinkle cheese over the frittata. Place skillet in the oven and broil until top is set and cheese is melted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ¾ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, roughly chop cashews.
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  12. 12
    To serve, cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Add reserved spinach to the plate, top with cashews, and drizzle with dressing. Enjoy!
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette

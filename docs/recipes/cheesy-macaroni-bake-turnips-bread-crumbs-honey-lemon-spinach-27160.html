Cheesy Macaroni Bake with Turnips, Bread Crumbs & Honey-Lemon Spinach

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (7) $2.27/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 mediumturnip
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ½lemon
  • nutmeg, ground
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 medium turnip
    ½ lemon
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil.
  4. 4
    While the water is heating, peel, trim, and medium dice turnip.
  5. 5
    Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, uncover, then add salt, turnip, and pasta; stir for a few seconds. Cook until turnip is just tender and pasta reaches desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, cut cream cheese into small pieces. Coarsely grate cheddar. Place both in an ovenproof skillet.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  7. 7
    Add milk, Dijon, and spices to the skillet with the cheese. Set over medium-high heat and bring to a simmer, stirring often. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium and cook, stirring frequently, until sauce is combined and thickened, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp nutmeg, ground
  8. 8
    When the pasta and turnip are done, drain in a colander, and transfer to the skillet with the sauce. Stir to combine and spread out in an even layer.
  9. 9
    Sprinkle bread crumbs over the cheesy macaroni. Place skillet in the oven and bake until bread crumbs are golden, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, honey, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Add spinach to the bowl with the dressing, then toss to coat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide cheesy macaroni and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Cheesy One-Pot Gnocchi with Italian Sausage & Spinach

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (235) $3.20/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice the onion. Peel and mince the garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add the onion and garlic to the pan; cook, stirring frequently, until slightly softened, 3-4 minutes.
  5. 5
    Use the tip of a knife to slice down the length of the sausages. Peel off the casings and discard. Add the pork to the pan and break apart with a spoon. Cook, stirring occasionally, until pork is browned and crumbly, 5-7 minutes.
    2 pork sausages
  6. 6
    Coarsely grate the mozzarella. Finely grate the Parmesan.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
    ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  7. 7
    Add gnocchi, diced tomatoes with their juices, water, Italian seasoning, and salt to the pan. Cover and cook on medium heat until gnocchi is cooked through, 3-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ¼ cup water
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Wash and dry the spinach (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.) Add spinach to the pan; stir until wilted and the ingredients are combined, 1-2 minutes.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  9. 9
    Sprinkle both cheeses over the gnocchi and cover. Let sit for 3-5 minutes until cheese is melted.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide gnocchi between bowls and enjoy!

Cheesy Pasta Marinara with Roasted Edamame, Mushrooms & Bell Pepper

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (8) $1.29/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board large pot measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; medium dice. Transfer to a baking dish.
    1 yellow bell pepper
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the baking dish with the bell pepper along with mushrooms and edamame. Drizzle with oil, season with spices, and toss to combine.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    1 cup edamame, shelled
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until veggies are just tender, 10-15 minutes.
  6. 6
    When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and return to the pot. Cover and set aside to keep warm.
  7. 7
    When the veggies are tender, remove from oven. Pour marinara sauce over the veggies, stir to combine, and spread out in an even layer. Sprinkle with cheese.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  8. 8
    Return baking dish to the oven and continue to bake until sauce is warmed through and cheese is melted, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Add the veggie sauce to the pot with the pasta and stir to combine.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Cheesy Pinto Bean, Carrot & Potato Soup with Lemon-Dijon Side Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 61% (7) $2.52/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) canpinto beans
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    1 medium carrot
    ½ lemon
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  2. 2
    Peel and medium dice potatoes. Place in a medium saucepan.
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and medium dice carrot. Add to the pan with the potatoes.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the pan.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Add broth, beans with their liquid (do not drain), and spices to the pan; stir to combine the soup, then bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until veggies and beans are tender, 12-15 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (15 oz) can pinto beans
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and mince additional garlic. Place in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic. Add mayo, Dijon, soy sauce, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp soy sauce
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
  9. 9
    When the soup is done, remove from the heat. Use a hand blender to purée the soup until smooth. (If using a regular blender, work in batches as needed, then return soup to the pan.)
  10. 10
    Coarsely grate cheese. Add to the soup and stir until melted and combined.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  11. 11
    Divide soup between bowls. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

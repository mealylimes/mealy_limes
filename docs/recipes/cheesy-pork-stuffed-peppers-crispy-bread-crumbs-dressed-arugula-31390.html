Cheesy Pork Stuffed Peppers with Crispy Bread Crumbs & Dressed Arugula

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (76) $2.97/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 2red bell peppers
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    2 red bell peppers
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and halve bell peppers lengthwise. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper; arrange cut-side down. Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, flipping once, until peppers are lightly charred and tender, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    While the pork is cooking, juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, honey, and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the dressing and set aside. (Reserve remaining parsley for the next step.)
  8. 8
    Finely grate Parmesan. When the pork is done, add Parmesan to the skillet, along with the reserved parsley, and stir to combine the filling. Remove from heat.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  9. 9
    Stuff peppers with pork filling, then top with bread crumbs. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to broil until bread crumbs are toasted, 1 minute more. Remove from oven.
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide stuffed peppers and arugula between plates. Enjoy!

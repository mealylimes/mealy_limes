Cheesy Pumpkin Sourdough Toasts with Apple & Arugula-Cranberry Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $2.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupdried cranberries
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • honey
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1egg
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½lemon
  • 4 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Coarsely grate cheese. Place in a medium bowl along with pumpkin, egg, and spices, then whisk until smooth. Set aside.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
    ½ (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
    1 egg
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp paprika
  3. 3
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven.
    4 slices sourdough bread
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    1 Granny Smith apple
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  5. 5
    Divide cheesy pumpkin mixture between toast slices, spreading it to the edges. Return toast to the oven and broil until topping is golden around the edges and warmed through, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool slightly.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a clean medium bowl. Add oil, honey, Dijon, and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Add arugula and cranberries to the bowl with the dressing, then toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
  8. 8
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple.
  9. 9
    Divide cheesy pumpkin toasts between plates and top with apple slices. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

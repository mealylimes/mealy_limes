Cheesy Roast Beef "Reuben in a Bowl" with Cabbage & Special Sauce

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (14) $0.24/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ketchup
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 1 (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  • hot sauce
  • 8 ozdeli roast beef
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place half in a small bowl and set remaining aside for serving.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, stack roast beef slices one on top of another, then medium dice. Add to the bowl with the green onion.
    8 oz deli roast beef
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add green onions, roast beef, shredded cabbage, and spices. Stir to combine, then cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until cabbage is tender, 5-6 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place dressing, ketchup, and hot sauce in the reserved bowl. Stir to combine the special sauce.
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
    1 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp hot sauce
  6. 6
    When the cabbage is tender, remove skillet from heat, and sprinkle cheese over top. Re-cover and let sit until cheese starts to melt, about 2 minutes.
    1 cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  7. 7
    To serve, divide cheesy cabbage and roast beef between bowls. Drizzle with special sauce and sprinkle with reserved green onions. Enjoy!

Cheesy Roast Beef & Sauerkraut "Reuben" Quesadilla with Cucumber Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $1.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 1English cucumber
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • brown sugar
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 1 cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ⅔ cupsauerkraut
  • 8 ozdeli roast beef
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, and shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl along with yogurt, vinegar, sugar, and salt, then stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 English cucumber
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
    ½ shallot
  4. 4
    Drain sauerkraut in a colander to remove excess liquid. Set aside to drain further.
    ⅔ cup sauerkraut
  5. 5
    Stack roast beef slices one on top of another, then medium dice.
    8 oz deli roast beef
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, lay tortillas on a flat surface and divide drained sauerkraut, roast beef, and cheese between them, arranging on one half of each tortilla. Fold in half to close.
    2 large flour tortillas
    1 cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add quesadillas and cook, flipping halfway through, until cheese is melted and tortillas are golden on both sides, 5-6 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    Cut each quesadilla into wedges.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide quesadillas and cucumber salad between plates. Enjoy!

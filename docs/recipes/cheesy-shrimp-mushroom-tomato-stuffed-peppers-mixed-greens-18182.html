Cheesy Shrimp, Mushroom & Tomato Stuffed Peppers with Mixed Greens

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $7.17/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 2orange bell peppers
  • 1tomato
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack about 6-inches from the top.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 orange bell peppers
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 tomato
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and halve bell peppers lengthwise. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, season with salt and pepper, then arrange cut-side down.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, flipping once, until peppers are lightly charred and tender, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, thinly slice mushrooms and place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the mushrooms.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry, then roughly chop into bite-sized pieces.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add shrimp, mushrooms, onion, garlic, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender and shrimp is cooked through, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, medium dice tomato and place in the reserved bowl.
  11. 11
    Coarsely grate cheese; add half to the bowl with the tomato (reserving the rest for topping the peppers).
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  12. 12
    When the shrimp and veggies are done, add tomato and cheese to the skillet; stir to combine the filling. (Reserve bowl for the salad.)
  13. 13
    Divide shrimp filling between pepper halves and top with reserved cheese. Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until cheese is golden, 2-3 minutes; remove from oven.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    Add mixed greens and pumpkin seeds to the bowl with the dressing; toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  16. 16
    To serve, divide peppers and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Cheesy Spinach & Scallion Enchiladas

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (18) $3.46/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • cumin, ground
  • onion powder
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 6small corn tortillas
Cookware
baking dish can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the ends of the green onions; cut the onions crosswise into small pieces. Roughly chop the spinach.
  4. 4
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Add spinach and half of the green onions to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally until spinach is wilted, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
  6. 6
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Once hot, add the oil. Let the oil heat up for about 30 seconds, then whisk in the flour. Continuing to whisk, cook the flour for another 2 minutes.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  7. 7
    Slowly whisk in the broth and tomato sauce. Stir in the spices. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer the enchilada sauce for 4-5 minutes, until thick. Remove from heat.
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    2 tbsp chili powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp oregano
  8. 8
    Grate the cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    In a large bowl, combine the spinach mixture, ricotta, yogurt, and salt.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ½ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Spoon ¼-cup of the spinach mixture onto the center of each tortilla. Roll up and place seam side down in a baking dish. Pour enchilada sauce over top and sprinkle with the cheese.
    6 small corn tortillas
  11. 11
    Place in the oven and bake for 10 minutes, until cheese is melted.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide enchiladas between plates and top with remaining green onions. Enjoy!

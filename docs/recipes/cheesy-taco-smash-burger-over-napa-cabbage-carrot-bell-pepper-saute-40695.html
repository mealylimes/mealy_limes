Cheesy Taco Smash Burger over Napa Cabbage, Carrot & Bell Pepper Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (26) $3.45/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 1 tbsptomato paste
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
aluminum foil can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    1 medium carrot
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Peel and trim carrot; using a peeler, peel the carrot into noodle-like strips. Add to the bowl with the cabbage.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  5. 5
    Place beef, tomato paste, and spices in another medium bowl. Use your hands to combine the beef mixture. Form into 2 equal-sized meatballs and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 tbsp tomato paste
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add meatballs to the skillet, spacing them apart; press down with the bottom of a spatula to form ½-inch thick patties. Cook, undisturbed, until golden on one side, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    When the burgers are done on one side, flip and top with cheese. Continue to cook until cheese melts and burgers are cooked through, 3-4 minutes more. Transfer burgers to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
  10. 10
    While the burgers are cooking, peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the veggies.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, garlic, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. When the veggies are done, add cilantro to the skillet, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide vegetable sauté between plates or bowls, then top with cheesy burgers and yogurt. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

Cheesy Tex-Mex Beef, Carrot & Zucchini Noodle Skillet with Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (36) $4.38/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • cumin, ground
  • 1tomato
  • 1yellow bell pepper
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 medium carrot
    1 yellow bell pepper
    1 tomato
  2. 2
    Trim zucchini and carrot; use a julienne (or regular) peeler to make noodles. Place both in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the veggie noodles and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add onion, garlic, beef, and spices. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    2 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, grate cheddar and set aside.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  9. 9
    When the beef is done, add veggie noodles, bell pepper, and water to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the liquid has evaporated and veggies are tender, 3-4 minutes more.
    ¼ cup water
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, medium dice tomato.
  11. 11
    When the veggies are tender, add tomato and tomato sauce to the skillet; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, remove from heat and sprinkle with cheddar.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  12. 12
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until cheese is melted and golden, 2-3 minutes.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide skillet between plates or bowls and top with a dollop of yogurt. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt

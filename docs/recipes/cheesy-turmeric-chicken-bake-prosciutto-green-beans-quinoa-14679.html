Cheesy Turmeric Chicken Bake with Prosciutto, Green Beans & Quinoa

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 65% (5) $4.78/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lemon
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts small saucepan spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. 3
    Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin cutlets.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  4. 4
    Place chicken in a baking dish and season with spices on both sides.
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, cut or tear prosciutto into pieces and place on top of the seasoned chicken.
    4 slices prosciutto
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, and trim beans; divide into loose bundles and arrange on top of each cutlet.
    ½ lb green beans
  7. 7
    Crumble cheese over the beans.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
  8. 8
    Wash and dry lemon. Zest and juice it over the chicken stacks; drizzle with oil.
    ½ lemon
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Place baking dish in the oven and bake until chicken is cooked through, about 20 minutes.
  10. 10
    When the chicken is done, change oven setting to broil on high and cook until the chicken and cheese are golden, 1-2 minutes.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide quinoa and chicken-bean stacks between plates; drizzle with sauce from the dish and enjoy!

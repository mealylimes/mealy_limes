Cheesy Veggie Burger with Guacamole, Salsa & Lime-Cilantro Greens

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $1.34/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • mayonnaise
  • ¼ cupsalsa
  • 15 mlItalian salad dressing
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 2veggie burger patties, plant-based
  • ½ cupguacamole
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook until golden brown on one side, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 veggie burger patties, plant-based
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Juice lime into the bowl with the cilantro. Add mayo, dressing, and chili powder; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp Italian salad dressing
    ⅛ tsp chili powder
  6. 6
    Flip the burger patties, top with cheese, and continue to cook until cheese is melted and patties are golden brown and cooked through, 4-5 minutes more (or cook patties according to package instructions). Remove to a plate.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  8. 8
    Add mixed greens to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  9. 9
    Place bottom half of each bun on a plate, then layer with a cheesy burger patty, guacamole, and salsa; close with the top half of each bun. Serve with salad on the side. Enjoy!
    ½ cup guacamole
    ¼ cup salsa

Cheesy Zucchini Fritters with Lime Crema & Mexican "Street Corn" Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (50) $3.82/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1egg
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 ½ cupsfrozen corn
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  2. 2
    Trim and coarsely grate zucchini. Transfer to a colander and sprinkle with salt. Place the colander in a bowl to catch the excess moisture and set aside.
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lime. Divide between a small and medium bowl. Add yogurt and spices to the small bowl, then whisk to combine the lime crema. Set aside. (Reserve medium bowl for the next step.)
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the medium bowl with the lime along with additional yogurt, mayo, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Coarsely grate mozzarella. Place in another medium bowl along with egg and spices. Stir to combine.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
    1 egg
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-low heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, use paper towels or a clean cloth to squeeze excess moisture out of the grated zucchini. Transfer to the bowl with the mozzarella mixture, along with flour, and stir once more to combine the batter.
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Using a ¼-cup measure, drop batter into the skillet, spacing scoops a few inches apart; flatten slightly with the back of a spoon. Cook, flipping once, until fritters are golden brown, 3-5 minutes per side. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl (reserving a small handful of green onions for serving).
  12. 12
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Small dice and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  13. 13
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  14. 14
    Place corn in the colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Add to the bowl, along with feta, and stir to combine the salad.
    1 ½ cups frozen corn
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  15. 15
    To serve, divide salad and fritters between plates or bowls. Top fritters with lime crema and reserved green onions. Enjoy!

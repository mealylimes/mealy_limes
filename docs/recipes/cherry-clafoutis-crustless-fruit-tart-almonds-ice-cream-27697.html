Cherry Clafoutis (Crustless Fruit Tart) with Almonds & Ice Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $0.28/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1 ½ fl ozwhole milk
  • 1egg
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • granulated sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • ½ cupfrozen cherries
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls muffin pan oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 375°F. Coat 2 muffin cups with butter and set aside.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  2. 2
    Melt additional butter in a small saucepan set over medium heat. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place milk, egg, sugar, and vanilla in a small bowl, then whisk until sugar has dissolved.
    1 ½ fl oz whole milk
    1 egg
    2 tbsp granulated sugar
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  4. 4
    Add melted butter, flour, and salt to the bowl with the milk mixture. Whisk until batter is just combined (take care not to over-mix it).
    3 tbsp all-purpose flour
    1 pinch salt
  5. 5
    Divide batter and frozen cherries between muffin cups.
    ½ cup frozen cherries
  6. 6
    Place muffin pan in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until clafoutis are golden, puffed, and a knife or skewer inserted in the center comes out almost clean, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes (the clafoutis will shrink a little as they cool).
  7. 7
    To serve, divide clafoutis and ice cream between bowls; sprinkle with almonds. Enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced

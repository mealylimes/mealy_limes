Chicken, Bacon, Cabbage & Portobello Mushroom Stir-Up with Walnuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $4.26/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ¾ lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
  4. 4
    While the bacon is cooking, pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces.
    ¾ lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  5. 5
    Return skillet with drippings to medium-high heat; add chicken and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until chicken is golden and cooked through, 5-6 minutes.
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Wash and dry cabbage, then trim off and discard root end; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the garlic.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
  8. 8
    Wash, dry, and halve mushrooms, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick slices; add to the bowl.
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
  9. 9
    When the chicken is done, add veggies and additional salt to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are fork-tender, 5-6 minutes more.
    ¼ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, roughly chop walnuts.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  11. 11
    When the veggies are done, drain off any excess cooking liquid from the skillet, then add crispy bacon, walnuts, and vinegar. Stir to combine and remove from heat.
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  12. 12
    To serve, divide stir-up between plates or bowls and enjoy!

Chicken Caesar Salad with Roasted Broccoli, Tomatoes & Almonds

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $1.39/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • ¼ cupCaesar dressing
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Transfer broccoli to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing broccoli halfway through, until fork-tender, about 15 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise. Add to the bowl with the lettuce.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  6. 6
    Place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces and add to the bowl. Set aside.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  7. 7
    When the broccoli is done, remove from the oven and let cool, about 5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Add roasted broccoli, Parmesan, almonds, and dressing to the bowl with the chicken. Toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
    ¼ cup Caesar dressing
  9. 9
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

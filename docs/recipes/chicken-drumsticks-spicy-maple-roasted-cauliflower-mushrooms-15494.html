Chicken Drumsticks with Spicy Maple-Roasted Cauliflower & Mushrooms

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 65% (10) $3.15/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
aluminum foil baking dish baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Place chicken in a medium bowl, drizzle with oil, season with spices, and toss to coat. Transfer to the baking sheet and arrange in a single layer.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until the chicken is cooked through and golden, 30-35 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ lb white mushrooms
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  5. 5
    Place maple syrup, oil, soy sauce, chili-garlic sauce, and paprika in a clean medium bowl; whisk to combine the sauce.
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp paprika
  6. 6
    Cut off leaves from the cauliflower, separate into small florets, and cut the thick stems into smaller pieces; add to the bowl with the sauce.
  7. 7
    Quarter mushrooms, add to the bowl, and toss to combine. Transfer veggies to a baking dish and spread out in an even layer.
  8. 8
    Add baking dish to the oven and roast, tossing halfway through, until the veggies are fork-tender, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    While the veggies are roasting, trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide chicken and veggies between plates; top with green onions and enjoy!

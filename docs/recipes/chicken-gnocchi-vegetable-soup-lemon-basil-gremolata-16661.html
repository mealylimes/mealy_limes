Chicken, Gnocchi & Vegetable Soup with Lemon-Basil Gremolata

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (6) $4.44/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½lemon
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lemon
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrots; trim and small dice celery. Place both in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the veggies, chicken, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies start to soften and chicken is almost cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Add broth to the pan, stir to combine, and bring to a boil.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. 8
    Once the soup is boiling, reduce heat to medium, cover, and simmer until veggies are tender and chicken is cooked through, about 10 minutes.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; place in a small bowl.
  10. 10
    Peel and mince additional garlic; add to the bowl with the basil.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Zest and juice lemon into the bowl; add oil and stir to combine the gremolata. Set aside.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    When the veggies and chicken and done, add gnocchi to the soup and continue to simmer, uncovered, until gnocchi start to float to the surface, about 4 minutes.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
  13. 13
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and top with gremolata. Enjoy!

Chicken & Green Bean Casserole with Mushrooms & Thyme

Dinner Meat
⏱ 45 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (10) $4.07/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 lbgreen beans
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ small pkgfresh thyme
  • ¼ lbwhite mushrooms
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • all-purpose flour
  • butter, unsalted
  • black pepper
  • panko bread crumbs
  • salt
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water and bring to a boil.
  3. 3
    Wash green beans and trim ends. Add to boiling water. Cook until bright green and tender-crisp, 2-3 minutes. Drain in a colander and rinse under cold water.
    1 lb green beans
  4. 4
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Wash and slice mushrooms. Transfer everything to a plate.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
    ¼ lb white mushrooms
  5. 5
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Melt butter in skillet. Add chicken and cook until no longer pink, 2-3 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Melt more butter in skillet. Add onion, garlic, and mushrooms. Stirring occasionally, cook until vegetables are tender, 3-4 minutes.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    Grate ¼ cup of Parmesan. In a small bowl, mix together bread crumbs and Parmesan. Set aside.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
  10. 10
    Wash thyme and slide leaves off the stems.
    ½ small pkg fresh thyme
  11. 11
    Sprinkle flour over top vegetables; stir and cook for about 1 minute. Add thyme, chicken, yogurt, salt, and pepper to skillet. Stir to combine and remove from heat.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Transfer mixture to a small baking dish, add green beans, and stir to combine. Sprinkle with parmesan mixture over top.
  13. 13
    Place in the oven and cook for 10 minutes. Then broil for 1-2 minutes until topping is slightly browned.
  14. 14
    Serve in a bowl and enjoy!

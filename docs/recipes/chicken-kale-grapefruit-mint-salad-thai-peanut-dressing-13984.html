Chicken, Kale, Grapefruit & Mint Salad with Thai Peanut Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.18/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 1grapefruit
  • 1 bunchkale
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • rice vinegar
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 grapefruit
    1 bunch kale
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Cut off skin and white pith from grapefruit. Slice between the membranes to release segments, transfer to a small bowl, and set aside.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl, along with oil, peanut butter, vinegar, honey, fish sauce, and soy sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp natural peanut butter
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp honey
    1 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp soy sauce
  4. 4
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add the chicken and grill until cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side; remove from heat and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and transfer to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the kale and toss to combine the salad.
  9. 9
    Roughly chop peanuts.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  10. 10
    Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Top with grapefruit segments, grilled chicken, and peanuts. Enjoy!

Chicken Kebabs with Greek Salad & Tzatziki Sauce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (101) $3.70/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ½English cucumber
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½lemon
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • oregano, dried
  • red wine vinegar
  • salt
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince the garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  2. 2
    In a small bowl, whisk together the minced garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, vinegar, oregano, salt, and pepper.
    juice of ¼ lemon
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp oregano
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Cube the chicken and transfer to a medium bowl. Pour half of the dressing over top (save the rest for the salad) and toss to coat.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  4. 4
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Thread the chicken onto 2 skewers. Alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the grill/skillet without using skewers.
  6. 6
    Add the chicken to the grill or skillet and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 8-10 minutes.
  7. 7
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ head romaine lettuce
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  8. 8
    Finely dice ¼ of the cucumber. Peel and mince the remaining garlic. In a small bowl, prepare the tzatziki sauce by mixing together the cucumber, garlic, lemon juice, yogurt, and salt.
    1 clove garlic
    juice of ¼ lemon
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Chop the lettuce crosswise into 1 inch strips; transfer to a salad bowl. Quarter the remaining cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into chunks; add to the bowl. Halve the tomatoes; add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Add the olives, feta, and reserved dressing to the salad and toss.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  11. 11
    To serve, place salad on a plate and add a chicken skewer on top. Serve with tzatziki sauce and enjoy!

Chicken Pasta Salad with Apple, Pecans, Greens & Orange-Dijon Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (4) $3.36/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½Granny Smith apple
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½orange
  • ⅛ cuppecan halves
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ orange
    ½ Granny Smith apple
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Juice orange into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Add oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the orange juice; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken to the skillet and pan-fry until cooked through and golden brown, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel, halve, and thinly slice shallot into half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple; add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Coarsely chop pecans and add to the bowl.
    ⅛ cup pecans, dry roasted
  11. 11
    When the pasta is done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool; add to the bowl along with the mixed greens. Toss to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the salad between plates or bowls and top with sliced chicken. Enjoy!

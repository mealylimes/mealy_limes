Chicken Skewers with Roasted Strawberry, Arugula, Basil & Feta Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $5.11/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½shallot
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1lemon
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 2
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 lemon
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  4. 4
    Trim and quarter strawberries lengthwise. Transfer to a small baking dish, drizzle with honey, and season with salt. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tsp honey
    1 pinch salt
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until strawberries are soft, about 25 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, zest lemon into a medium bowl (reserve lemon for later use). Add mayo and spices to the bowl, then whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the marinade and stir to coat.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  8. 8
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the pan heats up, thread chicken onto skewers and place on a plate. (Alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.)
  10. 10
    Once the pan is hot, add the skewers and grill, turning occasionally, until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    ½ shallot
  12. 12
    Juice half of the reserved lemon into the bowl with the shallot. Add oil, pepper, and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing. (Reserve remaining lemon for serving.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  13. 13
    Pick basil leaves off the stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  14. 14
    Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the bowl along with arugula, cooled strawberries, and feta. Gently toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  15. 15
    To serve, divide skewers and salad between plates. Squeeze reserved lemon over the skewers and enjoy!

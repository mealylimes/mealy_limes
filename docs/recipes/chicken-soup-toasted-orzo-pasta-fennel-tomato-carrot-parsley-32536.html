Chicken Soup with Toasted Orzo Pasta, Fennel, Tomato, Carrot & Parsley

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (18) $3.33/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1fennel bulb
  • 3 ozorzo pasta
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 fennel bulb
    1 medium carrot
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels and medium dice.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until cooked through and golden, 4-5 minutes. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, trim off root end and stalks of the fennel, leaving only the white bulb. Halve fennel bulb lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and slice carrot crosswise into ¼-inch thick rounds. Add to the bowl with the fennel.
  7. 7
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  8. 8
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the bowl with the other veggies.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 7-8 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add orzo and cook, stirring frequently, until golden and toasted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    3 oz orzo pasta
  11. 11
    When the veggies are soft, add chicken, toasted orzo, broth, and tomatoes to the pan. Stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook until orzo is tender, 8-10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  12. 12
    While the soup is cooking, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the soup and stir to combine. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  13. 13
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

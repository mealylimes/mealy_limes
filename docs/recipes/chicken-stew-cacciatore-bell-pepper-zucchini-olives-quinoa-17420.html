Chicken Stew (Cacciatore) with Bell Pepper, Zucchini, Olives & Quinoa

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $5.22/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • cornstarch
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice onion; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a small bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; trim and quarter zucchini lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces. Add both to the bowl with the onion and garlic; set aside.
  5. 5
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the pan heats up, cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Sprinkle with cornstarch and season with spices on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, 3-4 minutes. (The chicken will not be cooked all the way through.)
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Add veggies and spices to the pan with the chicken; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, roughly chop olives.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, add tomatoes, broth, and olives to the pan; stir to combine and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until stew has thickened and chicken is cooked through, 6-8 minutes more.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  11. 11
    While the stew is simmering, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  12. 12
    When the stew is done, add half of the basil to the pan; stir to combine and remove from heat. (Reserve remaining basil for garnish.)
  13. 13
    To serve, divide quinoa and chicken stew between bowls; top with reserved basil and enjoy!

Chicken Taco Soup with Bell Pepper, Cheese & Tortilla Chips

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (73) $1.86/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 22 ½ mltaco seasoning
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 2 oztortilla chips
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, trim, and small dice celery. Place in a medium bowl.
    1 stick celery
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; small dice. Add to the bowl with the celery.
    1 red bell pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  7. 7
    When the veggies are soft, add chicken, broth, tomatoes, and spices to the pan; stir to combine. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat and simmer until flavors blend, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    1 ½ tbsp taco seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  8. 8
    To serve, divide soup between bowls. Top with cheese and chips. Enjoy!
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
    2 oz tortilla chips

Chicken Tagine with Apricot & Chickpea Cauliflower Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 66% (8) $3.93/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • cumin, ground
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 6dried apricots
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
Cookware
aluminum foil can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater or food processor large pot measuring cups measuring spoons slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ medium head cauliflower
  2. 2
    Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat. When the pot is hot add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add chicken to the pot and sear, flipping once, until golden brown on both sides, about 6 minutes. Once seared, remove chicken from the pot and set aside on a plate.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel and small dice the onion and carrots.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    After removing the chicken, add onion and carrot to the pot and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince the garlic and ginger.
    2 cloves garlic
  8. 8
    Add garlic to the pot and cook for 30 seconds. Add ginger and spices and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    1 tsp coriander
    1 tsp cumin
    1 tsp cinnamon
    ½ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Add broth and scrape any brown bits from the bottom of the pot. Return chicken to the pot, fitting the pieces into a single layer. Increase heat to high and bring liquid to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until chicken is cooked through, about 8 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, cut the cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves. Using a box grater or food processor, coarsely grate the cauliflower and transfer to a large bowl.
  11. 11
    Drain and rinse the chickpeas; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  12. 12
    Small dice apricots.
    6 dried apricots
  13. 13
    Using tongs, remove chicken from pot; transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
  14. 14
    Add cauliflower, chickpeas, almonds, and apricots to the pot. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook for 5 minutes.
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
  15. 15
    Use a slotted spoon to dish the cauliflower mixture onto plates, leaving any excess liquid in the pot. Top with chicken and enjoy!

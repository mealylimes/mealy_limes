Chicken Tenders with Za'atar Yogurt & Minty Arugula-Pistachio Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (46) $3.57/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • honey
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½English cucumber
  • sesame seeds
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ⅛ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 375°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Place mayo and spices in a medium bowl, then stir to combine.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels, then slice crosswise into ¾-inch strips. Add to the bowl with the mayo mixture and toss to coat.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  4. 4
    Add bread crumbs to the bowl with the chicken and turn to coat, pressing gently to adhere. Transfer chicken to the baking sheet and arrange in a single layer.
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
  5. 5
    Place chicken in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway, until golden and cooked through, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place yogurt, mayo, and spices in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the za'atar yogurt and set aside.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp sesame seeds
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  8. 8
    Place oil, vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper in a clean medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the bowl.
    ⅛ cup pistachios, unsalted
  12. 12
    When the chicken is almost done, add arugula to the bowl, and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Divide chicken tenders and salad between plates. Serve with za'atar yogurt for dipping and enjoy!

Chicken Waldorf Salad with Yogurt Dressing & Romaine Lettuce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $3.58/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • Dijon mustard
  • black pepper
  • ¼ lbred grapes
  • salt
  • honey
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½lemon
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 1Gala apple
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • ½ cupwalnuts
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) small saucepan stainless steel skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place chicken in a small saucepan. Season with salt and pepper. Cover with water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until chicken is cooked through, 8-12 minutes. Remove from liquid and place on a plate.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    In a medium bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together yogurt, lemon juice, honey, mustard, salt, and pepper.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    juice of ½ lemon
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    Roughly chop walnuts.
    ½ cup walnuts
  5. 5
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add walnuts and, stirring constantly, toast until golden brown, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat to cool.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Wash apple, celery, grapes, and chives. Core, medium dice, and add apple to bowl with dressing. Trim ends, medium dice, and add celery to bowl. Halve grapes and add to bowl. Chop chives and add to bowl.
    1 Gala apple
    2 sticks celery
    ¼ lb red grapes
    ½ small pkg chives
  7. 7
    Wash and dry lettuce. Chop crosswise into 1 inch strips.
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  8. 8
    Pat dry the chicken with paper towels, medium dice, and add to bowl. Toss salad until well combined.
  9. 9
    To serve, arrange a bed of lettuce leaves on a plate and top with salad. Enjoy!

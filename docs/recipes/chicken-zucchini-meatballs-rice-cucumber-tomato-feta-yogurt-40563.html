Chicken-Zucchini Meatballs with Rice, Cucumber, Tomato & Feta Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (27) $3.86/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½English cucumber
  • 4sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    While the rice is cooking, trim and coarsely grate zucchini. Transfer to a colander, then sprinkle with salt and toss to coat. Place the colander in a bowl to catch excess liquid and set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Roughly chop sun-dried tomatoes. Place in a medium bowl along with chicken, egg, bread crumbs, and spices.
    4 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    ½ lb ground chicken
    1 egg
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    ¼ tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Use paper towels or a clean cloth to squeeze excess moisture out of the grated zucchini. Add to the bowl with the chicken and mix with your hands until well combined. Using a scant ¼-cup measure, form the chicken mixture into meatballs. Transfer to the baking sheet.
  8. 8
    Place meatballs in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until golden and cooked through, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl along with feta, yogurt, water, and spices. Stir to combine the feta sauce and set aside.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 ½ tbsp water
    1 pinch garlic powder
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Place on a plate.
  11. 11
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the plate with the cucumber.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice and meatballs between bowls. Top with cucumber and tomato, then drizzle with feta sauce. Enjoy!

Chickpea-Kale Sauté with Raisins, Couscous & Toasted Pine Nuts

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (9) $3.56/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ⅛ cupraisins
  • ¼ cuppine nuts
  • 1 bunchkale
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes; fluff with a fork right before serving.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add pine nuts and cook, stirring often, until golden and fragrant, about 2 minutes. Transfer toasted nuts to a small bowl. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    ¼ cup pine nuts
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice onion crosswise into half-moons. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Return skillet to medium heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and garlic; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    While the onion is cooking, drain and rinse chickpeas.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  8. 8
    When the onion is soft, add chickpeas to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the chickpeas are golden and warmed through, about 5 minutes.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash and dry kale. Fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces.
    1 bunch kale
  10. 10
    Add kale to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the kale to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  11. 11
    Add broth, raisins, and spices to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until raisins have softened and sauce has thickened, 3-4 minutes more.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ⅛ cup raisins
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Add vinegar to the skillet, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
  13. 13
    To serve, divide couscous and chickpea-kale sauté between plates or bowls; sprinkle with pine nuts. Enjoy!

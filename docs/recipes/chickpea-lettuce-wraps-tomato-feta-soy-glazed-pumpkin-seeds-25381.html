Chickpea Lettuce Wraps with Tomato, Feta & Soy-Glazed Pumpkin Seeds

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $3.14/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • rice vinegar
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Place pumpkin seeds, soy sauce, and maple syrup in a medium bowl; stir to combine. Transfer seeds to the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
    1 ½ tsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp pure maple syrup
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring seeds halfway through, until golden and toasted, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool (the seeds will harden as they cool).
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  5. 5
    Zest and juice lime into the reserved bowl. Add oil, vinegar, salt, and crushed red pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Halve or quarter tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Drain and rinse chickpeas in a colander. Add to the bowl.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  10. 10
    Place corn in the colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Add to the bowl.
    1 cup frozen corn
  11. 11
    Break apart the cooled pumpkin seeds. Add to the bowl, along with feta, and stir to combine the chickpea salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  12. 12
    Separate the lettuce into leaves. Pile leaves, in stacks of two per wrap, onto plates; fill with chickpea salad. Enjoy!

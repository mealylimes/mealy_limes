Chickpea Tacos with Avocado Cream

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (16) $2.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • chili powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1avocado
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • ½lime
  • 1green bell pepper
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain and rinse the beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 green bell pepper
    1 avocado
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Seed and small dice the bell peppers; set aside.
  4. 4
    Peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Add the bell peppers, onion, garlic, and chickpeas. Cook until the chickpeas are slightly golden, stirring occasionally, about 3-4 minutes.
  9. 9
    Add soy sauce, chili powder, and paprika to the chickpeas and sauté for 5-7 more minutes, until golden brown.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp paprika
  10. 10
    Add the avocado, water, and salt to a blender. Juice the lime into the blender. Purée until smooth.
    2 tbsp water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Wrap the tortillas in damp paper towels and microwave until heated through, 15-30 seconds.
    6 small flour tortillas
  12. 12
    To assemble, add the chickpea vegetable mixture to each tortilla. Top with a drizzle of the avocado cream. Enjoy!

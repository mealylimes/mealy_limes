Chickpea-Tomato Noodle Soup with Kale Caesar & Breadsticks

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $1.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • 3 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • 1shallot
  • ½lemon
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1flatbread or naan
  • ½ bunchkale
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot and garlic. Place half the garlic in a medium bowl and set aside for the salad dressing.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shallot and remaining garlic to the pan; cook, stirring occasionally, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Add broth, tomato sauce, pasta, and spices to the pan; stir to combine. Bring to a simmer, reduce heat to medium, cover, and cook until noodles are just tender, about 8 minutes.
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    3 oz penne pasta
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, cut flatbread into 1½-inch wide strips, place on a baking sheet pan, and drizzle with oil. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until crispy, 8-10 minutes. Remove from the oven and set aside.
    1 flatbread or naan
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    While the breadsticks are baking, wash, dry, and juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic. Add mayo, Dijon, soy sauce, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ lemon
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp soy sauce
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Wash, dry, and fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and transfer to the bowl with the dressing; toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    ½ bunch kale
  9. 9
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Once the pasta is tender, add chickpeas to the pan and stir to combine. Continue to cook until heated through, 1-2 minutes.
    ½ (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  10. 10
    Finely grate cheese.
    ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Ladle soup into bowls and serve with salad and breadsticks on the side. Top soup and salad with cheese and enjoy!

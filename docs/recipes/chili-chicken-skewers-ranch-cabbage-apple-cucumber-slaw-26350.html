Chili Chicken Skewers with Ranch Cabbage, Apple & Cucumber Slaw

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (8) $3.13/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1avocado
  • 45 mlranch dressing
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels, cut into 1-inch cubes, and season with spices. Thread onto skewers and place on a plate. (If you don't have skewers, you can cook the chicken directly on the grill pan.)
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    2 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil; add the skewers and cook, turning occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    1 Granny Smith apple
    1 avocado
  6. 6
    Using a clean cutting board, trim and medium dice cucumber. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Quarter, core, and medium dice apple. Add to the bowl with the cucumber.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl along with shredded cabbage and dressing, then stir to combine the slaw.
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    3 tbsp ranch dressing
  9. 9
    To serve, divide skewers and slaw between plates. Enjoy!

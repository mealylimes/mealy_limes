Chili Crunch Shrimp Noodle Bowl with Carrot, Cucumber & Bell Pepper

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (111) $3.95/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • rice vinegar
  • brown sugar
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) julienne peeler (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, remove from heat and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes (or prepare according to package instructions). Drain in a colander and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 medium carrot
    ½ English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
  3. 3
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place white and pale green parts in a small bowl. Place remaining (dark green) onion on a plate and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the green onions.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add green onions and garlic; cook, stirring frequently, until onion is golden and crisp, 2-3 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
  7. 7
    Add crushed red pepper, sesame seeds, sugar, and salt to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Transfer to the reserved bowl, along with soy sauce and vinegar, then stir to combine the chili crunch. Set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp crushed red pepper
    1 tsp sesame seeds
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp rice vinegar
  8. 8
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  9. 9
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp, salt, and pepper; cook until opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, peel and trim carrot; using a julienne (or regular) peeler, peel the carrot into noodle-like strips. Add to the plate with the reserved green onions.
  11. 11
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the plate.
  12. 12
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the plate.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide noodles, shrimp, and veggies between bowls. Top with reserved green onions and drizzle with chili crunch. Enjoy!

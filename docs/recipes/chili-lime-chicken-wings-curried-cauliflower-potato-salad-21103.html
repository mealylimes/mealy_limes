Chili-Lime Chicken Wings with Curried Cauliflower "Potato Salad"

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.26/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • ½lime
  • mayonnaise
  • 1red chili pepper
  • 1green bell pepper
  • brown sugar
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean up easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    1 red chili pepper
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lime into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Quarter chili pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl with the lime. (Be careful with chilis; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl along with oil, sugar, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the sauce.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Pat chicken wings dry with paper towels and add to the bowl with the sauce; toss to coat. Transfer wings to the baking sheet, drizzle with any sauce left in the bowl, and spread out in an even layer.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
  7. 7
    Place wings in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  9. 9
    While the water heats up, separate cauliflower into small bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
  10. 10
    Once the water is boiling, uncover and add cauliflower and salt; stir for a few seconds. Cook until crisp-tender, about 3 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside to drain further.
    ½ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, place mayo, Dijon, vinegar, and spices in a clean medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    ½ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl along with the cooked cauliflower and stir to combine the salad; set aside.
  13. 13
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Sprinkle over the wings and toss to combine.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide wings and cauliflower salad between plates. Enjoy!

Chili Salmon Tacos with Orange-Avocado Slaw, Cilantro & Lime Crema

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (3) $5.59/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1shallot
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 2oranges
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • 6small corn tortillas
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    2 oranges
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Juice half of the lime into a small bowl. Add yogurt and salt; stir to combine the crema and set aside. (Reserve remaining lime for later use.)
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place oil and spices in medium bowl, then stir to combine the spice oil.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp chili powder
    ¾ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Pat salmon dry with paper towels. Cut into 1-inch thick fingers and add to the bowl with the spice oil. Stir to coat, then transfer to the baking sheet.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until salmon is cooked through, about 8 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    1 shallot
  8. 8
    Juice reserved lime into the bowl with the shallot, then add oil and salt. Stir to combine the dressing.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl with the dressing. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  10. 10
    Peel oranges, separate into segments, and roughly chop the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl, along with the broccoli slaw, and gently stir to combine.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  13. 13
    Warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small corn tortillas
  14. 14
    Divide tortillas between plates, then fill with salmon and a small portion of slaw; drizzle with lime crema. Serve with remaining slaw on the side and enjoy!

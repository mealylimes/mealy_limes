Chili-Spiced Pumpkin Soup with Pork, Cauliflower, Cilantro & Lime

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $2.70/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½lime
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
  2. 2
    Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the cilantro to the bowl with the cauliflower (reserving the rest for serving).
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add pork and spices. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    1 tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the cauliflower and cilantro.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    When the pork is done, add veggies to the pan and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
  8. 8
    Add broth and pumpkin to the pan; stir to combine and bring the soup to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the cauliflower is soft, about 10 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
  9. 9
    When the soup is done, juice lime into the pan, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with reserved cilantro. Enjoy!

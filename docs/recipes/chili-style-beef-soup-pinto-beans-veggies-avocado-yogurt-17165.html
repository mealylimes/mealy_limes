Chili-Style Beef Soup with Pinto Beans, Veggies, Avocado & Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (6) $4.01/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • coriander, ground
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) canpinto beans
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lime
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 red bell pepper
    2 sticks celery
    ½ lime
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Place in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; trim and thinly slice celery. Add both to the bowl with the green onion.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the veggies.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add beef and cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can pinto beans
  8. 8
    When the beef is done, add veggies and salt to the pan; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  9. 9
    When the veggies are soft, add spices to the pan and cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp chili powder
    2 tsp cumin, ground
    1 tsp paprika
    ¾ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  10. 10
    Add beans, water, and tomato sauce to the pan; stir to combine the soup, then bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until beans are tender, 12-15 minutes.
    2 cup water
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  11. 11
    While the soup simmers, cut lime into wedges.
  12. 12
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and small dice the flesh.
  13. 13
    Divide soup between bowls; top with avocado and yogurt. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing over top. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt

Chimichurri Shrimp with Mashed Potatoes & Garlicky Sautéed Spinach

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (59) $5.75/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2 fl ozwhole milk
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 3 mediumyellow potatoes
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 medium yellow potatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Peel and medium dice potatoes; place in a medium saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Cover and bring to a boil over high heat; once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook until soft, 10-12 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, shave parsley and cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place herbs in a small bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince shallot and garlic. Add both to the bowl with herbs along with oil, vinegar, and spices. Stir to combine the chimichurri and set aside.
    ½ shallot
    1 clove garlic
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, place shrimp in a medium bowl, along with half of the chimichurri, and stir to coat. (Reserve remaining chimichurri for serving.)
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp and cook until they are opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Transfer shrimp to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince additional garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, 15-30 seconds.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for it to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Season with salt and pepper, stir to combine, then remove from heat. Cover to keep warm.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    When the potatoes are tender, drain in a clean colander and return to the pan. Add milk, oil, salt, and pepper, then mash until smooth.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide mashed potatoes between plates or bowls, then top with garlicky spinach and shrimp. Drizzle with reserved chimichurri and enjoy!

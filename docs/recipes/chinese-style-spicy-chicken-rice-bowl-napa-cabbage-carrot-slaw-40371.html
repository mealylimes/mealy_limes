Chinese-Style Spicy Chicken Rice Bowl with Napa Cabbage & Carrot Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (57) $2.93/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • rice vinegar
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    2 medium carrots
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and using a julienne (or regular) peeler, peel carrots into thin noodle-like sticks. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add half to the bowl, toss to combine the slaw, and set aside. (Reserve remaining green onions for serving.)
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels and large dice. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through, 6-7 minutes.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, place mayo, chili-garlic sauce, and vinegar in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    1 tbsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp rice vinegar
  12. 12
    When the chicken is done, add sauce to the skillet and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide rice, chicken, and slaw between bowls, then top with reserved green onions. Enjoy!

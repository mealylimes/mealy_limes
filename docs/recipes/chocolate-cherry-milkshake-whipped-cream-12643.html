Chocolate Cherry Milkshake with Whipped Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $0.39/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • cocoa powder
  • granulated sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • ½ cupfrozen cherries
  • 16 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
hand or regular blender ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill, 2-3 minutes.
  2. 2
    Once the bowl is chilled, remove from the freezer. Measure cold cream, sugar, and vanilla into the bowl.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy cream
    1 tsp granulated sugar
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
  3. 3
    Use a whisk to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, about 2 minutes. (For medium-soft peaks, when you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.) Set aside.
  4. 4
    Place ice cream, milk, cocoa powder, and cherries in a blender. Blend on high until smooth.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) vanilla ice cream
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
    1 tbsp cocoa powder
    ½ cup frozen cherries
  5. 5
    To serve, divide milkshake between glasses, top with whipped cream, and enjoy!

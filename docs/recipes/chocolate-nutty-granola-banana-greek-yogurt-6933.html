Chocolate Nutty Granola with Banana & Greek Yogurt

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $1.10/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupalmonds
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 1banana
  • oats, quick cooking
  • cocoa powder
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls parchment paper small saucepan spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Add oats to a medium mixing bowl.
    ½ cup oats, quick cooking
  3. 3
    Roughly chop nuts and add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup almonds
    ¼ cup walnuts
  4. 4
    Melt coconut oil in a small saucepan. Add maple syrup, salt, cocoa powder, and cinnamon; whisk to combine.
    2 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    2 tsp cocoa powder
    1 tsp cinnamon
  5. 5
    Pour oil mixture over oats and nuts and stir to coat.
  6. 6
    Spread out on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 6-8 minutes, stirring halfway through cooking. Watch carefully as the nuts can burn easily.
  7. 7
    Peel the banana and slice.
    1 banana
  8. 8
    Spoon yogurt into bowls and top with granola and banana. Enjoy!
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt

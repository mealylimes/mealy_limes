Chocolate, Peanut Butter, Banana & Date Protein Shake

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (7) $0.18/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • natural peanut butter
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1banana
  • cocoa powder
  • ⅛ cupmedjool dates, pitted
  • water
  • vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
Cookware
hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Not all protein powders are created equal. If desired, adjust shake consistency by adding more or less liquid per package instructions. This recipe will make about 3 cups of shake. Work in batches to best fit your blender.
  2. 2
    Peel banana, break into chunks, and place in a blender.
    1 banana
  3. 3
    Add milk, water, protein powder, dates, cocoa powder, and peanut butter to the blender. Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
    4 fl oz (½ cup) water
    2 servings vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
    ⅛ cup medjool dates, pitted
    1 ½ tbsp cocoa powder
    1 ½ tbsp natural peanut butter
  4. 4
    To serve, divide shake between glasses, adding ice if desired. Enjoy!

Chocolate-Strawberry Oatmeal with Sliced Almonds

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (8) $1.01/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • ¼ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ½ cupalmonds, sliced
  • oats, quick cooking
  • cocoa powder
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place salt and water in a small saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
    1 pinch salt
    1 ¾ cup water
  2. 2
    Once boiling, add oats, cocoa powder, and maple syrup; whisk to combine. Cover and let simmer, on low, 3-5 minutes, or until oats reach desired consistency.
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
    2 tbsp cocoa powder
    1 ½ tbsp pure maple syrup
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, trim, and thinly slice strawberries.
    ¼ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
  4. 4
    To serve, divide oats between bowls and top with strawberries, almonds, and milk. Enjoy!
    ½ cup almonds, sliced
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk

Chopped Veggie Salad with Sun-Dried Tomato Dressing & Breadsticks

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $4.79/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 9sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1lemon
  • 1flatbread or naan
  • 1tomato
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 medium carrot
    2 sticks celery
    ½ English cucumber
    1 tomato
    ½ head romaine lettuce
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic. Mince the sun-dried tomatoes. Add both to a large salad bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
    9 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  4. 4
    Zest and juice half the lemon into the salad bowl. Thinly slice remaining lemon into rounds and set aside for garnish.
  5. 5
    Add oil, honey, Dijon, and spices to the bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ tsp honey
    ¾ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¾ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Drizzle 1 tbsp dressing over the flatbread, then cut into 1½-inch wide strips and place on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until crispy, 8-10 minutes. Remove from the oven.
    1 flatbread or naan
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and thinly slice the onion. Peel and thinly slice carrot into rounds. Add both to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ medium red onion
  8. 8
    Trim celery, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim and medium dice cucumber. Medium dice tomato; add both to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Halve romaine leaves lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise into shreds; add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Drain and rinse the beans; add to the bowl.
    ½ (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  13. 13
    Drain and thinly slice artichokes; add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates and top with feta. Serve with breadsticks and lemon slices. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

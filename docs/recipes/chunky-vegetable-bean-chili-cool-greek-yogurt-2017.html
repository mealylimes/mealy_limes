Chunky Vegetable and Bean Chili with Cool Greek Yogurt

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $2.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 (15 oz) canskidney beans
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • cayenne pepper
  • chili powder
  • cumin, ground
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Coat bottom of saucepan with oil. Add onion and garlic.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  4. 4
    Wash celery and bell pepper. Medium dice and add celery to a saucepan. Seed, medium dice, and add pepper to saucepan. Stirring frequently, cook until vegetables have softened, 4-5 minutes.
    2 sticks celery
    1 red bell pepper
  5. 5
    Drain and rinse beans.
    2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans
  6. 6
    In a small bowl, mix together chili powder, cumin, salt, black pepper, and cayenne pepper.
    1 tbsp chili powder
    1 tsp cumin
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  7. 7
    Add spice mixture, beans, tomatoes, and broth to saucepan. Stir to combine and bring to a boil. Then reduce heat to medium, cover, and simmer until vegetables are tender and chili has thickened, about 10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. 8
    Wash green onions, trim and discard root ends, and chop. Stir ½ into the chili and save the rest for garnishing.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  9. 9
    Serve chili in a bowl, garnish with remaining green onions and spoonfuls of yogurt. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

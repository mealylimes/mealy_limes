Cilantro Lime Chicken Salad with Mango, Avocado & Bell Pepper

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (12) $3.93/serving
Ingredients
  • 1mango
  • 1avocado
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • honey
  • salt
  • crushed red pepper
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 avocado
    1 lime
    1 mango
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ head romaine lettuce
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince the garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Juice the lime into a small bowl, then add ½ of the cilantro (transfer the rest to a small bowl and save for garnishing), minced garlic, olive oil, honey, crushed red pepper, salt, and pepper; whisk together. Pour ½ of the sauce into a medium bowl and set the rest aside.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    Add the chicken to the medium bowl and coat with the sauce; set aside to marinade for 10 minutes.
  7. 7
    Seed and cut the pepper into strips lengthwise; transfer to a plate.
  8. 8
    Peel the mango and cut the flesh away from the pit; medium dice the flesh. Add to the plate next to the pepper.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit the avocado; scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the plate next to the pepper and mango.
  10. 10
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  11. 11
    Halve the lettuce lengthwise, then chop crosswise into 1-inch strips; transfer to a medium bowl.
  12. 12
    Add olive oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Remove the chicken from the marinade and pat dry with paper towels; place the fillets in the skillet and cook until browned and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
  14. 14
    Slice the chicken crosswise into strips.
  15. 15
    Divide the lettuce between two plates (or one plate and one lunch container) and layer the peppers, mangoes, avocado, and chicken over top. Drizzle with the remaining sauce and garnish with the remaining cilantro. Enjoy!

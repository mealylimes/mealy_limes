Cilantro Shrimp Fritters with Spicy Cabbage Slaw & Avocado Corn Salsa

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (31) $4.81/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1avocado
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • cornstarch
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • ⅔ cupfrozen corn
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 avocado
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic. Divide between two medium bowls and set one bowl aside for the fritters.
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Juice half of the lime into the remaining bowl with the garlic. Add yogurt and salt; stir to combine the dressing. (Reserve remaining lime for later use.)
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Roughly chop and add to the bowl with the dressing. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  5. 5
    Trim and medium dice cucumber. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw. Set aside.
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the other bowl with the garlic. (Reserve remaining cilantro for later use.)
  8. 8
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  9. 9
    Roughly chop shrimp into small pieces. Add to the bowl with the garlic and cilantro along with egg, bread crumbs, cornstarch, salt, and cayenne pepper. Stir to combine the batter.
    1 egg
    ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  10. 10
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    While the skillet heats up, place corn in a clean colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Set aside to drain further.
    ⅔ cup frozen corn
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Using a ¼-cup measure, drop batter into the skillet, spacing scoops a few inches apart; press down with a spatula to form round, ½-inch thick fritters.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Cook fritters, carefully flipping once (the fritters are delicate but will firm up as they finish cooking on the other side), until golden brown, 4-5 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, juice the reserved lime into a small bowl.
  15. 15
    Using a clean cutting board, halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the bowl with the lime juice along with the reserved cilantro, corn, and salt. Stir to combine the salsa.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  16. 16
    To serve, divide salad and shrimp fritters between plates. Top fritters with avocado-corn salsa and enjoy!

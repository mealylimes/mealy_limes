Classic Beef Chili with Avocado & Crusty Bread

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (16) $3.34/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1whole grain bun or roll
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½avocado
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 1 (15 oz) cankidney beans
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ mediumred onion
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Wash, seed, and mince jalapeño.
    ½ medium red onion
    2 cloves garlic
    1 jalapeño pepper
  3. 3
    Coat bottom of saucepan with oil. Add onion and jalapeño. Stirring frequently, cook until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  4. 4
    Drain and rinse beans.
    1 (15 oz) can kidney beans
  5. 5
    Add garlic, chili powder, and cumin to saucepan. Stir and cook for 30 seconds.
    1 tbsp chili powder
    1 tsp cumin
  6. 6
    Add beef and, breaking apart with a spoon, cook until crumbly and browned, 2-3 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  7. 7
    Add beans, tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook until chili thickens, about 10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Scoop out the avocado flesh and medium dice.
    ½ avocado
  9. 9
    Toast bread if desired.
    1 whole grain bun or roll
  10. 10
    To serve, place chili in a bowl and garnish with avocado. Enjoy with bread!

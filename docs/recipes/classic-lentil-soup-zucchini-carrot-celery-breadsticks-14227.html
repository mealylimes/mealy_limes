Classic Lentil Soup with Zucchini, Carrot, Celery & Breadsticks

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 74% (35) $2.34/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1flatbread or naan
  • ⅔ cupgreen lentils, dried
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    While the onion is cooking, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    1 stick celery
    1 medium zucchini squash
  5. 5
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse lentils under cold water; drain and transfer to the pan with the onion.
    ⅔ cup green lentils, dried
  6. 6
    Add broth, diced tomatoes, and spices to the pan; stir to combine, cover, and bring soup to a boil over medium-high heat.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¾ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    While the soup is heating, peel, trim, and slice carrots crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Trim and slice celery crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Add both to the soup.
  8. 8
    Trim and medium dice zucchini; add to the soup.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince garlic; add half the garlic to the soup (reserving the rest for the bread sticks) and stir to combine. Once the soup comes to a boil, reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook until veggies and lentils are tender, 15-20 minutes.
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to 425°F.
  11. 11
    Place reserved garlic in a small bowl along with oil and spices; stir to combine the garlic oil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  12. 12
    Drizzle garlic oil over the flatbread; cut into 1½-inch wide strips and transfer to a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until crispy, 8-10 minutes. Remove from the oven.
    1 flatbread or naan
  13. 13
    When the soup is done, add vinegar and stir to combine.
    ½ tsp red wine vinegar
  14. 14
    Divide soup between bowls and serve with breadsticks on the side. Enjoy!

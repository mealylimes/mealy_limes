Classic Steak & Egg Breakfast with Avocado, Tomato and Toast

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $3.40/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • ½avocado
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½tomato
  • ½ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife cutting board measuring spoons spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ avocado
    ½ tomato
  2. 2
    Thinly slice tomato into half rounds; set aside.
  3. 3
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ½ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Return skillet to medium-high, add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Gently crack eggs into the skillet and season with salt and pepper. Cook until the tops of the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, 3-4 minutes.
    2 eggs
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, toast bread and slice in half on the diagonal.
    2 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide steak, eggs, and toast between plates. Garnish with tomato and avocado. Enjoy!

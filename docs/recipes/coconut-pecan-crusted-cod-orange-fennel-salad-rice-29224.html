Coconut-Pecan Crusted Cod with Orange Fennel Salad & Rice

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (8) $3.83/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅛ cupdried cranberries
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • 1orange
  • mayonnaise
  • ⅛ cupshredded unsweetened coconut
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • ⅛ cuppecan halves
  • 1fennel bulb
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil and set aside.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, mince pecans. Place in a small bowl along with coconut and crushed red pepper, then stir to combine the topping.
    ⅛ cup pecan halves
    ⅛ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    While the rice is cooking, pat cod dry with paper towels, season with salt and pepper, then transfer to the baking sheet. Spread top side with mayo, then sprinkle evenly with the topping, pressing gently to adhere.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
  6. 6
    Place cod in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until cooked through, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    While the fish is baking, place oil, vinegar, maple syrup, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Wash and dry fennel; trim off root end and dark green stalks, leaving only the white bulb. Halve fennel bulb lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
    1 fennel bulb
  10. 10
    Wash, dry, and peel orange; separate into segments and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl, along with cranberries, and stir to combine the salad.
    1 orange
    ⅛ cup dried cranberries
  11. 11
    To serve, divide fish, rice, and salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

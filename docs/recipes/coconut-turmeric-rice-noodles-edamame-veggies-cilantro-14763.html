Coconut-Turmeric Rice Noodles with Edamame, Veggies & Cilantro

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (10) $2.73/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • fish sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • honey
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap) and bring to a boil. Remove from heat and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes or according to package directions; drain in a colander and rinse.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 crown broccoli
    1 medium carrot
    1 red bell pepper
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    1 cup edamame, shelled
  4. 4
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and cut carrot crosswise into ¼-inch thick rounds. Add to the bowl with the broccoli.
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, about 5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 ½ tsp virgin coconut oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Peel and small dice shallot.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 shallot
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, return them to the reserved bowl.
  11. 11
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic, ginger, and shallot to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 2 minutes.
    1 ½ tsp virgin coconut oil
  12. 12
    Add coconut milk, water, fish sauce, honey, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine the sauce and bring to a simmer.
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ¼ cup water
    1 tbsp fish sauce
    ½ tsp honey
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  13. 13
    Once the sauce is simmering, add drained rice noodles, edamame, and cooked veggies to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until noodles are warmed through and sauce has thickened slightly, 3-4 minutes.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, cut lime into wedges; set aside for serving.
  15. 15
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add cilantro to the skillet, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide saucy noodles and veggies between bowls; top with a squeeze of lime and enjoy!

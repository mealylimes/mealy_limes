Cod Spinach Florentine with Couscous & Garlic-Parmesan Green Beans

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (42) $5.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ¾ lbgreen beans
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • ½lemon
  • ½ cupcottage cheese
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ lemon
    ¾ lb green beans
  4. 4
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, uncover, then add salt and spinach. Stir until wilted, 30-60 seconds, then drain in a colander and rinse with cold water to cool. (Reserve pan for the next step.)
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place water and salt in the reserved pan and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes; fluff with a fork right before serving.
    1 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using your hands, squeeze as much water from the spinach as you can, then transfer to a cutting board. Roughly chop spinach and place in a small bowl.
  7. 7
    Zest lemon into the bowl with the spinach. Cut lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic. Add half to the bowl. (Set remaining garlic aside for later use.)
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Place cottage cheese in a colander or strainer and press with the back of a spoon to remove excess liquid. Add to the bowl, along with spices, and stir to combine the topping.
    ½ cup cottage cheese
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  10. 10
    Pat cod dry with paper towels and arrange on one half of a baking sheet pan. Season the top side of the fish with salt and pepper, then spread with spinach topping. Drizzle with oil.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Trim green beans. Add to the other half of the baking sheet, then drizzle with oil. Sprinkle with reserved garlic, salt, and pepper, then stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, stirring the green beans halfway, until fish is almost cooked through and beans are fork-tender, about 12 minutes.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  14. 14
    When the fish is almost cooked through, remove baking sheet from the oven and sprinkle green beans with Parmesan. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to roast until fish is cooked through and cheese is melted, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide couscous, cod, and green beans between plates. Squeeze lemon over top and enjoy!

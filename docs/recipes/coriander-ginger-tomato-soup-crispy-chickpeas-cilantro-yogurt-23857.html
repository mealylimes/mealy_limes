Coriander-Ginger Tomato Soup with Crispy Chickpeas, Cilantro & Yogurt

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 25% (2) $2.42/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 2 (14.5 oz) cansdiced tomatoes
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) hand blender or potato masher ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    1 medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-low heat.
  4. 4
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, coriander, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, about 10 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse chickpeas. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and pat dry with paper towels.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  6. 6
    Add whole (unpeeled) garlic cloves to the baking sheet with the chickpeas. Drizzle with oil, then season with additional coriander and salt. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    3 cloves garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring chickpeas halfway through, until crispy and golden, 15-18 minutes.
  8. 8
    While the chickpeas are roasting, wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
  9. 9
    When the onions are golden, add ginger, tomatoes, water, and coconut milk; bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, about 10 minutes.
    2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
    1 cup water
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  11. 11
    When the chickpeas and garlic are done, remove from oven. Carefully transfer roasted garlic to the cutting board. Using the back of a spoon, press on the cloves to remove the skins. Set aside the crispy chickpeas.
  12. 12
    Add roasted garlic and half of the cilantro to the soup, stir to combine, and remove from heat. Use a hand blender to purée the soup until smooth. Alternatively, use a potato masher to crush the veggies (the soup will be chunkier).
  13. 13
    To serve, divide soup between bowls; top with yogurt, crispy chickpeas, and remaining cilantro. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

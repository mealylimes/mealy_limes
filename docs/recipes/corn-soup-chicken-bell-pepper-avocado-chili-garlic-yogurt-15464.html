Corn Soup with Chicken, Bell Pepper, Avocado & Chili-Garlic Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $4.33/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1avocado
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½lime
  • 1green bell pepper
  • 2 cupsfrozen corn
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and cook, flipping once, until golden brown and cooked through, 7-8 minutes. Remove to a plate and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 green bell pepper
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 avocado
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the onion and garlic.
  7. 7
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  8. 8
    Return the saucepan (with cooking juices) to medium heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the onion, garlic, bell pepper, and jalapeño to the pan; cook, stirring often, until veggies soften, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    While the veggies cook, juice lime into the reserved bowl. Add yogurt, chili-garlic sauce, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the yogurt sauce and set aside.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; transfer to a plate.
  11. 11
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Add to the plate with the green onions.
  12. 12
    Add broth, corn, salt, and pepper to the pan. Cover and bring to a boil; once boiling, reduce heat and let simmer for 5 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 cups frozen corn
    1 tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, using two forks, shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  14. 14
    Transfer half the soup to a blender and purée until smooth. Return puréed soup to the pan, add the shredded chicken, and stir to combine. (Alternatively, use a hand blender to partially blend the soup directly in the pan.) Continue to cook, stirring often, until warmed through, 1-2 minutes.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide soup between bowls. Top each bowl with yogurt sauce, green onions, and avocado. Enjoy!

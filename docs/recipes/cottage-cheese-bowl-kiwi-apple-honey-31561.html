Cottage Cheese Bowl with Kiwi, Apple & Honey

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (13) $1.01/serving
Ingredients
  • honey
  • 2kiwifruit
  • 1Gala apple
  • 2 cupscottage cheese
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, quarter, and core apple; medium dice.
    1 Gala apple
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, peel, and trim kiwis; medium dice.
    2 kiwifruit
  3. 3
    To serve, divide cottage cheese between bowls. Top with apple, kiwi, and a drizzle of honey. Enjoy!
    2 cups cottage cheese
    1 tbsp honey

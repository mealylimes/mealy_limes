Couscous-Romaine Salad with Warm Peaches, Almonds, Feta & Dill

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.86/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • ⅓ cupalmonds
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1lemon
  • 2peaches
  • brown sugar
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring water and salt to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes; fluff with a fork.
    1 cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    ½ English cucumber
    1 head romaine lettuce
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    2 peaches
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice along with oil, Dijon, sugar, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Trim and small dice cucumber. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Halve lettuce lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl and set the salad aside. (Reserve remaining dill for serving.)
  8. 8
    When the couscous is done, transfer to a baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool slightly.
  9. 9
    Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Slice each quarter into ½-inch thick wedges.
  10. 10
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  11. 11
    While the skillet heats up, roughly chop almonds.
    ⅓ cup almonds
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add peaches, almonds, sugar, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until peaches are golden brown and fork-tender, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Add cooled couscous to the bowl with the salad and gently toss to combine.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with warm peaches, almonds, feta, and reserved dill. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

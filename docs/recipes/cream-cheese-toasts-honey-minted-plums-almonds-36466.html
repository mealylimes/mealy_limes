Cream Cheese Toasts with Honey-Minted Plums & Almonds

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (2) $1.01/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅛ cupalmonds
  • honey
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½lemon
  • ¼ small pkgfresh mint
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
  • 2plums
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ¼ small pkg fresh mint
    2 plums
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the lemon, along with honey, and stir to combine.
    2 tsp honey
  5. 5
    Halve plums lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Cut each half into thin slices, add to the bowl, and stir to combine. Set aside.
  6. 6
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    4 slices whole grain bread
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, roughly chop almonds.
    ⅛ cup almonds
  8. 8
    Spread cream cheese over the toast, then top with plums and almonds.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  9. 9
    To serve, divide toasts between plates and enjoy!

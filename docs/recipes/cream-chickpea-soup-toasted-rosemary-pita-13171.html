Cream of Chickpea Soup with Toasted Rosemary Pita

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (14) $1.94/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    1 medium zucchini squash
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel, trim, and small dice carrots. Transfer both to a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Trim and small dice celery; add to the bowl.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the saucepan heats up, peel and mince garlic. Place half in a small bowl and set aside.
    3 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once the saucepan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Add veggies, remaining garlic, and spices to the saucepan. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are soft, 5-6 minutes.
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse chickpeas; transfer to a blender, along with broth, and blend until smooth.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  9. 9
    Trim and medium dice zucchini.
  10. 10
    Add chickpea mixture and zucchini to the saucepan, stir to combine, and bring soup to a simmer. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender, 5-7 minutes.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high.
  12. 12
    Place pitas on a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and sprinkle with reserved garlic, salt, and rosemary. Place in the oven and broil until golden and toasted, 2-3 minutes. Remove from the oven.
    2 whole grain pitas
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch rosemary
  13. 13
    Cut pitas into quarters.
  14. 14
    Divide soup between bowls and drizzle with oil. Serve with pita on the side and enjoy!
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil

Creamy Cajun Orzo Pasta with Butternut Squash, Mushrooms & Bell Pepper

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (158) $3.88/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ smallbutternut squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • Cajun seasoning
  • 5 ozorzo pasta
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ small butternut squash
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Trim and peel squash; halve lengthwise, scrape out the seeds, and small dice. Add to the baking sheet with the bell pepper.
  5. 5
    Quarter mushrooms and add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil, then sprinkle with Cajun seasoning and salt. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until veggies soften, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Add broth, orzo, and spices to the skillet with the onion; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low and cook, stirring occasionally, until liquid is mostly absorbed, about 10 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    5 oz orzo pasta
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Set aside a small handful for serving.
  12. 12
    Add roasted veggies, remaining parsley, and cream to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until thickened, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  13. 13
    To serve, divide orzo between plates or bowls. Top with reserved parsley and enjoy!

Creamy Cheddar-Tuna Pasta with Sautéed Broccoli & Zucchini

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (22) $3.04/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and transfer to a small bowl, reserving saucepan for later use.
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and separate broccoli into bite-sized florets; cut stems into smaller pieces. Place in another small bowl.
    1 crown broccoli
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, trim, and halve zucchini lengthwise; slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Add to the bowl with the broccoli.
    1 medium zucchini squash
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add broccoli, zucchini, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring often, until veggies are tender-crisp, 6-7 minutes. Cover, remove from heat, and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, cut cream cheese into small pieces; coarsely grate cheddar.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Once the pasta has been drained, place both cheeses, milk, salt, and paprika in the reserved saucepan. Set over medium heat and bring the mixture to a simmer, whisking frequently, until cheeses melt and combine, 2-3 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp paprika
  9. 9
    Drain tuna and flake with a fork; add to the cheese sauce along with the cooked pasta. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 2-3 minutes more.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta and veggies between plates or bowls. Top pasta with crushed red pepper and enjoy!
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper

Creamy Chocolate Almond Smoothie with Banana & Avocado

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (15) $0.59/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • natural almond butter
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½avocado
  • 3bananas
  • 12 fl ozalmond milk, unsweetened
  • cocoa powder
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel bananas, break into chunks, and add to blender.
    3 bananas
  2. 2
    Wash, halve, and pit avocado; add to blender.
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Add almond butter, cocoa powder, almond milk, maple syrup, cinnamon, salt, and 4 ice cubes to the blender.
    ¼ cup natural almond butter
    2 tbsp cocoa powder
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) almond milk, unsweetened
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
    1 pinch salt
  4. 4
    Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
  5. 5
    Pour the smoothie into glasses and enjoy!

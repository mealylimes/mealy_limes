Creamy Coriander Chicken "Enchilada" Skillet with Spinach & Mushrooms

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (29) $5.58/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • cumin, ground
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2small flour tortillas
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 1tomato
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add flour and cook, whisking constantly, until lightly golden, about 1 minute.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
  2. 2
    Slowly whisk in broth, salt, and coriander. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until thickened, 2-3 minutes. Remove sauce from heat and set aside.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 tomato
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  4. 4
    Medium dice tomato; thinly slice mushrooms. Place both in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the veggies and set aside.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add veggies, chicken, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies have softened and chicken is cooked through, 6-7 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¾ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  10. 10
    Coarsely grate mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  11. 11
    Add yogurt to the pan with the sauce and whisk until smooth.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  12. 12
    Using a clean cutting board, slice tortillas into 2-inch long strips. When the chicken is done, add tortillas and sauce to the skillet; stir to combine.
    2 small flour tortillas
  13. 13
    Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Stir to combine and remove from heat.
  14. 14
    Top skillet with cheese and place in the oven; broil until the cheese is melted and bubbly, 3-5 minutes.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide "enchilada" skillet between plates or bowls and enjoy!

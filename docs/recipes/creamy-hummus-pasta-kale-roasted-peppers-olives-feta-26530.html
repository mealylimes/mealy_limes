Creamy Hummus Pasta with Kale, Roasted Peppers, Olives & Feta

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (10) $1.57/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ cuphummus
  • ½ (10 oz) bagkale, cut and ready to use
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board large pot measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until almost done, 6-8 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, place hummus, milk, salt, and pepper in a small saucepan. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer over medium heat. Once simmering, remove sauce from heat, cover with a lid to keep warm, and set aside.
    ½ cup hummus
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    While the sauce is heating, drain and roughly chop roasted peppers and olives. Place both in a small bowl.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; roll up crosswise and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl with the peppers and olives, then set aside.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  5. 5
    When the pasta is almost done, add kale to the pot and stir to combine. Continue to cook until pasta and kale are tender, 2-3 minutes more.
    ½ (10 oz) bag kale, cut and ready to use
  6. 6
    Drain pasta and kale in a colander. Return to the pot along with the sauce, peppers, olives, and basil; gently stir to combine.
  7. 7
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls, then top with feta. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

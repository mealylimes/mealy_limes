Creamy Indian Tomato-Eggplant Curry with Basmati Rice & Yogurt

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (11) $2.27/serving
Ingredients
  • 1eggplant
  • curry powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and quarter eggplant lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ½-inch thick pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
    1 eggplant
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the bowl with the eggplant.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the pan heats up, wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger. Add to the bowl.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add eggplant, onion, ginger, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until eggplant softens, 3-5 minutes.
    1 ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
    2 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Add marinara sauce and cream to the pan; stir combine the curry and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until eggplant is tender and curry thickens, 3-5 minutes more. Remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  9. 9
    To serve, divide rice and curry between plates or bowls, then top with yogurt. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

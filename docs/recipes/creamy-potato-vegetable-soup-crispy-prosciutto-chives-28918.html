Creamy Potato-Vegetable Soup with Crispy Prosciutto & Chives

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $1.19/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • 2 cupsfrozen shredded hash brown potatoes
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, trim, and small dice celery. Place in a medium saucepan.
    2 sticks celery
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and chop chives into small pieces. Add half to the pan with the celery. (Set remaining chives aside for serving.)
    ½ small pkg chives
  3. 3
    Add broth, shredded potatoes, carrots, and spices to the pan, then bring to a boil over high heat. Once the soup is boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until the veggies are soft, about 15 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 cups frozen shredded hash brown potatoes
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, cut or tear prosciutto into bite-sized pieces.
    4 slices prosciutto
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add prosciutto and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    While the prosciutto is cooking, cut cream cheese into small pieces.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  8. 8
    When the veggies are soft, add cream cheese to the pan. Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until the cream cheese is melted and combined, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide soup between bowls; sprinkle with crispy prosciutto and reserved chives. Enjoy!

Creamy Pumpkin, Pork & Apple Skillet with Cranberry Mixed Green Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $4.76/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¼ cupdried cranberries
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • sage, dried
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2Gala apples
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and cook until golden, about 2 minutes per side (they will not be cooked all the way through). Transfer to a clean plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, quarter, and core apples; slice into ¼-inch thick wedges. Place in a medium bowl.
    2 Gala apples
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the apple.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Return the skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add apple, onion, garlic, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp sage, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  7. 7
    While the apple and onion are cooking, place pumpkin, broth, and cream in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine.
    ½ (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy whipping cream
  8. 8
    When the apple and onion are soft, add pumpkin mixture to the skillet and stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens slightly, 2-3 minutes more.
  9. 9
    Return pork to the skillet and use a spoon to coat in sauce; reduce heat to medium-low and cover with a lid. Simmer until pork is cooked through, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, and spices in a clean medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing, along with cranberries, and toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
  12. 12
    Divide pork, apples, and sauce between plates or bowls. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

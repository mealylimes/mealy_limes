Creamy Red Lentil Soup with Parsnip, Carrot, Lime & Cilantro

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (72) $2.23/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • 2 mediumparsnips
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • red lentils, dried
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium parsnips
    2 medium carrots
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and medium dice parsnips and carrots. Place both in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the bowl with the other veggies.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a strainer or colander, rinse lentils under cold water; drain and set aside.
    ½ cup red lentils, dried
  7. 7
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in the reserved bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the ginger and garlic. (Set remaining cilantro aside for serving.)
  9. 9
    When the veggies are soft, add ginger, garlic, and cilantro to the pan. Cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  10. 10
    Add lentils and broth to the pan; stir to combine and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies and lentils are tender, 10-12 minutes.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  11. 11
    Using a hand blender, purée the soup until smooth. (If using a regular blender, work in batches as needed, then return soup to the pan.)
  12. 12
    Juice lime into the pan, add cream, and stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  13. 13
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and top with reserved cilantro. Enjoy!

Creamy Roasted Red Pepper Soup with Tomato, Cucumber & Hummus Pita

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $1.96/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½English cucumber
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ½ cuphummus
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 1 (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel garlic and place in a blender.
    1 clove garlic
  2. 2
    Drain roasted peppers. Add to the blender along with broth, cream, salt, and pepper. Blend the soup on high until smooth, 1-2 minutes.
    1 (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Pour soup into a medium saucepan and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally. Once simmering, remove from heat.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and thinly slice tomatoes. Place on a plate.
    2 tomatoes
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, trim, and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the plate with the tomato, then season with salt and pepper.
    ½ English cucumber
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Cut pitas in half and carefully open the pockets. Spread each pocket with hummus, then stuff with tomato and cucumber slices.
    2 whole grain pitas
    ½ cup hummus
  7. 7
    Divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with Parmesan; serve with pita pockets on the side. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

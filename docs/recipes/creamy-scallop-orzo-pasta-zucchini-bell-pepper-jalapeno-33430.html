Creamy Scallop Orzo Pasta with Zucchini, Bell Pepper & Jalapeño

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (122) $7.88/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ¾ lbsea scallops
  • 5 ozorzo pasta
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and set aside. (Reserve a ¼-cup pasta water before draining.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz orzo pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    1 red bell pepper
    1 jalapeño pepper
  3. 3
    Trim and small dice zucchini. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
  5. 5
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince shallot and garlic. Add both to the bowl with the other veggies.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, pat the scallops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb sea scallops
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, add scallops and cream to the skillet, then gently stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until scallops are opaque, about 5 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  11. 11
    Add cooked orzo to the skillet and stir to combine. Add reserved pasta water, a few tablespoons at a time, if necessary, to reach a saucy consistency. Continue to cook until orzo is warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide the creamy scallop and pasta sauté between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

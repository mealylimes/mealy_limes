Creamy Shrimp Salad with Cucumber, Tomato, Dill, Pine Nuts & Feta

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $7.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1lemon
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • rice vinegar
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls slotted spoon stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Set aside.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  3. 3
    Zest and juice half the lemon into a medium saucepan (zest and juice remaining lemon into a medium bowl and set aside).
  4. 4
    Add water, vinegar, and salt to the pan with the lemon zest and juice. Cover the pan and bring the liquid to a boil over high heat.
    3 cup water
    ½ cup rice vinegar
    1 tbsp salt
  5. 5
    Once the liquid in the saucepan is boiling, uncover, add shrimp, and stir for a few seconds. Remove from heat, and let sit until cooked through, about 3 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer shrimp to another medium bowl and place in the refrigerator to cool, 8-10 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the lemon zest and juice.
  7. 7
    Add cream, salt, and pepper to the bowl and whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then thinly slice into half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Remove shrimp from the refrigerator and add to the bowl with the dressing and veggies; stir to combine the shrimp salad and set aside.
  11. 11
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide lettuce between plates or bowls and drizzle with oil. Top with shrimp salad, pine nuts, and feta. Enjoy!
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

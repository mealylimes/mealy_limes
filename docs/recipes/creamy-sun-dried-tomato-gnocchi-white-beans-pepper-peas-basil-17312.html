Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Gnocchi with White Beans, Pepper, Peas & Basil

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 59% (14) $4.67/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 4sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • ⅛ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • cornstarch
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • 8 fl ozalmond milk, unsweetened
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  2. 2
    Wash and dry bell pepper, then trim, seed, and small dice. Place in a small bowl.
    1 yellow bell pepper
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the bell pepper.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Mince sun-dried tomatoes and add to the bowl.
    4 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  5. 5
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies to the pan; cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, place water and cornstarch in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the slurry.
    1 tbsp water
    2 tsp cornstarch
  8. 8
    When the veggies are soft, add cornstarch slurry, almond milk, nutritional yeast, salt, and pepper to the pan; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens, 3-4 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) almond milk, unsweetened
    ⅛ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    While the sauce simmers, wash and dry basil. Pick leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  10. 10
    Add beans, gnocchi, peas, and half of the basil to the pan (reserve remaining basil for garnish). Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 4-5 minutes more.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    1 cup frozen peas
  11. 11
    To serve, divide creamy gnocchi between bowls. Top with remaining basil and enjoy!

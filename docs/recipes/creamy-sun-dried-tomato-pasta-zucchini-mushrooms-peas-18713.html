Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Pasta with Zucchini, Mushrooms & Peas

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (9) $4.27/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1shallot
  • 4sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve ½-cup cooking water before draining and set aside.)
    ¼ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons; thinly slice mushrooms. Place both in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, peel and thinly slice shallot; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the other veggies.
    1 shallot
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in the reserved bowl and set aside.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  8. 8
    Thinly slice sun-dried tomatoes.
    4 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  9. 9
    When the veggies are done, add sun-dried tomatoes, cream, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens, 2-3 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  10. 10
    While the sauce simmers, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  11. 11
    Add cooked pasta, Parmesan, basil, and peas to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until cheese is melted and peas are tender, 1-2 minutes more.
    ½ cup frozen peas
  12. 12
    Add reserved pasta water to the skillet, a few tablespoons at a time, while stirring, to reach a saucy consistency (you may not need all of the reserved water). Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and enjoy!

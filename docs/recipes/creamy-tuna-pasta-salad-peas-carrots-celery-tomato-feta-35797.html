Creamy Tuna Pasta Salad with Peas, Carrots, Celery, Tomato & Feta

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (25) $4.66/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with yogurt, mayo, vinegar, Dijon, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp dill, dried
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    2 sticks celery
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  4. 4
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrot. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  5. 5
    Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Rinse peas under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Add to the bowl.
    ½ cup frozen peas
  8. 8
    Drain tuna, add to the bowl, and flake with a fork.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  9. 9
    Add cooled pasta to the bowl, along with feta, and stir to combine the pasta salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Creamy Tuscan Soup with Potatoes, Pork & Kale

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (97) $2.38/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1 mediumred potato
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ bunchkale
  • 2pork sausages
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) large pot measuring cups measuring spoons stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a large pot over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Use the tip of a knife to slice down the length of the sausages. Peel off the casing and discard.
    2 pork sausages
  3. 3
    Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork and break apart with a spoon. Cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and crumbly, 5-7 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Peel and small dice onion.
    2 cloves garlic
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Add garlic and onion to the pot and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
  6. 6
    Drain and rinse beans.
    ½ (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  7. 7
    Add beans, broth, and spices to the pot. Cover and bring to a simmer.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash the potato. Cut into quarters, then thinly slice into ⅛-inch pieces. Add to the pot.
    1 medium red potato
  9. 9
    Wash and dry kale. Fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and add to the pot.
    ½ bunch kale
  10. 10
    Let the soup simmer, covered, until potatoes are fork tender, 10-15 minutes. Add cream and stir until well combined.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy cream
  11. 11
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and enjoy!

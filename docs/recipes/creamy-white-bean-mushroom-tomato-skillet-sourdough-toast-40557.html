Creamy White Bean, Mushroom & Tomato Skillet with Sourdough Toast

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (57) $2.51/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • 1 tbsptomato paste
  • 2 slicessourdough bread
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
    ¼ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
  5. 5
    When the onion is softened, add mushrooms to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms are tender, 5-7 minutes.
  6. 6
    While the mushrooms are cooking, drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  7. 7
    When the mushrooms are tender, add tomato paste to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, 1-2 minutes more.
    1 tbsp tomato paste
  8. 8
    Add beans, tomatoes, broth, and cream to the skillet. Stir to combine bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until sauce has thickened, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  10. 10
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until lightly toasted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven and cut in half. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    2 slices sourdough bread
  11. 11
    Divide skillet between plates or bowls. Serve with toast on the side and enjoy!

Crispy Ginger Pork Stir-Fry with Broccoli, Pepper, Mango & Cashews

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (97) $4.90/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ¼ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1mango
  • cornstarch
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork dry with paper towels and slice crosswise into thin strips. Place in a medium bowl, along with cornstarch, salt, and pepper, then toss to coat.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    2 tsp cornstarch
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, crispy, and cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet with cooking juices for later use.)
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 crown broccoli
    1 mango
    1 red bell pepper
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Place in a clean medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl with the broccoli.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  10. 10
    Return the skillet (with cooking juices) to medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add veggies, mango, ginger, and garlic; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, place soy sauce, water, chili-garlic sauce, and honey in the reserved bowl, then whisk to combine the sauce.
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    ¼ cup water
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp honey
  12. 12
    When the veggies are starting to soften, add sauce and cashews to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce has thickened slightly, 1-2 minutes more.
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the skillet, along with the crispy pork, and stir once more to combine. Remove from heat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide stir-fry between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

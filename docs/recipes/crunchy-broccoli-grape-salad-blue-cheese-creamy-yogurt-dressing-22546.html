Crunchy Broccoli-Grape Salad with Blue Cheese & Creamy Yogurt Dressing

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $1.62/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • onion powder
  • ½ lbred grapes
  • mayonnaise
  • brown sugar
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place yogurt, mayo, vinegar, sugar, and spices in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 ½ tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and separate broccoli into bite-sized florets; cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 crown broccoli
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, and halve grapes. Add to the bowl.
    ½ lb red grapes
  4. 4
    Add blue cheese to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
    ¼ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  5. 5
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and enjoy!

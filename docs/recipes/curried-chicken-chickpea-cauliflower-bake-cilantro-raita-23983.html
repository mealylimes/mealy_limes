Curried Chicken, Chickpea & Cauliflower Bake with Cilantro Raita

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (5) $3.94/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • curry powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • honey
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ¾ lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • rice vinegar
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place vinegar, honey, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp rice vinegar
    ¾ tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ English cucumber
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and pat dry with paper towels.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  6. 6
    Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the baking sheet with the chickpeas.
  7. 7
    Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the baking sheet, drizzle with oil, then season with curry powder and salt. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    ¾ lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, stirring halfway through, until chickpeas are crisp, cauliflower is fork-tender, and chicken is cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel, trim, and grate cucumber. Use paper towels or a clean cloth to squeeze excess moisture out of the grated cucumber. Place in another small bowl.
  10. 10
    Juice half of the lime into the bowl with the cucumber. Cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with yogurt, additional honey, crushed red pepper, and salt; stir to combine the raita.
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Divide chicken bake between plates or bowls; top with pickled onion. Serve with raita on the side and enjoy!

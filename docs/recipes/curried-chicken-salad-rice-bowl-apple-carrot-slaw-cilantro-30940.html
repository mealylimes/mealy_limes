Curried Chicken Salad Rice Bowl with Apple-Carrot Slaw & Cilantro

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (25) $3.65/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • Dijon mustard
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½shallot
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • 2Gala apples
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    While the water is heating, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    2 Gala apples
    2 medium carrots
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, cut chicken into bite-sized pieces.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through, 6-7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside to cool slightly.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the slaw dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Using a clean cutting board, quarter, core, and medium dice apples. Add to the bowl with the slaw dressing.
  9. 9
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the slaw. Set aside.
  10. 10
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in another medium bowl. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  11. 11
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the cilantro along with yogurt, mayo, and spices. Whisk to combine the curry yogurt dressing.
    ½ shallot
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp turmeric, ground
  12. 12
    When the chicken is cool, add to the bowl with the curry yogurt dressing, and stir to combine.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide rice, chicken salad, and slaw between bowls. Top with reserved cilantro and enjoy!

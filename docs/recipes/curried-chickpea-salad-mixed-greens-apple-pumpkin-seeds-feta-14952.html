Curried Chickpea Salad with Mixed Greens, Apple, Pumpkin Seeds & Feta

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $4.45/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 1Gala apple
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls potato masher stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
    1 stick celery
    ½ small pkg chives
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    1 Gala apple
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Juice half the lemon into a medium bowl. (Set remaining lemon aside for later.)
  3. 3
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Transfer half the chickpeas to the bowl with the lemon juice; add mayo and spices. Using a fork or potato masher, mash until smooth.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  4. 4
    Add remaining chickpeas to the bowl and stir to combine.
  5. 5
    Trim and small dice celery; add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Chop chives into small pieces and add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the chickpea salad. Set aside.
  8. 8
    Juice remaining lemon into another medium bowl. Add oil, honey, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Quarter and core apple, then thinly slice each quarter; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Add mixed greens, feta, and pumpkin seeds to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  11. 11
    To serve, divide green salad between plates or bowls, top with curried chickpea salad, and enjoy!

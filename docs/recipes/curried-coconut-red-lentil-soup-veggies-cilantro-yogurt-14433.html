Curried Coconut-Red Lentil Soup with Veggies, Cilantro & Yogurt

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (13) $2.25/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • ½ (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • red lentils, dried
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion; transfer to a medium bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrots; add to the bowl with the onion.
  4. 4
    Trim and small dice zucchini; add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the pan heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, garlic, curry powder, and salt to the pan; cook, stirring often, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
    1 ½ tbsp curry powder
    1 tsp salt
  8. 8
    Add broth, coconut milk, tomatoes, and lentils to the pan; stir to combine, cover, and bring to a boil.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ½ (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ¾ cup red lentils, dried
  9. 9
    Once the soup is boiling, reduce heat to medium-low; simmer, stirring occasionally, until lentils and veggies are tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  11. 11
    When the soup is done, add cilantro and stir to combine.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide soup between bowls, top with yogurt, and enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

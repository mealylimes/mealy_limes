Curried Cottage Cheese Fritters with Mint Raita & Mango-Pepper Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (60) $3.76/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2eggs
  • ⅔ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2red bell peppers
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lemon
  • 1mango
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • garam masala
  • 1 cupcottage cheese
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lemon
    1 mango
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    2 red bell peppers
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Divide between two medium bowls. (Set one bowl aside for the salad.)
  3. 3
    Add cottage cheese, eggs, flour, bread crumbs, and spices to the remaining bowl with the cilantro. Stir to combine the batter.
    1 cup cottage cheese
    2 eggs
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp garam masala
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Using a ¼-cup measure, drop batter into the skillet, spacing scoops a few inches apart; flatten slightly with the back of a spoon. Cook, flipping once, until fritters are golden brown, 3-5 minutes. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a small bowl. Pour half of the juice into the medium bowl for the salad. (Set the small bowl aside for the raita.)
  7. 7
    Add oil, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the cilantro and lemon juice. Stir to combine the salad dressing.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; roughly chop the flesh. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level, then small dice. Add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
  11. 11
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the small bowl with the lemon juice along with yogurt and spices. Stir to combine the raita.
    ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Divide fritters and salad between plates. Serve with raita and enjoy!

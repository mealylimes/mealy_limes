Curried Egg Salad Pita Wrap with Avocado & Alfalfa Sprouts

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $0.60/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • dill, dried
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 stickcelery
  • ½avocado
  • mayonnaise
  • 1whole grain pita
  • 3hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  • ½ (3 oz) pkgalfalfa sprouts
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 stick celery
    ½ avocado
    ½ (3 oz) pkg alfalfa sprouts
  2. 2
    Trim and small dice celery. Place in a medium bowl along with mayo, Dijon, and spices. Stir to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Roughly chop eggs. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the egg salad.
    3 hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  5. 5
    Cut pita in half and carefully open the pockets. Divide egg salad and sprouts between pockets.
    1 whole grain pita
  6. 6
    To serve, divide pita pockets between plates and enjoy!

"Currywurst" Sausage Wrap with Red Cabbage, Bell Pepper & Apple Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $3.21/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ½lemon
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • mayonnaise
  • ketchup
  • ½ small headred cabbage
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ small head red cabbage
    1 Granny Smith apple
    1 yellow bell pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil; add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  4. 4
    While the sausages are cooking, place ketchup, water, Worcestershire, vinegar, and spices in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp ketchup
    1 tbsp water
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl; add yogurt, mayo, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Quarter cabbage lengthwise, then cut out and discard the core; slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Quarter, core, and small dice apple; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim, halve, seed, and slice bell pepper into thin strips; add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw. Set aside.
  9. 9
    When the sausages are done, transfer to a cutting board and slice crosswise into thin rounds.
  10. 10
    Warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    2 large flour tortillas
  11. 11
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and top each with a small portion of slaw; arrange sausage slices on top and drizzle with sauce. Roll tightly to close. (Reserve remaining slaw for serving.)
  12. 12
    Cut wraps in half and divide between plates; serve with remaining slaw on the side. Enjoy!

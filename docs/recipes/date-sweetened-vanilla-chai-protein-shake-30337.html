Date-Sweetened Vanilla Chai Protein Shake

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (14) $0.39/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • 12 fl ozwhole milk
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • cardamom, ground
  • cloves, ground
  • vanilla extract
  • ¼ cupmedjool dates, pitted
  • vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
Cookware
hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Not all protein powders are created equal. If desired, adjust shake consistency by adding more or less liquid per package instructions. This recipe will make about 4 cups of shake. Work in batches to best fit your blender.
  2. 2
    Place milk, water, protein powder, yogurt, dates, vanilla, and spices in a blender. Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) whole milk
    ¼ cup water
    2 servings vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ cup medjool dates, pitted
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
    ½ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ¼ tsp cardamom, ground
    ⅛ tsp cloves, ground
    1 pinch salt
  3. 3
    To serve, divide shake between glasses, adding ice if desired. Enjoy!

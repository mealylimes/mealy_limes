Deli Roast Beef Stew with Sauerkraut, Potatoes, Carrots & Peas

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $1.07/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • 1 ½ cupssauerkraut
  • 2 cupsfrozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  • 8 ozdeli roast beef
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add half of the green onions (reserving the rest for serving), shredded carrots, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, drain sauerkraut in a colander.
    1 ½ cups sauerkraut
  5. 5
    When the carrots have softened, add sauerkraut, broth, and shredded potatoes to the pan; stir to combine the stew and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 cups frozen shredded hash brown potatoes
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, stack roast beef slices one on top of another, then medium dice.
    8 oz deli roast beef
  7. 7
    When the potatoes are tender, add roast beef and peas to the stew. Stir to combine and continue to cook until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    ½ cup frozen peas
  8. 8
    To serve, divide stew between bowls. Top with reserved green onions and enjoy!

Deli Turkey Sandwich with Sun-Dried Tomato Aioli & Side Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (12) $3.02/serving
Ingredients
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • 1tomato
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 8 ozoven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  • ¼ cupsun-dried tomato pesto
  • ¼ cupbalsamic vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
    1 tomato
  2. 2
    Trim, seed, and roughly chop bell pepper; place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  4. 4
    Separate lettuce leaves; set aside two leaves per sandwich and chop or tear remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces. Add chopped lettuce to the bowl and set aside.
  5. 5
    Thinly slice tomato.
  6. 6
    Toast bread if desired.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  7. 7
    Place pesto and mayo in a small bowl; stir to combine the aioli.
    ¼ cup sun-dried tomato pesto
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
  8. 8
    Spread aioli over each bread slice; layer half of the slices with lettuce, tomato, and turkey. Close sandwiches with remaining bread slices, then slice in half.
    8 oz oven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  9. 9
    Add vinaigrette to the bowl with the veggies and toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup balsamic vinaigrette
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad and sandwiches between plates. Enjoy!

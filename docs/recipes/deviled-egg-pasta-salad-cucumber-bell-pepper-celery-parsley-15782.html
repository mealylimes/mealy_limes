"Deviled Egg" Pasta Salad with Cucumber, Bell Pepper, Celery & Parsley

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (34) $2.36/serving
Ingredients
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • 3eggs
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 stickcelery
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with hot water (from the tap) and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    3 eggs
  2. 2
    While the eggs are cooking, fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and rinse under cold water to cool completely; set aside.
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 stick celery
    ½ English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  4. 4
    Trim and small dice celery; place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim and medium dice cucumber; add to the bowl with the celery.
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl and set aside.
    1 shallot
  9. 9
    Once the eggs have cooled, peel and halve lengthwise. Using a spoon, separate the yolks from the whites and place the yolks in a small bowl. Roughly chop the egg whites and add to the bowl with the veggies.
  10. 10
    Gently mash the yolks with a fork. Add mayo, Dijon, vinegar, and spices to the bowl; stir to combine the dressing.
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Add pasta and dressing to the bowl with the veggies; stir to combine the pasta salad.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Dijon Kale Salad with Crispy Chickpeas, Apple, Avocado & Almonds

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (15) $2.52/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½shallot
  • ½avocado
  • ½lemon
  • 1 bunchkale
  • mayonnaise
  • 1Gala apple
  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and pat dry with paper towels. Drizzle with oil and season with spices. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring chickpeas halfway through, until crispy and golden, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    1 bunch kale
    1 Gala apple
    ½ avocado
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add mayo, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the dressing and stir to coat.
    ½ shallot
  7. 7
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl. Using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes.
  8. 8
    Quarter, core, and slice apple into thin wedges. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add half to the bowl, along with almonds, and toss to combine the salad. (Reserve remaining Parmesan for serving.)
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
  10. 10
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice crosswise into thin half-moons.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with crispy chickpeas, avocado, and remaining Parmesan. Enjoy!

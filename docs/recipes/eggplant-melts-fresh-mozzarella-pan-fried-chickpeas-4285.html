Eggplant Melts with Fresh Mozzarella & Pan-Fried Chickpeas

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 31% (8) $2.46/serving
Ingredients
  • 1eggplant
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • virgin coconut oil
  • paprika
  • cumin, ground
  • 1tomato
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 400°F and place the rack in the center position.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 eggplant
    1 tomato
  3. 3
    Halve the eggplant lengthwise and place cut-side-up on a baking sheet. Rub with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place the eggplant in the oven and roast until softened, 15 to 20 minutes.
  5. 5
    Slice the tomato into rounds and place on a plate lined with paper towels.
  6. 6
    Drain the mozzarella and pat dry with paper towels; slice into rounds.
    ½ (8oz) pkg fresh mozzarella
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Drain and rinse the chickpeas; transfer to paper towels or a clean towel and pat dry.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  9. 9
    Add coconut oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Add the chickpeas to the skillet and shake to distribute. Cook the chickpeas, shaking occasionally, until they are golden brown and crispy, 5 to 7 minutes.
  11. 11
    In a medium bowl, combine and mix together paprika, cumin, and salt.
    1 tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Using a slotted spoon, transfer the chickpeas to a paper towel lined plate.
  13. 13
    Remove the baking sheet from the oven. Top the eggplant halves with tomato and fresh mozzarella slices.
  14. 14
    Return the eggplant to the oven and bake until the cheese has melted, another 3 to 5 minutes.
  15. 15
    Add the chickpeas to the bowl with the spices and toss to coat.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide the eggplant melts and chickpeas between plates. Enjoy!

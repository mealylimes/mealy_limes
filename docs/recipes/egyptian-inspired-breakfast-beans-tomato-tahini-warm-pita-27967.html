Egyptian-Inspired Breakfast Beans with Tomato, Tahini & Warm Pita

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $1.73/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lemon
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • tahini
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans in a colander.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  6. 6
    When the onion is soft, add beans and tomatoes to the pan. Stir to combine and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer until the sauce has thickened and beans are tender, about 10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  7. 7
    While the beans are cooking, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the pan with the beans and stir to combine (reserve the remaining for serving).
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  8. 8
    Cut pita breads into wedges and arrange on a baking sheet pan. When the beans are almost done, place pita bread in the oven and bake until warmed through, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 whole grain pitas
  9. 9
    When the beans are done, wash, dry, and juice lemon into the pan. Add tahini, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    ½ lemon
    1 tbsp tahini
  10. 10
    Divide beans between plates or bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Serve with pita on the side and enjoy!

English Muffin Pizzas with Basil Pesto, Goat Cheese & Tomatoes

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (4) $1.61/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 45 mlbasil pesto
  • 2English muffins
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Split muffins and arrange, cut-side down, on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 English muffins
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, crumble goat cheese into a small bowl.
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  5. 5
    Spread toasted muffins with pesto, then top with cheese, tomatoes, salt, and pepper.
    3 tbsp basil pesto
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until pizzas are warmed through, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    To serve, divide pizzas between plates and enjoy!

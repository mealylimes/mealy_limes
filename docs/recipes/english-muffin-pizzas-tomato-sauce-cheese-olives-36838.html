English Muffin Pizzas with Tomato Sauce, Cheese & Olives

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $0.08/serving
Ingredients
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • onion powder
  • ⅓ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (3.8 oz) cansliced black olives
  • 2English muffins
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Split muffins and arrange, cut-side down, on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 English muffins
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place tomato sauce and spices in a small bowl. Stir to combine.
    ½ (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    1 pinch onion powder
    1 pinch garlic powder
    1 pinch salt
  4. 4
    Drain olives.
    ½ (3.8 oz) can sliced black olives
  5. 5
    Spread toasted muffins with tomato sauce, then top with cheese and olives.
    ⅓ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  6. 6
    Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until pizzas are warmed through and cheese is melted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    To serve, divide pizzas between plates and enjoy!

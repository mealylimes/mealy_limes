"Everything Spice" Crusted Salmon with Avocado, Cucumber & Caper Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (8) $5.16/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1English cucumber
  • sesame seeds
  • 1avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • onion powder
  • poppy seeds
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil and set aside.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 English cucumber
    1 avocado
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  3. 3
    Place seeds and spices in a small bowl; stir to combine the "everything spice" mixture.
    2 tsp sesame seeds
    1 tsp poppy seeds
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Pat the salmon dry with paper towels and place, skin-side down, on the baking sheet. Spread with Dijon, then sprinkle evenly with the spice mixture, pressing gently to adhere.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  5. 5
    Place fish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, capers, and salt in a medium bowl; stir to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 tbsp capers
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice onion crosswise into half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ medium red onion
  8. 8
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit avocado; cut crosswise while still in the skin, then scoop out with a spoon into the bowl.
  10. 10
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the salmon and salad between plates. Enjoy!

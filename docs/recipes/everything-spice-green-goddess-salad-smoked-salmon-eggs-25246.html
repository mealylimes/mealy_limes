"Everything Spice" Green Goddess Salad with Smoked Salmon & Eggs

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $4.14/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 ½ tbspcapers
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 4eggs
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½English cucumber
  • sesame seeds
  • 1avocado
  • ½lemon
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • poppy seeds
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 1anchovy fillet, packed in oil
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    4 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, place seeds and spices in a small bowl; stir to combine the "everything spice" mixture. Set aside for serving.
    2 tsp sesame seeds
    1 tsp poppy seeds
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ small pkg chives
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 avocado
    1 head romaine lettuce
  4. 4
    Juice lemon into a blender.
  5. 5
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the blender.
  6. 6
    Roughly chop chives. Add to the blender along with yogurt, oil, capers, anchovy fillet, and spices. Blend on high until dressing is smooth; set aside.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tbsp capers
    1 anchovy fillet, packed in oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Trim and large dice cucumber. Place on a plate.
  8. 8
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise. Add to the plate with the cucumber.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and large dice the flesh. Add to the plate.
  10. 10
    Peel and quarter eggs. Add to the plate.
  11. 11
    Chop or tear smoked salmon into bite-sized pieces. Add to the plate.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  12. 12
    Chop or tear lettuce leaves into bite-sized pieces.
  13. 13
    Divide lettuce between plates or bowls. Top with cucumber, tomatoes, avocado, eggs, smoked salmon, and additional capers. Sprinkle with everything spice and serve with dressing on the side. Enjoy!
    1 tbsp capers

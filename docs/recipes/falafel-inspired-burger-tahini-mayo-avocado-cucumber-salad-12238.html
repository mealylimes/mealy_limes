Falafel-Inspired Burger with Tahini Mayo, Avocado & Cucumber Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $2.85/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ⅛ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • 1English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lemon
  • ½tomato
  • mayonnaise
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • baking powder
  • tahini
  • ½ (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher spatula toaster (optional) tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 English cucumber
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ avocado
    ½ tomato
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse the beans; set aside to drain further.
    ½ (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  3. 3
    Trim and thinly slice cucumber. Place in a medium bowl, along with oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic and shallot. Place half the garlic and shallot in the bowl with the cucumber and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 shallot
  5. 5
    Place remaining garlic and shallot in another medium bowl. Juice lemon into the same bowl.
  6. 6
    Add beans and chili-garlic sauce to the bowl with the lemon juice. Crush the beans with a fork or potato masher until they are broken down.
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  7. 7
    Shave parsley and cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Drain and mince artichokes. Grate cheese. Add both to the bowl.
    ½ (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
    ⅛ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    Add the egg, bread crumbs, baking powder, and spices to the bowl and stir to combine. Mash the chickpeas once more, until a cohesive mixture is formed.
    1 egg
    ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
    ½ tsp baking powder
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp coriander
    ¼ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Divide the mixture into 2 portions (about ⅔ cup each). Using your hands, form each portion into a ball, then flatten into a 1-inch thick patty. (The patties will be moist and delicate; they will firm up upon cooking.)
  11. 11
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Carefully transfer patties to the skillet and cook until dark brown on one side, 5-6 minutes. Gently flip the patties and cook until dark brown on the other side, 5 minutes more. Remove to a plate.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, place mayo, tahini, water, and salt in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the tahini mayo.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp tahini
    1 tsp water
    1 pinch salt
  14. 14
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out. Slice tomato into rounds.
  15. 15
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  16. 16
    Place the bottom half of a bun on each plate. Layer with a falafel patty, tahini mayo, avocado, and tomato; close with the top half of the bun. Serve with cucumber salad on the side and enjoy!

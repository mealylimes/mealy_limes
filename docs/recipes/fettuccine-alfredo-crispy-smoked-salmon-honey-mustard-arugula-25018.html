Fettuccine Alfredo with Crispy Smoked Salmon & Honey-Mustard Arugula

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $1.03/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ¾ cupfrozen peas
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • 30 mlhoney mustard dressing
Cookware
colander measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add pasta and salt, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
    ½ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, tear smoked salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add salmon and cook, stirring often, until crispy, about 4 minutes. Use a slotted spoon to transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet with cooking juices for the next step.)
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Return skillet (with cooking juices) to medium heat. Add peas and cook, stirring often, until tender, 1-2 minutes.
    ¾ cup frozen peas
  6. 6
    Add Alfredo sauce to the skillet with the peas. Bring to a simmer, stirring occasionally, then remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
    ½ (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Place in a medium bowl along with dressing and additional salt; toss to coat.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    2 tbsp honey mustard dressing
    ⅛ tsp salt
  8. 8
    When the pasta is done, drain and add to the skillet with the sauce; toss to combine.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pasta and arugula between plates; top pasta with crispy salmon. Enjoy!

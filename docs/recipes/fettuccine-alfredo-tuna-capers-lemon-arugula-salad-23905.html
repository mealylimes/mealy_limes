Fettuccine Alfredo with Tuna, Capers, Lemon & Arugula Salad

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $4.62/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 tbspcapers
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½lemon
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
  • 30 mlItalian salad dressing
  • ½ (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a small saucepan.
  4. 4
    Drain tuna, add to the pan with the lemon juice, and break into bite-sized pieces.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  5. 5
    Add Alfredo sauce, capers, and spices to the pan. Stir to combine the sauce and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally.
    ½ (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
    2 tbsp capers
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, halve tomatoes and set aside for serving.
  7. 7
    Once the sauce is simmering, remove from heat and cover with a lid to keep warm.
  8. 8
    When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and return to the pan. Add the warm sauce and toss to coat.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pasta and arugula between plates. Top arugula with tomatoes, then drizzle with dressing. Enjoy!
    2 tbsp Italian salad dressing

Fettuccine with Tomatoes, Ricotta & Lemony Dressed Arugula

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (11) $2.25/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½shallot
  • ½lemon
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve a ½-cup of pasta cooking water before draining.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and crushed red pepper; cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant and golden, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Add tomatoes and salt to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until simmering. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low, and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 12-15 minutes more.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in a small bowl along with ricotta and salt, then stir to combine. Set aside.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, zest, and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
    ½ lemon
  8. 8
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the lemon along with oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Wash and dry arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  10. 10
    Add drained pasta and reserved pasta water to the skillet with the sauce. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until heated through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pasta and arugula between plates or bowls. Top pasta with ricotta and crushed red pepper. Enjoy!
    1 pinch crushed red pepper

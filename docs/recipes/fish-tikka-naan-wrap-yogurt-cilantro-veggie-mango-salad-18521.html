Fish Tikka Naan Wrap with Yogurt, Cilantro & Veggie-Mango Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (5) $5.34/serving Pro

  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • chili powder

  • turmeric, ground

  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root

Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 lemon
    ½ mango
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic; place both in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Juice half of the lemon into the bowl with the ginger and garlic (set remaining lemon aside for later use). Add yogurt, spices, and sugar; whisk to combine the marinade.
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
    ¼ tsp brown sugar
  4. 4
    Cut fish into 1-inch pieces; add to the bowl with the marinade, gently stir to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
  5. 5
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack about 6 inches from the top. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  6. 6
    Juice remaining lemon into another medium bowl; add oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  7. 7
    Using a clean cutting board, peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; small dice and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim and small dice cucumber; halve tomatoes lengthwise. Add both to the bowl and set aside.
  9. 9
    Transfer fish to the baking sheet and arrange in a single layer. Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, turning the fish pieces halfway through, until golden and flesh flakes with a fork, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing and veggies, then stir to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    Just before serving, arrange naan on another baking sheet. Place in the oven and broil, flipping once, until toasted 1-2 minutes; remove from oven.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  12. 12
    Spread yogurt over each naan and top with a small portion of salad and the fish. (Reserve remaining salad for serving.)
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  13. 13
    To make wraps easier to eat, place each naan on a square of parchment paper. Use the paper to tightly roll and secure each wrap. Otherwise, eat as is and get a little messy! Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

Flank Steak with Cauliflower, Pickled Onions & Tahini-Yogurt Sauce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (4) $2.40/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1 medium headcauliflower
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • tahini
  • ¾ lbflank steak
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place vinegar, honey, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium head cauliflower
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  5. 5
    Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt. Toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until the cauliflower is golden and fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, zest and juice lemon into another small bowl.
  8. 8
    Add yogurt, tahini, water, pepper, and salt to the bowl with the lemon. Whisk to combine.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp tahini
    1 tbsp water
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the yogurt, whisk once more to combine the sauce, and set aside. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  10. 10
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb flank steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-3 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide steak and yogurt sauce between plates; top yogurt with cauliflower and pickled onions. Sprinkle with reserved parsley and enjoy!

Fresh Corn, Peach, Avocado & Goat Cheese Salad with Garlic Breadsticks

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (6) $3.74/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 3 earscorn
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1flatbread or naan
  • 1peach
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a small bowl along with oil and spices. Stir to combine the garlic oil.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Drizzle garlic oil over the flatbread, cut into 1½-inch wide strips, and transfer to a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until crispy, 8-10 minutes. Remove from the oven.
    1 flatbread or naan
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 ears corn
    1 peach
    ½ avocado
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. 6
    Remove husks and silks from the corn. Using a knife, carefully slice corn kernels off the cobs; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Quarter peach lengthwise (around the pit); twist to separate and remove pit. Medium dice and add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Peel and small dice onion. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  10. 10
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Crumble goat cheese into the bowl and gently stir to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad and breadsticks between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

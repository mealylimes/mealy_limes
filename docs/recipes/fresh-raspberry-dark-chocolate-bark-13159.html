Fresh Raspberry-Dark Chocolate Bark

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $0.62/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cupdark chocolate chips
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper silicone spatula small saucepan
Instructions
  1. 1
    Line the bottom of a small baking dish with parchment paper and set aside.
  2. 2
    Fill a small saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry raspberries.
    ½ (6 oz) pkg raspberries
  4. 4
    Uncover the pan and place a small, heat-proof, bowl over the simmering water, allowing it to sit on the rim. Add chocolate and oil to the bowl; using a silicone spatula, stir continuously, until melted.
    ⅓ cup dark chocolate chips
    ½ tsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Remove bowl from the heat. Add vanilla and salt; stir until smooth.
    ⅛ tsp vanilla extract
    1 pinch salt
  6. 6
    Pour chocolate mixture into the baking dish. Using a silicone spatula, spread out to a thickness of ¼-inch.
  7. 7
    Sprinkle raspberries over the chocolate, pressing gently into the surface to adhere. Place baking dish in the refrigerator and chill until firm, 10-15 minutes.
  8. 8
    Remove bark from the refrigerator, transfer to a cutting board, and chop into large pieces. Store in the refrigerator until ready to serve.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide chocolate bark between plates and enjoy!

Fried Egg Breakfast Tostadas with Tomato, Avocado, Feta & Hot Sauce

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $1.66/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 4eggs
  • ½avocado
  • 1tomato
  • 4small corn tortillas
  • hot sauce
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Arrange tortillas on a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil on both sides, then season with salt.
    4 small corn tortillas
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until tortillas are golden and crispy, 5-7 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Gently crack eggs into the skillet and cook until the tops of the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat and season with spices.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 eggs
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    While the eggs are cooking, wash, dry, and small dice tomato.
    1 tomato
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, halve, and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice crosswise into thin half-moons.
    ½ avocado
  8. 8
    To serve, divide crispy tortillas between plates; top with eggs, tomatoes, avocado, and feta. Drizzle with hot sauce and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    1 tsp hot sauce

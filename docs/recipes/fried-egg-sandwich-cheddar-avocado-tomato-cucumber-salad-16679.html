Fried Egg Sandwich with Cheddar, Avocado & Tomato-Cucumber Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $2.93/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 4eggs
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Place oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ shallot
  4. 4
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise into half-moons. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad; set aside.
  7. 7
    Preheat oven to broil on high.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Gently crack eggs into the skillet and season with salt and pepper. Cook until the tops of the whites are set but yolks are still runny, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 eggs
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    While the eggs are cooking, thinly slice cheddar.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  11. 11
    Place bread on a baking sheet pan; place in the oven and broil until bread is toasted on one side, 1-2 minutes.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  12. 12
    Remove baking sheet from the oven, flip bread, and arrange cheese over half of the slices. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to broil until bread is toasted and cheese is melted, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
  14. 14
    Layer each cheesy toast slice with two fried eggs and avocado. Spread mayo over remaining toast slices and place on top to close sandwiches.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
  15. 15
    To serve, divide sandwiches and salad between plates. Enjoy!

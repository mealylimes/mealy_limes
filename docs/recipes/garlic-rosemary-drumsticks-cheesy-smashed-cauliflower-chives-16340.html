Garlic-Rosemary Drumsticks & Cheesy Smashed Cauliflower with Chives

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $3.23/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ½ small pkgfresh rosemary
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts potato masher spatula stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pkg fresh rosemary
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ small pkg chives
  3. 3
    Slide rosemary leaves off the sprigs; discard sprigs and mince the leaves. Peel and mince garlic. Place both in a small bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the herb paste.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place chicken on the baking sheet; using your hands, coat drumsticks with herb paste and arrange in a single layer.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until golden brown and cooked through, 30-35 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  7. 7
    While the water is heating, cut cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard leaves and thick stem. Separate into 2-inch wide florets.
  8. 8
    Once the water comes to a boil, uncover and add cauliflower; stir for a few seconds. Cover and return to a boil; cook until tender, about 5 minutes.
  9. 9
    While the cauliflower is cooking, grate the cheese. Chop chives into small pieces.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  10. 10
    Drain cauliflower, then carefully transfer the florets to another baking sheet; drizzle with oil and season with spices.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Using a fork or potato masher, gently crush each cauliflower floret, flattening them to about a ½-inch thickness. Gather any loose cauliflower pieces into small piles of about the same size.
  12. 12
    Sprinkle cauliflower with cheese and chives.
  13. 13
    Add baking sheet to the oven and bake until cheese is golden and bubbly, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide drumsticks and cheesy cauliflower between plates. Enjoy!

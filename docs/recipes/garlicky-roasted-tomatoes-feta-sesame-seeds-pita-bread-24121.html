Garlicky Roasted Tomatoes with Feta, Sesame Seeds & Pita Bread

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $1.36/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1whole grain pita
Cookware
baking dish baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise. Place in a small baking dish, just large enough to fit the tomatoes in a single layer.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic. Sprinkle garlic and feta evenly over the tomatoes.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  4. 4
    Sprinkle sesame seeds and spices over the feta, then drizzle with oil.
    2 tsp sesame seeds
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch salt
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until tomatoes have softened and juices start to bubble, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, cut pita bread into wedges and arrange on a baking sheet pan. When the tomatoes are almost done, add pita bread to the oven and bake until warmed through, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
    1 whole grain pita
  7. 7
    Divide roasted tomatoes and feta between plates. Serve with warm pita bread. Enjoy!

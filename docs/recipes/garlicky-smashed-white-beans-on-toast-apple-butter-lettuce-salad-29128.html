Garlicky Smashed White Beans on Toast with Apple-Butter Lettuce Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.02/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½lemon
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 1Gala apple
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • tahini
  • 4 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts potato masher stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pkg chives
    ½ lemon
    1 Gala apple
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    Chop chives into small pieces. Place half in a medium bowl (set remaining chives aside for serving).
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lemon into the bowl with the chives.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Drain and rinse beans. Add to the bowl along with tahini, oil, and spices. Using a fork or potato masher, roughly mash the beans until semi-smooth. Set aside.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
    1 tbsp tahini
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  7. 7
    Place oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper in another medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, 2-3 minutes; remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    4 slices sourdough bread
  11. 11
    Divide toast between plates, spread with smashed beans, and sprinkle with reserved chives. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

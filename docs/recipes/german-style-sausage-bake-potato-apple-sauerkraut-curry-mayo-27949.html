German-Style Sausage Bake with Potato, Apple, Sauerkraut & Curry Mayo

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (86) $2.85/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbnew potatoes
  • mayonnaise
  • 1Gala apple
  • 2pork sausages
  • 1 cupsauerkraut
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ¾ lb new potatoes
    1 Gala apple
  3. 3
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl and set aside for later use.
  4. 4
    Quarter potatoes and place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Quarter, core, and large dice apple. Add to the bowl with the potatoes.
  6. 6
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into ¼-inch thick strips. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Slice sausages into 1-inch thick rounds. Add to the bowl along with oil and spices, then stir to combine. Transfer mixture to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer.
    2 pork sausages
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until the potatoes are tender and sausage is cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, place mayo, water, curry powder, and salt in another small bowl. Stir to combine the curry mayo and set aside.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp water
    ½ tsp curry powder
    1 pinch salt
  10. 10
    When the sausage bake has about 5 minutes left, place sauerkraut in a small saucepan. Set over high heat and bring to a simmer, stirring occasionally. Cover and remove from heat.
    1 cup sauerkraut
  11. 11
    Add parsley to the baking sheet with the veggies and sausage, then stir to combine.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide sausage bake between plates or bowls. Drizzle with curry mayo and top with warm sauerkraut. Enjoy!

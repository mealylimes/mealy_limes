Ginger Marinated Tofu Noodle Bowl with Spinach, Carrot & Peanuts

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 66% (8) $3.43/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • 1lime
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut the tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich the tofu between clean towels or paper towels and lay side by side on a baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce (skip the spinach if it came pre-washed).
    1 lime
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 medium carrots
    1 red bell pepper
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Juice half the lime into a medium bowl. Grate the ginger and add to the bowl with the juice. Add maple syrup, fish sauce, soy sauce and oil; whisk to combine.
    1 ½ tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  4. 4
    Cut the tofu into 1-inch squares. Place tofu in the bowl with the marinade and gently toss until well coated; set aside.
  5. 5
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  6. 6
    Peel the carrots; run the peeler along the carrot to make long, noodle-like strips.
  7. 7
    Remove saucepan from heat and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes, or according to package directions, then drain in a colander and rinse.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  8. 8
    Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Use a slotted spoon to add the tofu to the skillet leaving the marinade in the bowl for later use. Cook until golden brown, 2-3 minutes per side.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  9. 9
    While the tofu cooks, seed and cut the bell pepper into thin sticks.
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard the ends of the green onions; cut the onions crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle.
  11. 11
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  12. 12
    Once the tofu is cooked, transfer to a plate, returning the skillet to the heat. Add the remaining marinade, bell pepper, carrot, green onions, cilantro, and spinach to the hot skillet and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes. Add the noodles to the pan and toss to combine.
  13. 13
    Cut the remaining lime into wedges for serving.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide noodles between bowls and top with tofu. Garnish with cilantro, peanuts, and a squeeze of lime; enjoy!
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted

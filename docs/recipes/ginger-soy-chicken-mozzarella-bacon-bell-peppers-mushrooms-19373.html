Ginger-Soy Chicken with Mozzarella, Bacon, Bell Peppers & Mushrooms

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (7) $4.95/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • soy sauce
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • honey
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • 1green bell pepper
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 green bell pepper
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ lb white mushrooms
  3. 3
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic; place both in a small bowl along with soy sauce, vinegar, and honey. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp honey
  4. 4
    Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets; arrange in a baking dish. Pour half of the sauce over the chicken and turn to coat (reserve remaining sauce for later use).
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  5. 5
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until chicken is almost cooked through, 15-18 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side. Remove bacon to a paper towel-lined plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    4 slices bacon
  8. 8
    While the bacon cooks, using a clean cutting board, trim, seed, and thinly slice bell peppers; place in a medium bowl.
  9. 9
    Thinly slice mushrooms and add to the bowl with the bell peppers.
  10. 10
    Peel, halve, and slice onion into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  11. 11
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat; add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 6-7 minutes.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  13. 13
    When the chicken is almost cooked through, remove from oven. Change oven setting to broil on high.
  14. 14
    Layer chicken with bacon and cheese. Return baking dish to the oven and broil until cheese is melted and chicken is cooked through, about 5 minutes; remove from oven.
  15. 15
    When the veggies are done, add remaining sauce to the skillet, stir to combine, and continue to cook until heated through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide veggies and chicken between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Greek Beef Skillet with Bell Pepper, Zucchini, Tomato, Yogurt & Feta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (66) $5.53/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add beef, salt, and pepper; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    2 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl (reserve remaining parsley for serving).
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  8. 8
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, parsley, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp paprika
  9. 9
    Halve grape tomatoes. Place in the reserved bowl.
  10. 10
    Halve olives lengthwise. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  11. 11
    When the veggies are done, add beef, tomatoes, and olives to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide beef and veggies between plates or bowls. Top with feta, yogurt, and reserved parsley. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

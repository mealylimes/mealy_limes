Greek Chicken Pasta Salad with Tomato, Bell Pepper, Parsley & Feta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (14) $5.04/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lemon along with oil and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Place chicken in another medium bowl along with ⅓ of the dressing; turn to coat. (Reserve bowl with remaining dressing for the salad.)
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add the chicken and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat and set aside.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the remaining dressing.
    1 shallot
  9. 9
    Trim and large dice cucumber. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and large dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Halve tomatoes crosswise and add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    When the pasta is done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Add to the bowl, along with the feta, and stir to combine the pasta salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  14. 14
    Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls and top with sliced chicken. Enjoy!

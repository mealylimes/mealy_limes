Greek Chicken Smash Tacos with Dill Yogurt & Cucumber-Tomato Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (158) $2.96/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • ½orange bell pepper
  • 2tomatoes
  • brown sugar
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ orange bell pepper
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ lemon
  2. 2
    Place oil, vinegar, and spices in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Small dice tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Small dice cucumber. Add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad. Set aside.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  7. 7
    Peel and mince shallot. Place in another medium bowl along with chicken and spices. Mix with your hands until well combined.
    ½ shallot
    ½ lb ground chicken
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Arrange tortillas on a flat surface, then divide the chicken mixture between them (about 2 tablespoons per tortilla). Using a knife (or your hands), spread the chicken mixture over the surface of each tortilla in an even layer.
    6 small flour tortillas
  9. 9
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches, add tacos, meat-side down. Cook, pressing down once with the bottom of a spatula halfway through cooking, until the chicken is cooked through, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Flip tacos, chicken-side up, and continue to cook until tortillas are golden underneath, 1 minute more. Transfer to a plate and carefully fold in half to form tacos.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
  13. 13
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the dill. Add yogurt, sugar, and salt, then stir to combine the dill yogurt.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
  14. 14
    Divide tacos between plates. Fill each with a small portion of salad and drizzle with dill yogurt. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!

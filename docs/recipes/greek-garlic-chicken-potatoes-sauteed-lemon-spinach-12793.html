Greek Garlic Chicken & Potatoes with Sautéed Lemon Spinach

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (31) $4.56/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 3 mediumyellow potatoes
  • 1lemon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    3 medium yellow potatoes
    1 lemon
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Slice potatoes into ¼-inch thick rounds; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the baking sheet with the potatoes along with oil and spices; toss to combine, then spread out in an even layer.
    3 cloves garlic
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place potatoes in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake for 6-8 minutes while you prepare the chicken.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken to the skillet; cook until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side (the chicken will not be cooked all the way through).
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Remove baking sheet from the oven and place seared chicken on top of the potatoes. Juice half of the lemon over the chicken and potatoes. Return to the oven and continue to bake until potatoes are tender and chicken is cooked through, 12-15 minutes.
  10. 10
    When there are 5 minutes left on the potatoes and chicken, return skillet to medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Juice remaining lemon into the skillet and season with salt and pepper; stir to combine.
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide potatoes, chicken, and spinach between plates. Enjoy!

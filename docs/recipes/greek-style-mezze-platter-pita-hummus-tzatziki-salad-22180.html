Greek-Style Mezze Platter with Pita, Hummus, Tzatziki & Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $3.00/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • oregano, dried
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ⅓ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • ½ cuphummus
  • ¼ cupbalsamic vinaigrette
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 orange bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  2. 2
    Trim, seed, and halve bell pepper crosswise, then slice into thin strips. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  4. 4
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Drain and roughly chop artichokes. Add to the bowl.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  7. 7
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl, along with the feta, vinaigrette, and oregano. Toss to combine the salad and set aside.
    ⅓ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ¼ cup balsamic vinaigrette
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
  8. 8
    Toast pita if desired, then cut into wedges.
    2 whole grain pitas
  9. 9
    Divide salad and pita between plates; serve with hummus and tzatziki. Enjoy!
    ½ cup hummus
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce

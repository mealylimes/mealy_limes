Greek-Style Steak Pita Wrap with Bell Pepper, Cucumber, Onion & Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (14) $5.53/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1green bell pepper
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts parchment paper stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a small bowl. Add yogurt and spices; whisk to combine the yogurt sauce and set aside.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Pat steak dry with paper towels and slice crosswise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the bell peppers along with oil and spices; toss to combine.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add peppers and steak. Cook, stirring occasionally, until peppers are soft and steak is cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons.
    ½ medium red onion
  9. 9
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds.
  10. 10
    Arrange pitas on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and bake, flipping halfway through, until warmed through, 4-5 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 whole grain pitas
  11. 11
    To assemble, divide steak, peppers, onion, and cucumber between pitas. To make wraps easier to eat, place each pita on a square of parchment paper. Use the paper to tightly roll and secure each wrap. Otherwise, eat as is and get a little messy! Drizzle with yogurt sauce and enjoy!

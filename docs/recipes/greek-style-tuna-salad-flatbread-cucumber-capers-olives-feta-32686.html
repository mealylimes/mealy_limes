Greek-Style Tuna Salad Flatbread with Cucumber, Capers, Olives & Feta

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (4) $5.95/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ⅛ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1tomato
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ English cucumber
    1 tomato
    ½ yellow bell pepper
  3. 3
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Divide between two medium bowls. (Set one bowl aside for the Greek salad.)
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the remaining bowl with the dill.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Roughly chop capers. Add to the bowl along with half of the feta, yogurt, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the creamy dressing. (Reserve remaining feta for serving.)
    1 tbsp capers
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Drain tuna, add to the bowl with the creamy dressing, and flake with a fork. Stir to combine the tuna salad and set aside.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  7. 7
    Arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, about 8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool slightly.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, add oil, vinegar, and spices to the remaining medium bowl with the dill. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Medium dice tomato. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the Greek salad.
    ⅛ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  13. 13
    Spread the tuna salad over each flatbread, then top with the Greek salad and reserved feta.
  14. 14
    To serve, cut flatbreads into wedges and divide between plates. Enjoy!

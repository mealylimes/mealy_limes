Greek Turkey Burger Patties with Tzatziki Sauce & Greek Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (61) $4.48/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • 1English cucumber
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Dijon mustard
  • oregano, dried
  • ½lemon
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 English cucumber
    ½ lemon
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Transfer ½ of the garlic to a medium bowl (that will hold the ground turkey mixture) and transfer the other ½ to a small bowl (that will hold the tzatziki sauce).
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Juice the lemon; add ½ of the juice to the medium bowl and the other ½ to the small bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; small dice ½ of the onion and transfer to the medium bowl, then thinly slice the other ½ crosswise and transfer to a salad bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    To the medium bowl, add the ground turkey, ½ of the feta (save the rest for the salad), Dijon, oregano, salt, and pepper; mix until well combined. Form the mixture into 4 patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp oregano
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add olive oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Place the patties in the skillet and cook until cooked through and golden brown, 4 to 6 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  9. 9
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; coarsely grate ¼ of the cucumber and add to the small bowl with the garlic and lemon juice, then quarter the remaining ¾ of the cucumber lengthwise and cut crosswise into ¼-inch-thick pieces. Add to the salad bowl.
  10. 10
    Halve the grape tomatoes and add to the salad bowl.
  11. 11
    To the salad bowl, add the olives, olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper; toss to coat.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Add yogurt to the bowl with the garlic, lemon juice, and cucumber; season with salt and pepper and mix together.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the salad and burger patties between plates; top the salad with the remaining feta and serve the tzatziki sauce with the patties. Enjoy!

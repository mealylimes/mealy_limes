Greek Yogurt Bowl with Honey-Strawberry Compote, Kiwi & Pistachios

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (12) $2.53/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • honey
  • 2 cupsplain Greek yogurt
  • ½lemon
  • 2kiwifruit
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ⅛ cuppistachios, unsalted
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    2 kiwifruit
  2. 2
    Juice lemon into a medium saucepan.
  3. 3
    Trim and quarter strawberries. Add to the pan with the lemon juice, along with honey and salt, then stir to combine.
    1 tbsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Set pan over medium heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until the strawberries soften and release their juices, about 5 minutes. Remove compote from heat and set aside to cool slightly, 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel, trim, and halve kiwis lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Place on a plate and set aside.
  6. 6
    Roughly chop pistachios.
    ⅛ cup pistachios, unsalted
  7. 7
    To serve, divide yogurt between bowls. Top with strawberry compote, kiwi, and pistachios. Enjoy!
    2 cups plain Greek yogurt

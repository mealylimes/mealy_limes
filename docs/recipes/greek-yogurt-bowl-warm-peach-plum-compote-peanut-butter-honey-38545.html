Greek Yogurt Bowl with Warm Peach-Plum Compote, Peanut Butter & Honey

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (14) $1.61/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • 1 ½ cupsplain Greek yogurt
  • 2peaches
  • nutmeg, ground
  • cardamom, ground
  • vanilla extract
  • 2plums
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, and quarter peaches and plums lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Slice each quarter into thin wedges and place in a medium saucepan along with honey, vanilla, and spices.
    2 peaches
    2 plums
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
    ⅛ tsp cardamom, ground
    ⅛ tsp nutmeg, ground
    1 pinch salt
  2. 2
    Set the pan over medium-high and cook, stirring occasionally, until fruit is tender and saucy, 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside to cool slightly, 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    To serve, divide yogurt, peanut butter, and peach-plum compote between bowls. Drizzle with honey and enjoy!
    1 ½ cups plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ cup natural peanut butter
    2 tsp honey

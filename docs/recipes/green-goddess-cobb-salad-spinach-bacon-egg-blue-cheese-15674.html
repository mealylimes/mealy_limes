Green Goddess Cobb Salad with Spinach, Bacon, Egg & Blue Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (6) $5.04/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • ½ tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • parsley flakes
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½avocado
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lemon
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a medium saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain eggs and transfer to a small bowl; add ice or run under cold water to cool. (Reserve saucepan for later use.)
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    ½ small pkg chives
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a blender.
  4. 4
    Peel garlic and add to the blender.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Roughly chop chives and add to the blender.
  6. 6
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the blender.
  7. 7
    Add yogurt, oil, water, capers, and spices to the blender. Blend on high until almost smooth, adding additional water, 1 tablespoon at a time, if needed, to reach a drizzling consistency. Set aside.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp water
    ½ tbsp capers
    ½ tsp parsley flakes
    ½ tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Halve tomatoes and place on a plate.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Add to the plate with the tomatoes.
  10. 10
    When the eggs are done, wipe the saucepan clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    While the pan heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  12. 12
    Once the pan is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate.
  13. 13
    While the bacon is cooking, peel and quarter eggs.
  14. 14
    Divide spinach between plates or bowls; top with eggs, tomatoes, avocado, bacon, and blue cheese. Serve with dressing on the side and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese

Grilled Balsamic Steak Skewers with Greek Salad & Tzatziki Sauce

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (12) $6.45/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ⅛ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 45 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Pat steak dry with paper towels, cut into 1-inch cubes, and season with salt; thread onto skewers, place on a plate, and drizzle with vinaigrette. (If you don't have skewers, you can cook the steak directly on the grill pan.)
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 ½ tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
  3. 3
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add the skewers and cook, turning occasionally, until the steak is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  6. 6
    Using a clean cutting board, trim and medium dice cucumber; trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Place both in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the other veggies.
  8. 8
    Thinly slice olives and add to the bowl.
    ⅛ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  9. 9
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot into thin half-moons; add to the bowl along with the feta and vinaigrette. Stir to combine the salad.
    ½ shallot
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    1 ½ tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
  10. 10
    To serve, divide skewers, salad, and tzatziki between plates. Enjoy!
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce

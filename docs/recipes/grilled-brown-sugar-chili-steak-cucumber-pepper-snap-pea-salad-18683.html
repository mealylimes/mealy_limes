Grilled Brown Sugar-Chili Steak with Cucumber, Pepper & Snap Pea Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (9) $6.93/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • rice vinegar
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
Cookware
aluminum foil baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a baking dish along with soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  2. 2
    Pat steak dry with paper towels and add to the baking dish with the marinade; turn to coat and set aside.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  4. 4
    Peel and mince additional garlic; place in a medium bowl along with oil, soy sauce, vinegar, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Cut snap peas crosswise into ½-inch pieces and add to the bowl; stir to combine the salad and set aside.
  9. 9
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer steak to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  11. 11
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide steak and salad between plates. Enjoy!

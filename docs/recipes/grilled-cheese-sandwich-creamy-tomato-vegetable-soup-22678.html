Grilled Cheese Sandwich with Creamy Tomato-Vegetable Soup

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (8) $1.24/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice celery crosswise.
    1 stick celery
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add celery and carrots. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  4. 4
    Add broth, marinara sauce, and salt to the pan; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until flavors blend, 8-10 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, place the bread slices on a flat surface. Top half of the slices with cheese. Cover sandwiches with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add additional butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Place sandwiches in the skillet and cook until the bottoms are golden brown, 2-4 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Remove the sandwiches to a plate and add more butter to the skillet. Return sandwiches to the skillet, browned-side up, and cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese is melted, 2-4 minutes more. Transfer to a cutting board and slice in half.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    When the soup is done, add cream and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  10. 10
    Divide the soup between bowls and serve with sandwiches on the side. Enjoy!

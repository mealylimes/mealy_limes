Grilled Chicken Skewers with Spicy Plum, Tomato, Corn & Feta Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 64% (7) $3.69/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • 2plums
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    2 plums
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Zest half of the lime into a medium bowl (reserve zested half for later use). Cut remaining lime half into wedges and set aside for serving.
  4. 4
    Add mayo and spices to the bowl with the lime zest, then whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the marinade and stir to coat.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the pan heats up, thread chicken onto skewers and place on a plate. (Alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the pan without using skewers.)
  8. 8
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add skewers and grill, turning occasionally, until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, juice reserved lime into a clean medium bowl.
  10. 10
    Using a clean cutting board, trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the lime juice along with oil, honey, salt, and crushed red pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  11. 11
    Place corn in the colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 cup frozen corn
  12. 12
    Halve plums lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Cut each half into ¼-inch thick slices. Add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  14. 14
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and add the leaves to the bowl along with feta. Gently stir to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  15. 15
    Divide skewers and salad between plates. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing and enjoy!

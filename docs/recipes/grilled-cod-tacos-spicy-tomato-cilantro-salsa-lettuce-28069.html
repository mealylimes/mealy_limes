Grilled Cod Tacos with Spicy Tomato-Cilantro Salsa & Lettuce

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (12) $4.92/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • ½lime
  • 2tomatoes
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place oil and spices in a medium bowl; stir to combine the spice paste.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the pan heats up, add cod to the bowl with the spice paste and turn to coat.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
  4. 4
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add cod and grill until the flesh is opaque and flakey, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    2 tomatoes
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  6. 6
    Zest and juice lime into a clean medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the lime juice.
  8. 8
    Small dice tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  10. 10
    Add oil, chili-garlic sauce, salt, and pepper to the bowl. Stir to combine the salsa and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Separate lettuce leaves, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips.
  12. 12
    Use a fork to break the cod into bite-sized pieces.
  13. 13
    Warm tortillas in the grill pan, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  14. 14
    To serve, divide tortillas between plates; fill with grilled cod, salsa, and lettuce. Enjoy!

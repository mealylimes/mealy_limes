Grilled Cumin Chicken Thighs with Yogurt Sauce & Pita Salad (Fattoush)

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (10) $4.59/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place yogurt, mayo, water, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp water
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ English cucumber
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  4. 4
    Zest lemon into a medium bowl (reserve lemon for the dressing). Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl along with oil and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels, add to the bowl with the marinade, and turn to coat; set aside.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    Place pita breads on a cutting board. Holding a knife parallel to the board, cut each pita in half horizontally to separate the two sides; cut or tear the bread into 1-inch pieces and place in another medium bowl.
    2 whole grain pitas
  7. 7
    Add oil, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the pita; toss with your hands to coat. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing halfway through, until the pita chips are crisp, 5-6 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    Once the grill pan is hot, add chicken; grill until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  11. 11
    While the chicken cooks, juice the lemon into the reserved bowl; add oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Trim and small dice cucumber; halve tomatoes. Add both to the bowl with the dressing.
  13. 13
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  14. 14
    Just before serving, chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl, along with pita chips, and gently toss to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide salad and chicken between plates or bowls; drizzle chicken with sauce and enjoy!

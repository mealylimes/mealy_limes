Grilled Garlic-Ginger Steak Skewers & Zucchini with Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $4.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl along with soy sauce, oil, salt, and crushed red pepper, then stir to combine the marinade.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Pat steak dry with paper towels, cut into 1-inch cubes, and add to the bowl with the marinade. Toss to coat and set aside.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  6. 6
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the pan heats up, wash and dry zucchini. Using a clean cutting board, trim and slice lengthwise into ¼-inch thick planks. Transfer to a plate, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the pan is hot, add zucchini and grill until charred and tender, 2-3 minutes per side. Return to the plate and cover loosely with aluminum foil.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, thread steak onto skewers and place on another plate. (If you don't have skewers, you can cook the steak directly on the grill pan.)
  10. 10
    When the zucchini is done, add the skewers to the pan. Cook, turning occasionally, until the steak reaches desired doneness, 6-10 minutes. Add to the plate with the zucchini.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide rice, zucchini, and steak skewers between plates. Enjoy!

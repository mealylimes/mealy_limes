Grilled Lemon Chicken with Strawberry, Cucumber & Mint Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (9) $3.58/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 1English cucumber
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½lemon
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • honey
  • balsamic vinegar
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 English cucumber
    ½ lemon
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    In a medium salad bowl, combine and whisk together olive oil, balsamic vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp balsamic vinegar
    ¼ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the stem ends of the strawberries; quarter the strawberries lengthwise and add to the salad bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; quarter the cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch-thick pieces at an angle. Add to the salad bowl.
  5. 5
    Pick the mint leaves off the stems; discard the stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the salad bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim off and discard the root end of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Juice the lemon into a medium bowl (that is big enough to hold the chicken breasts). Then, add the minced garlic, olive oil, salt, and pepper; whisk together.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin cutlets.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  10. 10
    Place the chicken cutlets in the medium bowl and turn to thoroughly coat in the marinade.
  11. 11
    Once the grill pan / skillet is hot, add the chicken cutlets and pour any remaining marinade overtop. Cook until the chicken is cooked through, 2 to 4 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate. Cook in batches if necessary.
  12. 12
    Toss the salad until well coated with the dressing.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the chicken cutlets between plates and top with the salad. Enjoy!

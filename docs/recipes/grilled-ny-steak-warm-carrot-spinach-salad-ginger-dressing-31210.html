Grilled NY Steak & Warm Carrot-Spinach Salad with Ginger Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $4.99/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 4 mediumcarrots
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels. Place on a plate, then season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest. (Reserve pan for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    4 medium carrots
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and quarter carrots lengthwise, then cut crosswise into 3-inch long pieces. Place on another plate, drizzle with oil, and season with salt and pepper.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Return grill pan to medium heat. Add carrots and grill, turning occasionally, until lightly charred and tender, 6-8 minutes. Return to the plate.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger. Place in a medium bowl.
  8. 8
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the ginger. Add oil, honey, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Add grilled carrots and spinach to the bowl with the dressing. Gently toss to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide steak and salad between plates. Enjoy!

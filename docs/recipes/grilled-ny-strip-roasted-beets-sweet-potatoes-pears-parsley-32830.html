Grilled NY Strip with Roasted Beets, Sweet Potatoes, Pears & Parsley

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $5.89/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 mediumbeet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • onion powder
  • 2pears
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    1 medium beet
    2 pears
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and medium dice sweet potato. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel and trim beet; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Add to the baking sheet with the sweet potato.
  5. 5
    Quarter, core, and slice pears lengthwise into ½-inch wedges. Add to the baking sheet.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies and pears with oil, then season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies and pears are fork-tender, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    When the roasted veggies and pears have about 12 minutes left, preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide steak, roasted sweet potatoes, beets, and pears between plates. Sprinkle with reserved parsley and enjoy!

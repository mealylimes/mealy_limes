Grilled Oregano Chicken Thighs with Spicy Marinated Tomatoes & Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (32) $3.89/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 3tomatoes
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • brown sugar
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, sugar, and salt. Whisk to combine the marinade and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 jalapeño pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
    3 tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  5. 5
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level, then mince. Add to the bowl with the marinade. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise, then cut into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl, stir to combine, and set aside.
  9. 9
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the pan heats up, pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season with spices on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add chicken and grill until charred and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice, chicken, and marinated tomatoes between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

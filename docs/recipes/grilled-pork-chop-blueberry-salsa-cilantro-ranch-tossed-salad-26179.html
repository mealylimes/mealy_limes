Grilled Pork Chop with Blueberry Salsa & Cilantro-Ranch Tossed Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (9) $4.05/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  5. 5
    Roughly chop half of the blueberries. Place both chopped and remaining whole blueberries in a small bowl along with vinegar, oil, honey, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Mince and add to the bowl with the blueberries. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl and stir once more to combine the salsa; set aside. (Reserve remaining cilantro for the next step.)
  8. 8
    Place remaining cilantro in a medium bowl along with mixed greens, carrots, and dressing. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pork chops and salad between plates; top chops with blueberry salsa. Enjoy!

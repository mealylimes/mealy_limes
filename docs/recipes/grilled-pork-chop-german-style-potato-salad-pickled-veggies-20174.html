Grilled Pork Chop with German-Style Potato Salad & Pickled Veggies

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (2) $4.12/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ¾ lbnew potatoes
  • brown sugar
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb new potatoes
    1 medium carrot
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 Granny Smith apple
  2. 2
    Quarter potatoes; place in a medium saucepan, along with salt, and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, and simmer until fork-tender, 10-15 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, sugar, dill, and salt in a small bowl; whisk to combine the pickling liquid.
    2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp dill, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Peel, trim, and halve carrot lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the pickling liquid.
  5. 5
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl, stir to combine, and set aside.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the parsley along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the grill pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and let rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    When the potatoes are done, drain and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
    ½ shallot
  13. 13
    Quarter, core, and small dice apple; add to the bowl and stir to combine the potato salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pork chops, potato salad, and pickled veggies between plates. Enjoy!

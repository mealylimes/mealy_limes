Grilled Pork Chop over Strawberry-Kiwi Spinach Salad with Pistachios

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $5.24/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • white wine vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½shallot
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • sesame seeds
  • 2kiwifruit
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ⅓ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • brown sugar
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    2 kiwifruit
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  5. 5
    Peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, sesame seeds, Worcestershire, sugar, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tbsp sesame seeds
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp paprika
  6. 6
    Trim and thinly slice strawberries. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Peel, trim, and quarter kiwis lengthwise, then slice crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the bowl, along with the spinach, and toss to combine the salad.
    ⅓ cup pistachios, unsalted
  9. 9
    Thinly slice pork chops.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Top with sliced pork and enjoy!

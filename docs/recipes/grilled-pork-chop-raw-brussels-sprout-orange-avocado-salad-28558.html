Grilled Pork Chop with Raw Brussels Sprout, Orange & Avocado Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $4.40/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1avocado
  • 2oranges
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet mandoline slicer (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add pork chops and grill until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 oranges
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 avocado
  5. 5
    Juice half of one orange into a medium bowl. Add fish sauce, soy sauce, and oil, then stir to combine the dressing. (Reserve remaining oranges for the next step.)
    1 ½ tsp fish sauce
    1 ½ tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Peel and halve remaining oranges, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Trim Brussels sprouts, then using a mandoline slicer (optional), shave or very thinly slice the sprouts. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl, along with pumpkin seeds, and stir to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  10. 10
    Thinly slice pork chops.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pork chops and salad between plates. Enjoy!

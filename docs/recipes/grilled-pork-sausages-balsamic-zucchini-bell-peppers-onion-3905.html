Grilled Pork Sausages with Balsamic Zucchini, Bell Peppers & Onion

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (17) $4.19/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 3pork sausages
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • balsamic vinegar
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1yellow bell pepper
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    1 red bell pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Place the sausages on a plate and rub with a little olive oil.
    3 pork sausages
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Once the grill pan / skillet is hot, add the sausages and reduce the heat to medium. Cook, turning occasionally, until the sausages are cooked through, 12 to 15 minutes. Once done, transfer to a plate and cover with foil to keep warm.
  5. 5
    While the sausages cook, pick the basil leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Transfer to a large bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add to the bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    To the basil and garlic, add olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper; whisk together.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Trim off and discard the ends of the zucchini; halve the zucchini crosswise, then cut each half lengthwise into 4 to 6 wedges. Add to the bowl with the marinade.
  9. 9
    Quarter the bell peppers lengthwise; remove and discard the stem, seeds, and membranes. Cut each quarter into thirds lengthwise and add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; cut lengthwise into ½-inch-thick wedges and add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  11. 11
    Gently toss the vegetables until evenly coated with the marinade.
  12. 12
    Once the sausages have been transferred, add the vegetables to the grill pan / skillet. Cook, tossing occasionally, until they are tender and charred, 5 to 8 minutes. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  13. 13
    Cut the sausages at an angle into 1-inch pieces.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide the grilled sausages and vegetables between plates. Enjoy!

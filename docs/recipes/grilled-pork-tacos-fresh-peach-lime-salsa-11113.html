Grilled Pork Tacos with Fresh Peach-Lime Salsa

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (15) $3.64/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • onion powder
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • 1lime
  • 2peaches
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    2 peaches
    1 lime
  2. 2
    Combine spices in a small bowl.
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch garlic powder
    1 pinch onion powder
    1 pinch black pepper
  3. 3
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the grill pan heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and season with spice mixture on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  5. 5
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Add pork and cook, flipping occasionally, until cooked through, about 8 minutes. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Halve jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Finely dice and add to the bowl. (Be careful, with jalapeños, do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  8. 8
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Halve and pit peaches. Add peach halves to the grill pan, cut-side down, and cook until the flesh is soft and charred, about 4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a cutting board, medium dice, and add to the bowl with the cilantro.
  10. 10
    Juice lime into the bowl, season with salt, and stir to combine the salsa.
    ¼ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Warm tortillas in the grill pan, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, thinly slice the pork chops crosswise into strips.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide tortillas between plates and fill with pork and salsa. Enjoy!

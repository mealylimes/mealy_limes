Grilled Ribeye with Mint, Orange, Snap Pea & Radish Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (10) $6.04/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½orange
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • 1 bunchradish
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ orange
    1 bunch radish
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  5. 5
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
  6. 6
    Zest and juice orange into the bowl with the mint. Add water, Dijon, and salt, then stir to combine the sauce. Set aside.
    2 tbsp water
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Trim and quarter radishes lengthwise.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add radishes, garlic, snap peas, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring often, until just fork-tender, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Add sauce to the skillet and continue to cook, stirring often, until veggies are tender and coated in sauce, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide steak and veggie sauté between plates. Enjoy!

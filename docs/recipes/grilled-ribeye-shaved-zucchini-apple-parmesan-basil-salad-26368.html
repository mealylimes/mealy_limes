Grilled Ribeye with Shaved Zucchini, Apple, Parmesan & Basil Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $5.51/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    1 Granny Smith apple
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  5. 5
    Trim zucchini, then run a vegetable peeler along the length of the zucchini to make ribbons. Place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
  7. 7
    Pick basil leaves off the stems; roll up crosswise and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the bowl, along with Parmesan and vinaigrette, then gently toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette
  8. 8
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide steak and salad between plates. Enjoy!

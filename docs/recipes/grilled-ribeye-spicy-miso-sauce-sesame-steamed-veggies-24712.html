Grilled Ribeye with Spicy Miso Sauce & Sesame Steamed Veggies

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $4.29/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • sesame seeds
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • 22 ½ mlmiso paste, white
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the grill pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan with 2-inches of hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
  5. 5
    While the water comes to a boil, peel and mince shallot. Place in a small bowl along with miso paste, chili-garlic sauce, oil, sesame seeds, sugar, and garlic powder. Stir to combine the spicy miso sauce and set aside.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp miso paste, white
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp sesame seeds
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  6. 6
    Once the water is boiling, reduce heat to medium, then add cauliflower and broccoli. Re-cover and steam until veggies are fork tender, 5-6 minutes.
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  7. 7
    When the veggies are done, drain and return to the pan. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp sesame seeds
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide steak and veggies between plates; drizzle steak with spicy miso sauce. Enjoy!

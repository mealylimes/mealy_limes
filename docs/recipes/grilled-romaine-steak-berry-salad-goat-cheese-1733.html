Grilled Romaine, Steak & Berry Salad with Goat Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 91% (8) $7.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • virgin coconut oil
  • salt
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Season steak generously with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Wash and quarter lettuce lengthwise. Rub cut sides with a little olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
    1 head romaine lettuce
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Coat grill or bottom of skillet with oil. Place steak on grill/skillet and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Place lettuce quarters, cut side down, on grill/skillet. Cook until charred and leaves begin to wilt, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic. In a small saucepan, combine vinegar and garlic; bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce heat to low. Stirring occasionally, simmer until liquid is reduced and thickened, 5-10 minutes.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ cup balsamic vinegar
  7. 7
    Wash, trim stem ends, and slice strawberries. Wash blueberries.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  8. 8
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  9. 9
    To serve, place lettuce cut side up on a plate and top with berries and steak slices. Crumble goat cheese on top and drizzle with balsamic reduction. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese

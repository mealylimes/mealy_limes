Grilled Romaine & Steak Salad with Creamy Feta Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $6.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • virgin coconut oil
  • salt
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • dill, dried
  • Dijon mustard
  • black pepper
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½lemon
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • honey
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Wash and quarter lettuce lengthwise. Rub cut sides with a little olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
    1 head romaine lettuce
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Season steak generously with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Coat grill or bottom of skillet with oil. Place steak on grill/skillet and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Place romaine halves, cut side down, on grill/skillet. Cook until charred and leaves begin to wilt, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  6. 6
    Wash and halve tomatoes. Wash and chop chives.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg chives
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic. In a medium bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together garlic, yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon, honey, dill, salt, and pepper. Add feta and mix.
    1 clove garlic
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    juice of ¼ lemon
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp dill
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  8. 8
    Cut remaining lemon into wedges.
    ¼ lemon
  9. 9
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  10. 10
    To serve, place romaine hearts cut side up on a plate and top with tomatoes and steak slices. Drizzle with dressing and garnish with chives and lemon wedges. Enjoy!

Grilled Salmon with Avocado-Corn Salsa & Dressed Arugula

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (15) $4.57/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1avocado
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat salmon dry with paper towels. Place on a plate and season the top side with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add salmon, skin-side down, and cook, flipping once, until skin is crispy and flesh is opaque, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place corn in a colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Transfer to a small bowl.
    1 cup frozen corn
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, halve, and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the bowl with the corn, along with the salsa, and stir to combine; set aside.
    1 avocado
    ½ cup salsa
  6. 6
    Wash and dry arugula (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  7. 7
    To serve, divide salmon, salsa, and arugula between plates; drizzle arugula with vinaigrette. Enjoy!
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette

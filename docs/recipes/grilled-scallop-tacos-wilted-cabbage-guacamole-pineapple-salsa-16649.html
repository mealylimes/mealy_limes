Grilled Scallop Tacos with Wilted Cabbage, Guacamole & Pineapple Salsa

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 42% (3) $9.04/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • ½red bell pepper
  • 1avocado
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • 1lime
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ½ small headred cabbage
  • ¾ lbsea scallops
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 avocado
    1 lime
    ½ small pineapple
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ small head red cabbage
  2. 2
    Halve and pit avocado. Scoop out into a small bowl and mash with a fork.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the avocado.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Juice half of the lime into the bowl with the avocado; add salt, stir to combine the guacamole, and set aside. (Reserve remaining lime for the salsa.)
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Cut off skin from pineapple, core, and small dice. Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; place both in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the pineapple and bell pepper.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl.
    ½ shallot
  8. 8
    Juice remaining lime into the bowl; add salt, stir to combine the pineapple salsa, and set aside.
    ¼ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the pan heats up, quarter the cabbage lengthwise, then cut out and discard the core; slice crosswise into thin strips.
  11. 11
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add cabbage and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 3-4 minutes. Transfer cabbage to a plate and cover with aluminum foil to keep warm. (Reserve pan for the next step).
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    While the cabbage is cooking, pat scallops dry with paper towels and place on another plate; rub with oil and season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb sea scallops
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Return pan to medium-high heat. Add scallops in a single layer; cook until they develop a nice sear and are opaque throughout, 1½-2 minutes per side. Once done, add to the plate with the cabbage.
  14. 14
    Warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  15. 15
    To serve, spread tortillas with guacamole and top with scallops (cutting them in half if necessary); garnish with wilted cabbage and pineapple salsa. Enjoy!

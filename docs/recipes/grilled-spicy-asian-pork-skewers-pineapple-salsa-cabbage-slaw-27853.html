Grilled Spicy Asian Pork Skewers with Pineapple Salsa & Cabbage Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (4) $3.93/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • salt
  • ¼ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • ½shallot
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pineapple
    1 lime
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and slice lengthwise into thick planks. Place planks on a plate, then drizzle with oil and season with salt on both sides.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor skillet, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add the pineapple planks and grill, turning occasionally, until tender and charred in spots, about 8 minutes. Return to the plate.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place mayo, chili-garlic sauce, and salt in a medium bowl, then whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Pat the pork dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes. Add to the bowl with the marinade and stir to coat.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  8. 8
    Thread pork onto skewers and place on another plate. Alternatively, you can cook the pork pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.
  9. 9
    Return the pan to medium-high heat; add the pork skewers and grill, turning occasionally, until the pork is charred and cooked through, about 8 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    ½ shallot
  11. 11
    Juice lime into the bowl with the shallot, then add fish sauce, soy sauce, sugar, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  12. 12
    Small dice pineapple, return to the plate, and drizzle with half of the dressing. Stir to combine the salsa and set aside. (Reserve remaining dressing for the next step.)
  13. 13
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the reserved dressing.
  14. 14
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  15. 15
    Roughly chop cashews. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw.
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  16. 16
    Divide skewers and slaw between plates. Serve with pineapple salsa on the side and enjoy!

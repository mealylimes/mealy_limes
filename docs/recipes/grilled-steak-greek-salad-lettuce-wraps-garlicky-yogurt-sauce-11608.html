Grilled Steak & Greek Salad Lettuce Wraps with Garlicky Yogurt Sauce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $6.44/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1tomato
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp oregano
  2. 2
    Pat the steak dry with paper towels, then slice against the grain into ¼-inch thick strips. Transfer steak to a small bowl, pour half of the dressing over the steak, and turn to coat. Set aside.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    1 tomato
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  4. 4
    Using a clean cutting board, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons. Transfer to the bowl with the remaining dressing.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Trim and medium dice cucumber; add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Medium dice tomato and add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Drain and thinly slice artichokes. Roughly chop olives. Add both to the bowl and stir to combine the salad; set aside.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  8. 8
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the grill pan heats up, peel and mince additional garlic. Place in a small bowl along with yogurt, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the yogurt sauce.
    1 clove garlic
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the grill pan is hot, add the steak strips and cook to desired doneness, about 1 minute per side for medium-rare. Transfer to a plate.
  11. 11
    Separate lettuce into leaves. Pile leaves, in stacks of two per wrap, onto plates. Fill with Greek salad, steak, and feta. Serve with yogurt sauce on the side and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

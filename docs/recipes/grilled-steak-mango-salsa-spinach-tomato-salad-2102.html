Grilled Steak with Mango Salsa & Spinach-Tomato Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 25% (1) $6.67/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1lime
  • 1mango
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • honey
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    In a small bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together olive oil, lime juice, honey, salt, and pepper.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    juice of ½ lime
    ½ tsp honey
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  3. 3
    Season steak with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Coat grill or bottom of skillet with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Wash mango and jalapeño. Peel the mango, slice flesh away from the pit, small dice, and transfer to a medium bowl. Seed, mince, and add jalapeño to bowl. Peel, small dice, and add onion to bowl.
    1 mango
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ medium red onion
  6. 6
    Add lime juice, honey, and salt to salsa; mix to combine.
    juice of ½ lime
    ¼ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Wash and dry spinach. Transfer to a medium bowl.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  8. 8
    Wash and halve tomatoes. Add to bowl with spinach.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  9. 9
    Place steak on a plate and top with mango salsa. Add salad on the side and drizzle with dressing. Enjoy!

Grilled Steak Wrap with Red Onion, Mixed Greens, Tomato & Blue Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $6.00/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • 1tomato
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with vinegar, oil, maple syrup, Dijon, and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Pat the steak dry with paper towels, then slice against the grain into ¼-inch thick strips. Add to the bowl with the marinade, turn to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Peel and thinly slice onion into rings.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Transfer onions to the grill pan and cook until tender, 3-4 minutes per side.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the mixed greens. (Skip if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  7. 7
    Wash and dry tomato. Using a clean cutting board, halve tomato and thinly slice into half-moons. Transfer to a plate and season with salt and pepper; set aside.
    1 tomato
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    When the onions are done cooking, transfer to the plate with the tomatoes.
  9. 9
    Place steak strips on the grill pan, and cook to desired doneness, about 1 minute per side for medium-rare. Transfer to the plate with the veggies.
  10. 10
    Place tortillas on a flat surface. Divide steak, onions, mixed greens, tomatoes, and blue cheese between them. Roll tightly to close and cut in half.
    2 large flour tortillas
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  11. 11
    To serve, divide wraps between plates and enjoy!

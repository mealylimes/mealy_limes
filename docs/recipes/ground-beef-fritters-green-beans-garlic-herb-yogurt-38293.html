Ground Beef Fritters & Green Beans with Garlic-Herb Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (25) $3.54/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ¾ lbgreen beans
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1shallot
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, trim, and cut green beans crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Place in a medium saucepan, along with salt, and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil over high heat.
    ¾ lb green beans
    ½ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Once the water is boiling, reduce to medium-low and simmer until beans are fork-tender, 1-2 minutes. Drain and set aside to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Divide between a small and a medium bowl. Set the small bowl aside for later use.
    1 shallot
  4. 4
    Add beef, egg, bread crumbs, Worcestershire, and spices to the medium bowl with the shallot. Using your hands, mix well to combine. Use a ¼-cup measure to form the beef mixture into ½-inch thick fritters and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 egg
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add fritters and pan-fry until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the small bowl with the shallot (reserving a small handful for serving).
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl along with yogurt, mayo, water, and spices, then stir to combine the yogurt sauce.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp water
    ½ tsp dill, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  9. 9
    To serve, divide fritters and green beans between plates or bowls. Drizzle with yogurt sauce and sprinkle with reserved mint. Enjoy!

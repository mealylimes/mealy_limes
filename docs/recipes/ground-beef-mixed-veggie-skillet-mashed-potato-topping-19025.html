Ground Beef & Mixed Veggie Skillet with Mashed Potato Topping

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (40) $3.38/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • all-purpose flour
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts potato masher stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
  2. 2
    Peel and medium dice potatoes; place in a medium saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Cover and bring to a boil over high heat; once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook until soft, 10-12 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, peel, trim, and small dice carrots; trim and small dice celery. Place both in a small bowl.
  4. 4
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add beef and cook, breaking it apart with a spoon, until just browned, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  6. 6
    While the beef cooks, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    When the beef is done, add veggies and spices to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies soften, 4-5 minutes more.
    ½ tsp rosemary, dried
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack about 6-inches from the top.
  9. 9
    When the potatoes are done, drain in a colander and return to the saucepan. Add milk, salt, and pepper; using a potato masher, mash until smooth. Cover to keep warm and set aside.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    When the veggies are soft, add flour and cook, stirring frequently, 1 minute more.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  11. 11
    Add tomato sauce, broth, peas, and Worcestershire to the skillet; stir to combine the beef filling and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup frozen peas
    2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  12. 12
    Spoon mashed potatoes over the beef filling; using a fork, evenly spread mashed potatoes over the top, creating ridges.
  13. 13
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until the top is set and lightly golden, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide the skillet between plates or bowls and enjoy!

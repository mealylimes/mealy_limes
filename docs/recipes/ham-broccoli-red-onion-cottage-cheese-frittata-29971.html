Ham, Broccoli, Red Onion & Cottage Cheese Frittata

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (21) $0.96/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 8 ozham steak, boneless
  • ⅔ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
  • ½ cupcottage cheese
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon strainer whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Small dice ham, place in a small bowl, and set aside.
    8 oz ham steak, boneless
  2. 2
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and broccoli; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place cottage cheese in a strainer to remove excess liquid. Transfer to a medium bowl, along with eggs and spices, then whisk to combine.
    ½ cup cottage cheese
    6 eggs
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    When the broccoli is tender, reduce heat to medium-low; pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny 3-5 minutes.
  7. 7
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  8. 8
    Sprinkle diced ham and shredded cheese over the frittata. Place skillet in the oven and broil until top is set and cheese is melted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven and let set, about 5 min.
    ⅔ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  9. 9
    To serve, cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Enjoy!

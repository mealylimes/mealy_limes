Hawaiian Beef & Pineapple Chili with Spinach & Avocado

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (26) $4.50/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • chili powder
  • cumin, ground
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • 1avocado
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small pineapple
    1 avocado
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice the onion; transfer to a small bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  3. 3
    Peel and mince the garlic; add to the bowl of onion.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Cut off and discard the skin of the pineapple; halve or quarter the pineapple lengthwise and cut off and discard the core from the pieces. Cut the flesh into bite-sized pieces and transfer to a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Add the onion and ground beef to the saucepan; cook, breaking apart the meat into smaller pieces, until the beef is browned and the onion is tender, 3 to 5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  7. 7
    Season the beef with chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper; stir until fragrant, 15 to 30 seconds.
    1 tbsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Add almost all of the pineapple (save a few pieces for garnishing) and diced tomatoes to the saucepan; stir to combine.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  9. 9
    Increase the heat and bring the chili to a boil. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for 10 minutes.
  10. 10
    Halve and pit the avocado; scoop out the flesh and discard the skins. Medium dice the flesh and transfer to a small bowl.
  11. 11
    Stir the spinach into the chili until it wilts.
  12. 12
    Divide the chili between two bowls (or one bowl and one lunch container) and garnish with the remaining pineapple and avocado chunks. Enjoy!

Hawaiian Pork Burger with Citrus Grilled Pineapple, Zucchini & Onion

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (15) $1.82/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • mayonnaise
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls spatula tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small pineapple
    ½ lime
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    Trim zucchini and cut crosswise into 1-inch rounds. Peel, halve, and cut onion lengthwise into 1-inch wide wedges; place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Cut off skin from pineapple, halve, and remove the core. Cut a portion of the pineapple into 4 thin slices to place on top of the burgers; set aside for serving. Large dice remaining pineapple and add to the bowl with the veggies.
  5. 5
    Zest lime into the bowl with the veggies and pineapple (set lime aside for later); drizzle with oil, season with salt, and toss to combine.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Thread veggies and pineapple onto skewers and place on a plate; alternatively, you can cook the veggies and pineapple directly on the grill pan. (Reserve the bowl for later use.)
  7. 7
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the grill pan is hot, add skewers and cook, turning occasionally, until the veggies are charred and tender, 4-5 minutes. Return to the plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic and ginger; place both in the reserved bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Add pork, bread crumbs, soy sauce, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the garlic and ginger; use your hands to thoroughly combine. Form into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    2 tbsp panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp soy sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    When the skewers are done, coat the grill with oil. Place burger patties on the grill and cook, undisturbed, for 5 minutes; flip patties, and grill an additional 3-4 minutes, until cooked through. Once done, transfer to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    While the burgers are cooking, cut reserved lime into wedges.
  13. 13
    Toast buns on the grill (if desired).
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  14. 14
    Spread mayo over the bottom half of each bun; top with a pork burger and two slices of pineapple. Close with the top half of each bun.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
  15. 15
    To serve, divide burgers and skewers between plates. Squeeze lime over the skewers and enjoy!

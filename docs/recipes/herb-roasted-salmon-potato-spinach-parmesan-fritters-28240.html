Herb-Roasted Salmon with Potato, Spinach & Parmesan Fritters

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.21/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2eggs
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
  • 2 cupsfrozen shredded hash brown potatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan colander grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Pat salmon dry with paper towels. Place on the baking sheet, skin-side down, and sprinkle with spices.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp thyme, dried
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until salmon is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place both hash browns and spinach in a colander; rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Using your hands, squeeze as much water from the hash browns and spinach as you can. Place in a medium bowl.
    2 cups frozen shredded hash brown potatoes
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
  5. 5
    Add Parmesan, eggs, flour, and spices to the bowl with the hash browns and spinach. Stir to combine the potato mixture.
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    2 eggs
    ¼ cup all-purpose flour
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Using a ¼-cup measure, drop potato mixture into the skillet, spacing scoops a few inches apart; press down to form round fritters. Cook, flipping once, until fritters are golden brown, 3-5 minutes. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    To serve, divide salmon and fritters between plates. Enjoy!

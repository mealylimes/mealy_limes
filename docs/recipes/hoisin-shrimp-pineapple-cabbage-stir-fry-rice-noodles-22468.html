Hoisin Shrimp, Pineapple & Cabbage Stir-Fry with Rice Noodles

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (4) $3.44/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • hoisin sauce
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Remove from heat, uncover, and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes or according to package directions. Drain in a colander and rinse under cold water.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and medium dice the flesh. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small pineapple
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry. Add to the bowl with the pineapple.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pineapple, shrimp, onion, garlic, shredded cabbage, and salt. Stir-fry until the pineapple is tender and shrimp are cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    When the shrimp are done, add noodles, hoisin sauce, and water to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    3 tbsp hoisin sauce
    3 tbsp water
  8. 8
    To serve, divide stir-fry between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

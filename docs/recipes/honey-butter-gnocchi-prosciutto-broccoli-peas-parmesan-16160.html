Honey-Butter Gnocchi with Prosciutto, Broccoli, Peas & Parmesan

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (31) $2.42/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½lemon
  • ¾ cupfrozen peas
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, working in batches if necessary, add the prosciutto and cook until crispy, about 1 minute per side. Remove prosciutto to a plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    6 slices prosciutto
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Wash and dry broccoli. Separate into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
    1 crown broccoli
  5. 5
    Return skillet to medium heat. Add butter, garlic, broccoli, salt, and red pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until broccoli is bright green and tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan, place in a small bowl, and set aside.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  7. 7
    Add gnocchi, peas, and hot water (from the tap) to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the water reduces and gnocchi are soft, 3-4 minutes more.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    ¾ cup frozen peas
    ⅓ cup water
  8. 8
    While the gnocchi is cooking, crumble or chop prosciutto into bite-sized pieces.
  9. 9
    Wash, dry, and juice lemon into the skillet. Add honey and stir to combine; remove from heat.
    ½ lemon
    1 tbsp honey
  10. 10
    To serve, divide gnocchi and vegetables between bowls. Top with crispy prosciutto and Parmesan. Enjoy!

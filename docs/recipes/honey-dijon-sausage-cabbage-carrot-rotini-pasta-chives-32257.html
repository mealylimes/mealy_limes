Honey-Dijon Sausage, Cabbage & Carrot Rotini Pasta with Chives

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (47) $1.28/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • ½ lbground pork sausage
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and set aside. (Reserve a ¼-cup pasta water before draining.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ small pkg chives
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add garlic and sausage. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    ½ lb ground pork sausage
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the carrot.
  8. 8
    Chop chives into small pieces. Add half to the bowl. (Reserve remaining chives for serving.)
  9. 9
    When the sausage is done, add carrot, cabbage, chives, and spices to the skillet. Stir to combine, then cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until cabbage is tender, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    While the cabbage is cooking, place water, Dijon, and honey in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    2 tbsp water
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp honey
  11. 11
    When the cabbage is tender, add sauce to the skillet, along with drained pasta, and stir to combine; add reserved pasta water, a few tablespoons at a time, if necessary, to reach a saucy consistency. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Top with reserved chives and enjoy!

Honey Mustard Chicken Tenders with Broccoli Orange Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (10) $3.30/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • paprika
  • honey
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Dijon mustard
  • black pepper
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1orange
  • ½lemon
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • 1 lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Using a paper towel, lightly coat a small baking dish with oil.
    about ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Grate ¼ cup of Parmesan. In a small bowl, mix together Parmesan, bread crumbs, salt, pepper, and paprika.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp paprika
  3. 3
    In a different small bowl, mix together Dijon, honey, and oil.
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
    1 ½ tbsp honey
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Slice chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Cut each fillet into strips.
    1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  5. 5
    One by one, dip chicken strips in mustard mixture, then coat in bread crumb mixture, and place in baking dish.
  6. 6
    Place in the oven and bake, turning over halfway through, until tenders are golden brown, 15-20 minutes.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic. In a small bowl, prepare sauce by mixing together ½ of the garlic (save the rest for the slaw), Dijon, and honey. Set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
    2 tbsp honey
  8. 8
    In a large bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together remaining garlic, lemon juice, oil, salt, and pepper.
    ½ lemon
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Add broccoli slaw to bowl with dressing. Wash, peel, and medium dice orange. Add to bowl and toss salad.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
    1 orange
  10. 10
    To serve, place chicken tenders and broccoli slaw on a plate and have honey mustard sauce on the side. Enjoy!

Honey Mustard Mixed Greens Salad with Ham, Egg, Cheese & Pear

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 25% (1) $3.28/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1shallot
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1pear
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  • 8 ozham steak, boneless
  • 80 mlhoney mustard dressing
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups mixing bowls tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 pear
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Peel and slice shallot crosswise into thin rounds. Place in a medium bowl.
    1 shallot
  3. 3
    Quarter, core, and medium dice pear. Add to the bowl with the shallot.
  4. 4
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise and add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Roughly chop eggs and add to the bowl.
    2 hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  7. 7
    Small dice ham and add to the bowl.
    8 oz ham steak, boneless
  8. 8
    Add mixed greens, carrots, cheese, and dressing to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
    ⅓ cup honey mustard dressing
  9. 9
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

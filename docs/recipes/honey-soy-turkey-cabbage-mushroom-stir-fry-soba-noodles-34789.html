Honey Soy Turkey, Cabbage & Mushroom Stir-Fry with Soba Noodles

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (49) $4.00/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • honey
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • sesame seeds
  • ½lime
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 5 ozsoba (buckwheat) noodles
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add turkey and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate. (Reserve skillet with cooking juices for later use.)
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
  5. 5
    While the water comes to a boil, trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  6. 6
    Once the water is boiling, add soba noodles and cook until tender, about 5 minutes (or cook according to package instructions). Drain and rinse in a colander; set aside.
    5 oz soba (buckwheat) noodles
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, thinly slice mushrooms. Add to the bowl with the cabbage.
  8. 8
    Return skillet (with cooking juices) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add cabbage and mushrooms; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender, 6-7 minutes.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  9. 9
    While the veggies are cooking, juice lime into a small bowl.
  10. 10
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lime juice along with soy sauce, honey, pepper, and salt. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp honey
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  11. 11
    When the veggies are tender, return turkey to the skillet along with drained noodles and sauce. Continue to cook, tossing occasionally, until noodles are warmed through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide stir-fry between plates or bowls. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and enjoy!
    2 tsp sesame seeds

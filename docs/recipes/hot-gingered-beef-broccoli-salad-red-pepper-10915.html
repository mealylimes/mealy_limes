Hot Gingered Beef & Broccoli Salad with Red Pepper

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $6.82/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1red bell pepper
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 crown broccoli
    1 red bell pepper
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic and ginger; place both in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Add oils, soy sauce, vinegar, and chili-garlic sauce to the bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  4. 4
    Slice the steak into thin strips and place in another medium bowl. Add ⅓ of the dressing and toss to coat; set aside to marinate.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Add broccoli to the skillet and cook, stirring often, until bright green and almost cooked through, about 5 minutes.
  9. 9
    Add steak strips and marinade to the skillet. Cook, stirring often, until desired doneness, 2-3 minutes for medium. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, seed and slice bell pepper into thin strips.
  11. 11
    Add bell pepper and mixed greens to the bowl with the remaining dressing; toss to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and top with steak-broccoli stir-fry and sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

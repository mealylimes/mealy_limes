Hot Honey Wings with Celery, Carrot, Radish & Blue Cheese Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.25/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½shallot
  • 3 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • ½ bunchradish
  • hot sauce
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Pat chicken wings dry with paper towels and place in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with cornstarch and spices, then toss to coat. Transfer to the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
    2 tsp cornstarch
    1 tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place wings in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place honey and hot sauce in a clean medium bowl. Whisk to combine the hot honey and set aside.
    2 tbsp honey
    1 tbsp hot sauce
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 sticks celery
    2 medium carrots
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. 6
    Peel and mince shallot. Place in another medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp rice vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Trim and thinly slice celery. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Peel and trim carrots; run the peeler along each carrot to make long, noodle-like strips. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim and thinly slice radishes. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl, along with blue cheese, and gently toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  11. 11
    When the wings are done, remove from the oven and add to the bowl with the hot honey. Toss to coat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide wings and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Huevos Rancheros with Pico de Gallo & Avocado

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $2.05/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) canpinto beans
  • 2eggs
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 2tomatoes
  • 2small corn tortillas
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher small saucepan spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 jalapeño pepper
    1 lime
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Heat a small saucepan over medium heat. Once the saucepan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add half of the diced onions and salt to the saucepan; sauté until the onions are soft, about 3-4 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Drain and rise the beans.
    1 (15 oz) can pinto beans
  6. 6
    Add the beans, cumin, and water to the saucepan with the onions. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low heat for 5 minutes.
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ cup water
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, small dice the tomatoes and add to a medium bowl. Add the other half of the diced onion to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  8. 8
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  9. 9
    Quarter the jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs of the jalapeño with a spoon. Finely mince and add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  10. 10
    Juice half of the lime into the bowl with the tomatoes and add salt. Stir to combine the pico de gallo and set aside.
    ¼ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Once the beans are done, remove from the heat and use a fork or potato masher to mash into small pieces. Juice the other half of the lime into the saucepan with the beans and stir to combine.
  12. 12
    Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  13. 13
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  14. 14
    Gently crack the eggs into the skillet. Cook until the tops of the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, 3-4 minutes. Once done, remove from heat and set aside.
    2 eggs
  15. 15
    Halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out and set aside.
  16. 16
    To assemble, divide the bean mixture on top of the tortillas. Top with the fried egg, pico de gallo, and sliced avocado. Enjoy!
    2 small corn tortillas

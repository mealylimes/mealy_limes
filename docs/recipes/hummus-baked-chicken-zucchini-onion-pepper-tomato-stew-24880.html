Hummus Baked Chicken with Zucchini, Onion, Pepper & Tomato Stew

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $2.56/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ cuphummus
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Transfer to the baking sheet.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  3. 3
    Season chicken with salt and pepper on both sides. Spread ¼-cup hummus over the top of each fillet, then drizzle with more oil.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ½ cup hummus
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until chicken is cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry zucchini. Using a clean cutting board, trim and medium dice the zucchini; place in a medium bowl.
    2 medium zucchini squash
  6. 6
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  7. 7
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  8. 8
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add zucchini and onion; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, drain and roughly chop roasted peppers.
    1 (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  10. 10
    Add roasted peppers, marinara sauce, and spices to the pan; stir to combine the stew and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook until veggies are tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide veggie stew between plates or bowls; top with chicken. Enjoy!

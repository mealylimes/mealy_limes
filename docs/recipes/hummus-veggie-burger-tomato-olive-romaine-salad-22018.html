Hummus Veggie Burger with Tomato, Olive & Romaine Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (1) $2.03/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 2tomatoes
  • ½ cuphummus
  • 30 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
  • 2veggie burger patties, plant-based
  • 1 heartromaine hearts
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    1 red bell pepper
    1 heart romaine hearts
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook, flipping once, until golden brown and cooked through, 8-10 minutes (or cook patties according to package instructions). Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 veggie burger patties, plant-based
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, thinly slice one tomato and place on a plate; season with salt. Medium dice remaining tomato and place in a medium bowl.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the tomato.
  6. 6
    Separate lettuce into leaves. Set aside one leaf per burger, adding them to the plate with the sliced tomato. Chop (or tear) remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl, along with the vinaigrette, and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    2 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
  8. 8
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  9. 9
    Spread top and bottom halves of each bun with hummus. Layer the bottom halves with lettuce, tomato, and burger patties, then close with the top half of each bun.
    ½ cup hummus
  10. 10
    To serve, divide burgers and salad between plates. Enjoy!

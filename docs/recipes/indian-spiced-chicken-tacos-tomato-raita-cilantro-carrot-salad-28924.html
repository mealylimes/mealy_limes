Indian Spiced Chicken Tacos with Tomato Raita & Cilantro-Carrot Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (12) $1.28/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½shallot
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • 3 mediumcarrots
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • rice vinegar
  • 1tomato
  • garam masala
  • ½ lbground chicken
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 medium carrots
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 tomato
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  5. 5
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    1 ½ tsp garam masala
    1 ½ tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Small dice tomato. Add to the bowl with the shallot along with yogurt, water, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the raita. Set aside.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp water
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  11. 11
    Divide tortillas between plates, fill with chicken, then top with raita. Serve with carrot salad on the side and enjoy!

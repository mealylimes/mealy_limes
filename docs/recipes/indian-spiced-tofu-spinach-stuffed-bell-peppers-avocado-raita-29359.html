Indian Spiced Tofu & Spinach Stuffed Bell Peppers with Avocado Raita

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (6) $4.78/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • garam masala
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts potato masher stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    2 yellow bell peppers
    1 jalapeño pepper
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    1 avocado
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and halve bell peppers lengthwise. Transfer to another baking sheet, drizzle with oil, season with salt and pepper, then arrange cut-side up.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, flipping once, until peppers are lightly charred and tender, 8-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Small dice and place in a small bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl with the jalapeño.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Using your fingers, crumble tofu into the skillet, then add jalapeño, onion, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until onion is soft and tofu is golden, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    1 ½ tsp garam masala
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger. Add to the skillet and stir to combine.
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the skillet. (Place remaining cilantro in the reserved bowl and set aside for the raita.)
  12. 12
    When the tofu and onion are done, add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for it to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Add yogurt, stir to combine the filling, and remove from heat.
    ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
  13. 13
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh, then add to the bowl with the cilantro along with additional yogurt, cumin, and salt. Mash with a fork or potato masher until the raita is smooth and combined.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ⅛ tsp salt
  14. 14
    Divide peppers between plates and stuff with tofu-spinach filling. Serve with avocado raita and enjoy!

Indian Tomato-Spinach Soup with Blue Cheese & Roasted Red Pepper Naan

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $2.50/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • garam masala
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice celery crosswise.
    2 sticks celery
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add celery and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Add broth, marinara, garam masala, and salt to the pan; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until flavors blend, 8-10 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    2 tsp garam masala
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  6. 6
    Arrange naan on a baking sheet pan; place in the oven and broil, flipping once, until golden brown and toasted, 1-2 minutes.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  7. 7
    While the naan is toasting, slice peppers into strips.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  8. 8
    Remove naan from oven and top with sliced peppers and blue cheese.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  9. 9
    When the soup is almost done, return naan to the oven and broil until cheese is melted and warmed through, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven and cut into quarters.
  10. 10
    When the soup is done, wash and dry the spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed). Add spinach to the pan in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Remove from heat.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  11. 11
    Divide soup between bowls and serve with naan on the side. Enjoy!

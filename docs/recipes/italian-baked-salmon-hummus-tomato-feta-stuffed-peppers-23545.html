Italian Baked Salmon with Hummus, Tomato & Feta Stuffed Peppers

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $5.67/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1tomato
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • ½ cuphummus
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Arrange rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 yellow bell peppers
    1 tomato
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Pat salmon dry with paper towels. Place on one half of a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil, then season with salt and Italian seasoning.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and halve bell peppers lengthwise; add to the other half of the baking sheet. Drizzle with additional oil, season with salt and pepper, then arrange cut-side down.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until peppers are fork-tender and salmon is cooked through, 10-12 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, small dice tomato. Place in a small bowl.
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the tomato along with feta. Stir to combine and set aside.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  8. 8
    When the salmon is done, remove baking sheet from the oven. Transfer the salmon to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
  9. 9
    Change oven to broil on high.
  10. 10
    Divide hummus between pepper halves and top with the tomato-feta mixture. Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until cheese is soft and starting to brown, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ½ cup hummus
  11. 11
    To serve, divide stuffed peppers and salmon between plates. Enjoy!

Italian Chicken-Gnocchi Skillet with Broccoli, Snap Peas & Lemon

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (14) $4.52/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 crown broccoli
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, add gnocchi and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, about 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    While the gnocchi are cooking, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Place both in a small bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, chicken, and spices. Cook, stirring frequently, until chicken is golden and cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add broccoli and snap peas to the skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
  9. 9
    Zest and juice lemon into the skillet, then add cooked gnocchi and stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi is warmed through, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide gnocchi, veggies, and chicken between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

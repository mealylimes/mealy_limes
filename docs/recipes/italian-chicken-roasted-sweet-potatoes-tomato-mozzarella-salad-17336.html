Italian Chicken with Roasted Sweet Potatoes & Tomato-Mozzarella Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (6) $4.65/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Peel and cut sweet potato lengthwise into ¼-inch thick strips; place in a medium bowl. Add oil and salt, toss to coat, then spread out on a baking sheet pan. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place sweet potato in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing once, until golden and fork-tender, about 25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, oil, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Medium dice mozzarella and add to the bowl.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
  10. 10
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with spices.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    To serve, divide chicken, sweet potatoes, and salad between plates. Enjoy!

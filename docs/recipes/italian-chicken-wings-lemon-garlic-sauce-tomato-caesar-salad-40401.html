Italian Chicken Wings with Lemon-Garlic Sauce & Tomato Caesar Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (15) $3.15/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • honey
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1lemon
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • 1tomato
  • cornstarch
  • mayonnaise
  • 2anchovy fillets, packed in oil
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 500°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Pat chicken wings dry with paper towels and place in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with cornstarch and spices, then toss to coat. Transfer to the baking sheet, spread out in an even layer, and drizzle with oil.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  3. 3
    Place wings in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 tomato
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic. Divide between a clean medium bowl and a small bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Zest lemon into the medium bowl with the garlic. Juice lemon into the small bowl with the garlic, then add mayo, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the lemon-garlic dipping sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Mince anchovy fillets. Add to the medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, honey, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 anchovy fillets, packed in oil
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Halve tomato lengthwise, then cut into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    When the wings are almost done, using a peeler, shave Parmesan into curls. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Divide wings and salad between plates. Serve with dipping sauce on the side and enjoy!

Italian Pasta Salad with Roasted Chicken, Fennel, Peppers & Artichokes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (13) $4.11/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 6sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 1fennel bulb
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 fennel bulb
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Trim off root end and stalks of the fennel, leaving only the white bulb. Halve fennel bulb lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and large dice bell pepper. Add to the baking sheet with the fennel.
  5. 5
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels, large dice, and add to the baking sheet. Drizzle chicken and veggies with oil, then season with spices. Stir to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until chicken is cooked through and veggies are tender, about 20 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  8. 8
    While the pasta is cooking, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, salt, and crushed red pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Drain and thinly slice artichoke hearts and sun-dried tomatoes. Add both to the bowl.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
    6 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  11. 11
    When the chicken and veggies are done, remove from the oven. Add to the bowl, along with cooled pasta, and stir to combine.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

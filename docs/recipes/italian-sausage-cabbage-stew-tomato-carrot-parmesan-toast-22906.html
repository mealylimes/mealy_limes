Italian Sausage-Cabbage Stew with Tomato, Carrot, Parmesan & Toast

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $2.29/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • ½ lbground pork sausage
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    1 medium carrot
    1 stick celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, ground sausage, salt, and pepper. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground pork sausage
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel, trim, and halve carrot lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the cabbage.
  7. 7
    Trim and thinly slice celery crosswise. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    When the sausage is done, add veggies, broth, and tomatoes to the pan. Stir to combine the stew and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the pan and stir to combine (set remaining parsley aside for serving).
  10. 10
    Once the stew is simmering, reduce heat to medium and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are soft, about 8 minutes. Remove from heat.
  11. 11
    While the stew is cooking, preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  12. 12
    Arrange bread slices on a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil, then sprinkle with Italian seasoning. Place in the oven and broil until toasted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven and cut toasts in half.
    2 slices whole grain bread
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
  13. 13
    Finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  14. 14
    Divide stew between bowls; sprinkle with Parmesan and reserved parsley. Serve with toast on the side and enjoy!

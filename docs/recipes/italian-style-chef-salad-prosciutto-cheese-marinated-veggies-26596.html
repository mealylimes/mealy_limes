Italian-Style Chef Salad with Prosciutto, Cheese & Marinated Veggies

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $0.62/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • ¼ cupItalian salad dressing
  • 4hard-cooked eggs, peeled
  • ⅔ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place dressing, mayo, and salt in a small bowl. Stir to combine and set aside.
    ¼ cup Italian salad dressing
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers and artichoke hearts. Transfer both to a plate.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  3. 3
    Cut or tear each slice of prosciutto in half; roll each piece and add to the plate with the roasted peppers and artichokes.
    6 slices prosciutto
  4. 4
    Slice eggs crosswise. Season with salt and add to the plate.
    4 hard-cooked eggs, peeled
    1 pinch salt
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise. Add to the plate.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, and chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces.
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  7. 7
    To serve, divide lettuce between plates or bowls. Top with roasted peppers, artichoke hearts, prosciutto, eggs, tomatoes, and cheese. Drizzle with the dressing and enjoy!
    ⅔ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend

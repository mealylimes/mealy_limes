Italian-Style Turkey Burger with Mozzarella & Tomato Caesar Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $3.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1anchovy fillet, packed in oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place turkey, egg, bread crumbs, and spices in a medium bowl; mix with your hands until well combined. Form the turkey mixture into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    1 egg
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook, undisturbed, for 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, thinly slice cheese.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  5. 5
    When the patties are done on one side, flip, then top with cheese. Continue to cook until burgers are cooked through, 5-7 minutes more. Transfer to a clean plate.
  6. 6
    While the burgers are cooking, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  7. 7
    Juice lemon into a clean medium bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic; mince anchovy fillet. Add both to the bowl with the lemon juice, along with mayo, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 anchovy fillet, packed in oil
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Remove 1 whole lettuce leaf, cut in half, and set aside for the burgers. Chop (or tear) remaining lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
  11. 11
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  12. 12
    Spread mayo over the bottom half of each bun; top with burger patties and reserved lettuce. Close with the top half of each bun. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!
    1 tbsp mayonnaise

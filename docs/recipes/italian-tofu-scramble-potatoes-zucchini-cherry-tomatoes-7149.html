Italian Tofu Scramble with Potatoes, Zucchini & Cherry Tomatoes

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 60% (5) $2.33/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • basil, dried
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 mediumyellow potato
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut the tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich the tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium yellow potato
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  3. 3
    Small dice the potato (cut into ¼-inch cubes). Transfer to a medium saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil over high heat; reduce heat and simmer, covered, for 7 minutes. Once finished, drain the potatoes and set aside.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and garlic, and cook, stirring occasionally, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Halve zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into half rounds. Add the zucchini and spices to the skillet and stir to combine.
    ½ tsp turmeric
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp basil
    ¼ tsp oregano
  8. 8
    Crumble the tofu with your fingers into the skillet. Add the cooked potatoes and stir to combine. Sauté until the veggies are softened, 5-7 minutes.
  9. 9
    Halve the grape tomatoes.
  10. 10
    Remove from the heat and stir in the grape tomatoes. Divide between plates and enjoy!

Jalapeño-Peach Chicken Thighs with Creamy Avocado & Artichoke Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (8) $3.47/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½avocado
  • ½lemon
  • 2peaches
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and pan-fry, without turning, until golden underneath, 5-6 minutes. When the chicken is golden, flip, and remove skillet from the heat (the chicken will not be cooked all the way through).
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 peaches
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ lemon
    ½ avocado
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  6. 6
    Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Cut each quarter into wedges and place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Small dice and add to the bowl with the peaches. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  8. 8
    When the chicken has been removed from the heat, zest and juice lemon into the skillet; top chicken with peaches and jalapeño, then drizzle with honey (reserve bowl for the next step). Place skillet in the oven and bake until the chicken is cooked through, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 tsp honey
  9. 9
    While the chicken is cooking, peel and mince garlic; transfer to the reserved bowl. Add mayo, vinegar, honey, Dijon, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Drain and thinly slice artichokes; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  11. 11
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear romaine into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad, chicken, and peaches between plates. Enjoy!

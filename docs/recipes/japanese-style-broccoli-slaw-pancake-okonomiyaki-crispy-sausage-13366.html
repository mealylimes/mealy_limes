Japanese-Style Broccoli Slaw Pancake (Okonomiyaki) with Crispy Sausage

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (14) $2.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2eggs
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • 2pork sausages
  • baking powder
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small pkg chives
    ½ lime
  2. 2
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Transfer both to a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Add eggs and broth to the bowl with the ginger and garlic; whisk to combine and set aside.
    2 eggs
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  4. 4
    Place flour, baking powder, and salt in another medium bowl; whisk to combine. Gradually add the egg mixture to the bowl with the flour, whisking to create a smooth batter.
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    1 tsp baking powder
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, add broccoli slaw to the bowl with the batter and stir until thoroughly combined.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add half the pancake batter (1¼-cups per pancake) to the skillet and smooth out into a large, ½-inch thick pancake. Cook, flipping once, until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate and cover with foil.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  8. 8
    While the pancake is cooking, preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the pan heats up, use the tip of a knife to slice down the length of each sausage. Peel off casings and discard.
    2 pork sausages
  10. 10
    Add sausage meat to the pan and cook, breaking apart with a spoon, until crispy and cooked through, 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat, cover, and set aside.
  11. 11
    Once the first pancake is done cooking, add more oil to the skillet and repeat with remaining batter.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, chop chives into small pieces and set aside.
  13. 13
    Juice the lime into a small bowl. Add mayo and chili-garlic sauce; whisk to combine the spicy mayo.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  14. 14
    To serve, place a pancake on each plate. Top with crispy sausage, drizzle with spicy mayo, and sprinkle with chives. Enjoy!

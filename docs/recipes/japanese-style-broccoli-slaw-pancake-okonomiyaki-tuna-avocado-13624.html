Japanese-Style Broccoli Slaw Pancake (Okonomiyaki) with Tuna & Avocado

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $3.42/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • toasted sesame oil
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • sesame seeds
  • ½avocado
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • mayonnaise
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • baking powder
Cookware
aluminum foil can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Drain tuna and transfer to a medium bowl; flake with a fork.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  3. 3
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add both to the bowl with the tuna.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Add eggs and broth to the bowl with the tuna; whisk to combine.
    2 eggs
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  5. 5
    Place flour, baking powder, and salt in another medium bowl; whisk to combine. Gradually add the egg mixture to the bowl with the flour, whisking to create a smooth batter.
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    1 tsp baking powder
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, add broccoli slaw to the bowl with the batter and stir until thoroughly combined.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add half the pancake batter (1½-cups per pancake) to the skillet and smooth out into a large, ½-inch thick pancake. Cook, flipping once, until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate and cover with aluminum foil.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a small bowl.
  10. 10
    Peel and mince additional garlic. Add to the bowl with the lime, along with mayo and chili-garlic sauce; whisk to combine the spicy mayo and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  11. 11
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; transfer to a plate.
  12. 12
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Add to the plate with the green onions.
  13. 13
    Once the first pancake is done cooking, add more oil to the skillet and repeat with remaining batter.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  14. 14
    To serve, place a pancake on each plate. Top with green onions and avocado; drizzle with spicy mayo and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    2 tsp sesame seeds

Kale Caesar Salad with Seared Strip Steak & Parmesan Crisps

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $4.76/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½lemon
  • 1 bunchkale
  • mayonnaise
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1anchovy fillet, packed in oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts parchment paper tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper and set aside.
  2. 2
    Finely grate Parmesan. Place half in a medium bowl and set aside for the salad. (Reserve remaining Parmesan for the next step.)
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  3. 3
    To make crisps from the remaining cheese, measure out 1 tbsp of grated Parmesan and place in a pile on the baking sheet. Repeat with remaining Parmesan, keeping the piles a few inches apart. Season with pepper.
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until Parmesan is lightly browned and crisp, 8-10 minutes. (Watch carefully because they can burn quickly.) Once done, remove from the oven and set aside to cool.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Mince anchovy fillet. Add both to the bowl with the reserved Parmesan.
    1 clove garlic
    1 anchovy fillet, packed in oil
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, and juice lemon into the bowl. Add mayo, oil, vinegar, pepper, and salt; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ lemon
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Wash and dry kale. Fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing. Using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes. Set aside.
    1 bunch kale
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, about 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, break Parmesan crisps into bite-sized pieces.
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with Parmesan crisps and steak. Enjoy!

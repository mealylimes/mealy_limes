Kale Cobb Salad with Prosciutto, Sweet Potato, Apple & Blue Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 65% (5) $2.53/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • ½shallot
  • 1 bunchkale
  • 1Gala apple
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    2 eggs
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    1 bunch kale
    1 Gala apple
  4. 4
    Peel and medium dice sweet potato. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until sweet potato is golden and fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool for 5 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing and, using your hands, massage kale until wilted, 1-2 minutes. Set aside.
  8. 8
    Peel and quarter eggs, then season with salt and pepper. Place on a plate.
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  9. 9
    Chop or tear prosciutto into bite-sized pieces. Add to the plate with the eggs.
    6 slices prosciutto
  10. 10
    Quarter, core, and medium dice apple. Add to the plate.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide kale between plates or bowls. Top with prosciutto, eggs, sweet potato, apple, and blue cheese. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese

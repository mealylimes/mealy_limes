Kale Salad with Bacon, Pineapple, Coconut & Orange Yogurt Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $2.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1 bunchkale
  • ½orange
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ⅓ cupshredded unsweetened coconut
  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • ½ cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ orange
    1 bunch kale
    ½ small pineapple
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, zest and juice orange into a medium bowl. Add yogurt, oil, vinegar, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing. Using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes.
  7. 7
    Cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost. Add to the bowl, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
    ½ cup edamame, shelled
  9. 9
    Wipe skillet clean with paper towels, then return to medium heat. Add coconut and almonds; cook, stirring frequently, until golden and toasted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ⅓ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with bacon, coconut, and almonds. Enjoy!

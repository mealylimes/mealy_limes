Kale Salad with Chicken, Apple, Dates, Pumpkin Seeds & Crispy Parmesan

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (10) $3.26/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 1 bunchkale
  • mayonnaise
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
  • ⅛ cupmedjool dates, pitted
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts parchment paper tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper and set aside.
  2. 2
    Peel and mince shallot and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, mayo, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 clove garlic
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Wash and dry kale. Fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; finely chop leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing. Using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes. Set aside.
    1 bunch kale
  4. 4
    Finely grate Parmesan. Place in a pile on the baking sheet and spread out into a 6-inch circle.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until Parmesan is lightly browned and crisp, 5-7 minutes. (Watch carefully as the Parmesan can burn quickly.) Remove from the oven and set aside to cool.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash and dry apple; using a clean cutting board, quarter, core, and thinly slice. Add to the bowl with the kale.
    1 Granny Smith apple
  10. 10
    Roughly chop dates and add to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup medjool dates, pitted
  11. 11
    Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  12. 12
    Break the crispy Parmesan into bite-sized pieces.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the salad between plates or bowls and top with sliced chicken, pumpkin seeds, and crispy Parmesan. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds

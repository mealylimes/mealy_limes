Kale Salad with Pork Sausage, Grapes, Pumpkin Seeds & Cheddar

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $2.32/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1lemon
  • ⅓ lbred grapes
  • 1 bunchkale
  • ⅛ cuppumpkin seeds
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 bunch kale
    ⅓ lb red grapes
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until browned and cooked through, 10-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, add oil, honey, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the lemon juice; whisk to combine the dressing.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl with the dressing. Using your hands, massage the dressing into the kale.
  7. 7
    Halve grapes and add to the bowl with the kale.
  8. 8
    Small dice cheese and set aside for serving.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    When the sausages are done, transfer to a cutting board and slice crosswise into bite-sized rounds.
  10. 10
    Add sausage and pumpkin seeds to the bowl with the kale; toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup pumpkin seeds
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad between plates and top with diced cheese. Enjoy!

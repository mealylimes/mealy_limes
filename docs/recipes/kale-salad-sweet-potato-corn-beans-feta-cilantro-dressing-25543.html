Kale Salad with Sweet Potato, Corn, Beans, Feta & Cilantro Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (7) $2.48/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) canpinto beans
  • honey
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 bunchkale
  • 1lime
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    1 lime
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 bunch kale
  3. 3
    Peel and medium dice sweet potato. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with spices. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until sweet potatoes are browned and fork-tender, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool, about 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a blender.
  6. 6
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Roughly chop and add to the blender. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the blender along with yogurt, oil, honey, and salt. Blend on high until smooth, adding 1 tablespoon of water, if needed, to reach a drizzling consistency. Set dressing aside.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
  9. 9
    Add dressing to the bowl with the kale and, using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes.
  10. 10
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander. Add to the bowl with the kale.
    1 (15 oz) can pinto beans
  11. 11
    Place corn in the colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Add to the bowl.
    1 cup frozen corn
  12. 12
    Add cooled sweet potato and feta to the bowl. Gently toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

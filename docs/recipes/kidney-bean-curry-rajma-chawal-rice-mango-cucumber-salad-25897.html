Kidney Bean Curry (Rajma Chawal) with Rice & Mango-Cucumber Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (20) $2.89/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 (15 oz) cankidney beans
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • 2tomatoes
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • garam masala
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    2 tomatoes
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
    1 mango
    ½ English cucumber
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until beginning to soften, about 3 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    While the onion is cooking, medium dice tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the ginger and garlic.
  9. 9
    When the onion is soft, add ginger, garlic, tomatoes, and spices to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tomato is soft, about 5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 ½ tsp garam masala
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in the reserved bowl (setting aside a small handful for serving).
  11. 11
    Juice lime into the bowl with the cilantro. Add oil, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander. When the tomato is soft, add beans and water to the skillet; stir to combine the curry and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring occasionally, until curry has thickened, about 5 minutes.
    1 (15 oz) can kidney beans
    ¾ cup water
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  14. 14
    Trim and medium dice the cucumber. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
  15. 15
    When the curry is done, remove from heat. Add yogurt and stir to combine.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
  16. 16
    To serve, divide rice, curry, and salad between plates or bowls; top curry with reserved cilantro. Enjoy!

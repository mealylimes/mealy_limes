Korean-Style Steak Skewers with Spicy Cucumber & Carrot Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (15) $5.63/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • cayenne pepper
  • chili powder
  • honey
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls small saucepan tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
    1 medium carrot
  3. 3
    Trim green onions; mince half and place in a medium bowl. Slice the other half crosswise into ¼-inch pieces, place in another medium bowl, and set aside.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic; add to the bowl with the minced green onions, along with vinegar, soy sauce, honey, fish sauce, oil, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 clove garlic
    3 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp honey
    2 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  5. 5
    Pat steak dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes; add to the bowl with the marinade, turn to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  6. 6
    Add soy sauce, oil, vinegar, sesame seeds, honey, chili powder, and cayenne to the bowl with the sliced green onion; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp sesame seeds
    ½ tsp honey
    ½ tsp chili powder
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  7. 7
    Using a clean cutting board, trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot. Add both to the bowl and stir to combine the salad. Set aside.
  9. 9
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the grill pan heats up, thread steak onto skewers and place on a plate, reserving marinade for later use. (If you don't have skewers, you can cook the steak directly on the grill pan.)
  11. 11
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add the skewers and cook, turning occasionally, until the steak is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, pour reserved marinade into a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, then reduce heat to low, and simmer until sauce is slightly thickened, 3-4 minutes; remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide skewers and salad between plates; drizzle skewers with sauce and enjoy!

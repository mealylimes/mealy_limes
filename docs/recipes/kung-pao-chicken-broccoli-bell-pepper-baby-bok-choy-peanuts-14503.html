"Kung Pao" Chicken with Broccoli, Bell Pepper, Baby Bok Choy & Peanuts

⏱ 35 min 🍽 4 servings
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • 2red bell peppers
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 4 stickscelery
  • 2 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • 6baby bok choy
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 crowns broccoli
    2 red bell peppers
    4 sticks celery
    6 baby bok choy
    1 jalapeño pepper
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
  2. 2
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Place in a large bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell peppers. Trim and slice celery into ½-inch thick pieces; add both to the bowl with the broccoli.
  4. 4
    Trim bok choy ends and cut crosswise into 1-inch pieces; add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful, with jalapeños, do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  6. 6
    Medium dice chicken and place in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with cornstarch, salt, and pepper; toss to coat.
    2 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    2 tbsp cornstarch
    1 tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken to the skillet and stir-fry until golden brown and just cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat and add more oil, swirling to coat the bottom. Add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until crisp-tender, about 5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince or grate ginger; place in the reserved bowl.
  11. 11
    Add cold water, vinegar, soy sauce, chili-garlic sauce, additional cornstarch, and sugar to the bowl with the ginger; whisk to combine the sauce.
    ½ cup water
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    4 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp cornstarch
    2 tsp brown sugar
  12. 12
    Add cooked chicken and sauce to the skillet with the veggies. Bring sauce to a boil and cook, stirring frequently, until it thickens, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; add to the skillet along with the peanuts and stir to combine.
    ½ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  14. 14
    To serve, divide stir-fry between bowls and enjoy!

"Kung Pao" Chicken with Broccoli, Celery, Bell Pepper, Peanuts & Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (38) $3.99/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ⅛ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 crown broccoli
    1 red bell pepper
    1 stick celery
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Trim and slice celery into ½-inch thick pieces; add both to the bowl with the broccoli.
  6. 6
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful, with jalapeños, do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice chicken and place in another medium bowl. Sprinkle with cornstarch, salt, and pepper; toss to coat.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken to the skillet and stir-fry until golden brown and just cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    Return skillet to medium-high heat and add more oil, swirling to coat the bottom. Add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until crisp-tender, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince or grate ginger; place in the reserved bowl.
  12. 12
    Add cold water, vinegar, soy sauce, chili-garlic sauce, additional cornstarch, and sugar to the bowl with the ginger; whisk to combine the sauce.
    ¼ cup water
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp cornstarch
    1 tsp brown sugar
  13. 13
    Add cooked chicken and sauce to the skillet with the veggies. Bring sauce to a boil and cook, stirring frequently, until it thickens, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  14. 14
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; add to the skillet along with the peanuts and stir to combine.
    ⅛ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  15. 15
    To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between bowls. Enjoy!

Lamb Chops with Middle Eastern Cilantro Sauce (Chermoula) & Veggies

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (5) $6.64/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lemon
  • 4lamb loin chops
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lemon
    2 medium carrots
    2 medium zucchini squash
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; transfer to a small bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the garlic.
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into the bowl. Add oil and spices; stir to combine the chermoula and set aside.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and thinly slice carrots into ¼-inch thick rounds. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  7. 7
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; cut crosswise into ½-inch thick half-moons and add to the baking sheet with the carrots.
  8. 8
    Drizzle veggies with oil and season with salt and pepper. Toss to combine, then spread out in a single layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender, about 15 minutes.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    While the skillet heats up, pat the lamb chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    4 lamb loin chops
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add lamb chops and cook, flipping once, until cooked through, 5-6 minutes. Remove to a plate, cover loosely with aluminum foil, and rest 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    To serve, divide lamb chops and roasted veggies between plates; drizzle with chermoula and enjoy!

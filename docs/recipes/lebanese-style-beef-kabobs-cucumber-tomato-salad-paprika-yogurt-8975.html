Lebanese-Style Beef Kabobs with Cucumber-Tomato Salad & Paprika Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (12) $3.43/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • 2tomatoes
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Peel and mince the garlic; transfer to a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Peel the onion. Thinly slice half of the onion and add to the bowl with the garlic. Mince remaining onion and place in a separate, medium bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Add vinegar and salt to the bowl with the thinly sliced onion and garlic; stir to combine, then set aside to marinate.
    2 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ English cucumber
    2 tomatoes
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the parsley to the bowl with the marinating onions.
  7. 7
    Add remaining parsley, ground beef, and spices to the bowl with the minced onion; mix with your hands until well combined.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Using a ¼ cup measure, form the beef mixture into 8 short, sausage-like shapes, approximately 3-inches long; thread 2 onto each skewer. (Alternatively, you can cook directly on the grill/skillet without using skewers.)
  9. 9
    Place kabobs closely together on a baking sheet pan. Drizzle oil over the kabobs, then rotate the kabobs to coat in a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, combine yogurt, paprika, and salt in a small bowl and whisk to combine; set aside.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ½ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Coat the grill or skillet with oil; place kabobs on the grill, rotating every 2½-3 minutes, until browned and cooked through, 10-12 minutes. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Peel and medium dice the cucumber; medium dice the tomatoes. Add both to the bowl with the marinated onions.
  14. 14
    Add oil, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the tomato and cucumber; stir to combine the salad.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, divide salad and kabobs between plates. Enjoy with paprika yogurt on the side!

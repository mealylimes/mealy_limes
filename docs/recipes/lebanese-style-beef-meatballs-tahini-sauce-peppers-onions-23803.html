Lebanese-Style Beef Meatballs with Tahini Sauce, Peppers & Onions

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 74% (21) $5.44/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • 1lemon
  • 1 small pkgfresh mint
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • tahini
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 orange bell pepper
    1 small pkg fresh mint
    1 lemon
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into thin strips; add to the baking sheet with the peppers. Drizzle veggies with oil, then season with salt and pepper; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 medium red onion
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until veggies are starting to soften, 10-12 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in a medium bowl. (Reserve the other half for serving.)
  7. 7
    Add beef, bread crumbs, egg, and spices to the bowl with the mint; use your hands to combine the beef mixture. Using a tablespoon, form the mixture into rounded meatballs and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ¾ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    ¾ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  8. 8
    When the veggies are soft, remove from the oven. Add meatballs to the baking sheet, nestling them into the veggies.
  9. 9
    Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to roast until the veggies are golden brown and meatballs are cooked through, about 12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, juice half of the lemon into a small bowl. Cut remaining lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
  11. 11
    Add tahini, water, pepper, and salt to the bowl with the lemon juice. Whisk to combine the sauce.
    2 tbsp tahini
    2 tbsp water
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch salt
  12. 12
    Divide veggies and meatballs between plates; drizzle meatballs with tahini sauce and sprinkle veggies with reserved mint. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

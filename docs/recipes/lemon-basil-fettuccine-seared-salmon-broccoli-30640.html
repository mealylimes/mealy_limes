Lemon-Basil Fettuccine with Seared Salmon & Broccoli

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (50) $5.20/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • ½lemon
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) large pot measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until almost done, about 8 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil and spices.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    If using salmon with skin, place the fillet, skin-side down, on a cutting board. Holding the tail end (or corner) firmly, use a knife to cut between the flesh and skin; continue to cut through the length of the fillet (take care not to cut through the skin); discard the skin.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  4. 4
    Cut the salmon into 1-inch cubes. Add to the bowl with the garlic-oil, stir to coat, and set aside to marinate.
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 crowns broccoli
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lemon
  6. 6
    Using a clean cutting board, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
  7. 7
    When the pasta is almost done, add broccoli to the pot. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta reaches desired firmness and broccoli is tender, 2-3 minutes more. Drain pasta and set aside. (Reserve a ¼-cup pasta water before draining.)
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add salmon and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 3-4 minutes.
  10. 10
    While the salmon is cooking, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  11. 11
    When the salmon is done, juice lemon into the skillet.
  12. 12
    Add drained pasta, broccoli, additional oil and spices, reserved pasta cooking water, and half of the basil to the skillet. Toss to combine and continue to cook until heated through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and top with reserved basil. Enjoy!

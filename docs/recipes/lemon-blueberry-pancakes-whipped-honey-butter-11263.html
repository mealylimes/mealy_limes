Lemon-Blueberry Pancakes with Whipped Honey Butter

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (13) $0.95/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1egg
  • ½lemon
  • brown sugar
  • baking powder
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut butter into small pieces and place in a small bowl. Set aside to soften.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a medium bowl.
    ¾ cup all-purpose flour
    1 ½ tbsp brown sugar
    1 tsp baking powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Place egg and milk in a small bowl and whisk to combine.
    1 egg
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
  4. 4
    Melt additional butter in a large skillet over low heat. Quickly whisk melted butter into the egg mixture to combine.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Create a well in the center of the flour mixture. Add the egg mixture and whisk until just combined.
  6. 6
    Wash and dry lemon. Zest and juice into the pancake batter; whisk until smooth, being careful not to over-mix the batter.
    ½ lemon
  7. 7
    Gently fold blueberries into the pancake batter.
    ½ (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  8. 8
    Return skillet to medium-high heat. Add more butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    Working in batches, use a ¼-cup measure to pour the batter into the skillet. Cook for 1-2 minutes on each side, until golden and cooked through.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, add honey to the bowl with the softened butter; stir vigorously with a fork to combine and whip the honey butter.
    2 tsp honey
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pancakes between plates and top with honey butter. Enjoy!

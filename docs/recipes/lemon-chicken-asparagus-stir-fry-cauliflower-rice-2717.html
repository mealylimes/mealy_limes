Lemon Chicken & Asparagus Stir Fry with Cauliflower Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (26) $4.22/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • 1 medium headcauliflower
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lemon
  • black pepper
  • cornstarch
  • honey
  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board garlic press (optional) grater or food processor measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash the cauliflower and remove the leaves. Using a food processor or box grater, grate to rice-like texture. Transfer to a large bowl and set aside.
    1 medium head cauliflower
  2. 2
    In a skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat. Add the cauliflower and cook, stirring occasionally, until the rice is slightly browned and tender, 7-10 minutes. Return to the bowl and set aside.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  3. 3
    Peel and mince the garlic. Peel and mince or grate the ginger. Grate zest from the lemon.
    1 clove garlic
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger
    ½ lemon
  4. 4
    In a small bowl, create a slurry by whisking together water with cornstarch until smooth. Add the minced garlic and ginger, lemon zest and juice, soy sauce, and honey and whisk together. Set aside.
    3 tbsp water
    1 tsp cornstarch
    zest and juice from ½ lemon
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp honey
  5. 5
    Wash the asparagus and snap (or cut) off the woody ends. Cut on an angle into 2 inch pieces. Transfer to a bowl.
    1 bunch asparagus
  6. 6
    Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Season with salt and pepper.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the cauliflower rice has been removed, add more oil to the skillet. Add the chicken and cook until browned and cooked through, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Once the chicken has been removed, add more oil to the skillet. Add asparagus and cook, stirring occasionally, until bright green and tender-crisp, 3-4 minutes.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  9. 9
    Return the chicken to the skillet and pour in the sauce. Stir-fry until heated through and sauce thickens, 1-2 minutes.
  10. 10
    Place cauliflower rice in a bowl and top with the stir fry. Enjoy!

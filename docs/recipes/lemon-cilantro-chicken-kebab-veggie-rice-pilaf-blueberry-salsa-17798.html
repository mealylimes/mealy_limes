Lemon-Cilantro Chicken Kebab with Veggie Rice Pilaf & Blueberry Salsa

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 97% (9) $4.24/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lemon
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 2-4 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lemon
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrot; peel, halve, and small dice onion. Place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, trim and small dice zucchini; add to the bowl with the carrot and onion.
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    While the veggies are cooking, using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
  8. 8
    Add rice and water to the pan, stir to combine, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once the liquid comes to a boil, cover the pan and reduce heat to low. Cook rice pilaf until liquid is mostly absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove from heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
    1 cup water
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place garlic and half of the cilantro in the reserved bowl. (Place remaining cilantro in a small bowl and set aside for the salsa.)
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Zest and juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic and cilantro, then add oil and spices; stir to combine the marinade.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces, add to the bowl with the marinade, and stir to coat. Thread chicken onto skewers and place on a plate; alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  12. 12
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  13. 13
    Once the grill pan is hot, add the kebabs and cook, turning occasionally, until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Finely dice and add to the small bowl with the cilantro. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  15. 15
    Roughly chop blueberries and add to the bowl along with vinegar, oil, honey, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the salsa and set aside.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  16. 16
    To serve, divide rice pilaf, chicken kebabs, and blueberry salsa between plates. Enjoy!

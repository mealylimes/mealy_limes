Lemon-Dill Chicken Skewers with Quinoa & Minty Snap Pea Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (14) $4.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1lemon
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½ cupquinoa
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    In a small saucepan, combine water, quinoa, and salt; bring the mixture to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until liquid is fully absorbed, about 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
    1 cup water
    ½ cup quinoa
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  4. 4
    Zest and juice half of the lemon into a medium bowl. Cut remaining lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
  5. 5
    Mince capers. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice, along with yogurt and spices, then whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 tbsp capers
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and large dice. Add to the bowl with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  7. 7
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in another medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp pure maple syrup
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Cut snap peas crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  10. 10
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    While the pan heats up, thread chicken onto skewers and place on a plate. (Alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the pan without using skewers.)
  12. 12
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add skewers and grill, turning occasionally, until chicken is cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Just before serving, add mixed greens to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    Divide quinoa, salad, and skewers between plates or bowls. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing and enjoy!

Lemon Fettuccine Alfredo with Chicken & Broccoli

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (29) $2.35/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • all-purpose flour
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 4 ozfettuccine pasta
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½lemon
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stainless steel sauté pan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water; cover and bring to a boil.
  2. 2
    Heat a sauté pan over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets; season with salt and pepper.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Add butter to the pan and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Place the chicken in the pan and cook until golden brown and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  6. 6
    While the chicken cooks, wash and dry the knife and cutting board. Then peel and mince the garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, season with salt and add the pasta; stir for the few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 9 to 10 minutes.
    1 tbsp salt
    4 oz (1 quarter coin = 2 oz) fettuccine pasta
  8. 8
    Once the chicken has been transferred, reduce the heat to medium-low and add more butter to the pan.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    Add the garlic and flour to the pan and whisk for about 1 minute.
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
  10. 10
    Add the broth and whisk until smooth, then add the milk and cook, stirring frequently, until the sauce is thickened, 4 to 5 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
  11. 11
    Wash and dry the broccoli; cut the head into bite-sized florets and medium dice the stems. Transfer to a medium bowl. Add to the pasta during the last 3 to 4 minutes of cooking time.
    1 crown broccoli
  12. 12
    Finely grate ¼ cup of Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  13. 13
    Add the Parmesan to the sauce and season with salt and pepper; stir until smooth, then reduce the heat to low until the pasta is ready.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    Wash the lemon; grate all of the zest, then juice the lemon.
    ½ lemon
  15. 15
    Drain the pasta and broccoli in a colander, reserving about ½ cup of the pasta water.
  16. 16
    Slice the chicken crosswise.
  17. 17
    Add the pasta, broccoli, chicken, lemon zest and juice, and ½ of the reserved pasta water to the sauce; toss to combine. Add more pasta water to thin out the sauce if needed.
  18. 18
    Divide the fettuccine alfredo between two plates (or one plate and one lunch container). Enjoy!

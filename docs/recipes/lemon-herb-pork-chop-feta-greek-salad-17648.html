Lemon-Herb Pork Chop with Feta & Greek Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (18) $4.49/serving
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • ⅛ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1tomato
  • 1yellow bell pepper
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ English cucumber
    1 tomato
    1 yellow bell pepper
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic; add oil and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place in a small baking dish. Pour marinade over the chops, turn to coat, and set aside. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    2 pork chops, bone-in
  5. 5
    While the chops are marinating, peel and mince additional garlic; place in the reserved bowl along with oil, vinegar, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice shallot; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 shallot
  7. 7
    Trim and medium dice cucumber; medium dice tomato. Add both to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Thinly slice olives and add to the bowl; stir to combine the salad and set aside.
    ⅛ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  10. 10
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add chops and marinade; pan-fry until golden brown on one side, 3-4 minutes.
  12. 12
    Flip chops and sprinkle the top side with half of the feta (reserve remaining feta for the salad). Continue to pan-fry until cheese has softened and chops are cooked through, 3-4 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide chops and salad between plates; top salad with reserved feta and enjoy!

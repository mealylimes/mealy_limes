Lemon Pepper Steak with Mashed Potatoes & Green Beans

Dinner Meat
⏱ 45 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (8) $5.88/serving
Ingredients
  • virgin coconut oil
  • salt
  • oregano, dried
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • crushed red pepper
  • black pepper
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • ½lemon
  • ¾ lbgreen beans
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls potato masher salad spinner (optional) stainless steel sauté pan tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil.
    about 7 cups water
  2. 2
    Wash, peel, and large dice potatoes; add to saucepan. Once boiling, cook until potatoes are tender, 10-12 minutes.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic. Grate 1 tsp of lemon zest. In a medium bowl, prepare marinade by mixing together ¼ of the garlic (save the rest for the green beans), lemon zest, lemon juice, oil, oregano, pepper, and salt.
    4 cloves garlic
    ½ lemon
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place steak in bowl and coat with marinade.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
  5. 5
    Wash green beans and trim ends.
    ¾ lb green beans
  6. 6
    Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Coat grill or bottom of skillet with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    about ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Heat a sauté pan over medium heat and add coconut oil. Add remaining garlic and crushed red pepper. Cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds.
    about ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Add green beans to the sauté pan and, stirring occasionally, cook until tender crisp, 5-7 minutes.
  10. 10
    Wash and dry parsley. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  11. 11
    Drain potatoes and return back to saucepan. Add parsley, chicken broth, oil, salt, and pepper. Mash until smooth.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Remove beans from heat. Season with salt and pepper; toss to coat.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, arrange steak, mashed potatoes and green beans on a plate. Enjoy!

Lemon-Raspberry Fool with Dark Chocolate Chips

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings $1.68/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • granulated sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • ¼ cupdark chocolate chips
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls silicone spatula strainer whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill. (If space is limited, place in the refrigerator.)
  2. 2
    Wash and dry raspberries. Set aside a few berries per person for serving, then place remaining berries in a blender.
    1 (6 oz) pkg raspberries
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, zest, and juice lemon into the blender, then add sugar and vanilla. Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
    ½ lemon
    3 tbsp granulated sugar
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
  4. 4
    Pour raspberry purée into a strainer placed over another medium bowl. Using a silicone spatula, press purée through the strainer to remove seeds; discard seeds and set sauce aside.
  5. 5
    Chop chocolate chips into small pieces. Set aside.
    ¼ cup dark chocolate chips
  6. 6
    Remove bowl from the freezer. Pour cold cream into the bowl.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  7. 7
    Use a whisk (or hand blender) to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, about 3 minutes if whisking. (When you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.)
  8. 8
    Add raspberry sauce and chocolate to the bowl with the whipped cream. Gently fold a few times so that there are streaks of sauce running through the fool.
  9. 9
    Divide the fool between small bowls, cups, or jars; garnish each with reserved berries and enjoy!

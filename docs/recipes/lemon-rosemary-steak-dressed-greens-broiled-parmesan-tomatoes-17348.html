Lemon-Rosemary Steak & Dressed Greens with Broiled Parmesan Tomatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $6.71/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½lemon
  • 3tomatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ small pkgfresh rosemary
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pkg fresh rosemary
    ½ lemon
    3 tomatoes
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    Slide rosemary leaves off the sprigs; discard sprigs and mince the leaves. Divide between two medium bowls.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; add to one of the bowls with the rosemary.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Zest lemon into the bowl with the rosemary and garlic; add oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the marinade, add the steak, and turn to coat; set aside. (Reserve lemon for the next step.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  5. 5
    While the steak is marinating, juice lemon into the second bowl with the rosemary; add oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, halve tomatoes and transfer to a baking sheet pan, cut-side up; season with salt and pepper. Place in the oven and broil until softened, about 8 minutes.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    While the tomatoes are broiling, finely grate Parmesan and place in a small bowl.
    ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Peel and mince additional garlic; add to the bowl with the Parmesan, along with Italian seasoning, and stir to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  12. 12
    When the tomatoes are soft, remove from the oven and top with the Parmesan mixture. Return to the oven and continue to broil until the cheese is melted and golden, 2-3 minutes more.
  13. 13
    While the tomatoes are broiling, chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces, add to the bowl with the dressing, and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide salad and broiled tomatoes between plates; top salad with steak and enjoy!

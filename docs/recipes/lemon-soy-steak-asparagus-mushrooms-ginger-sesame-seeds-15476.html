Lemon-Soy Steak with Asparagus, Mushrooms, Ginger & Sesame Seeds

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 93% (7) $6.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    1 small bunch asparagus
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the lemon juice, along with soy sauce, oil, and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Add steak to the bowl with the marinade, turn to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  6. 6
    Snap or cut off the woody ends of the asparagus; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  7. 7
    Slice mushrooms into ¼-inch thick pieces and add to the baking sheet with the asparagus.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince ginger and additional garlic; sprinkle over the veggies.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Drizzle veggies with oil and soy sauce; sprinkle with sesame seeds, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp sesame seeds
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, tossing once, until veggies are fork-tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the steak and marinade; pan-fry until steak reaches desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and let rest 5 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  13. 13
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide veggies and steak between plates. Enjoy!

Lemony Cucumber-Edamame Salad with Feta Cheese & Mint

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 12 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (15) $1.29/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • ¼ small pkgfresh mint
  • 1 ¼ cupsedamame
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ English cucumber
    ¼ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    1 ¼ cups edamame
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, honey, Dijon, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp honey
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp oregano, dried
  4. 4
    Trim and small dice cucumber. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  5. 5
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with edamame and feta. Stir to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  6. 6
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and enjoy!

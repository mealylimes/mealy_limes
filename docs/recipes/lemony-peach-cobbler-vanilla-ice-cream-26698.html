Lemony Peach Cobbler with Vanilla Ice Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $1.01/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 3 fl ozwhole milk
  • ½lemon
  • 2peaches
  • brown sugar
  • baking powder
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a small baking dish with butter.
    ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Slice each quarter into ½-inch thick wedges. Transfer to the baking dish and spread out in an even layer.
    2 peaches
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, zest, and juice lemon over peaches in the baking dish; set aside.
    ½ lemon
  4. 4
    Place flour, milk, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a small bowl. Stir to combine.
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    3 fl oz (⅜ cup) whole milk
    3 tbsp brown sugar
    ¾ tsp baking powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place butter in a small saucepan set over medium-low and heat. Once the butter is melted, add to the bowl with the flour and stir to combine the topping (being careful not to over-mix).
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
  6. 6
    Spoon topping over the peaches, then spread out evenly. Sprinkle additional sugar on top.
    1 tsp brown sugar
  7. 7
    Place cobbler in the oven and bake until topping is golden brown and peaches are tender, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide peach cobbler and ice cream between bowls. Enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

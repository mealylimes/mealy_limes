Lentil & Mushroom Sloppy Joes with Avocado-Spinach Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (8) $3.74/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ½ cupbrown lentils, dried
  • 1green bell pepper
  • brown sugar
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse lentils under cold water; drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and salt, then bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook until tender, 15-20 minutes.
    ½ cup brown lentils
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ avocado
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Peel and mince onion and garlic; transfer both to a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Roughly chop mushrooms. Trim, seed, and roughly chop pepper. Add both to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Add vegetables, sugar, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender-crisp, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp chili powder
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, sugar, and salt in the bowl used for the veggies. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 pinch salt
  8. 8
    Add tomato sauce to the skillet, stir to combine, and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low, and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender, 3-4 minutes.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, halve and pit avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out into the bowl with the dressing. Add spinach and toss to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  11. 11
    When the lentils are tender, drain, and add to the skillet with the vegetables; stir to combine the sloppy joe mix.
  12. 12
    To serve, place the bottom half of a bun on each plate. Top with sloppy joe mix and close with top half of the bun. Divide salad between plates and enjoy!

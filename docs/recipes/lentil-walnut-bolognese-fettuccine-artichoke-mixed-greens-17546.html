Lentil-Walnut "Bolognese" Fettuccine with Artichoke Mixed Greens

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (10) $2.07/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1tomato
  • mayonnaise
  • ⅛ cupwalnuts
  • ¼ cupbrown lentils, dried
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls potato masher stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 stick celery
    1 tomato
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrot; trim and small dice celery. Place both in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Medium dice tomato; add to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; add to the bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
  7. 7
    Add tomato sauce, water, lentils, and walnuts to the pan; stir to combine the bolognese. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until lentils are soft, about 18 minutes.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ¾ cup water
    ¼ cup brown lentils, dried
    ⅛ cup walnuts
  8. 8
    While the bolognese is cooking, fill another medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta in a colander.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic; place in the reserved bowl. Add mayo, vinegar, Dijon, maple syrup, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Drain and thinly slice artichokes; add to the bowl with the dressing and set aside.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  11. 11
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. 12
    When the lentils are soft, use a potato masher to mash the bolognese until semi-smooth, about 1 minute.
  13. 13
    Add drained pasta and half of the parsley to the pan with the bolognese, then toss to combine; remove from heat. (Reserve remaining parsley for garnish.)
  14. 14
    Add mixed greens to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pasta and salad between plates; top pasta with remaining parsley and enjoy!

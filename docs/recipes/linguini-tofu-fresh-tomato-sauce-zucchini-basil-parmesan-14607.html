Linguini with Tofu, Fresh Tomato Sauce, Zucchini, Basil & Parmesan

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.79/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • 5 ozlinguine pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz linguine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    2 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lemon
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, cut the tofu into ¾-inch cubes.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tofu to the skillet and sprinkle with spices; cook, turning often, until golden on all sides, about 6 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, halve tomatoes and transfer to a medium bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes and set aside.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    When the tofu is done, remove to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
  11. 11
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat and add more oil, swirling to coat the bottom. Add garlic and crushed red pepper; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  12. 12
    Add tomatoes, zucchini, broth, Italian seasoning, and salt to the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer until zucchini is soft and sauce has thickened, 6-7 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Cut lemon into wedges; set both aside.
  14. 14
    Finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  15. 15
    When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and return to the pan. Add zucchini-tomato sauce and tofu to the pan; gently toss to combine.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide pasta between plates; top with Parmesan, basil, and a squeeze of lemon. Enjoy!

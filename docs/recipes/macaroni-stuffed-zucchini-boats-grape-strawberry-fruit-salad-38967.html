Macaroni-Stuffed Zucchini Boats with Grape & Strawberry Fruit Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (9) $2.17/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ⅓ lbred grapes
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and set aside. (Reserve pan for later use.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ⅓ lb red grapes
  4. 4
    Trim, and halve zucchini lengthwise. Scrape out the soft flesh from the middle of each half, place in a small bowl, and set aside for later use.
  5. 5
    Arrange zucchini halves, cut-side up, on a baking sheet pan; drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until softened, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    While the zucchini is baking, trim and quarter strawberries lengthwise. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Halve grapes. Add to the bowl with the strawberries, stir to combine the fruit salad, and set aside.
  8. 8
    Coarsely grate cheese. Set aside.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    Roughly chop reserved zucchini flesh.
  10. 10
    When the pasta is done, return the pan to medium-high heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chopped zucchini and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 2 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Add flour to the pan and continue to cook, stirring constantly, until zucchini is well coated, about 30 seconds.
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
  12. 12
    Slowly pour milk into the pan, whisking constantly. Continue to cook, whisking constantly, until sauce has thickened, 1-2 minutes more.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
  13. 13
    Add cheese and spices to the pan. Continue whisking until cheese is melted and sauce is smooth, about 1 minute. Add cooked pasta, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  14. 14
    Divide zucchini boats between plates, then fill with macaroni and cheese. Serve with fruit salad on the side and enjoy!

Mango, Avocado & Arugula Salad with Cilantro-Lime Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $4.56/serving
Ingredients
  • 1avocado
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 1mango
  • ¼ cuppine nuts
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • honey
  • salt
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together lime juice, olive oil, honey, cumin, salt, and pepper.
    juice of 1 lime
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp cumin
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  2. 2
    Wash and dry cilantro. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to dressing and mix. Set aside.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Wash and dry arugula.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  4. 4
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out and medium dice the flesh. Wash and peel mango; slice flesh away from pit and medium dice. Peel and thinly slice onion into half rings.
    1 avocado
    1 mango
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    To serve, arrange a bed of arugula on a plate and top with onion, avocado, and mango. Drizzle with dressing and sprinkle with pine nuts. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup pine nuts

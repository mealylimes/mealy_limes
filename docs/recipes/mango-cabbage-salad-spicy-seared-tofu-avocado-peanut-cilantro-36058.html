Mango-Cabbage Salad with Spicy Seared Tofu, Avocado, Peanut & Cilantro

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (10) $3.76/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1avocado
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½ small headred cabbage
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ small head red cabbage
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
    1 mango
    1 avocado
  3. 3
    Juice lime into a small bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince ginger. Add to the bowl with the lime juice along with soy sauce, maple syrup, and chili-garlic sauce, then whisk to combine. Set aside.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, cut the tofu planks in half, then cut each half into 2 triangles. Place in a medium bowl, then sprinkle with cornstarch, salt, and pepper. Gently turn to coat.
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tofu and cook, flipping halfway through, until golden and crispy, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat. (Wipe the bowl clean with paper towels and reserve for next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Place in the reserved bowl. (Set aside a small handful of cilantro for serving.)
  9. 9
    Add half of the sauce to the bowl with the cilantro, along with oil and vinegar, then whisk to combine the dressing. (Set remaining sauce aside for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp rice vinegar
  10. 10
    Halve cabbage lengthwise, cut out the core, and slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl.
  14. 14
    Roughly chop peanuts. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad; set aside.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  15. 15
    Add remaining sauce to the skillet with the tofu and gently toss to coat.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls, then top with tofu and reserved cilantro. Enjoy!

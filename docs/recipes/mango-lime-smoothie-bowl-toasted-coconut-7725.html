Mango Lime Smoothie Bowl with Toasted Coconut

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (11) $1.33/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • turmeric, ground
  • pure maple syrup
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • ½ cupshredded unsweetened coconut
  • 2 cupsfrozen mangoes
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 mango
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    When the skillet is hot, add coconut to the pan. Cook, stirring often, until it becomes golden brown around the edges, 3-5 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl to cool.
    ½ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
  4. 4
    Peel the mango and slice the flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides. Medium dice the mango and set aside.
  5. 5
    Grate zest from the lime and set aside; juice lime into blender.
  6. 6
    Add frozen mango, yogurt, milk, maple syrup, and spices to blender.
    2 cup frozen mangoes
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    1 ½ tbsp pure maple syrup
    ⅛ tsp turmeric
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon
  7. 7
    Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
  8. 8
    Divide smoothie between bowls and top with fresh mango, toasted coconut, and lime zest. Enjoy!

Maple-Bacon Chicken Wings with Cucumber, Bell Pepper & Parsley Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (45) $4.30/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1English cucumber
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • cornstarch
  • ketchup
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • baking powder
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Pat chicken wings dry with paper towels and place in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with baking powder, salt, and pepper, then toss to coat. Transfer to the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
    1 tsp baking powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place wings in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    While the chicken wings are baking, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until almost crispy, 3-4 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince garlic. Place in a clean medium bowl along with ketchup, water, maple syrup, soy sauce, vinegar, and cornstarch. Whisk to combine a smooth slurry.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp ketchup
    2 tbsp water
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp cornstarch
  8. 8
    When the bacon is almost crispy, pour slurry into the skillet, stirring constantly. Bring to a boil over high heat and cook, stirring frequently, until sauce thickens, 2-3 minutes. Return sauce to the bowl and set aside.
  9. 9
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 English cucumber
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  10. 10
    Place oil, vinegar, maple syrup, salt, and pepper in another medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    When the wings are done, remove from the oven. Add to the bowl with the sauce and toss to coat.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide wings and salad between plates. Enjoy!

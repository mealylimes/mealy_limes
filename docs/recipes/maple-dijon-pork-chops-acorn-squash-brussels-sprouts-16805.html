Maple-Dijon Pork Chops with Acorn Squash & Brussels Sprouts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $3.71/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1acorn squash
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil for easier cleanup (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry acorn squash; halve lengthwise, trim off the ends, then scoop out seeds. Cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick slices and place in a medium bowl.
    1 acorn squash
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, and trim Brussels sprouts; halve lengthwise and add to the bowl with the squash.
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons; add to the bowl.
    1 medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Drizzle oil over veggies and season with spices; toss to coat.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp rosemary, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Transfer veggies to the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer. Place in the oven and bake, tossing halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender, 15-18 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    While the chops are cooking, place maple syrup, Dijon, and salt in a small bowl; whisk to combine the maple-Dijon sauce.
    1 ½ tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
  12. 12
    When the chops are done, remove to a clean plate, pour maple-Dijon sauce over top, and turn to coat both sides.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide pork chops, sauce, and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

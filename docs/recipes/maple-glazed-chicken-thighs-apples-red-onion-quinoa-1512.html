Maple Glazed Chicken Thighs, Apples & Red Onion with Quinoa

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (6) $3.83/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2Gala apples
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • black pepper
  • pure maple syrup
  • red wine vinegar
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stainless steel skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa, broth, and water; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    6 fl oz (¾ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ cup water
  2. 2
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Season chicken with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add chicken and cook until browned and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side.
    about 1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Wash, core, and cut apples into wedges. Peel and cut onion into wedges.
    2 Gala apples
    ½ medium red onion
  6. 6
    Transfer chicken to a plate and return skillet to stove. Add more oil, apples, onion, and thyme. Stirring occasionally, cook until apples are just tender and golden, 5-6 minutes.
    about ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ½ tsp thyme
  7. 7
    In a small bowl, mix together broth, maple syrup, and vinegar.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
    2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  8. 8
    Return chicken to skillet and pour in sauce. Stir and cook until sauce thickens, 3-4 minutes.
  9. 9
    Uncover quinoa and fluff with a fork.
  10. 10
    To serve, place quinoa on a plate and top with chicken, apples, and onion. Enjoy!

Maple-Pecan Oatmeal with Fresh Peaches

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $0.90/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1peach
  • ⅓ cuppecan halves
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place water and salt in a small saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
    1 ¾ cup water
    1 pinch salt
  2. 2
    Once boiling, stir in oats and maple syrup. Cover and let simmer, on low, 3-5 minutes, or until oats reach desired consistency.
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, halve and pit peach, then medium dice.
    1 peach
  4. 4
    Roughly chop pecans.
    ⅓ cup pecans, dry roasted
  5. 5
    To serve, divide oats between bowls and top with peaches, pecans, and maple syrup. Serve with milk and enjoy!
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk

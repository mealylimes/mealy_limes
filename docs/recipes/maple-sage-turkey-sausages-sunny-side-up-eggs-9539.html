Maple Sage Turkey Sausages with Sunny-Side Up Eggs

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $1.72/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • sage, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • pure maple syrup
  • 4eggs
  • ½ lbground turkey
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
Cookware
aluminum foil measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place turkey, maple syrup, and spices in a medium bowl and mix with your hands until well combined.
    ½ lb ground turkey
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp sage, dried
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  2. 2
    Using a ¼ cup measure, portion and form the meat mixture into thin patties.
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot add oil and swirl to coat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Place patties in the skillet and cook until nicely browned and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
  5. 5
    Return skillet to medium heat and add more oil. Gently crack eggs into the skillet and cook until whites are set but yolks are still runny, about 3 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    4 eggs
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, toast bread if desired.
    2 slices whole grain bread
  7. 7
    To serve, divide toast, eggs, and breakfast sausage patties between plates. Enjoy!

Meatball Spinach Salad with Tomato, Avocado & Dill-Yogurt Sauce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $5.11/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½avocado
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking dish with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Place beef and spices in a medium bowl; mix with your hands until well combined.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Using a scant ¼-cup measure, form the meat mixture into rounded meatballs and place in the prepared dish.
  4. 4
    Place dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until meatballs are cooked through, 15-18 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ avocado
  6. 6
    Zest lemon into a small bowl. Add yogurt and spices; whisk to combine the yogurt sauce and set aside.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ½ tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    1 pinch salt
  7. 7
    Juice the lemon into a clean medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Add spinach to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    Halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in the skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad, avocado, and meatballs between plates. Top meatballs with yogurt sauce and enjoy!

Mediterranean Chickpea-Rice Wrap with Tzatziki, Feta & Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (37) $2.62/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½English cucumber
  • cumin, ground
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • 1tomato
  • ¼ cupbasmati rice
  • 30 mlItalian salad dressing
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain and rinse chickpeas; place in a small saucepan.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and add to the pan with the chickpeas. Add water and spices; bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ¼ cup basmati rice
    ½ cup water
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook until liquid is fully absorbed, about 15 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 tomato
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  5. 5
    Medium dice tomato; trim and medium dice cucumber. Place both in a medium bowl and set aside.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic. When the chickpea-rice mixture is done, remove from heat, add the garlic and additional salt, then stir to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Add mixed greens, feta, and dressing to the bowl with the tomato and cucumber; toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    2 tbsp italian salad dressing
  8. 8
    To assemble the wraps, place tortillas on a flat surface and spread each with an even layer of tzatziki. Divide warm chickpea-rice mixture between tortillas, top with a small portion of salad (reserving the rest for serving), and roll tightly to close.
    2 large flour tortillas
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce
  9. 9
    Cut wraps in half and divide between plates. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!

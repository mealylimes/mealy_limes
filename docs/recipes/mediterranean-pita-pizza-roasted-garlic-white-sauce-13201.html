Mediterranean Pita Pizza with Roasted Garlic White Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $2.34/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and trim zucchini. Halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    1 medium zucchini squash
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice onion. Add to the baking sheet.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Peel garlic, place in the center of a small square of aluminum foil, and drizzle with oil. Fold foil around the garlic to close the packet. Place garlic packet on the baking sheet with the veggies.
    4 cloves garlic
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Place veggies in the oven and bake until zucchini is tender, 12-15 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and halve tomatoes; transfer to a medium bowl, add oil and spices, then toss to combine. Set aside to marinate.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Coarsely grate mozzarella. Cut cream cheese into small pieces. Set cheeses aside, keeping them separate.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
    ¼ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  9. 9
    When the vegetables are done, remove from the oven and carefully transfer to the bowl with the tomatoes; stir to combine and set aside. (Leave garlic packet on the baking sheet).
  10. 10
    Place pitas on the baking sheet with the garlic packet. Return to oven and toast for 5 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 whole grain pitas
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, preheat a small saucepan over medium heat. Once the pan is hot, add oil, flour, and spices. Whisk constantly for 30 seconds.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Reduce heat to low; whisk in broth and cream cheese until smooth, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  13. 13
    Carefully remove garlic from the packet and roughly chop; add to the pan with the white sauce and stir to combine.
  14. 14
    Top toasted pitas with white sauce, mozzarella, and veggies. Return pitas to the oven and bake until the cheese melts, 2-3 minutes.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pitas between plates and enjoy!

Mediterranean Salad with Crispy Chickpeas, Pita Chips, Olives & Feta

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $5.12/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • onion powder
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse chickpeas, then transfer to baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil, season with spices, and stir to coat; spread out in an even layer.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until chickpeas are golden and crispy, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place pitas on a flat surface. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the pitas in half horizontally to separate the two sides; cut or tear the bread into 2-inch pieces and transfer to another baking sheet.
    2 whole grain pitas
  5. 5
    Drizzle pita with oil and season with salt; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Add baking sheet to the oven and bake until pita chips are crisp, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  7. 7
    While the pita chips are baking, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    1 yellow bell pepper
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  8. 8
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add vinegar, oil, Dijon, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Halve olives and add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  13. 13
    Add crispy chickpeas, pita chips, spinach, and feta to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Mediterranean Spiced Chickpea Patties with Pita & Minty Tahini Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 66% (11) $2.63/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • cumin, ground
  • 1lemon
  • ¼ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • tahini
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher spatula toaster (optional) vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 lemon
    ¼ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse the chickpeas in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  3. 3
    Peel and mince shallot. Peel and mince garlic. Add chickpeas, shallot, and garlic to a medium bowl; mash the beans with a fork or potato masher until beans are broken down.
    ½ shallot
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Peel the carrot, then trim off the ends. Coarsely grate the carrots and add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Add egg, bread crumbs, and spices to the bowl and stir ingredients to combine. Mash the beans with a fork or potato masher once more, until a cohesive mixture is formed.
    1 egg
    2 tbsp panko bread crumbs
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
    ½ tsp coriander
    ½ tsp cumin
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon
  6. 6
    Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Divide chickpea mixture into 8 portions. Using your hands, form each portion into a ball, then flatten into a 2-inch diameter patty.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Place the chickpea patties in the skillet and cook until crispy and golden in color, about 5 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate to cool slightly.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Juice the lemon into the blender.
  10. 10
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard the stems and coarsely chop the leaves; add to the blender.
  11. 11
    Peel the garlic. Add garlic, tahini, and salt to the blender, and blend on high for 1-2 minutes to make a smooth sauce. Thin the sauce with 1-2 tbsp of cold water if necessary.
    1 clove garlic
    ¼ cup tahini
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Toast the pita bread if desired, then slice into quarters.
    2 whole grain pitas
  13. 13
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; slice the cucumber crosswise into thin rounds.
  14. 14
    Halve the grape tomatoes crosswise.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pita bread, chickpea patties, cucumbers and tomatoes between dishes and serve with a side of minty tahini sauce. Enjoy!

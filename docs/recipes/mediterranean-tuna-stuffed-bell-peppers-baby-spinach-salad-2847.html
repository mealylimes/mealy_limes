Mediterranean Tuna Stuffed Bell Peppers with Baby Spinach Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 46% (7) $6.13/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½lemon
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • black pepper
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts salad spinner (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the broiler. Line a baking sheet with foil.
  2. 2
    Wash, halve lengthwise, and seed the bell peppers.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 orange bell pepper
  3. 3
    Place the peppers cut-side down on the baking sheet. Place in the oven and broil until the peppers start to soften and brown, 3-4 minutes.
  4. 4
    Drain the tuna and transfer to a medium bowl.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  5. 5
    Wash and halve the tomatoes; add to the tuna bowl.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the parsley. Shave the leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  7. 7
    Peel and mince the shallot; add to the bowl.
    1 shallot
  8. 8
    Halves the olives and add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  9. 9
    Add the capers, feta, lemon juice, olive oil, Dijon, and pepper to tuna mixture. Mix until well combined.
    1 tbsp capers
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ¼ lemon
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Remove the peppers from the oven and carefully flip them over. Fill the peppers with the tuna mixture. Return to the oven and broil for another 3-4 minutes.
  11. 11
    Wash and dry the spinach.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  12. 12
    Cut the remaining piece of lemon into wedges.
    ¼ lemon
  13. 13
    Place the stuffed peppers on a plate. Arrange a bed of greens on the side; drizzle with olive oil and squeeze lemon juice over top. Enjoy!
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Melty Caprese Steak with Butter Lettuce, Bell Pepper & Basil Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 42% (3) $7.55/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1tomato
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
aluminum foil baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  2. 2
    Add oil, vinegar, and spices to the bowl with the garlic; whisk to combine the dressing. Pour half into a baking dish, then set the bowl aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Cut steak into portions, pat dry with paper towels, and add to the baking dish; turn to coat and set aside to marinate.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 yellow bell pepper
    1 tomato
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, using a clean cutting board, trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add bell pepper, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until fork-tender, 3-4 minutes. Remove to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    When the peppers are done, return the skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, thinly slice tomato and mozzarella crosswise into rounds.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  10. 10
    When the steak is done, remove skillet from heat; top with tomatoes and mozzarella. Cover with a lid and let sit until cheese starts to melt, 2-3 minutes.
  11. 11
    While the cheese melts, pick basil leaves off the stems; roll up crosswise and thinly slice into ribbons. Add half to the bowl with the dressing. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
  12. 12
    Chop or tear lettuce leaves into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl, along with the cooked peppers, and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad and steaks between plates. Top steaks with reserved basil and enjoy!

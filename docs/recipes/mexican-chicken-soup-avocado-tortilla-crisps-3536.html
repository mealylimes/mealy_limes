Mexican Chicken Soup with Avocado & Tortilla Crisps

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (14) $3.57/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 2small corn tortillas
  • cumin, ground
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1avocado
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F and place the rack in the center position.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 avocado
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Juice the lime into a small bowl.
  4. 4
    Stack the tortillas; cut in half, then cut crosswise into ½-inch-wide strips and transfer to a baking sheet.
    2 small corn tortillas
  5. 5
    Drizzle the tortilla strips with olive oil and season with salt; toss to coat then spread out in a single layer.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Place the tortilla strips in the oven and bake until golden brown and crispy, 6 to 8 minutes. Once done, remove from the oven and transfer to a paper towel lined plate.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels; holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, halve the breasts horizontally to form thin fillets. Season the chicken with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Add the chicken to the skillet; cook until golden brown and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate to cool.
  11. 11
    While the chicken cooks, preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  12. 12
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; small dice the onion (cut into ¼-inch pieces).
    ½ medium yellow onion
  13. 13
    Once the saucepan is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  14. 14
    Add the onion to the saucepan; cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 3 to 5 minutes.
  15. 15
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  16. 16
    Add the garlic, cumin, salt, and pepper to the onion; stir until fragrant, 15 to 30 seconds.
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  17. 17
    Add the broth, tomatoes, and lime juice to the saucepan. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 8 to 10 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomaotes
  18. 18
    Using two forks, shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces and add to the soup immediately.
  19. 19
    Halve the avocado lengthwise and twist the halves to separate, then remove the pit with a knife or spoon; scoop out and medium dice the flesh. Transfer to a small bowl.
  20. 20
    Using a knife, shave the cilantro leaves off the stems at a downward angle, working away from your body; discard the stems and finely chop the leaves. Stir the cilantro into the soup.
  21. 21
    To serve, divide the soup between bowls, then top with the avocado and tortilla crisps. Enjoy!

Mexican Pork Stew with Cheesy Tortilla Chips

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $3.86/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ⅛ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • cumin, ground
  • 1 small bunchcilantro
  • onion powder
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1lime
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • 2small corn tortillas
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 small bunch cilantro
    1 green bell pepper
    1 lime
  3. 3
    Slice the pork chops crosswise into thin strips.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    On a clean cutting board, shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; mince the stems and the leaves, keeping them separate. Set the leaves aside in one small bowl and the stems in another.
  6. 6
    Once the saucepan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Add pork strips to the saucepan and cook, tossing occasionally, until the pork is browned and cooked through, 3-4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, seed and medium dice the bell pepper. Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Return the saucepan to medium heat; add more oil, cilantro stems, peppers, and garlic. Cook, stirring often, until peppers soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Combine spices in a small bowl.
    1 tsp paprika
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Add half of the spice mix, pork, tomatoes, corn, and broth to the saucepan and stir to combine. Bring to a boil on high, then reduce heat to medium and simmer until slightly thickened, 8-10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ½ cup frozen corn
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, cut each tortilla into 8 triangles and place on a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil and remaining seasoning mix. Toss to coat, then spread out in a single layer. Place in the oven and bake until golden and crispy, 8-10 minutes.
    2 small corn tortillas
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Zest and juice half of the lime into a small bowl. Cut the remaining lime into wedges.
  14. 14
    Add yogurt and salt to the bowl with the lime zest and stir to combine.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
  15. 15
    Coarsely grate cheese. Remove tortillas from the oven and sprinkle with cheese. Return to the oven and bake until the cheese melts, 2-3 minutes.
    ⅛ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  16. 16
    To serve, divide pork stew between bowls and top with tortilla chips, cilantro leaves, and a dollop of lime yogurt. Enjoy!

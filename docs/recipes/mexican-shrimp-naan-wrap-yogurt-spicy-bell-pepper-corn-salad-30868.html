Mexican Shrimp Naan Wrap with Yogurt & Spicy Bell Pepper-Corn Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (89) $4.81/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1lime
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts parchment paper tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 jalapeño pepper
    1 green bell pepper
    1 red bell pepper
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil and spices, then whisk to combine.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry. Add to the bowl with the spice paste, stir to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Juice lime into another medium bowl. Add oil and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Rinse corn under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
    ½ cup frozen corn
  9. 9
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  10. 10
    Arrange naan bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until lightly toasted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add shrimp and cook until they are opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    Spread yogurt over each naan and top with a small portion of salad and the shrimp. (Reserve remaining salad for serving.)
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  14. 14
    To make wraps easier to eat, place each naan on a square of parchment paper. Use the paper to tightly roll and secure each wrap. Otherwise, eat as is and get a little messy! Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!

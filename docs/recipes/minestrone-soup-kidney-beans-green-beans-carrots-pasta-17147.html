Minestrone Soup with Kidney Beans, Green Beans, Carrots & Pasta

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (11) $2.22/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cankidney beans
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 stick celery
    ½ lb green beans
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrot; trim and small dice celery. Place both in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse kidney beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can kidney beans
  7. 7
    When the veggies are soft, add broth and tomatoes; stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  8. 8
    Once the soup is boiling, add the kidney beans and pasta; stir to combine. Reduce heat to medium, cover, and cook, stirring occasionally until the pasta is partially cooked, about 5 minutes.
    3 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, trim and cut green beans into 1-inch pieces.
  10. 10
    Add green beans to the soup and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta and veggies are tender, 3-5 minutes more.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and enjoy!

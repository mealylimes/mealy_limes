Mini Glazed Meatloaves with Mashed Potatoes & Roasted Carrots

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (259) $2.62/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • Dijon mustard
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • 1 mediumyellow potato
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 3 mediumcarrots
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ketchup
  • brown sugar
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls muffin pan oven mitts potato masher spatula vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Peel and mince onion; place in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Add ground beef, egg, milk, bread crumbs, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the onions. Mix with your hands until well combined.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    1 egg
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place ketchup, Dijon, and brown sugar in a small bowl; whisk to combine.
    1 ½ tbsp ketchup
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp brown sugar
  5. 5
    Divide meatloaf mixture between 6 muffin cups and top with ketchup glaze. Place in the oven and bake until cooked through, 20-25 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash, peel, and medium dice potato. Transfer to a medium saucepan, cover with hot water (from the tap), and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium and cook until potatoes can be easily pierced with a fork, 10-12 minutes.
    1 medium yellow potato
  7. 7
    Wash, trim, and peel carrots. Slice lengthwise into quarters. Place on a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt and pepper. Place in the oven and bake until golden and fork-tender, about 10 minutes.
    3 medium carrots
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    When the potatoes are done, drain, and return to the pan.
  9. 9
    Add milk, butter, salt, and pepper to the potatoes. Mash until smooth.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    To serve, divide the meatloaves, roasted carrots, and mashed potatoes between plates. Enjoy!

Mini Polenta Pizzas with Crispy Prosciutto & Italian Arugula Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 42% (3) $1.25/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 45 mlItalian salad dressing
  • 1 (18 oz) tubepolenta, pre-cooked
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Slice polenta crosswise into ½-inch thick rounds. Transfer to the baking sheet, arranging the slices in a single layer.
    1 (18 oz) tube polenta, pre-cooked
  3. 3
    Place polenta in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) while you prepare the toppings.
  4. 4
    Place tomato sauce, Italian seasoning, and crushed red pepper in a medium bowl; stir to combine and set aside.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Roughly chop or tear prosciutto into bite-sized pieces.
    6 slices prosciutto
  6. 6
    Remove baking sheet from the oven. Spoon sauce over the polenta slices, then top with cheese and prosciutto.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  7. 7
    Return baking sheet to the oven and bake until polenta pizzas are warmed through and cheese is melted, 5-6 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, drain and roughly chop artichokes. Place in a clean medium bowl.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  9. 9
    Roughly chop roasted red peppers and olives. Add both to the bowl with the artichokes.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  10. 10
    Wash and dry arugula (skip if it came pre-washed). Add to the bowl, along with the dressing, and toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    3 tbsp Italian salad dressing
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pizzas and salad between plates. Enjoy!

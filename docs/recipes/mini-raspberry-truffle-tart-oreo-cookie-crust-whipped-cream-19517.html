Mini Raspberry Truffle Tart with OREO Cookie Crust & Whipped Cream

Vegetarian Packaged
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $0.92/serving
Ingredients
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • brown sugar
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • vanilla extract
  • ¼ cupdark chocolate chips
  • 7OREO chocolate sandwich cookies
Cookware
colander grater or food processor measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls muffin pan plastic wrap silicone spatula small saucepan whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill. (If space is limited, place in the refrigerator.)
  2. 2
    Cut two large squares of plastic wrap. Line two cups of a muffin pan with the plastic wrap, making sure the plastic is flush against the bottom and sides of each cup, and overhangs the edges by 2-3 inches. Set aside.
  3. 3
    Using your hands, break Oreos in half and place in the base of a food processor; process to create fine cookie crumbs.
    7 OREO chocolate sandwich cookies
  4. 4
    Place butter in a small saucepan and melt over medium heat. Once melted, drizzle over the cookie crumbs, making sure to scrape all of the butter into the food processor. Process once more to thoroughly combine. (Set pan aside for later use.)
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Divide cookie crumb mixture between the prepared muffin cups (about a ¼-cup per tart); use your fingers to press the crumbs into the bottom and around the sides of each cup, forming a crust. Place in the refrigerator to help the crusts firm up.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry raspberries; set aside.
    ½ (6 oz) pkg raspberries
  7. 7
    Place cream, sugar, additional butter, and vanilla in the reserved pan. Bring just to a boil over medium heat.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy whipping cream
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  8. 8
    Once the cream has come to a boil, remove from heat, add the chocolate, and whisk until the ganache is smooth and shiny.
    ¼ cup dark chocolate chips
  9. 9
    Pour half of the ganache into the prepared cookie crusts, filling each cup halfway. Press 3 raspberries into each cup, then cover with remaining ganache. Return tarts to the refrigerator until ganache sets up, about 3 hours. (Place remaining raspberries in the refrigerator until ready to serve.)
  10. 10
    Once the truffle tarts are set, remove from the refrigerator. Using both hands, hold onto the corners of the plastic wrap to gently lift each tart out of the muffin pan; peel plastic wrap away from the tarts and divide between plates.
  11. 11
    Remove bowl from the freezer and pour cold cream into the bowl. Use a whisk to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, about 1 minute. (For medium-soft peaks, when you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.)
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy whipping cream
  12. 12
    Top each tart with remaining raspberries and serve with a dollop of whipped cream on the side. Enjoy!

Mini Warm Peach Crostata with Cream Cheese Pastry

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $0.84/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2peaches
  • granulated sugar
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper plastic wrap rolling pin spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Combine flour and salt in a medium bowl.
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate cold butter and cream cheese into the bowl with the flour. Using two hands, combine the ingredients by rubbing the mixture between your thumbs and fingers until it looks crumbly.
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  3. 3
    Add cold water to the bowl and continue to mix with your hands to combine the dough.
    1 tbsp water
  4. 4
    Divide the dough into 2 equal portions; pat each portion into a ½-inch thick disk. Wrap each disk tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate until firm, about 1 hour (or overnight).
  5. 5
    Once the dough has chilled, preheat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper.
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, and quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist the quarters to separate. Remove and discard pits. Cut each quarter lengthwise into ¼-inch thick slices and transfer to a medium bowl.
    2 peaches
  7. 7
    Add flour, sugar, and cinnamon to the bowl with the peaches and gently toss to coat. Set aside.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    2 tbsp granulated sugar
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
  8. 8
    Sprinkle a small amount of flour over a flat working surface. Unwrap one piece of dough (leaving remaining dough in the refrigerator), and place on top of the floured work surface; sprinkle dough with a little more flour.
    1 ½ tsp all-purpose flour
  9. 9
    Using a rolling pin, work the dough into a circle that is about ⅛-inch thick by 7-inches in diameter. Transfer dough to the baking sheet. Sprinkle your work surface with a little more flour and repeat with remaining dough.
    1 ½ tsp all-purpose flour
  10. 10
    Arrange peaches in the center of each dough circle (making sure to leave a 1½-inch border of dough around the peaches). Drizzle any leftover juices over the peaches.
  11. 11
    Gently fold crust edges up toward the center, overlapping folds slightly, and pinch each fold to enclose the fruit.
  12. 12
    Place crostatas in the oven and bake, rotating the baking sheet halfway through, until filling is bubbly and the pastry is golden brown, 25-30 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool slightly.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide warm crostatas between plates and enjoy!

Miso Butter Pasta with Deli Turkey, Roast Squash, Mushrooms & Walnuts

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $0.40/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 5 ozspaghetti pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 8 ozoven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  • 15 mlmiso paste, red
  • ⅛ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 2 cupsfrozen butternut squash, pieces
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Place butternut squash and mushrooms on a baking sheet pan. Season with salt and pepper, then spread out in an even layer.
    2 cup frozen butternut squash, pieces
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Small dice butter; sprinkle half over the veggies. (Place remaining butter in a small bowl and set aside.)
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring veggies halfway through, until fork-tender, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve a ¼-cup cooking water before draining and set aside.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz spaghetti pasta
  6. 6
    While the pasta is cooking, peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the remaining butter along with miso paste, additional salt, and pepper. Mash with the back of a spoon to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp miso paste, red
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Roughly chop walnuts. Add to the bowl with the miso butter and set aside.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  8. 8
    Stack turkey slices one on top of another, then medium dice.
    8 oz oven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  9. 9
    When the pasta is done, drain and return to the pan. Add roasted veggies, walnuts, miso butter, turkey, reserved pasta water, and Parmesan, then stir to combine.
    ⅛ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

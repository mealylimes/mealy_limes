Mixed Green Salad with Pork Sausage, Apple, Almonds & Italian Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (12) $3.49/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupalmonds
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½lemon
  • 1Gala apple
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    1 Gala apple
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add almonds and cook, stirring often, until lightly toasted and fragrant, 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside to cool. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
    ¼ cup almonds
  4. 4
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add to the bowl.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  8. 8
    When the sausages are almost done, add mixed greens and toasted almonds to the bowl, then toss to combine the salad.
  9. 9
    Slice sausages crosswise into thin rounds.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls, then top with sausage. Enjoy!

"Moo Shu" Shrimp Wraps with Egg, Mushroom, Cabbage & Green Onion

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (10) $3.21/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1egg
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • hoisin sauce
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until opaque, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place egg in a small bowl and whisk until smooth.
    1 egg
  5. 5
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  6. 6
    Add egg to the skillet and scramble using a combination of stirring and folding, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan. Cook until no longer runny; add to the plate with the shrimp and re-cover with the foil. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  8. 8
    Wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add green onion, ginger, and garlic powder; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
  11. 11
    Add mushrooms, cabbage, salt, and pepper to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender-crisp, 3-4 minutes more.
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Return shrimp and egg to the skillet, then add hoisin sauce; stir to combine. Continue to cook the "moo shu" filling until heated through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  13. 13
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  14. 14
    Divide tortillas and "moo shu" filling between plates. Serve with additional hoisin sauce on the side for dipping and enjoy!
    2 tbsp hoisin sauce

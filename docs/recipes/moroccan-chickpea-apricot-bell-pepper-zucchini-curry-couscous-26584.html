Moroccan Chickpea, Apricot, Bell Pepper & Zucchini Curry with Couscous

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (9) $4.71/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 8dried apricots
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • brown sugar
  • 2 tbsptomato paste
  • garam masala
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 red bell pepper
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and slice carrot crosswise into ¼-inch thick rounds. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and thinly slice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the carrot.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies start to soften, about 5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp chili powder
    1 ½ tsp garam masala
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Place in the reserved bowl.
  9. 9
    Roughly chop apricots and add to the bowl with the zucchini.
    8 dried apricots
  10. 10
    Drain and rinse chickpeas in a colander.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  11. 11
    Once the veggies start to soften, add zucchini, apricots, chickpeas, tomatoes, broth, tomato paste, and sugar to the skillet. Stir to combine the curry and bring to a boil over high heat.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tbsp tomato paste
    2 tsp brown sugar
  12. 12
    Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low, and cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies and chickpeas are tender, 10-15 minutes.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  14. 14
    When the curry is done, remove from heat, add half of the parsley, and stir to combine. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  15. 15
    To serve, divide couscous and curry between plates or bowls. Top with reserved parsley and pine nuts. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup pine nuts

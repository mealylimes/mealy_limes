Moroccan Grilled Meatballs with Yogurt Sauce & Beet Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (12) $4.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 mediumbeets
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ¾ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1shallot
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½orange
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • brown sugar
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium beets
    ½ lemon
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ orange
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and large dice beets; place in a small saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Divide between a small and medium bowl. Set the medium bowl aside for the meatballs.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Juice lemon into the small bowl with the garlic. Add yogurt, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the yogurt sauce. Set aside.
    ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook until beets are fork-tender, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat, drain, and run under cold water to cool; set aside to drain further.
  6. 6
    While the beets are cooking, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the medium bowl with the garlic. (Set remaining parsley aside for later use.)
  7. 7
    Peel and mince ginger. Add to the bowl along with beef, bread crumbs, egg, sugar, and spices. Using your hands, mix well to combine. Using a rounded tablespoon measure, form the beef mixture into rounded meatballs. Place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
  8. 8
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add meatballs and grill, turning occasionally with a fork or tongs, until golden brown and cooked through, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, zest and juice orange into a clean medium bowl. Add reserved parsley, oil, cumin, and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl, along with cooled beets, and stir to combine the salad.
    1 shallot
  13. 13
    To serve, divide yogurt sauce and salad between plates; top yogurt with meatballs. Enjoy!

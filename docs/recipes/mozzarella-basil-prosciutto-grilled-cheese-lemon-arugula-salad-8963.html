Mozzarella, Basil & Prosciutto Grilled Cheese with Lemon Arugula Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 58% (6) $3.13/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • salt
  • honey
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • 1lemon
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    1 lemon
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Thinly slice the mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  3. 3
    Pick basil leaves off the stems.
  4. 4
    Preheat a grill pan or nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Place the bread slices on a flat surface. Evenly top half of the slices with the prosciutto, basil leaves, and sliced mozzarella. Cover the sandwiches with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
    6 slices prosciutto
  6. 6
    Coat the grill pan or skillet with butter.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  7. 7
    Place the sandwiches in the pan and cook until the bottom is golden brown, 2-4 minutes.
  8. 8
    Remove the sandwiches to a plate and add more butter to the pan; return the sandwiches to the pan, browned-side-up, and cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese is melted, 2-4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a cutting board.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    While the sandwiches toast, juice the lemon into a large bowl. Whisk in the vinegar, oil, salt, and honey.
    2 tbsp white wine vinegar
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 tsp honey
  10. 10
    Use a vegetable peeler to shave the Parmesan into strips.
    ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Add the arugula and shaved Parmesan to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine.
  12. 12
    Cut the sandwiches in half on the diagonal.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide the salad and sandwiches between plates. Enjoy!

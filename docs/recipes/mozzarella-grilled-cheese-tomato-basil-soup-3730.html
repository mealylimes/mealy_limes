Mozzarella Grilled Cheese with Tomato-Basil Soup

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (13) $2.28/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • butter, unsalted
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
can opener chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; small dice the onion (cut into ¼-inch pieces) and transfer to a small bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add to the onion.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Once the saucepan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Add the onion and garlic to the saucepan; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until the onion is softened, 4 to 6 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Pick the basil leaves off the stems; discard the stems and slice the leaves into a chiffonade by stacking them and rolling tightly into a cigar, then slicing crosswise into thin ribbons. Transfer the basil to a small bowl.
  7. 7
    Coarsely grate 1 cup of mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  8. 8
    Add the diced tomatoes, broth, and basil to the saucepan; season with additional salt and pepper. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 8 to 10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Add butter to a nonstick skillet and heat over medium-low heat.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    Place the bread slices on a clean, dry work surface. Divide the cheese between 2 of the bread slices, then top with the remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  11. 11
    Once melted, spread the butter around the skillet, then season the bottom of the skillet with salt.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Place the sandwiches in the skillet and cook until golden brown and crispy on the first side, 3 to 4 minutes. Then, add more butter and carefully flip the sandwiches; cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese has melted, 3 to 4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a cutting board.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  13. 13
    Using a hand blender, purée the soup until smooth. Alternatively, use a potato masher to mash the tomatoes (the soup will be chunkier).
  14. 14
    Cut the grilled cheese sandwiches in half diagonally.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide the soup between small bowls and serve with the grilled cheese on the side. Enjoy!

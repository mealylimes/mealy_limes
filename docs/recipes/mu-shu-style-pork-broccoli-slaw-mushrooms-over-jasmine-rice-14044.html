Mu Shu-Style Pork with Broccoli Slaw & Mushrooms over Jasmine Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $4.03/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • cloves, ground
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms; transfer to a medium bowl and set aside.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board; thinly slice crosswise into bite-sized strips and season with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork strips and stir-fry until browned and cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic; place in a small bowl along with soy sauce, cornstarch, peanut butter, chili-garlic sauce, oil, vinegar, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    2 tsp natural peanut butter
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
    1 pinch clove, ground
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    When the pork is done, use a slotted spoon to transfer the strips to a plate and set aside.
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until they start to soften, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    Add broccoli slaw and sauce to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are tender-crisp and sauce thickens, 2-3 minutes.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  11. 11
    Return pork to the skillet, along with any accumulated juices; stir to combine and cook until heated through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice and pork stir-fry between plates. Enjoy!

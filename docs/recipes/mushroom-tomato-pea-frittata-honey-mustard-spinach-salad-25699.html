Mushroom, Tomato & Pea Frittata with Honey Mustard Spinach Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $2.28/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ⅛ cupdried cranberries
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 45 mlhoney mustard dressing
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms soften, about 5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place eggs, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine and set aside.
    6 eggs
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  5. 5
    When the mushrooms are soft, add tomatoes and peas to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes soften and peas are warmed through, 1-2 minutes more.
    1 cup frozen peas
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. 7
    Reduce heat to medium-low and pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, 3-5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Sprinkle cheese over the frittata. Place skillet in the oven and broil until top is set and cheese is melted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  10. 10
    To serve, cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Add spinach to the plate, drizzle with dressing, and top with cranberries. Enjoy!
    3 tbsp honey mustard dressing
    ⅛ cup dried cranberries

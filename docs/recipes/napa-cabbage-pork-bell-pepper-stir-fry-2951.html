Napa Cabbage, Pork & Bell Pepper Stir Fry

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $3.53/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • soy sauce
  • cornstarch
  • rice vinegar
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1red bell pepper
  • toasted sesame oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small bowl, combine and whisk together water, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and cornstarch.
    1 tbsp water
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp cornstarch
  2. 2
    Thinly slice the pork chops crosswise into strips; transfer to a medium bowl.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  3. 3
    Pour ½ of the sauce over the pork; toss to coat and set aside.
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the cutting board, knife, and fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    1 red bell pepper
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  5. 5
    Halve the cabbage lengthwise and cut out the core; chop crosswise into bite-sized pieces (about 1-inch-wide). Transfer to a large bowl.
  6. 6
    Cut the bell pepper into 4 wedges lengthwise, then remove and discard the seeds. Thinly slice into strips lengthwise and add to the bowl of cabbage.
  7. 7
    Trim and discard the ends of the green onions; chop the onions into ¼-inch pieces. Separate the white parts from the greens and transfer to two small bowls.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince or grate the ginger; add to the white parts of the green onions.
  10. 10
    Peel and mince the garlic; add to the onions and ginger.
    2 cloves garlic
  11. 11
    Add coconut oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  12. 12
    Place the pork strips in the skillet; cook until browned, 1 to 2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  13. 13
    Once the pork has been transferred, add more coconut oil to the skillet.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  14. 14
    Add the ginger, garlic, and white parts of green onions; cook until fragrant, 15 to 30 seconds.
  15. 15
    Add the bell pepper and cabbage to the skillet. Cook, stirring regularly, until the vegetables are tender-crisp and lightly browned, 3 to 4 minutes.
  16. 16
    Add the pork (along with any juices), remaining sauce, and ½ of the remaining green onions (save the rest for garnishing) to the skillet; stir to combine and cook until the sauce is slightly thickened, 1 to 2 minutes.
  17. 17
    Divide the stir fry between bowls and sprinkle with the remaining green onions. Enjoy!

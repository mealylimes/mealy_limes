New York Strip Steak with Bell Pepper & Creamed Spinach

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $8.00/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (5 oz) pkgsbaby spinach
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater large pot measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. 2
    While the water is heating, finely grate Parmesan; cut cream cheese into small pieces. Place both in a medium bowl and set aside.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    ¼ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Wash the spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    2 (5 oz) pkgs baby spinach
  5. 5
    Once the water in the pot is boiling, uncover, add salt and spinach, and stir until wilted, about 30 seconds. Drain in a colander and rinse with cold water to cool. (Reserve pot for next step.)
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Return pot to medium heat. Once the pot is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, salt, and pepper to the pot; cook, stirring occasionally, until onion is soft, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using your hands, squeeze as much water from the spinach as you can. Add spinach to the bowl with the Parmesan and cream cheese.
  8. 8
    Once the onion is soft, add Parmesan, cream cheese, spinach, and milk to the pot. Cook, stirring frequently, until cheeses melt and combine, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak to the skillet and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, wash and dry bell pepper; trim, seed, and slice into thin strips.
    1 red bell pepper
  13. 13
    Return skillet to medium heat; add more butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  14. 14
    While the pepper is cooking, reheat the spinach, if necessary. (Return the pot to low heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 2-3 minutes.)
  15. 15
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  16. 16
    Divide steak between plates and top with peppers; serve with spinach on the side. Enjoy!

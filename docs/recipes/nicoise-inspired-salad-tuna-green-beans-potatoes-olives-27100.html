"Niçoise" Inspired Salad with Tuna, Green Beans, Potatoes & Olives

Fish & Seafood Snacks
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $2.95/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ¼ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ¼ lbgreen beans
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ lbnew potatoes
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb new potatoes
    ¼ lb green beans
    ¼ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Quarter potatoes. Once the water in the saucepan is boiling, add potatoes and salt. Reduce heat to medium and simmer until starting to soften, about 8 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    While the potatoes are cooking, trim and cut green beans crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Once the potatoes start to soften, add green beans to the pan and continue to cook until veggies are tender, 2-3 minutes more. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Set aside.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Drain tuna and add to the bowl; break into chunks with a fork.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  8. 8
    Halve olives and add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  9. 9
    Add cooled potatoes and green beans to the bowl, then stir to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

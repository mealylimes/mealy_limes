NY Steak with Herbed Polenta Fries & Tomato Bell Pepper-Arugula Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $5.51/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 30 mlItalian salad dressing
  • 1 (18 oz) tubepolenta, pre-cooked
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 475°F.
  2. 2
    Halve polenta crosswise, then cut each half lengthwise into ½-inch thick "fries." Transfer to a baking sheet pan, spreading the fries out in a single layer.
    1 (18 oz) tube polenta, pre-cooked
  3. 3
    Drizzle fries with oil and season with spices; carefully toss to coat, then spread out on the baking sheet so that none of the fries are touching.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping fries halfway through, until golden brown and slightly crisp on the outside, 30-35 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    When the fries have about 10 minutes left of cooking, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  9. 9
    Halve tomatoes and place in a medium bowl.
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and halve bell pepper crosswise, then slice into thin strips; add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  11. 11
    Add arugula to the bowl, along with the dressing, and toss to combine the salad; set aside.
    2 tbsp Italian salad dressing
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide steak, polenta fries, and salad between plates. Enjoy!

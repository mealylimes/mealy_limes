NY Strip Steak with Shallot-Red Wine Vinegar Sauce & Swiss Chard

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $4.25/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½lemon
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • brown sugar
  • 1 bunchSwiss chard
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat steak dry with paper towels and cut into portions. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steaks and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate, cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry chard; fold leaves in half lengthwise and, using a clean cutting board, slice off the stems. Cut leaves into bite-sized pieces and thinly slice the stems, keeping them separate. Place leaves in a medium bowl, reserving stems for the next step.
    1 bunch Swiss chard
  5. 5
    Preheat another skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chard stems, salt, and pepper to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Add chard leaves to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the leaves to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, and juice lemon into the skillet with the chard and stir to combine. Cover and remove from heat.
    ½ lemon
  8. 8
    Peel and slice shallot crosswise into thin rounds.
    1 shallot
  9. 9
    Return reserved skillet (with steak drippings) to medium heat, add more butter, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shallot and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly browned, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    When the shallots are soft, add water, vinegar, and sugar to the skillet. Bring to a simmer and cook until sauce is reduced by half, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp water
    2 tbsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
  11. 11
    To serve, divide steaks and lemony chard between plates. Spoon shallot sauce over steaks and enjoy!

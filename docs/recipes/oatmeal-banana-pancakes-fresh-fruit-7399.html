Oatmeal Banana Pancakes with Fresh Fruit

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (11) $1.41/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • pure maple syrup
  • ¼ (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • butter, unsalted
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1kiwifruit
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 2bananas
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet spatula vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 kiwifruit
    ¼ (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  2. 2
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Add the bananas, oats, milk, and cinnamon to the blender. Purée for 30 seconds, until smooth.
    2 bananas
    1 ⅓ cup oats, quick cooking
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Using a ¼-cup measure, pour the batter into the skillet. Cook for 1-2 minutes on each side, until browned. Work in batches if necessary.
  6. 6
    Slice the strawberries in half. Peel and slice the kiwi.
  7. 7
    Divide the pancakes, strawberries, kiwi, and blueberries between plates. Top with maple syrup and enjoy!
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup

Oatmeal with Raspberries, Apricots, Cashews & Spiced Maple Syrup

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (10) $1.44/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ¼ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • 8dried apricots
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • oats, quick cooking
  • cardamom, ground
  • cloves, ground
  • water
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place water, maple syrup, and salt in a small saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat.
    14 fl oz (1 ¾ cups) water
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Once boiling, stir in oats. Cover and let simmer, on low, 3-5 minutes, or until oats reach desired consistency. Remove from heat.
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry raspberries. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ (6 oz) pkg raspberries
  4. 4
    Roughly chop apricots and cashews. Add both to the bowl with the raspberries, stir to combine, and set aside.
    8 dried apricots
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  5. 5
    Place additional maple syrup and spices in another small bowl. Stir to combine.
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp cardamom, ground
    1 pinch cloves, ground
  6. 6
    To serve, divide oatmeal between bowls, top with fruit and nuts, then drizzle with spiced maple syrup. Enjoy!

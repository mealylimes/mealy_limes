Olive Oil Cake with Oranges, Dates, Whipped Cream & Pistachios

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $1.43/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1egg
  • 2oranges
  • ⅛ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • brown sugar
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • baking powder
  • cardamom, ground
  • ⅛ cupmedjool dates, pitted
  • water
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls muffin pan oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Coat 2 muffin cups with oil and set aside.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill. (If space is limited, place in the refrigerator.)
  3. 3
    Wash and dry oranges. Zest half of one orange into a small bowl (reserve oranges for later use). Add flour, sugar, oil, water, egg, baking powder, and spices, then whisk to combine the batter.
    2 oranges
    ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ fl oz water
    1 egg
    ¼ tsp baking powder
    ⅛ tsp cardamom, ground
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Divide batter between the muffin cups. Place in the oven and bake until a knife or skewer inserted in the center comes out clean, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, cut off skin and white pith from the oranges; slice between the membranes to release segments. Place in another small bowl.
  6. 6
    Roughly chop dates. Add to the bowl with the oranges, stir to combine, and set aside.
    ⅛ cup medjool dates, pitted
  7. 7
    Roughly chop pistachios.
    ⅛ cup pistachios, unsalted
  8. 8
    Remove bowl from the freezer. Pour cold cream into the bowl; use a whisk to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, about 2 minutes. (For medium-soft peaks, when you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.)
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
  9. 9
    To serve, divide oranges, dates, and cakes between bowls; top with whipped cream and pistachios. Enjoy!

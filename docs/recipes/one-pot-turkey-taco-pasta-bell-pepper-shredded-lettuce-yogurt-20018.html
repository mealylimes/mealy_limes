One-Pot Turkey Taco Pasta with Bell Pepper, Shredded Lettuce & Yogurt

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (31) $2.60/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1green bell pepper
  • 1 cupsalsa
  • 45 mltaco seasoning
  • 1 heartromaine hearts
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 green bell pepper
    1 heart romaine hearts
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; place in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the bell pepper.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add veggies, turkey, taco seasoning, and salt. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    3 tbsp taco seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, cut lettuce crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place in the reserved bowl and set aside for serving.
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Set aside.
  8. 8
    When the turkey is done, add water, salsa, and pasta to the pan; stir to combine, cover, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until pasta is tender and the liquid is mostly absorbed, 8-10 minutes.
    1 cup water
    1 cup salsa
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  9. 9
    When the pasta is done, add cilantro, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide taco pasta between plates or bowls; top with shredded lettuce and yogurt. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

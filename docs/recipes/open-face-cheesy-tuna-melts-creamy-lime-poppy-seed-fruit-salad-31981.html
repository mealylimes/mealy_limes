Open-Face Cheesy Tuna Melts with Creamy Lime-Poppy Seed Fruit Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (51) $4.89/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • Dijon mustard
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½shallot
  • 1 stickcelery
  • ½lime
  • mayonnaise
  • brown sugar
  • 1Gala apple
  • poppy seeds
  • 2English muffins
  • 2plums
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Split muffins and arrange on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven. (Keep oven on for later use.)
    2 English muffins
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 stick celery
    ½ lime
    1 Gala apple
    2 plums
    ½ (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  4. 4
    Trim and small dice celery. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the celery.
    ½ shallot
  6. 6
    Drain tuna and flake with a fork. Add to the bowl along with mayo, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the tuna salad and set aside.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Thinly slice cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Arrange muffin halves cut-side up, then top with tuna salad and cheese. Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until cheese is melted and bubbly, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a clean medium bowl; add yogurt, poppy seeds, and sugar. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp poppy seeds
    1 tsp brown sugar
  10. 10
    Quarter, core, and medium dice apple; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Halve plums lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Medium dice and add to the bowl along with the blueberries. Stir to combine the fruit salad.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide tuna melts and fruit salad between plates. Enjoy!

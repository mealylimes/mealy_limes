Open-Faced Curried Tofu Salad Sandwiches with Lettuce & Cucumber

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (7) $2.84/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • onion powder
  • ⅛ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • rice vinegar
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • vegan mayonnaise
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 stick celery
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  3. 3
    Place mayo, nutritional yeast, Dijon, vinegar, and spices in a medium bowl; stir to combine the creamy dressing.
    ¼ cup vegan mayonnaise
    ⅛ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp curry powder
    ¾ tsp salt
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Crumble tofu with your fingers into the bowl with the dressing; using a fork, mash to combine the tofu mixture.
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot; add to the bowl with the tofu mixture.
  6. 6
    Trim and small dice celery; add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl. Stir to combine the tofu salad and set aside.
  8. 8
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a small bowl along with oil and salt. Stir to combine the garlic oil.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Arrange bread on the baking sheet and drizzle with garlic oil. Place in the oven and broil until golden brown and toasted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds.
  12. 12
    Separate lettuce into leaves.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide toast between plates; top with lettuce, tofu salad, and cucumber slices. Season cucumber with salt and pepper. Enjoy!
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper

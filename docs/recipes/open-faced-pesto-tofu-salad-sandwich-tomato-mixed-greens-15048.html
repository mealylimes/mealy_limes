Open-Faced Pesto Tofu Salad Sandwich with Tomato & Mixed Greens

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (5) $3.89/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • onion powder
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • ⅛ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • 2tomatoes
  • brown sugar
  • vegan mayonnaise
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    2 tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Place mayo, nutritional yeast, and spices in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the creamy dressing.
    ¼ cup vegan mayonnaise
    ⅛ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Finely chop pine nuts and add to the bowl with the creamy dressing.
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
  5. 5
    Pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Crumble tofu with your fingers into the bowl; using a fork, mash to combine the tofu salad. Set aside.
  7. 7
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  8. 8
    Slice tomatoes into rounds.
  9. 9
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet. Place in the oven and broil until bread is golden brown and toasted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, sugar, salt, and pepper in another medium bowl. Whisk to combine the vinaigrette.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    1 pinch brown sugar
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  11. 11
    Add mixed greens to the bowl with the vinaigrette and toss to coat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide toast between plates; top with dressed greens, tofu salad, and tomato slices. Season tomatoes with salt and pepper. Enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper

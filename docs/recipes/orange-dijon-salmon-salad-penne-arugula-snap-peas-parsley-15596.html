Orange-Dijon Salmon Salad with Penne, Arugula, Snap Peas & Parsley

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $5.36/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½shallot
  • 1orange
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil and set aside.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 orange
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Transfer half to a small bowl and half to a medium bowl; set the medium bowl aside.
  4. 4
    Zest orange into the small bowl with the parsley (set orange aside for later use), then add Dijon, oil, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the herb paste.
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Pat salmon dry with paper towels and place on the baking sheet; spread herb paste evenly over top.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until the salmon is opaque and flakes easily with a fork, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool slightly.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  8. 8
    While the pasta is cooking, peel and mince garlic; add to the medium bowl with the remaining parsley.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Juice reserved orange into the bowl, then add oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice shallot into half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing and stir to combine.
    ½ shallot
  11. 11
    Thinly slice snap peas crosswise and add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and rinse under cold water to cool. Add to the bowl with the dressing along with the arugula; toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    When the salmon is cool enough to handle, break into large chunks with your fingers.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and top with salmon. Enjoy!

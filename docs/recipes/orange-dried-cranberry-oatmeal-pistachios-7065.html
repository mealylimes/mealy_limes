Orange & Dried Cranberry Oatmeal with Pistachios

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $1.21/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupdried cranberries
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1orange
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and juice orange into a medium saucepan.
    1 orange
  2. 2
    Add salt and water to the saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, stir in oats, cranberries, cinnamon, and maple syrup; reduce heat to medium-low. Cover and let simmer for 3-5 minutes or until oats reach desired consistency.
    1 pinch salt
    1 ¾ cup water
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
    ½ cup dried cranberries
    ½ tsp cinnamon
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
  3. 3
    Roughly chop the pistachios.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
  4. 4
    To serve, divide oats between bowls and top with pistachios and milk. Enjoy!
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk

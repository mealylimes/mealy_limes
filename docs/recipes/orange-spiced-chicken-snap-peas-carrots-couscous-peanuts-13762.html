Orange-Spiced Chicken with Snap Peas, Carrots, Couscous & Peanuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (14) $3.40/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ⅛ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • ½orange
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • ketchup
  • brown sugar
  • cloves, ground
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Remove from heat, add couscous, and stir; cover and let stand for 10 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ orange
    2 medium carrots
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken to the skillet; cook, turning once, until golden brown on each side, 6-7 minutes (chicken will not be cooked through all the way).
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, zest and juice orange into a small bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the orange juice.
    2 cloves garlic
  8. 8
    Add water, sugar, soy sauce, ketchup, vinegar, and spices to the bowl; whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ¼ cup water
    1 ½ tbsp brown sugar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp ketchup
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp clove, ground
  9. 9
    Peel, trim, and thinly slice carrots. Add carrots and snap peas to the skillet with the chicken.
  10. 10
    Add sauce to the skillet and bring to a boil over high heat (reserve bowl for later use). Reduce heat and simmer, turning thighs occasionally, until chicken is cooked through and veggies are tender, about 10 minutes.
  11. 11
    While the chicken cooks, roughly chop peanuts.
    ⅛ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  12. 12
    Trim green onions and cut into ¼-inch pieces.
  13. 13
    In the reserved bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth. Add to the skillet in a steady stream, while stirring constantly; continue to simmer, until sauce thickens, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp water
    2 tsp cornstarch
  14. 14
    To serve, divide chicken-vegetable sauté and couscous between plates or bowls. Top with peanuts and green onions. Enjoy!

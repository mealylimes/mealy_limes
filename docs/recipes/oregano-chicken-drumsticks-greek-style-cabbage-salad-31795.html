Oregano Chicken Drumsticks with Greek-Style Cabbage Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (53) $2.79/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1tomato
  • ½green bell pepper
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and place in a medium bowl. Drizzle with oil, season with spices, and toss to coat. Transfer chicken to the baking sheet and spread it out in an even layer.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning halfway, until chicken is golden and cooked through, 25-30 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    1 tomato
    ½ green bell pepper
  5. 5
    Peel and mince shallot and garlic. Place both in a clean medium bowl, along with oil, vinegar, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Small dice tomato. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim, seed, and halve bell pepper crosswise, then slice into thin strips. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Drain and roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad. Set aside.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  10. 10
    To serve, divide drumsticks and salad between plates. Enjoy!

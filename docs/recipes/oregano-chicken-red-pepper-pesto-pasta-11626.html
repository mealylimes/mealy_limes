Oregano Chicken with Red Pepper Pesto Pasta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (83) $4.69/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 3sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) hand or regular blender large pot measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, seed, and slice bell pepper into thin strips.
    1 red bell pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add peppers and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 5-6 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ½ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the peppers are done, transfer to a blender and set aside to cool.
  7. 7
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat. Add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Place chicken in the skillet and cook until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems. Transfer half the basil to the blender with the peppers. Using a clean cutting board, roll up remaining basil leaves and thinly slice into ribbons; set aside for garnish.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  10. 10
    Peel garlic and add to the blender.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Add pinenuts, sun-dried tomatoes, oil, salt, and crushed red pepper to the blender. Blend on high, adding water 1 tbsp at a time, if necessary, to form a semi-smooth pesto.
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
    3 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  12. 12
    Drain pasta and return to the pot. Add pesto, stir to combine, and place over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring frequently, until heated through, 1-2 minutes.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, slice chicken into strips.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and top with chicken. Garnish with reserved basil and enjoy!

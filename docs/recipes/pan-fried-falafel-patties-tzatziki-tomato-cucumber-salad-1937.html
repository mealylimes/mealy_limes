Pan-Fried Falafel Patties with Tzatziki & Tomato-Cucumber Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (22) $2.96/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • coriander, ground
  • cumin, ground
  • black pepper
  • ½lemon
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • 1English cucumber
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 English cucumber
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse the chickpeas; place on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry. Transfer to a large bowl and, using a potato masher or fork, mash into a paste.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  3. 3
    Using a knife, shave the parsley leaves off the stems at a downward angle, working away from your body; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add ½ of the parsley to the bowl with the chickpeas and transfer the other ½ to a medium salad bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add ½ of the garlic to the bowl with the chickpeas and transfer the other ½ to a small bowl.
    3 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Juice the lemon; add ½ of the juice to the bowl with the chickpeas and add the other ½ to the small bowl with the garlic.
  6. 6
    Add olive oil to a nonstick skillet and heat over medium-high heat.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    To the bowl of chickpeas, add olive oil, flour, coriander, cumin, salt, and pepper; stir until well combined. Form the mixture into 6 patties and place on a plate.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
    1 tsp coriander
    1 tsp cumin
    ¾ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add the patties and cook until golden brown and crispy, 3 to 5 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  9. 9
    Halve the grape tomatoes and add to the salad bowl with the parsley.
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; coarsely grate ¼ of the cucumber and add to the bowl with the garlic and lemon juice, then quarter the remaining ¾ of the cucumber lengthwise and cut crosswise into ¼-inch-thick pieces. Add to the salad bowl.
  11. 11
    Add yogurt to the bowl with the garlic, lemon juice, and cucumber; season with salt and pepper and mix together.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    To the salad bowl, add olive oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper; toss to combine.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, place the patties on a plate, add the salad on the side, and serve with tzatziki sauce. Enjoy!

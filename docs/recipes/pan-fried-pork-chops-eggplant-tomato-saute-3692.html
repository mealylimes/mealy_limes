Pan-Fried Pork Chops with Eggplant & Tomato Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (8) $3.79/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • 1eggplant
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • virgin coconut oil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • balsamic vinegar
Cookware
aluminum foil can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet stainless steel sauté pan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium eggplant
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard the ends of the eggplant; medium dice the eggplant (cut into ½-inch cubes) and transfer to a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  6. 6
    Place the pork chops in the skillet and sear until they develop a nice crust, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Then, reduce the heat to medium and cook the chops, flipping occasionally, until they are cooked through (test using a thermometer or cut into a chop with a knife), another 3 to 5 minutes. Once done, transfer the chops to a plate and cover with foil to keep warm.
  7. 7
    Preheat a sauté pan over medium heat.
  8. 8
    While the pan heats up, trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add olive oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Add the garlic to the pan; stir until fragrant, 15 to 30 seconds.
  11. 11
    Add the eggplant to the pan and season with salt and pepper; increase the heat to medium-high. Cook, stirring frequently, until just tender, 5 to 7 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    While the eggplant cooks, pick the basil leaves off the stems; discard the stems and slice the leaves into a chiffonade by stacking them and rolling tightly into a cigar, then slicing crosswise into thin ribbons.
  13. 13
    Add the balsamic vinegar to the eggplant and stir for about a minute.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  14. 14
    Add the diced tomatoes and most of the basil (save a little for garnishing) to the pan; season the mixture with additional salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened, 4 to 5 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, divide the pork chops and eggplant sauté between plates and garnish with the remaining basil. Enjoy!

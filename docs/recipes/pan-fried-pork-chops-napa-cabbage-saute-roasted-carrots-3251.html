Pan-Fried Pork Chops with Napa Cabbage Sauté & Roasted Carrots

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (7) $2.68/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 3 mediumcarrots
  • apple cider vinegar
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F and place the rack in the center position.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 medium carrots
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
  3. 3
    Peel the carrots, then trim off the ends; discard the peels and trimmings. Cut the carrots crosswise into ¼-inch-thick rounds at a 45° angle and transfer to a baking sheet.
  4. 4
    Drizzle the carrots with olive oil and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat then spread out in a single layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place the carrots in the oven and roast until they are tender when pierced with a fork, 15 to 18 minutes.
  6. 6
    Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; halve the cabbage lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise into shreds and transfer to a bowl.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Place the pork chops in the skillet and sear until they develop a nice crust, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Then, reduce the heat to medium and cook the chops, flipping occasionally, until they are cooked through (test using a thermometer or cut into a chop with a knife), another 3 to 5 minutes. Once done, transfer the pork to a plate, reserving the juices in the skillet.
  11. 11
    While the pork cooks, trim off and discard the ends of the onion and remove the outer layer; halve the onion lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  12. 12
    Once the pork has been transferred, add more coconut oil to the skillet, followed by the onion. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is softened, 2 to 3 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  13. 13
    Add the cabbage to the skillet and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the cabbage is lightly browned and wilted, 4 to 5 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    Add the vinegar to the cabbage and cook, stirring occasionally, for another 30 seconds to 1 minute.
    ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
  15. 15
    To serve, place the cabbage on plate, top with a pork chop, and arrange the carrots on the side. Enjoy!

Pan-Fried Pork Chops with Pear, Arugula & Walnut Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $3.38/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1pear
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • virgin coconut oil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • balsamic vinegar
  • honey
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    1 pear
  2. 2
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the grill pan / skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the grill pan / skillet is hot, coat the bottom with coconut oil.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Place the pork chops in the grill pan / skillet and sear until they develop a nice crust, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Then, reduce the heat to medium and cook the chops, flipping occasionally, until they are cooked through (test using a thermometer or cut into a chop with a knife), another 3 to 5 minutes. Once done, transfer the pork to a plate.
  6. 6
    Transfer the arugula to a large bowl.
  7. 7
    Quarter the pear lengthwise, then cut out the core and stem from each section; cut each section lengthwise into 4 thin wedges and transfer to the bowl with the arugula.
  8. 8
    Roughly chop the walnuts and leave on cutting board.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  9. 9
    In a small bowl, combine and whisk together olive oil, balsamic vinegar, honey, salt, and pepper.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Drizzle the dressing over the arugula and pears; toss to coat.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the salad between plates and sprinkle with the walnuts; place the pork chops next to the salad. Enjoy!

Pan-Fried Pork Chops with Sautéed Spinach & Parsnip-Apple Purée

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $4.87/serving
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • 3 mediumparsnips
  • 1Gala apple
  • butter, unsalted
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) hand blender or potato masher measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 medium parsnips
    1 Gala apple
  2. 2
    Peel the parsnips, then trim off the ends; discard the peels and trimmings. Large dice the parsnips (cut into ¾-inch cubes) and transfer to a large bowl.
  3. 3
    Quarter the apple lengthwise, then cut out the core and stem from each section; large dice the apple (cut into ¾-inch cubes) and add to the bowl with the parsnips.
  4. 4
    Preheat a small saucepan over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the saucepan heats up, peel and mince or press the garlic; transfer to a small bowl.
    3 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Add butter to the saucepan, followed by the chopped parsnips, apples, and ½ of the garlic (save the rest for the spinach). Sauté the parsnips and apples for 3 to 4 minutes.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  7. 7
    Pour the broth into the saucepan and add an additional ¼ cup of water; increase the heat and bring the liquid to a boil. Once boiling, reduce the heat to a simmer, cover the saucepan with a lid, and cook the parsnips and apples until they are tender, about 10 minutes. Once done, remove the saucepan from the heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  11. 11
    Place the pork chops in the skillet and sear until they develop a nice crust, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Then, reduce the heat to medium and cook the chops, flipping occasionally, until they are cooked through (test using a thermometer or cut into a chop with a knife), another 2 to 4 minutes. Once done, transfer the pork to a plate.
  12. 12
    Once the pork has been transferred, add the remaining garlic to the skillet; cook until fragrant, 15 to 30 seconds.
  13. 13
    Add the spinach to the skillet and season with salt and pepper; cook, tossing, until the spinach has wilted, 1 to 2 minutes.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    Add salt and pepper to the parsnip-apple mixture and mash with a hand blender or potato masher until smooth.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, spoon the parsnip-apple purée onto a plate and top with the sautéed spinach and a pork chop. Enjoy!

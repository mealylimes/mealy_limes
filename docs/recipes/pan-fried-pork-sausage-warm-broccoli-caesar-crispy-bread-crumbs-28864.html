Pan-Fried Pork Sausage & Warm Broccoli Caesar with Crispy Bread Crumbs

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (11) $2.56/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • 2pork sausages
  • 1anchovy fillet, packed in oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and separate broccoli into bite-sized florets; cut stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 crowns broccoli
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until broccoli is golden brown and tender, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bread crumbs and salt; cook, stirring frequently, until golden and crispy, 3-5 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside to cool. (Reserve skillet for next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 pinch salt
  6. 6
    Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels. Return to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Transfer to another small bowl.
    ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic. Mince anchovy fillet. Add both to the bowl with the Parmesan.
    1 clove garlic
    1 anchovy fillet, packed in oil
  9. 9
    Wash, dry, and juice lemon into the bowl. Add mayo, oil, pepper, and salt; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ lemon
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    To serve, divide sausages and broccoli between plates. Drizzle broccoli with dressing and top with crispy bread crumbs. Enjoy!

Pan-Fried Sausage with Cheesy Brussels Sprouts & Broccoli

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 63% (21) $2.73/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 10 fl ozwhole milk
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Transfer sausages to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    2 pork sausages
  3. 3
    While the sausages are cooking, fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and trim Brussels sprouts.
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
  5. 5
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, uncover, add salt and Brussels sprouts, and stir for a few seconds. Reduce heat to medium and cook until starting to soften, about 10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry broccoli; separate into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
    1 crown broccoli
  7. 7
    Once the Brussels sprouts start to soften, add broccoli and continue to cook until veggies are fork-tender, 5-6 minutes more. Drain veggies and transfer to a medium bowl; set aside. (Reserve pan for later use.)
  8. 8
    While the veggies are cooking, coarsely grate cheese and set aside.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    Return pan to medium heat. Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add flour and whisk until smooth. Cook, whisking frequently, until lightly golden, 1 minute.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  10. 10
    Slowly pour milk into the pan, whisking constantly. Continue whisking until the sauce has thickened, 3-4 minutes.
    10 fl oz (1 ¼ cups) whole milk
  11. 11
    Add cheese and spices to the pan; whisk until cheese is melted and sauce is smooth.
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  12. 12
    Return drained veggies to the pan with the sauce and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide sausages and cheesy veggies between plates. Enjoy!

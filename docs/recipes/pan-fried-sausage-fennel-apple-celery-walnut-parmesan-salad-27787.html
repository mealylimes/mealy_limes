Pan-Fried Sausage with Fennel, Apple, Celery, Walnut & Parmesan Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (6) $2.46/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ½lemon
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 2pork sausages
  • 1fennel bulb
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, roughly chop walnuts.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add walnuts and cook, stirring frequently, until lightly toasted, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
  4. 4
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    1 fennel bulb
    2 sticks celery
    1 Granny Smith apple
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. 6
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Trim off root end and stalks of the fennel, leaving only the white bulb. Halve fennel bulb lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim and medium dice celery. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Pick parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    Using a peeler, shave Parmesan into curls.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  12. 12
    To serve, divide sausages and salad between plates; top salad with walnuts and Parmesan. Enjoy!

Pan-Fried Thai Red Curry Cod with Carrot, Avocado & Cashew Salad

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $4.25/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ¼ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • 1avocado
  • ½lime
  • 30 mlThai curry paste, red
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place curry paste, oil, soy sauce, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the spice paste.
    2 tbsp Thai curry paste, red
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Pat the cod dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Drizzle with half of the spice paste, then turn to coat the fish. (Reserve bowl with remaining spice paste for the salad.)
    ¾ lb cod fillet
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add cod and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    ½ English cucumber
    1 avocado
  6. 6
    Juice lime into the bowl with the remaining spice paste. Add honey and stir to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp honey
  7. 7
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Roughly chop cashews. Add to the bowl, along with the carrots, and stir to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  10. 10
    To serve, divide cod and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Pan-Fried Tilapia with Green Onion Miso Sauce, Pickled Veggies & Rice

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (3) $2.70/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbtilapia fillet
  • rice vinegar
  • brown sugar
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • 1 bunchradish
  • 15 mlmiso paste, white
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice radishes. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid along with the carrots; stir to combine and set aside.
    1 bunch radish
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  5. 5
    When the rice is almost done, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, pat the tilapia dry with paper towels.
    ¾ lb tilapia fillet
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the tilapia and cook, flipping once, until fish is crispy and easily flakes with a fork, about 6 minutes. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place in a small bowl along with water and miso paste. Stir to combine the sauce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    2 tbsp water
    1 tbsp miso paste, white
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium heat, add more butter, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sauce and cook, stirring constantly, until thickened and fragrant, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    To serve, divide rice, tilapia, and pickled veggies between plates or bowls; top fish with the sauce. Enjoy!

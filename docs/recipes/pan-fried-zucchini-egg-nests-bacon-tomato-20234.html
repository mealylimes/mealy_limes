Pan-Fried Zucchini Egg Nests with Bacon & Tomato

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $2.49/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 4eggs
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board julienne peeler (optional) measuring spoons nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side. Remove bacon to a paper towel-lined plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and trim zucchini; use a julienne (or regular) peeler to make noodles.
    2 medium zucchini squash
  4. 4
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat; add zucchini noodles and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 2 minutes.
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    When the zucchini noodles are soft, reduce heat to medium-low. Using a spatula, divide and shape noodles into 4 flattened mounds, making an indent at the center of each to create a "nest."
  6. 6
    Gently crack an egg into each "nest." Cover the skillet with a lid and cook until the tops of the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, 3-4 minutes; remove from heat.
    4 eggs
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and halve tomatoes.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  8. 8
    To serve, divide zucchini egg nests, bacon, and tomatoes between plates; season eggs with salt and pepper. Enjoy!
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper

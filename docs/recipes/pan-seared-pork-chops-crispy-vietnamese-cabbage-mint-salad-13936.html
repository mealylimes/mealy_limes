Pan Seared Pork Chops with Crispy Vietnamese Cabbage & Mint Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 58% (3) $3.82/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1lime
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ English cucumber
    1 medium carrot
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork chops and sear until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat and transfer to a clean plate. Cover loosely with aluminum foil and set aside.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place edamame in a colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    ½ cup edamame, shelled
  6. 6
    Juice lime into a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Add fish sauce, oil, sugar, and, chili-garlic sauce to the bowl with the lime juice; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  8. 8
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice the shallot into half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 shallot
  9. 9
    Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; halve the cabbage lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise into shreds. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Trim cucumber, halve lengthwise, and thinly slice crosswise; add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with the edamame and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Thinly slice pork chops.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad and pork chops between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Pan-Seared Scallops with Tomato-Basil Linguine

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (9) $7.19/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozlinguine pasta
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbsea scallops
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) large pot measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz linguine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat the scallops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; rub with oil and season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb sea scallops
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, carefully add the scallops in a single layer; cook until they develop a nice sear and are opaque throughout, 1½-2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel and small dice the onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Return skillet to medium heat, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 4 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  8. 8
    Add tomatoes, garlic, and crushed red pepper to the skillet with the onions; stir to combine. Bring to a boil over high heat then reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash basil, pick leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  10. 10
    Add scallops, capers, and basil to the sauce; simmer 1 minute more.
    1 tbsp capers
  11. 11
    Drain pasta, add to the skillet, and toss to coat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pasta, sauce, and scallops between plates. Enjoy!

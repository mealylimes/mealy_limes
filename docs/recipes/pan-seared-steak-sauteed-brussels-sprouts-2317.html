Pan-Seared Steak with Sautéed Brussels Sprouts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (12) $5.97/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 lbBrussels sprouts
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • 1shallot
  • balsamic vinegar
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash sprouts, trim rough ends and halve lengthwise. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.
    1 lb brussels sprouts
  2. 2
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Season steak generously with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Place steak in skillet and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil; set aside.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  5. 5
    Peel and thinly slice shallot. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 shallot
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    Once steak has been transferred, melt butter in skillet. Add brussels sprouts, shallot, and garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, until sprouts are browned, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  7. 7
    Add ½ cup water to sprouts and bring to a simmer. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the sprouts are tender and liquid has evaporated, 4-5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Drizzle with vinegar and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, for another 1-2 minutes.
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Serve steak with brussels sprouts on a plate. Enjoy!

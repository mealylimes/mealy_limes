Paprika Pork Chop with Bacon, Zucchini & Tomato Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $4.47/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 3-4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving the drippings in the skillet.
  4. 4
    While the bacon is cooking, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on another plate; season with spices on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    When the bacon is done, return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry zucchini. Using a clean cutting board, trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons.
    2 medium zucchini squash
  7. 7
    Wash and dry tomatoes.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Return skillet (with cooking juices) to medium-high heat; add zucchini, tomatoes, garlic, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the zucchini is tender and tomatoes start to burst, 6-7 minutes.
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, add water and continue to cook until the sauce has thickened, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    ¼ cup water
  11. 11
    To serve, divide chops, veggies, and sauce between plates; sprinkle with crispy bacon and enjoy!

Paprika Pork Chops with Yogurt-Dressed Waldorf Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (12) $3.70/serving
Ingredients
  • 2Gala apples
  • ½lemon
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ¼ cupraisins
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • black pepper
  • honey
  • salt
  • paprika
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Place pork chops in a small baking dish. Rub both sides with paprika, salt, and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place in the oven and bake until cooked through, 15-20 minutes.
  4. 4
    In a medium bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together lemon juice, yogurt, honey, salt, and pepper.
    ¼ lemon
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Wash and dry apples and celery. Core and medium dice apples. Medium dice celery. Add to bowl with dressing.
    2 Gala apples
    2 sticks celery
  6. 6
    Add raisins to bowl. Roughly chop walnuts and add to bowl. Toss salad.
    ¼ cup raisins
    ¼ cup walnuts
  7. 7
    Cut remaining piece of lemon into wedges for serving.
    ¼ lemon
  8. 8
    To serve, place pork chops and salad on a plate and garnish with lemon wedges. Enjoy!

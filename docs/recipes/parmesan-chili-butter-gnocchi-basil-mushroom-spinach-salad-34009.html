Parmesan-Chili Butter Gnocchi with Basil & Mushroom-Spinach Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (15) $3.48/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ⅓ lbwhite mushrooms
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • rice vinegar
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ⅓ lb white mushrooms
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Place half in a medium bowl along with mayo, vinegar, water, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp water
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Thinly slice mushrooms. Add to the bowl with the dressing and set aside.
  4. 4
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  5. 5
    While the water heats up, finely grate Parmesan. Set aside.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  6. 6
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, add gnocchi and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, about 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    ½ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chili-garlic sauce and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp chili-garlic sauce
  9. 9
    Add drained gnocchi and Parmesan to the skillet with the chili butter. Continue to cook, stirring often, until cheese is melted and gnocchi are coated in sauce, 1 minute more. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    Add spinach to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide gnocchi and salad between plates or bowls. Top gnocchi with reserved basil and enjoy!

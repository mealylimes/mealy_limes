Parmesan & Parsley Crusted Chicken with Roasted Romaine Lettuce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (6) $3.28/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½lemon
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • panko bread crumbs
  • salt
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Slice chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season with salt and pepper. Place on a baking sheet pan.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  3. 3
    Wash and dry parsley. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  4. 4
    Grate ¼ cup of Parmesan cheese. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    In a small bowl, combine parsley, Parmesan, garlic, bread crumbs, and olive oil. Mix well and pat onto chicken fillets.
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Place chicken in the oven and bake while you prepare the romaine, about 5 minutes.
  7. 7
    Wash and dry lettuce. Quarter lengthwise. Place on sheet with chicken and season with salt and pepper.
    1 head romaine lettuce
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Return sheet to the oven and roast until chicken is cooked through and romaine is browned at the edges, another 5-10 minutes.
  9. 9
    Cut ½ lemon into wedges.
  10. 10
    To serve, place chicken and romaine hearts on a plate. Garnish with lemon wedges and sprinkle romaine hearts with pine nuts. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup pine nuts

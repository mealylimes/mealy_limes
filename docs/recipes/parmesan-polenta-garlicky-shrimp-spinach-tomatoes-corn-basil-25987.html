Parmesan Polenta with Garlicky Shrimp, Spinach, Tomatoes, Corn & Basil

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $6.29/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½lemon
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ cuppolenta (cornmeal)
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring water to a boil. Reduce heat to low and gradually add polenta in a thin steady stream, while whisking, until the polenta is incorporated; add salt. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until polenta is soft and the texture is creamy, 15-25 minutes.
    3 cup water
    ¾ cup polenta (cornmeal)
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, peel and thinly slice garlic. Place in a small bowl and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tomatoes and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes are beginning to burst, about 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Place half in a medium bowl along with the spinach. (Set remaining basil aside for serving.)
  7. 7
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  8. 8
    When the tomatoes are done, add to the bowl with the garlic. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp, salt, and crushed red pepper; cook until just opaque and starting to color, 1 minute per side.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  10. 10
    When the shrimp are done, add garlic and tomatoes to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until garlic is fragrant, 1-2 minutes more.
  11. 11
    Add spinach and basil to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  12. 12
    Remove shrimp sauté from heat, juice lemon into the skillet, and stir to combine. Cover to keep warm and set aside.
  13. 13
    Finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  14. 14
    When the polenta is done, add Parmesan, corn, oil, and pepper to the pan; stir to combine. Continue to cook until corn is warmed through, 1-2 minutes more; if the polenta is too firm, add hot water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until you reach a desired consistency. Remove from heat.
    1 cup frozen corn
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, divide polenta between plates or bowls; top with shrimp-tomato sauté and reserved basil. Enjoy!

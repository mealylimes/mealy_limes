Parsnip Latkes with Yogurt, Smoked Salmon & Avocado-Radish Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (1) $3.38/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1egg
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1avocado
  • 1lemon
  • 2 mediumparsnips
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • baking powder
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    2 medium parsnips
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
    1 avocado
  2. 2
    Zest and juice half of the lemon into a small bowl. Juice remaining lemon into a medium bowl and set aside.
  3. 3
    Add yogurt, salt, and pepper to the small bowl with the lemon, then stir to combine the sauce. Set aside.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Add oil, salt, and pepper to the medium bowl with the lemon juice. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Place egg, baking powder, salt, and pepper in another medium bowl. Whisk to combine.
    1 egg
    ½ tsp baking powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate parsnips. Add to the bowl with the egg mixture.
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and coarsely grate onion. Add to the bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  8. 8
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add half to the bowl, along with flour, and stir to combine the latke mixture. (Reserve remaining dill for serving.)
    ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Using a ¼-cup measure, drop batter into the skillet, spacing scoops a few inches apart; press down to form ½-inch thick fritters. Cook, flipping once, until latkes are golden brown, 3-5 minutes. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, trim and thinly slice radishes. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Chop (or tear) lettuce leaves into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
  13. 13
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and thinly slice. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  14. 14
    Chop or tear smoked salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  15. 15
    To serve, divide latkes and salad between plates. Top latkes with yogurt sauce, salmon, and reserved dill. Top salad with avocado and enjoy!

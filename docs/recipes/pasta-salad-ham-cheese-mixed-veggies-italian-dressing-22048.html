Pasta Salad with Ham, Cheese, Mixed Veggies & Italian Dressing

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $1.39/serving
Ingredients
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • salt
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ¼ cupItalian salad dressing
  • 8 ozham steak, boneless
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until almost done, about 6 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    1 yellow bell pepper
    1 medium carrot
  3. 3
    Trim and medium dice cucumber. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the cucumber.
  5. 5
    Medium dice ham and add to the bowl. Set aside.
    8 oz ham steak, boneless
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and medium dice carrot.
  7. 7
    When the pasta is almost done, add carrot and broccoli to the pan. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta reaches desired firmness and veggies are tender, 3-4 minutes more.
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  8. 8
    Drain and rinse pasta and veggies under cold water to cool. Add to the bowl with the other ingredients.
  9. 9
    Add cheese and dressing to the bowl, then stir to combine the pasta salad.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
    ¼ cup Italian salad dressing
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

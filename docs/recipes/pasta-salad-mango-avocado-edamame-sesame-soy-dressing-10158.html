Pasta Salad with Mango, Avocado, Edamame & Sesame-Soy Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (10) $1.70/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½avocado
  • ⅛ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • ½mango
  • rice vinegar
  • brown sugar
  • ½ small headred cabbage
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 ½ tsp salt
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ mango
    ½ avocado
    2 medium carrots
    ½ small head red cabbage
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    1 cup edamame
  4. 4
    Combine soy sauce, oil, vinegar, and sugar in a large bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp brown sugar
  5. 5
    Peel mango and slice the flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides. Small dice and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Halve and pit the avocado. Slice lengthwise and crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate the carrots; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; halve the cabbage lengthwise and cut out the core; thinly slice crosswise and add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim green onions; cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces and add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Roughly chop peanuts and add to the bowl along with the edamame.
    ⅛ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  11. 11
    Once pasta is done, drain and rinse under cool water. Add pasta to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide salad between bowls. Enjoy!

Peach-Blueberry Pita Pizza with Ricotta, Basil & Pine Nuts

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (12) $4.19/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 2peaches
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 peaches
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  3. 3
    Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Slice each quarter into ¼-inch thick wedges. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add half to the bowl with the peaches. (Reserve remaining basil for later use.)
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl along with blueberries, pine nuts, oil, salt, and pepper, then stir to combine.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Arrange pitas on a baking sheet pan. Spread each with ricotta, then top with the peach mixture.
    2 whole grain pitas
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until peaches are tender and pizzas are warmed through, 12-15 minutes.
  8. 8
    When the pizzas are done, remove from the oven. Sprinkle with reserved basil, then drizzle with vinegar and honey. Cut into wedges.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    2 tsp honey
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pizzas between plates. Enjoy!

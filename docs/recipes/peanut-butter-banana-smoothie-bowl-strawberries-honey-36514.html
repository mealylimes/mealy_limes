Peanut Butter-Banana Smoothie Bowl with Strawberries & Honey

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (6) $1.44/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ⅛ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 2bananas
  • vanilla extract
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    This recipe will make about 3 cups of smoothie. Work in batches to best fit your blender.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice strawberries. Set aside.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
  3. 3
    Peel bananas, break into chunks, and place in a blender. Add yogurt, milk, peanut butter, honey, vanilla, and cinnamon, then blend on high speed until smooth, 1-2 minutes.
    2 bananas
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    3 tbsp natural peanut butter
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, roughly chop peanuts.
    ⅛ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  5. 5
    To serve, divide smoothie between bowls. Top with strawberries and peanuts, then drizzle with additional honey. Enjoy!
    2 tsp honey

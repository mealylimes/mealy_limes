Peanut Tofu-Cauliflower Bake with Bell Pepper, Edamame & Rice

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (7) $1.77/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • ½ cupedamame, shelled
  • ½ cuppeanut sauce
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    While the rice is cooking, place oil and spices in a medium bowl; stir to combine the spice paste.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Medium dice tofu. Add to the bowl with the spice paste, along with the cauliflower, and gently stir to coat. Transfer to the baking sheet and spread out in a single layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until tofu and cauliflower are golden brown, about 20 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; small dice. Place in the reserved bowl.
    1 red bell pepper
  9. 9
    Place edamame in the colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; add to the bowl with the bell pepper. Set aside.
    ½ cup edamame, shelled
  10. 10
    When the tofu and cauliflower are done, remove from the oven. Add to the bowl, along with the peanut sauce, and stir to combine.
    ½ cup peanut sauce
  11. 11
    To serve, divide rice between plates or bowls; top with tofu and veggies. Enjoy!

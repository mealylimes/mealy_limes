Pear & Banana Oatmeal Crisp with Vanilla Ice Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $1.26/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2pears
  • brown sugar
  • 1banana
  • oats, quick cooking
  • nutmeg, ground
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
  • water
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, quarter, and core pears; medium dice.
    2 pears
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pears, water, sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the pears are golden and starting to soften, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 fl oz (⅛ cup) water
    2 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    1 pinch nutmeg, ground
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place oats, additional butter, flour, and sugar in a small bowl. Using two hands, combine the topping by rubbing the mixture between your thumbs and fingers until it looks crumbly. Set aside.
    ¼ cup oats, quick cooking
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    1 ½ tbsp brown sugar
  6. 6
    Peel and thinly slice banana.
    1 banana
  7. 7
    Transfer pears to a small baking dish and spread out in an even layer. Arrange banana slices on top, then sprinkle evenly with topping.
  8. 8
    Place baking dish in the oven and bake until fruit is soft and topping is golden, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide crisp and ice cream between bowls. Enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

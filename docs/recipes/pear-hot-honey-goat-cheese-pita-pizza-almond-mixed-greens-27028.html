Pear & Hot Honey-Goat Cheese Pita Pizza with Almond Mixed Greens

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (5) $5.42/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • 2pears
  • ½ small pkgfresh thyme
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
Cookware
baking dish baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    2 pears
    ½ small pkg fresh thyme
    ½ lemon
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice pears. Transfer to a small baking dish.
  4. 4
    Slide thyme leaves off the stems; discard the stems. Add leaves to the baking dish with the pear.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into ¼-inch thick strips. Add to the baking dish, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper; stir to combine.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking dish in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until pears and onions are soft and starting to brown, 15-20 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, honey, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing. Set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp honey
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    Place goat cheese, honey, and crushed red pepper in a small bowl. Mash with the back of a spoon to combine and set aside.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
    1 ½ tbsp honey
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Arrange pitas on a baking sheet pan. Add to the oven and bake, flipping halfway through, until pitas are slightly crispy, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside.
    2 whole grain pitas
  10. 10
    When the pears and onion are done, remove from oven. Spread goat cheese over each pita, top with pears and onions, then cut into wedges.
  11. 11
    Add mixed greens and almonds to the bowl with the dressing, then toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pizzas and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Pecan & Maple Roasted Strawberry Oatmeal

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 64% (7) $1.61/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ¼ cuppecan halves
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan parchment paper
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry strawberries; trim stem ends and slice.
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
  3. 3
    Transfer strawberries to baking sheet, drizzle with maple syrup, and spread out in a single layer. Roast until softened, 10-13 minutes.
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
  4. 4
    Roughly chop pecans.
    ¼ cup pecans, dry roasted
  5. 5
    Add salt and water to a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, stir in oats and reduce heat to medium. Cover and let simmer for 3-5 minutes or until oats reach desired consistency.
    1 pinch salt
    1 ¾ cup water
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
  6. 6
    To serve, divide oats between bowls and top with strawberries, pecans, and milk. Enjoy!
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk

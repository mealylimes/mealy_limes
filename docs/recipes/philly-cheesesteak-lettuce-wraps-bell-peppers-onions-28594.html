"Philly Cheesesteak" Lettuce Wraps with Bell Peppers & Onions

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (15) $2.56/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ¾ lbflank steak
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with Worcestershire, oil, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Pat steak dry with paper towels, then slice against the grain into very thin strips. Add to the bowl with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb flank steak
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  4. 4
    Using a clean cutting board, trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Place in another medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the bell peppers. Set aside.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add steak strips and marinade. Cook to desired doneness, about 1 minute per side for medium-rare. Transfer steak to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
  8. 8
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper; stir-fry until veggies are fork-tender, 5-6 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, coarsely grate cheese.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  10. 10
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven.
  11. 11
    Add veggies to the baking sheet, spreading them over the steak, then top with cheese. Place baking sheet in the oven and broil until cheese melts, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
  12. 12
    Separate lettuce into leaves. Pile leaves, in stacks of two per wrap, onto plates. Fill with cheesy steak and veggies. Enjoy!

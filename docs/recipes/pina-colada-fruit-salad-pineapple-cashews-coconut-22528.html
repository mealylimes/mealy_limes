"Piña Colada" Fruit Salad with Pineapple, Cashews & Coconut

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 7 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (12) $1.37/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ¼ cupshredded unsweetened coconut
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board measuring cups mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, and cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and medium dice the flesh. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small pineapple
  2. 2
    Add cashews and coconut to the bowl. Stir to combine the fruit salad.
    ½ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
    ¼ cup shredded unsweetened coconut
  3. 3
    To serve, divide fruit salad between bowls and enjoy!

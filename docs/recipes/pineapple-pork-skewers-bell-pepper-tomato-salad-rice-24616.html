Pineapple Pork Skewers with Bell Pepper-Tomato Salad & Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $4.36/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½shallot
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 1tomato
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pineapple
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 lime
    1 orange bell pepper
    1 tomato
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    While the rice is cooking, cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and large dice the flesh. Place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Pat the pork dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes. Add to the bowl with the pineapple along with oil and spices; stir to coat.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Preheat a grill pan or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the pan heats up, thread pineapple and pork onto skewers, alternating ingredients; place on a plate. Alternatively, you can cook the pork and pineapple pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add the skewers and grill, turning occasionally, until pineapple is golden brown and pork is cooked through, about 10 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
  11. 11
    Juice half of the lime into the bowl with the cilantro. (Cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.)
  12. 12
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl along with oil, honey, and spices. Stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  14. 14
    Small dice tomato. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    Divide rice, salad, and skewers between plates. Serve with lime wedges on the side. Enjoy!

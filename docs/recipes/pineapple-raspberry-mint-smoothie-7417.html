Pineapple, Raspberry & Mint Smoothie

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 7 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (18) $1.52/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • pure maple syrup
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ¼ small pkgfresh mint
  • 1 cupfrozen raspberries
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pineapple
    ¼ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Cut skin off pineapple, core, and cut into large pieces; add to blender.
  3. 3
    Pick the mint leaves off the stems; discard the stems and add leaves to blender.
  4. 4
    Add raspberries, yogurt, milk, maple syrup, and 4 ice cubes to the blender.
    1 cup frozen raspberries
    1 cup plain Greek yogurt
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
  5. 5
    Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
  6. 6
    Pour the smoothie into glasses and enjoy!

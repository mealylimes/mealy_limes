Pinto Bean, Goat Cheese & Corn Quesadilla with Creamy Tex-Mex Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (4) $5.55/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) canpinto beans
  • pure maple syrup
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • cumin, ground
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ½green bell pepper
  • ⅔ cupfrozen corn
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ green bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can pinto beans
  3. 3
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add beans, onion, garlic, corn, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until corn is golden, 6-8 minutes; remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅔ cup frozen corn
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince additional garlic; add to the bowl with the lime juice along with yogurt, maple syrup, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    1 tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  8. 8
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl and set aside. (Reserve remaining cilantro for the next step.)
  11. 11
    To assemble the quesadillas, lay tortillas on a flat surface and divide bean and corn filling between them, arranging on one half of each tortilla. Crumble goat cheese over filling and sprinkle with remaining cilantro; fold in half to close. (Reserve skillet for the next step.)
    2 large flour tortillas
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
  12. 12
    Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat.
  13. 13
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Place assembled quesadillas in the skillet and cook, flipping halfway through, until golden on both sides, 5-6 minutes. Remove to a plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, cut or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl with the dressing and veggies. Toss to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    Cut each quesadilla into wedges and divide between plates; serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

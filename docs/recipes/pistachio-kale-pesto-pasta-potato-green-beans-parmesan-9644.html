Pistachio-Kale Pesto Pasta with Potato, Green Beans & Parmesan

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 61% (7) $3.05/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • 1 mediumyellow potato
  • ½lemon
  • ½ bunchkale
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater hand or regular blender large pot measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls slotted spoon stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ bunch kale
    1 medium yellow potato
    ½ lb green beans
    1 small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into 3-inch pieces; transfer to a large bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and dice potato into ¾-inch cubes.
  5. 5
    Trim and discard the ends of green beans, then cut in half.
  6. 6
    Once the water in the pot comes to a boil, uncover, add salt and kale, and stir. Cook until bright green and wilted, 30-60 seconds. Transfer kale to a colander using a slotted spoon. Reserve bowl for later.
    1 tsp salt
  7. 7
    Once the water in the pot returns to a boil, add potatoes and pasta; stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    5 oz rotini pasta
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, peel garlic; transfer to a blender along with cooked kale, water, pistachios, oil, and salt.
    1 clove garlic
    3 tbsp water
    ⅛ cup pistachios, unsalted
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Pick basil leaves off the stems; add to the blender. Juice lemon into the blender.
  10. 10
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add half to the blender; set the rest aside for garnish. Blend on high, adding 1 tbsp of water at a time, if needed, until smooth; transfer pesto to the reserved bowl.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Add green beans to the pot with the pasta during the last 5 minutes of cooking.
  12. 12
    Roughly chop additional pistachios.
    ⅛ cup pistachios, unsalted
  13. 13
    Drain the pasta and vegetables, then transfer to the bowl with the pesto; stir to combine.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta between bowls or plates. Garnish with reserved Parmesan, pistachios, and pepper. Enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp black pepper

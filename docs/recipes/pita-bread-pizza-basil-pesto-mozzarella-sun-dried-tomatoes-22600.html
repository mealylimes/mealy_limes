Pita Bread Pizza with Basil Pesto, Mozzarella & Sun-Dried Tomatoes

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 18 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (6) $0.76/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅛ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • Italian seasoning
  • 2sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 1whole grain pita
  • 30 mlbasil pesto
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board grater measuring spoons oven mitts
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Place pita on a baking sheet pan and spread with pesto.
    1 whole grain pita
    2 tbsp basil pesto
  3. 3
    Coarsely grate cheese. Sprinkle over the pesto.
    ⅛ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  4. 4
    Thinly slice sun-dried tomatoes. Arrange tomatoes over the cheese, then sprinkle with Italian seasoning.
    2 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    1 pinch Italian seasoning
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until pita bread is lightly crisp and cheese is melted, 5-7 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool slightly before cutting into wedges.
  6. 6
    To serve, divide pizza wedges between plates. Enjoy!

Popcorn-Style Shrimp with Cocktail Sauce & Chopped Veggie Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $4.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • white wine vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • mayonnaise
  • ketchup
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • brown sugar
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with foil and coat with a thin layer of oil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  3. 3
    Combine mayo and spices in a medium bowl.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp oregano
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  4. 4
    Place breadcrumbs in a second medium bowl.
    1 cup panko bread crumbs
  5. 5
    Toss shrimp in the mayo mixture to coat. Working in batches, transfer shrimp to the breadcrumb mixture and turn to coat, pressing gently to adhere. Transfer shrimp to the prepared baking sheet in a single layer.
  6. 6
    Place shrimp in the oven and bake 10 minutes, then flip and cook until golden and crisp, 5 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  8. 8
    Juice lemon into a small bowl. Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Add ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, sugar, and spices to the bowl with the garlic and lemon; stir to combine the cocktail sauce and set aside.
    ⅓ cup ketchup
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp chili powder
  10. 10
    Place mayo, oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  11. 11
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Trim cucumber, halve lengthwise, and thinly slice crosswise on an angle. Add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Seed and slice the bell pepper into thin strips; add to the bowl, along with the mixed greens, and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    Divide shrimp and salad between plates. Serve with cocktail sauce on the side and enjoy!

Pork Chop, Dijon Roasted Cabbage & Arugula-Pear Salad with Blue Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $4.80/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 1pear
  • ⅛ cupwalnuts
  • ½ small headred cabbage
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small head red cabbage
    1 pear
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Place Dijon, oil, water, honey, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the Dijon sauce.
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp water
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Halve cabbage lengthwise and cut out the core; slice crosswise into 1-inch thick wedges. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, then drizzle both sides of the cabbage with Dijon sauce. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, gently flipping cabbage halfway through, until tender and golden around the edges, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels. Place on a plate and season both sides with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    While the chops are cooking, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, and salt in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Peel, halve, and slice onion into thin half-moons; add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine.
    ½ medium red onion
  11. 11
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice pear; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Roughly chop walnuts and add to the bowl.
    ⅛ cup walnuts
  13. 13
    Just before serving, add arugula and blue cheese to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  14. 14
    To serve, divide roasted cabbage, chops, and salad between plates. Enjoy!

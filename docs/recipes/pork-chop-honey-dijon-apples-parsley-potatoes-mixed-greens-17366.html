Pork Chop with Honey-Dijon Apples, Parsley Potatoes & Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (2) $5.43/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • butter, unsalted
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¾ lbnew potatoes
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ¾ lb new potatoes
    1 Granny Smith apple
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Halve potatoes, place in a medium saucepan, cover with hot water (from the tap), and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer until potatoes can be easily pierced with a fork, 10-12 minutes. Drain and return to the saucepan; cover to keep warm.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  6. 6
    While the chops are cooking, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Quarter and core apple, then slice into ¼-inch thick wedges; add to the bowl with the onion and garlic.
  8. 8
    When the chops are done, return the skillet (with cooking juices) to medium-low heat. Add onion, garlic, and apple; cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion and apple are soft, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, mayo, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise and add to the bowl with the dressing; set aside.
  11. 11
    When the onion and apple are soft, add broth, Dijon, and honey to the skillet; bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  13. 13
    Return the saucepan with potatoes to low heat; add butter, parsley, and salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until butter and parsley coat the potatoes, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ⅛ tsp salt
  14. 14
    Add mixed greens to bowl with dressing and tomatoes; toss to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pork chops, apple sauté, potatoes, and salad between plates. Enjoy!

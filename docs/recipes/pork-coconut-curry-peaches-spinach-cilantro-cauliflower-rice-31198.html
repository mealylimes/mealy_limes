Pork Coconut Curry with Peaches, Spinach, Cilantro & Cauliflower Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (7) $4.85/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 2peaches
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    2 peaches
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Cut cauliflower head into quarters; using a box grater, hold the stem end of the cauliflower and coarsely grate. (Alternatively, transfer cauliflower to the bowl of a food processor; pulse until rice-like in consistency.) Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Add oil and salt to the bowl with the cauliflower rice, then stir to combine. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring cauliflower halfway through, until rice is tender and golden, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Medium dice and place in the reserved bowl.
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the peaches. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes. Season with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork and stir-fry until golden and almost cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 ½ tsp neutral vegetable oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, peel and mince ginger. Add to the bowl with the peaches and cilantro.
  12. 12
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add peaches, cilantro, ginger, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Return pork to the skillet, along with coconut milk, and stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pork is cooked through and curry thickens slightly, 2-3 minutes more.
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
  14. 14
    Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for it to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Remove from heat.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide cauliflower rice and curry between plates or bowls. Top with reserved cilantro and enjoy!

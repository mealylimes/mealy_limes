Pork Parmesan with Garlicky Asparagus & Mushroom Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 65% (13) $4.82/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Finely grate cheese and place half in a small bowl. Add bread crumbs and spices to bowl, then stir to combine the coating. (Set remaining cheese aside for later use.)
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels. Place on a plate and spread both sides with mayo. Sprinkle the coating over both sides and press gently to adhere.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork chops and pan-fry until golden and almost cooked through, about 2 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Pour tomato sauce over pork chops and sprinkle with reserved cheese.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  7. 7
    Place skillet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until pork is cooked through and cheese is melted, 6-7 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms; place in a medium bowl.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
  9. 9
    Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the mushrooms.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms and garlic; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Wash, dry, and snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears crosswise into 1-inch pieces.
    1 small bunch asparagus
  13. 13
    When the mushrooms are soft, add asparagus, salt, and pepper to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are fork-tender, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    To serve, divide veggies, pork, and sauce between plates. Enjoy!

Pork Sausage-Gnocchi Skillet with Bell Pepper, Mushroom & Sauerkraut

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (11) $2.78/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • 2pork sausages
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
  • ⅔ cupsauerkraut
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, add gnocchi and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, about 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    While the gnocchi are cooking, wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; medium dice. Add bell pepper and mushrooms to the bowl with the onion.
    ½ red bell pepper
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, slice sausages crosswise into 1-inch thick rounds.
    2 pork sausages
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, sausage, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies soften and sausage is cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, drain sauerkraut in a colander to remove excess liquid.
    ⅔ cup sauerkraut
  9. 9
    When the veggies and sausage are done, add cooked gnocchi and sauerkraut to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi are warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide gnocchi, sausage, and veggies between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

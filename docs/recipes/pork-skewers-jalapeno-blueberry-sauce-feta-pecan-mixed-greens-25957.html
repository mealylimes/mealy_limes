Pork Skewers with Jalapeño Blueberry Sauce & Feta-Pecan Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (7) $4.86/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ketchup
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • ⅛ cuppecan halves
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using wooden skewers, place 4-6 skewers in a shallow dish of water to soak until you are ready to use them.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Mince and place in a small saucepan. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  4. 4
    Add blueberries to the pan with the jalapeño along with maple syrup, vinegar, ketchup, Worcestershire, and salt. Stir to combine.
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tbsp ketchup
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Set pan over high heat and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until saucy and thickened, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, pat the pork dry with paper towels and cut into 1-inch cubes. Place in a medium bowl along with oil and spices; stir to coat.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor skillet, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the pan heats up, thread pork onto skewers and place on a plate. Alternatively, you can cook the pork pieces directly on the grill pan without using skewers.
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add the skewers and grill, turning occasionally, until pork is cooked through, about 8 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in a clean medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Place corn in the colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Add to the bowl with dressing.
    ½ cup frozen corn
  12. 12
    Roughly chop pecans. Add to the bowl along with mixed greens and feta. Gently toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup pecan halves
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide skewers and salad between plates. Top skewers with sauce and enjoy!

Portobello Mushroom, Asparagus & Ricotta Flatbread with Balsamic Glaze

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $4.38/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Position racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Remove mushroom stems, then slice caps into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the garlic.
  5. 5
    Snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Add oil and spices to the bowl; toss to coat. Transfer veggies to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet on upper rack of the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are tender, 12-15 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, arrange flatbreads on another baking sheet. Place on the lower rack and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, about 8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  9. 9
    While flatbreads are toasting, place vinegar in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until reduced by half, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
    ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Place half of the basil in a small bowl. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
  11. 11
    Add ricotta, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the basil; stir to combine.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    To assemble the flatbreads, spread ricotta over each flatbread and top with roasted veggies. Return to oven and bake until warmed through, 4-5 minutes. Remove from oven.
  13. 13
    To serve, slice flatbreads and divide between plates. Drizzle with balsamic glaze, top with reserved basil, and enjoy!

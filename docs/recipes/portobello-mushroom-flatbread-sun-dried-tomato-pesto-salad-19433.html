Portobello Mushroom Flatbread with Sun-Dried Tomato Pesto & Salad

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $3.30/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 80 mlsun-dried tomato pesto
  • 30 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Remove mushroom stems, cut caps into ¼-inch thick slices, and transfer to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with vinaigrette, season with salt and pepper, then toss to coat; spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place mushrooms in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until golden brown and tender, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, arrange flatbreads on another baking sheet. Add to the oven and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  6. 6
    When the mushrooms are done, assemble the flatbreads. Spread a thin layer of pesto over each flatbread, then top with cheese, mushrooms, and Italian seasoning.
    ⅓ cup sun-dried tomato pesto
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
  7. 7
    Return flatbreads to the oven and bake until warmed through and cheese is melted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, halve tomatoes. Place in a medium bowl along with spinach and vinaigrette; toss to combine the salad.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
  9. 9
    Slice flatbreads and divide between plates. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

Portobello Mushroom "Parmesan" with Zucchini, Olive & Pine Nut Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $6.69/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½shallot
  • ⅓ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ¼ cuppine nuts
  • 4 capsportobello mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and remove stems from portobello mushrooms, then carefully scrape out the gills. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, rub with oil, and season with salt and pepper on both sides; arrange cap-side up.
    4 caps portobello mushrooms
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place mushrooms in the oven and broil until starting to soften, about 6 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic. Place in a small saucepan along with tomato sauce, crushed red pepper, and salt. Stir to combine the sauce and bring to a simmer over high heat. Once simmering, remove from heat and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    While the sauce is heating, finely grate Parmesan. Place in a medium bowl along with bread crumbs and spices, then stir to combine the Parmesan topping.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Using tongs, carefully flip the mushrooms, cap-side down. Spread evenly with warm tomato sauce and sprinkle with Parmesan topping. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  7. 7
    Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to broil until the mushrooms are soft and cheese topping is golden brown, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, and spices in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ shallot
  10. 10
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl.
    ⅓ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  11. 11
    Wash, dry, and trim zucchini, then run a vegetable peeler along the length of the zucchini to make ribbons. Add to the bowl, along with the pine nuts, and toss to combine the salad.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    ¼ cup pine nuts
  12. 12
    To serve, divide the mushrooms and salad between plates. Enjoy!

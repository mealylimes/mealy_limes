Portobello Mushroom Tuna Melts with Baby Greens

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $7.61/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½lemon
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • 4 capsportobello mushrooms
  • 1tomato
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts salad spinner (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat broiler and position rack about 6 inches from the top.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry mushrooms; remove stems. Place mushroom caps on a baking sheet pan and brush with a little olive oil. Place in the oven and broil until almost tender, 6-8 minutes.
    4 caps portobello mushrooms
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  3. 3
    Small dice mushroom stems and transfer to a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Wash and dry celery and parsley. Small dice and add the celery to bowl with mushrooms. Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems, mince the leaves, and add to bowl. Peel, mince, and add garlic to bowl.
    2 sticks celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Drain tuna and add to bowl.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  6. 6
    Drizzle tuna with lemon juice and olive oil and season with salt and pepper; toss to combine.
    juice of ½ lemon
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Wash and slice tomato into rounds.
    1 tomato
  8. 8
    Grate ½ cup of mozzarella.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    Remove the mushrooms from the oven. Divide tuna mixture evenly among the mushrooms and stuff inside. Top with tomato slices and sprinkle mozzarella over top.
  10. 10
    Return to oven and broil until cheese melts and begins to turn golden brown, 2-3 minutes.
  11. 11
    Wash and dry mixed greens.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  12. 12
    Arrange a bed of mixed greens on a plate and place mushroom caps on top. Enjoy!

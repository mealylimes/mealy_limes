Potato Latkes with Smoked Salmon, Yogurt, Dill & Celery-Apple Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (6) $3.10/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2eggs
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • 2 stickscelery
  • cornstarch
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    1 Granny Smith apple
    2 sticks celery
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Place eggs, bread crumbs, cornstarch, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the egg mixture.
    2 eggs
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    2 tsp cornstarch
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add dill to the bowl with the egg mixture (reserving a small handful for garnish).
  4. 4
    Peel potatoes; using a box grater, coarsely grate, and place in a colander. Using your hands, squeeze as much water from the potato as you can.
  5. 5
    Add grated potato to the bowl with the egg mixture and use your hands to thoroughly combine the latke mixture.
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, using a ⅓-cup measure, form the latke mixture into ½-inch thick patties; place on a plate.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add patties and cook until golden brown and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Remove latkes to a clean, paper towel-lined plate to drain.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, and salt in another medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Quarter, core, and thinly slice apple; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Trim and slice celery crosswise into thin pieces; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  13. 13
    Just before serving, add arugula to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    Divide latkes between plates and season with pepper; top with salmon, yogurt, and reserved dill. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!
    1 pinch black pepper
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt

Prosciutto, Mozzarella, Tomato, Pepper & Basil Sandwich with Arugula

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $3.29/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½lemon
  • 1tomato
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • mayonnaise
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lemon
    1 tomato
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Divide between a medium and small bowl. (Set the medium bowl aside for the salad.)
  3. 3
    Zest lemon into the small bowl with the basil, then add mayo and spices. Stir to combine the basil aioli and set aside. (Reserve lemon for later use.)
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Slice tomato crosswise into thin rounds. Place on a plate and season with salt and pepper.
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the plate with the tomato.
  6. 6
    Slice mozzarella into thin rounds. Add to the plate and set aside.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  7. 7
    Juice reserved lemon into the medium bowl with the basil. Add oil, maple syrup, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Drain and roughly chop artichoke hearts. Add to the bowl with the dressing, along with arugula, and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  9. 9
    Toast bread if desired.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    To assemble the sandwiches, spread each bread slice with basil aioli. Layer half of the slices with tomato, bell pepper, mozzarella, and prosciutto; close with remaining slices and cut in half.
    6 slices prosciutto
  11. 11
    To serve, divide sandwiches and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Prosciutto, Spinach & Cheddar Breakfast Egg Muffins

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (19) $1.57/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • 5eggs
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls muffin pan nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F and position a rack near the bottom of the oven; grease 6 muffin cups with butter.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  2. 2
    Chop the prosciutto into ½-inch pieces.
    4 slices prosciutto
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    Wash and chop the spinach. Add to the skillet and wilt for 30-60 seconds.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  5. 5
    Divide the prosciutto and spinach between the greased muffin tins.
  6. 6
    Crack the eggs into a medium bowl and season with pepper; whisk together and pour on top of the prosciutto and spinach.
    5 eggs
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Coarsely grate the cheddar and add to the muffin tins.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  8. 8
    Bake for 12-15 minutes, until eggs are no longer soft in the center. Remove from the oven and let cool for 1 minute. Use a spatula to wedge each egg muffin out of its cup.
  9. 9
    Serve warm or cool for breakfast on the go. Enjoy!

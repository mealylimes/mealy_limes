Prosciutto-Wrapped Asparagus with Herbed Cheese & Mashed Potatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $3.39/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 3 mediumyellow potatoes
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts potato masher stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 medium yellow potatoes
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small pkg chives
  3. 3
    Peel and medium dice potatoes; place in a medium saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Cover and bring to a boil over high heat; once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook until soft, 10-12 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the potatoes are cooking, snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until asparagus is just tender, 5-7 minutes, depending on the thickness of the spears. Remove baking sheet from oven and set aside to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a box grater, coarsely grate cream cheese into a small bowl.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the cream cheese.
  8. 8
    Chop chives into small pieces. Add to the bowl along with salt and pepper, then stir to combine.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Lay prosciutto on a flat surface and spread each slice evenly with the cream cheese mixture.
    6 slices prosciutto
  10. 10
    When the asparagus is cool enough to handle, divide into 6 equal piles. Wrap each pile with a slice of the prepared prosciutto, gently pressing the cream cheese-side around the asparagus to secure. Arrange, seam-side down, on the baking sheet.
  11. 11
    Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to roast until prosciutto is golden and asparagus is fork-tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  12. 12
    When the potatoes are done, drain in a colander and return to the pan. Add oil, salt, and pepper, then using a potato masher, mash until smooth.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide mashed potatoes and prosciutto-wrapped asparagus between plates. Enjoy!

Prosciutto-Wrapped Cod with Caper Aioli & Roasted Broccoli Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $5.03/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • 1lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Position racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    2 crowns broccoli
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic; mince capers. Place both in a small bowl.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp capers
  4. 4
    Zest lemon into the bowl with the garlic and capers (reserve lemon for the next step). Add mayo, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the aioli and set aside.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
  7. 7
    Transfer broccoli to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to combine; spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet on lower rack of the oven and roast, tossing halfway through, until broccoli is fork-tender and lightly charred, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    While the broccoli is roasting, pat cod dry with paper towels, cut into portions (if necessary), and transfer to another baking sheet. Season with pepper, then wrap 2 slices of prosciutto around each portion; drizzle with oil.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    4 slices prosciutto
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Place baking sheet on upper rack of the oven and roast until the fish is cooked through, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the parsley to the bowl with the dressing (reserving the rest for garnish).
  12. 12
    When the broccoli is done, add it to the bowl, along with the almonds, and toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced
  13. 13
    To serve, divide cod and broccoli salad between plates; top fish with aioli and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

Prosciutto-Wrapped Cod with Tomato, Corn, Peach & Goat Cheese Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $5.84/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • 2peaches
  • 2tomatoes
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 tomatoes
    2 peaches
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  2. 2
    Place oil, vinegar, salt, and crushed red pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  3. 3
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise, then cut into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Slice each quarter into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat cod dry with paper towels and cut into portions (if necessary). Season with salt and pepper, then wrap 2 slices of prosciutto around each portion.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    4 slices prosciutto
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add cod and cook until golden on one side, 3-4 minutes; flip and continue to pan-fry until the fish is cooked through and the prosciutto is crisp, 3-4 minutes more. Remove to a plate.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place corn in the colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to defrost. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes and peaches.
    1 cup frozen corn
  9. 9
    Pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems and tear the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Crumble goat cheese into the bowl, then gently stir to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  11. 11
    To serve, divide prosciutto-wrapped cod and salad between plates. Enjoy!

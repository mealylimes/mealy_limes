Prosciutto-Wrapped Peaches over Arugula, Couscous & Goat Cheese Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 25% (1) $6.36/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblackberries
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1shallot
  • 1avocado
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½orange
  • 2peaches
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ orange
    2 peaches
    1 avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    1 (6 oz) pkg blackberries
  3. 3
    Peel and mince shallot and garlic; place both in a medium bowl.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Zest and juice orange into the bowl with the shallot and garlic. Add oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the grill pan heats up, halve peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Wrap one slice of prosciutto around each peach half.
    4 slices prosciutto
  7. 7
    Once the grill pan is hot, place peaches on the grill, cut-side down. Cook, turning halfway through, until peaches are warmed through and prosciutto is golden and crispy, about 5 minutes. Remove to a plate and set aside.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and small dice the flesh. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Add cooked couscous, arugula, and blackberries to the bowl. Gently toss to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide salad and peaches between plates or bowls. Crumble goat cheese over top and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese

Pumpkin Spice Oatmeal with Cinnamon-Sugar Apples & Pecans

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (9) $1.66/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • pure maple syrup
  • brown sugar
  • 1Gala apple
  • ½ cuppecan halves
  • 8 fl ozalmond milk, unsweetened
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
can opener chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Add salt and water to the saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, stir in oats, cinnamon, and maple syrup; reduce heat to medium-low. Cover and let simmer for 3-5 minutes or until oats reach desired consistency.
    1 pinch salt
    1 ¾ cup water
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
    ½ tsp cinnamon
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
  2. 2
    Combine additional cinnamon and sugar in a small bowl.
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
    1 tbsp brown sugar
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, and small dice the apple.
    1 Gala apple
  4. 4
    Add apple to the bowl with the cinnamon-sugar and toss to combine.
  5. 5
    Roughly chop pecans.
    ½ cup pecans, dry roasted
  6. 6
    When oats are done cooking, stir in pumpkin purée.
    ½ (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
  7. 7
    To serve, divide oats between bowls and top with cinnamon-sugar apples, pecans, and milk. Enjoy!
    8 fl oz (1 cup) almond milk, unsweetened

Pumpkin Spice Smoothie with Banana & Fresh Ginger

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 55% (10) $1.21/serving
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • 1 (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • pure maple syrup
  • 12 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1banana
  • nutmeg, ground
  • cardamom, ground
  • cloves, ground
Cookware
can opener chef's knife cutting board grater (optional) hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger; place in a blender.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  2. 2
    Peel the banana, break into chunks, and add to the blender.
    1 banana
  3. 3
    Add pumpkin purée, milk, maple syrup, spices, and 4 ice cubes to the blender.
    1 (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) whole milk
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 tsp cinnamon, ground
    ½ tsp nutmeg, ground
    ¼ tsp cardamom, ground
    ¼ tsp cloves, ground
  4. 4
    Blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
  5. 5
    Pour the smoothie into glasses. Top with a sprinkle of cinnamon and enjoy!
    1 pinch cinnamon, ground

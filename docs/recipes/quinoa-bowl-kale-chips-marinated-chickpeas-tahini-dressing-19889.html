Quinoa Bowl with Kale Chips, Marinated Chickpeas & Tahini Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $1.98/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1lemon
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ bunchkale
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • ¼ cupnutritional yeast flakes
  • tahini
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 2
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ bunch kale
    1 lemon
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ bunch radish
  4. 4
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; chop or tear leaves into large bite-sized pieces and place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Add nutritional yeast, oil, vinegar, and salt to the bowl with the kale; using your hands, toss the leaves well to coat. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in a single layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    ¼ cup nutritional yeast flakes
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp apple cider vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing once, until the kale is crispy, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse chickpeas; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  8. 8
    Zest and juice half of the lemon into the reserved bowl. (Reserve remaining lemon for the dressing.)
  9. 9
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice along with oil, maple syrup, Dijon, and spices; stir to combine the marinade. Add chickpeas, stir to combine, and set aside to marinate.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  10. 10
    Peel and mince additional garlic; place in a small bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Juice the reserved lemon into the bowl with the garlic. Add tahini, water, maple syrup, Dijon, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    3 tbsp tahini
    2 tbsp water
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces.
  13. 13
    Trim and thinly slice radishes.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide quinoa, kale chips, chickpeas, cucumber, and radish between bowls. Drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing and enjoy!

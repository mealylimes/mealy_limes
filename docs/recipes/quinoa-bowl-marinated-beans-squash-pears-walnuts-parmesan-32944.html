Quinoa Bowl with Marinated Beans, Squash, Pears, Walnuts & Parmesan

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $3.31/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ smallbutternut squash
  • ½shallot
  • ½lemon
  • 1pear
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa, water, and salt; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    1 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small butternut squash
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 pear
  4. 4
    Trim and peel squash; halve lengthwise, scrape out the seeds, and medium dice the flesh. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until squash is golden and fork-tender, about 25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ shallot
  7. 7
    Zest and juice lemon into the bowl with the shallot.
  8. 8
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Drain and rinse beans. Add to the bowl with the dressing, stir to combine, and set aside to marinate.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  10. 10
    Roughly chop walnuts. Place on a plate.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  11. 11
    Using a peeler, shave Parmesan into curls. Add to the plate with the walnuts.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  12. 12
    Quarter, core, and medium dice pear. Add to the plate.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide quinoa between bowls. Top with marinated beans, roasted squash, pear, walnuts, and Parmesan. Enjoy!

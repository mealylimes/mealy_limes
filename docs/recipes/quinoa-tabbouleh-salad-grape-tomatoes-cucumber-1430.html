Quinoa Tabbouleh Salad with Grape Tomatoes & Cucumber

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (13) $4.06/serving
Ingredients
  • salt
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cumin, ground
  • black pepper
  • ¾ cupquinoa
  • 1lemon
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1English cucumber
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) small saucepan whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ¾ cup quinoa
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic. In a large bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together garlic, lemon juice, oil, salt, pepper, and cumin.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 lemon
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cumin
  3. 3
    Wash and dry tomatoes, cucumber, parsley, and mint. Halve and add tomatoes to bowl with dressing. Medium dice and add cucumber to bowl. Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems, mince the leaves, and add to bowl. Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard the stems, mince the leaves, and add to bowl.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 English cucumber
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  4. 4
    Uncover quinoa, fluff with a fork, and drain any excess liquid. Add quinoa to bowl and toss salad.
  5. 5
    To serve, place salad in a bowl. Enjoy!

Quinoa Tabbouleh Salad with Tomato, Parsley & Mint

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 91% (8) $2.01/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½shallot
  • cumin, ground
  • 1lemon
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ⅓ cupquinoa
  • 1tomato
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa, water, and salt; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ⅓ cup quinoa
    ⅔ cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 tomato
    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice along with oil and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Small dice tomato. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl, stir to combine, and set aside.
  8. 8
    When the quinoa is done, transfer to a baking sheet pan. Spread out in an even layer and let cool for 5 minutes. Once cool, add to the bowl with the other ingredients and stir to combine the tabbouleh.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide tabbouleh between plates or bowls and enjoy!

Rainbow Mango Salad with Edamame & Thai Sesame Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $4.03/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • crushed red pepper
  • ½ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • ½shallot
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • rice vinegar
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    1 mango
    ½ lime
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Place frozen edamame in a medium bowl, cover with hot water (from the tap), and let sit 5 minutes. Drain in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 cup edamame
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice the shallot into half rings; place in a medium salad bowl.
    ½ shallot
  4. 4
    Trim, peel, and coarsely grate the carrots; add to the bowl with the shallots.
  5. 5
    Halve the sugar snap peas at an angle and add to the salad bowl.
  6. 6
    Halve the tomatoes and add to the salad bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel the mango and slice the flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice and place in the salad bowl along with the edamame.
  8. 8
    Juice lime over the veggies in the salad bowl.
  9. 9
    Coarsely chop the cashews.
    ½ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  10. 10
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard the stems and coarsely chop the leaves.
  11. 11
    Combine vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and crushed red pepper in a small bowl; whisk to combine.
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp sesame seeds
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  12. 12
    To serve, divide salad between plates, top with cashews and chopped mint. Drizzle with dressing and enjoy!

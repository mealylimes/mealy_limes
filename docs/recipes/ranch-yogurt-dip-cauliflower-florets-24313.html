Ranch Yogurt Dip with Cauliflower Florets

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 33% (3) $0.75/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • garlic powder
  • parsley flakes
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½lime
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, and mince chives. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ small pkg chives
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and juice lime into the bowl with the chives.
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Add yogurt, mayo, and spices to the bowl; whisk to combine the ranch dip.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp parsley flakes
    ¼ tsp dill, dried
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    To serve, divide dip and cauliflower florets between plates. Enjoy!
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets

"Ratatouille" Bruschetta with Eggplant, Zucchini, Bell Pepper & Tomato

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (19) $3.28/serving
Ingredients
  • 1eggplant
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 4 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 eggplant
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and thinly slice garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with tomato sauce, oil, and spices. Stir to combine the sauce.
    1 clove garlic
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Trim and medium dice eggplant and zucchini. Add both to the bowl with the sauce.
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add half to the bowl and stir to combine the ratatouille veggies. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  7. 7
    Transfer ratatouille veggies to a large baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place oil and spices in a small bowl. Stir to combine the spice oil.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Arrange bread on another baking sheet and drizzle with spice oil on both sides.
    4 slices sourdough bread
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, place bread in the oven and bake, flipping halfway through, until toasted, 5-6 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  12. 12
    To serve, divide toast between plates, then top with ratatouille, Parmesan, and reserved parsley. Enjoy!

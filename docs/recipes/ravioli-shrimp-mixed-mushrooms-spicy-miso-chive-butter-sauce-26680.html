Ravioli with Shrimp, Mixed Mushrooms & Spicy Miso-Chive Butter Sauce

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (10) $4.41/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • 9 ozcheese ravioli, fresh or frozen
  • 30 mlmiso paste, white
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and ravioli, and stir for a few seconds. Cook, stirring occasionally, until just tender, about 5 minutes (or cook according to package instructions). Drain and set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    9 oz cheese ravioli, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, wash, dry, and remove stems from portobello mushrooms; slice into ¼-inch thick strips.
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sliced portobello and white mushrooms to the skillet along with salt and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and chop chives into small pieces. Place in a small bowl along with miso paste, water, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ½ small pkg chives
    2 tbsp miso paste, white
    2 tbsp water
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  6. 6
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  7. 7
    When the mushrooms are soft, add shrimp to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until the shrimp are opaque and cooked through, 3-4 minutes.
  8. 8
    Add drained ravioli, miso sauce, and more butter to the skillet. Stir until butter is melted and pasta is coated in sauce, about 1 minute; remove from heat.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    To serve, divide ravioli, shrimp, and mushrooms between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Ravioli Skillet Bake with Deli Turkey, Zucchini & Mushrooms

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $1.08/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • 9 ozcheese ravioli, fresh or frozen
  • 8 ozoven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and ravioli, and stir for a few seconds. Cook, stirring occasionally, until just tender, about 5 minutes (or cook according to package instructions). Drain and set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    9 oz cheese ravioli, fresh or frozen
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, mushrooms, Italian seasoning, and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and medium dice zucchini.
    1 medium zucchini squash
  7. 7
    When the veggies are soft, add zucchini to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until the veggies are tender, 2-3 minutes more.
  8. 8
    While the zucchini is cooking, roughly chop turkey into bite-sized pieces.
    8 oz oven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  9. 9
    Wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; roll up crosswise and thinly slice into ribbons.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, add the turkey, half of the basil, and marinara sauce to the skillet. Stir to combine and continue to cook until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
  11. 11
    Add ravioli to the skillet, stir to combine, and sprinkle with cheese. Place in the oven and broil until cheese is melted and bubbly, 4-5 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  12. 12
    To serve, divide ravioli bake between plates or bowls and sprinkle with reserved basil. Enjoy!

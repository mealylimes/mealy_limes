Red Grape Granita (Shaved Fruit Ice) with a Hint of Lime

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $0.92/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 lbred grapes
  • ½lime
  • granulated sugar
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons plastic wrap silicone spatula strainer
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry grapes; transfer to a blender.
    1 lb red grapes
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and juice lime into the blender.
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Add sugar and blend on high speed for several minutes until smooth.
    ⅓ cup granulated sugar
  4. 4
    Pour grape pureé into a strainer placed over a medium bowl. Using a silicone spatula, press pureé through the strainer to remove skins; discard skins.
  5. 5
    Transfer strained grape pureé to a small baking dish. Cover with plastic wrap and place in the freezer. Let chill until completely frozen, about 8 hours (or overnight).
  6. 6
    Once the pureé is completely frozen, remove from the freezer. Using a fork, scrape the ice from end to end, to form shards. Re-cover with plastic wrap and return to the freezer until ready to serve.
  7. 7
    To serve, divide granita between bowls and enjoy!

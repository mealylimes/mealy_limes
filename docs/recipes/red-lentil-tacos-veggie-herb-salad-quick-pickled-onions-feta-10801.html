Red Lentil Tacos with Veggie-Herb Salad, Quick Pickled Onions & Feta

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (4) $4.41/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1lemon
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 4small flour tortillas
  • 2tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • red lentils, dried
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and slice half the onion into thin half-rings. Small dice remaining onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  2. 2
    Place sliced onion in a small bowl along with vinegar and salt. Toss to combine and set aside.
    2 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Preheat a small saucepan over medium heat. Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add diced onion and garlic to the pan; cook, stirring frequently, until onion begins to soften, 3-4 minutes.
  5. 5
    Rinse lentils under cold water and add to the pan along with the broth. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to a simmer and cook until tender, 15-20 minutes.
    ½ cup red lentils
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    ½ English cucumber
    2 tomatoes
  7. 7
    Juice lemon into a large bowl. Add olive oil and salt; stir to combine.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard the stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Roughly chop arugula and add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Trim and small dice cucumber and tomatoes; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Halve olives and add to bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  13. 13
    Once lentils are done, drain off excess liquid and return to pan. Add paprika and salt; stir to combine.
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
  14. 14
    To serve, divide tortillas between plates and fill with lentils. Top with salad, pickled onions, and feta. Enjoy!
    4 small flour tortillas
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

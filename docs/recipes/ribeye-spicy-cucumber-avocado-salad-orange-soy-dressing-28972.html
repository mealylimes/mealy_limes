Ribeye with Spicy Cucumber-Avocado Salad & Orange Soy Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $5.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½orange
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • ½ bunchradish
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ orange
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ avocado
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Zest and juice orange into a medium bowl. Add oil, soy sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the dressing and stir to combine.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Halve jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Slice crosswise into thin half-moons and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  8. 8
    Trim and slice cucumber crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim and slice radishes into thin rounds. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad between plates and top with steak. Enjoy!

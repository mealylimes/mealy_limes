Rich Cauliflower Mac & Cheese with Sausage

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (14) $2.63/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • butter, unsalted
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • 1pork sausage
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash cauliflower and cut off the leaves. Separate into small florets and cut the thick stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a medium saucepan.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
  2. 2
    Add hot water (from the tap) to the pan, so that the cauliflower is just covered. Bring to a boil over high heat. Cook until fork-tender, 4-5 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, roughly chop the sausage.
    1 pork sausage
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add the sausage. Cook, stirring often, until browned and cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Once done, transfer to a plate and reduce heat to low.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, wash, dry, and chop chives into small pieces; set aside.
    ½ small pkg chives
  7. 7
    Coarsely grate cheddar. Finely grate Parmesan.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Melt more butter in skillet. Add garlic and stir until fragrant, 1-2 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    Stir in cream, Dijon, paprika, and pepper.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    When the cream is warmed, add cheddar and Parmesan. Cook, stirring well, until the cheese is melted and the mixture is smooth.
  12. 12
    Add cauliflower to the cheese sauce and stir until well coated. Sprinkle with sausage and chives. Enjoy!

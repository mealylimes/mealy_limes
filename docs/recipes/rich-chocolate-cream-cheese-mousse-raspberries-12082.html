Rich Chocolate Cream Cheese Mousse with Raspberries

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (9) $0.92/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • cocoa powder
  • powdered sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
colander grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls plastic wrap silicone spatula strainer whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill. (If space is limited, place in the refrigerator.)
  2. 2
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate cream cheese into another medium bowl; add the vanilla. Use a silicone spatula or fork to soften and combine the cream cheese mixture, creating a smooth cream.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
  3. 3
    Using a strainer, sift the powdered sugar and cocoa powder into the bowl with the cream cheese. Use the spatula or fork to mash and combine the mixture until smooth and no lumps remain. Set aside.
    ⅓ cup powdered sugar
    4 tsp cocoa powder
  4. 4
    Remove bowl from the freezer. Pour cold cream into the bowl; use a whisk to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, about 2 minutes. (For medium-soft peaks, when you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.)
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy cream
  5. 5
    Transfer half the whipped cream to the bowl with the cream cheese mixture. Using a silicone spatula, gently fold the two together until almost combined. Add remaining whipped cream and continue to fold until just combined.
  6. 6
    Divide the mousse between small bowls, cups, or jars. Cover with plastic wrap or lids and refrigerate until set, about 1 hour (or overnight).
  7. 7
    Right before serving, wash and dry the raspberries.
    ½ (6 oz) pkg raspberries
  8. 8
    To serve, divide raspberries between mousse cups and sift powdered sugar over the top. Enjoy!
    ¼ tsp powdered sugar

Ricotta Flatbread with Roast Strawberries, Prosciutto & Arugula Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 58% (3) $2.65/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½shallot
  • ½lemon
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • 2 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
baking dish baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 375°F. Place oven racks in the upper and middle positions.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ½ lemon
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Trim and quarter strawberries lengthwise. Transfer to a small baking dish and spread out in an even layer. Drizzle with honey, then season with salt and pepper.
    1 tsp honey
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking dish on the upper rack of the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until strawberries are soft, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool slightly.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, zest lemon into a small bowl. Add ricotta, salt, and pepper, then stir to combine; set aside. (Reserve lemon for later use.)
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    When the strawberries have about 10 minutes left, arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Place on the lower rack and bake, flipping halfway through, until flatbreads are slightly crispy, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ shallot
  8. 8
    Juice reserved lemon into the bowl with the shallot. Add oil, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat. Set aside.
  10. 10
    Chop (or tear) prosciutto into bite-sized pieces.
    2 slices prosciutto
  11. 11
    Spread the lemon ricotta over each flatbread, then top with roasted strawberries and prosciutto. Cut flatbreads into wedges.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide flatbreads and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Roast Chicken Thighs with Tomato-Zucchini Couscous & Goat Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (10) $6.49/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    In a medium saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous and salt, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lemon
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and large dice onion; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Trim and quarter zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces; add to the baking sheet with the onion, along with the tomatoes.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic; sprinkle over the veggies, then drizzle with oil and season with spices. Toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 cloves garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
  7. 7
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides. Add chicken to the baking sheet and nestle into the veggies.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast until veggies are tender and chicken is cooked through, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, roughly chop olives; pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add both to the pan with the couscous.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  10. 10
    Juice lemon into the pan and stir to combine. Cover to keep warm.
  11. 11
    Just before serving, transfer roasted veggies to the saucepan with the couscous and stir to combine.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide veggie couscous and chicken thighs between plates; crumble goat cheese over couscous. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese

Roast Pepper-Pork Patties with Carrot, Cucumber, Almond & Dill Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $1.12/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½English cucumber
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers. Divide between two medium bowls. (Set one bowl aside for the salad.)
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  2. 2
    Add pork, bread crumbs, egg, salt, and pepper to the first bowl with the peppers. Use your hands to combine the pork mixture. Using a ¼-cup measure, form the mixture into ½-inch thick patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add patties and cook, flipping once, until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the second bowl with the peppers.
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, trim, and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
    ½ English cucumber
  7. 7
    Add carrots, almonds, and vinaigrette to the bowl, then stir to combine the salad.
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette
  8. 8
    To serve, divide patties and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Roast Potatoes with Bell Pepper, Chicken, Swiss Chard & Paprika Sauce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $2.56/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ¾ lbnew potatoes
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ lbground chicken
  • ½ bunchSwiss chard
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb new potatoes
    1 orange bell pepper
    ½ bunch Swiss chard
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Halve new potatoes. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and large dice bell pepper; add to the baking sheet with the potatoes.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and large dice onion; add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil, then season with salt and pepper; stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 medium yellow onion
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until potatoes are golden and fork-tender, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, place yogurt, mayo, water, and spices in a small bowl. Stir to combine the sauce and set aside.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp water
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, fold chard leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips and thinly slice the stems, keeping them separate. Place leaves in another small bowl, reserving stems for the next step. (Set leaves aside for later use.)
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chard stems, chicken, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    ¼ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves.
  12. 12
    When the chicken is done, add chard leaves to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the leaves to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  13. 13
    Remove skillet from the heat, add half of the cilantro, and stir to combine. Cover with a lid to keep warm. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  14. 14
    To serve, divide roasted veggies and chicken sauté between plates or bowls. Drizzle with sauce and top with reserved cilantro. Enjoy!

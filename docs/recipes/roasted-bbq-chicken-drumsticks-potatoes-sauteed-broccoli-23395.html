Roasted BBQ Chicken Drumsticks & Potatoes with Sautéed Broccoli

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (45) $1.88/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • ½ lbnew potatoes
  • barbecue sauce
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean-up easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and halve potatoes; transfer to the baking sheet. Drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Toss to coat and spread out on one half of the baking sheet.
    ½ lb new potatoes
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Coat the other half of the baking sheet with a thin layer of oil. Arrange chicken on the oiled half of the baking sheet and drizzle with barbecue sauce.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
    ¼ cup barbecue sauce
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing potatoes and flipping chicken halfway through, until potatoes are fork-tender and chicken is cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    When the chicken is almost done, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add additional oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add broccoli and salt to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    To serve, divide chicken, potatoes, and broccoli between plates. Enjoy!

Roasted Carrot-Onion Couscous Bowl with Kale & Turmeric Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (49) $2.99/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • honey
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 3 mediumcarrots
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ bunchkale
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • tahini
  • ⅛ cupmedjool dates, pitted
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    3 medium carrots
    ½ lemon
    ½ bunch kale
  3. 3
    Peel, trim, and quarter carrots lengthwise, then cut crosswise into 3-inch long sticks. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the baking sheet with the carrots, drizzle with oil, and season with salt. Toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    1 medium red onion
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are golden brown and tender, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    While the veggies are roasting, in a small saucepan, bring broth to a boil. Remove from heat, add couscous, and stir. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a small bowl. Add tahini, water, honey, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp tahini
    2 tbsp water
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces. Place in a medium bowl and sprinkle with salt.
    1 pinch salt
  9. 9
    Add half of the dressing to the bowl with the kale and, using your hands, massage kale with the dressing until wilted, 1-2 minutes. (Reserve remaining dressing for serving.)
  10. 10
    Roughly chop dates. Transfer to a plate.
    ⅛ cup medjool dates, pitted
  11. 11
    Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the plate with the dates.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
  12. 12
    Crumble goat cheese onto the plate.
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide couscous between bowls; top with kale, roasted veggies, dates, pistachios, and goat cheese. Drizzle with reserved dressing and enjoy!

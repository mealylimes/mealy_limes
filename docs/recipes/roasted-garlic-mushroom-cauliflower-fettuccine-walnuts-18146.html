Roasted Garlic, Mushroom & Cauliflower Fettuccine with Walnuts

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (9) $3.03/serving
Ingredients
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½lemon
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 1tomato
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 tomato
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Quarter mushrooms and add to the baking sheet with the cauliflower. Drizzle with oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to combine; spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Peel garlic, place in the center of a small square of aluminum foil, and drizzle with oil; fold foil around garlic to close the packet. Add to the baking sheet with the veggies.
    4 cloves garlic
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing veggies halfway through, until golden brown and tender (and garlic is soft), 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    While the veggies are roasting, fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain and return pasta to the pan. (Reserve ½-cup pasta cooking water.)
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
  9. 9
    Small dice tomato; add to the bowl with the parsley.
  10. 10
    Finely grate Parmesan; add to the bowl.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Once the garlic is ready, carefully open the packet; use a fork to mash the roasted garlic. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Juice lemon into the pan with the pasta; add roasted veggies, parsley, tomato, Parmesan, roasted garlic, and reserved pasta water. Return to low heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta is heated through and cheese is melted, 2-3 minutes.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, roughly chop walnuts.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and top with chopped walnuts. Enjoy!

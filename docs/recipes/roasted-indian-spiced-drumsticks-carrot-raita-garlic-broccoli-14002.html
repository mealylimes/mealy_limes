Roasted Indian-Spiced Drumsticks with Carrot Raita & Garlic Broccoli

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $2.63/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ¾ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • cumin, ground
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • nutmeg, ground
  • cardamom, ground
  • cloves, ground
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Place spices in a large bowl and stir to combine.
    ¾ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp cardamom, ground
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp clove, ground
    ⅛ tsp nutmeg, ground
  3. 3
    Add chicken to the bowl with the spice mix and toss to thoroughly coat; transfer to the baking sheet.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until chicken is golden brown and cooked through, 30-35 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    1 crown broccoli
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot; transfer to a small bowl. Add yogurt, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the raita and set aside.
    ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; place in a medium bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, trim green onions and slice crosswise into ¼-inch pieces.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add ginger, garlic, green onions, and salt; cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Add broccoli and water to the skillet; cook, stirring often, until the broccoli is bright green and fork-tender, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp water
  13. 13
    To serve, divide spiced drumsticks, broccoli, and carrot raita between plates. Enjoy!

Roasted Pineapple Sundae with Vanilla Ice Cream & Caramel Sauce

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $0.74/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • honey
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • brown sugar
  • 1 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • cardamom, ground
  • cloves, ground
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife cutting board grater ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry pineapple. Cut off skin, quarter, core, and cut into ½-inch pieces; transfer to a small baking dish.
    ½ small pineapple
  3. 3
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate cold butter. Sprinkle butter, sugar, clove, and cardamom over the pineapple; stir to combine, then spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    1 pinch clove, ground
    1 pinch cardamom, ground
  4. 4
    Place pineapple in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender and lightly caramelized, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool slightly.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, melt additional butter in a small saucepan over low heat.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  6. 6
    Once butter is melted, whisk in cream, sugar, honey, and salt. Increase heat to medium and simmer, whisking frequently, until caramel sauce is slightly thickened and glossy, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool slightly to further thicken the sauce.
    1 fl oz (⅛ cup) heavy cream
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    1 tbsp honey
    1 pinch salt
  7. 7
    To serve, place one scoop of ice cream in the bottom of each bowl; top with half the caramel sauce and the roasted pineapple. Place a second scoop of ice cream on top of the pineapple, drizzle with remaining caramel sauce, and enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

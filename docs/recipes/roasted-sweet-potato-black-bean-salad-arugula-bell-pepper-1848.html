Roasted Sweet Potato & Black Bean Salad with Arugula & Bell Pepper

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $2.26/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • ½lime
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • Dijon mustard
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • honey
  • red wine vinegar
  • salt
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, peel, and medium dice potatoes. Transfer to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat.
    ½ lb sweet potatoes
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place in the oven and roast until tender, 15-20 minutes.
  4. 4
    Drain and rinse beans. Place on paper towels and pat dry.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic. In a small bowl, prepare dressing by whisking together garlic, lime juice, olive oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, cumin, salt, and pepper.
    1 clove garlic
    juice of ½ lime
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp cumin
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Wash and dry arugula.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  7. 7
    Wash, seed, and medium dice bell pepper.
    1 red bell pepper
  8. 8
    To serve, arrange a bed of arugula on a plate, top with potatoes, beans, and bell pepper. Drizzle with dressing and enjoy!

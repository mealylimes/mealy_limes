Roasted Sweet Potato Wedges with Garlicky Parsley-Tahini Sauce

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (4) $0.62/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ¼ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lemon
  • tahini
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender measuring spoons oven mitts silicone spatula tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    ½ lemon
    ¼ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and cut each sweet potato lengthwise into 6-8 wedges, depending on size, then transfer to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp cumin, ground
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, flipping halfway through, until potatoes are golden brown and fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, juice lemon into a blender. (Alternatively, you can prepare the sauce using a food processor.)
  6. 6
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the blender.
  7. 7
    Peel garlic. Add to the blender along with tahini, water, and salt. Blend on high until smooth, adding additional water, 1 tablespoon at a time, if needed, to reach a drizzling consistency.
    1 clove garlic
    3 tbsp tahini
    2 tbsp water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Divide sweet potato wedges between plates. Serve with tahini sauce for dipping. Enjoy!

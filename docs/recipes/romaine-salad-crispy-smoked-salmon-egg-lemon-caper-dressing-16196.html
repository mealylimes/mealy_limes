Romaine Salad with Crispy Smoked Salmon, Egg & Lemon-Caper Dressing

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 91% (8) $2.25/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • ½shallot
  • 1avocado
  • ½lemon
  • 1tomato
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ bunch radish
    1 tomato
    1 avocado
    1 head romaine lettuce
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl with the lemon zest.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Mince capers; add to the bowl along with cream, mayo, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    ½ tbsp capers
    1 fl oz (⅛ cup) heavy whipping cream
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches if necessary, add smoked salmon slices to the skillet in a single layer; cook, flipping once, until crispy, about 4 minutes. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and thinly slice shallot; add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Trim and thinly slice radishes; add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Medium dice tomato and add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Halve avocado and remove pit; scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Peel and slice eggs crosswise into thin rounds. Chop or tear crispy smoked salmon into bite-sized pieces.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with eggs and crispy smoked salmon. Enjoy!

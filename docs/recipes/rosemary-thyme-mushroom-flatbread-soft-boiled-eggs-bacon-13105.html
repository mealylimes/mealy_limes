Rosemary-Thyme Mushroom Flatbread with Soft-Boiled Eggs & Bacon

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $3.43/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • black pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 2eggs
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1 lbcremini mushrooms
  • 2 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon small saucepan stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry mushrooms; roughly chop into small pieces and place in a medium bowl. Set aside.
    1 lb cremini mushrooms
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Add bacon to the skillet and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side.
    2 slices bacon
  5. 5
    While the bacon is cooking, peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Once the bacon is cooked, transfer to a plate lined with paper towels; pat to remove excess oil.
  7. 7
    Return skillet (with bacon fat) to the stove over medium heat.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-5 minutes.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    Add mushrooms, garlic, vinegar, and spices to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms have softened, 5-7 minutes.
    1 tsp balsamic vinegar
    ¼ tsp rosemary
    ¼ tsp thyme
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    While the mushrooms are cooking, toast flatbreads (if desired).
    2 flatbreads or naan
  12. 12
    Peel and halve eggs.
  13. 13
    Chop or crumble bacon into small pieces.
  14. 14
    To serve, place a flatbread on each plate. Use a slotted spoon to divide mushrooms between flatbreads, then top with bacon and eggs. Enjoy!

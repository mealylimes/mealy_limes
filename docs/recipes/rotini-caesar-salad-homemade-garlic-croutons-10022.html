Rotini Caesar Salad with Homemade Garlic Croutons

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (14) $1.98/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 slicewhole grain bread
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2anchovy fillets, packed in oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet salad spinner (optional) stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    4 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, cut bread into ¾-inch cubes.
    1 slice whole grain bread
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Add bread cubes to the skillet and sprinkle with spices. Toast the bread, tossing occasionally, until croutons are golden brown, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    1 head romaine lettuce
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  7. 7
    Grate Parmesan into a large bowl.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic; add to bowl with the Parmesan.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Mince anchovy fillets and add to the bowl.
    2 anchovy fillets, packed in oil
  10. 10
    Juice lemon into the bowl and add mayonnaise, oil, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Chop or tear romaine leaves into bite-sized pieces and transfer to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Halve tomatoes crosswise and add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Once the pasta is done, drain and rinse under cool water. Add pasta and croutons to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide the pasta salad between bowls and enjoy!

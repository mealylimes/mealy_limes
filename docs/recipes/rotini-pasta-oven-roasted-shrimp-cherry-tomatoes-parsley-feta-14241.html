Rotini Pasta with Oven-Roasted Shrimp, Cherry Tomatoes, Parsley & Feta

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (7) $5.52/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1shallot
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Transfer shrimp to a large baking dish, spreading them out in an even layer; season with spices.
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Peel and thinly slice garlic. Peel, halve, and thinly slice shallot. Add both to the baking dish with the shrimp.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Wash and dry tomatoes; cut in half crosswise and add to the baking dish.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  7. 7
    Add oil and capers to the baking dish, toss to combine, then spread everything out in an even layer.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp capers
  8. 8
    Sprinkle feta over the top of the shrimp and tomato mixture.
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  9. 9
    Place baking dish in the oven and roast until tomatoes are soft and shrimp are cooked through, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Set aside.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  11. 11
    When the pasta is done, drain in a colander. Return pasta to the saucepan, cover, and set aside to keep warm.
  12. 12
    When the shrimp and tomato mixture is done, carefully transfer to the pan with the pasta; add parsley and gently stir to combine.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and enjoy!

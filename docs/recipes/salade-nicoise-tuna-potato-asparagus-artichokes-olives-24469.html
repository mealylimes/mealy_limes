"Salade Niçoise" with Tuna, Potato, Asparagus, Artichokes & Olives

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $5.13/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 3eggs
  • ½shallot
  • 1lemon
  • ½ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ lbnew potatoes
  • ½ bunchradish
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb new potatoes
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ bunch radish
    1 lemon
  2. 2
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    3 eggs
  3. 3
    While the eggs are cooking, halve potatoes. Place in a medium saucepan, along with salt, and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil, reduce heat to medium, and simmer until starting to soften, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, snap (or cut) off the woody ends of asparagus; cut spears in half crosswise. Once the potatoes start to soften, add asparagus to the pan and continue to cook until veggies are tender, 2-3 minutes more. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool.
  5. 5
    While the veggies are cooking, trim and halve radishes lengthwise. Transfer to a plate.
  6. 6
    Drain artichokes. Add to the plate with the radish.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  7. 7
    Halve olives lengthwise and add to the plate. Set aside.
    ½ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  8. 8
    Peel and mince shallot. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the shallot. Add oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    When potatoes and asparagus are cool, return to the pan and drizzle with half of the dressing. Gently toss to coat and set aside. (Reserve remaining dressing for serving.)
  11. 11
    Drain tuna, place in another small bowl, and flake with a fork.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  12. 12
    Peel and halve eggs. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  13. 13
    To serve, divide tuna, eggs, potatoes, asparagus, radishes, artichokes, and olives between plates or bowls. Top with capers, drizzle with remaining dressing, and enjoy!
    1 tbsp capers

Salmon, Avocado & Cucumber Lettuce Wraps with Garlic-Yogurt Sauce

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (6) $6.14/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1avocado
  • ½lemon
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clovegarlic
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
    ½ English cucumber
    1 avocado
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  2. 2
    Trim and discard the ends of the cucumber; halve the cucumber lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise on an angle. Transfer to a large bowl.
  3. 3
    Halve and pit the avocado; scoop out the flesh and thinly slice crosswise. Add to the bowl.
  4. 4
    Juice the lemon into a small bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Place the salmon on a plate; drizzle with ½ of the lemon juice (save the rest for the sauce), and season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Add olive oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Place the salmon in the skillet; cook until it is opaque and cooked through, 3 to 5 minutes per side (depending on the thickness). Once done, transfer to a plate.
  9. 9
    Separate the lettuce into leaves and place on a plate.
  10. 10
    Trim and discard the root ends of the green onions; chop the onions into ¼-inch pieces. Add about ½ of the onions to the bowl with the remaining lemon juice and the other ½ to the large bowl.
  11. 11
    Peel and mince the garlic; add to the small bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  12. 12
    Add yogurt, olive oil, salt, and pepper to the small bowl and mix together.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Using your hands or a fork, flake the salmon and add to the large bowl. Toss the ingredients together.
  14. 14
    To serve, make the lettuce wraps by spooning the salmon mixture into the lettuce leaves, then topping with the yogurt sauce. Enjoy!

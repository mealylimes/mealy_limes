Salmon Caesar Salad with Homemade Parmesan Croutons

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (9) $4.28/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1whole grain bun or roll
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Dijon mustard
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2anchovy fillets, packed in oil
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Trim off and discard the root end of the lettuce and remove any blemished leaves; halve the head lengthwise, then chop crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Rinse and spin dry in a salad spinner or rinse in a colander, then transfer to a clean towel and pat dry. Transfer to a large salad bowl.
    1 head romaine lettuce
  2. 2
    Trim off and discard the root ends of the garlic; peel and mince or press the garlic. Add ½ of the garlic to a medium bowl (that will hold the croutons) and the other ½ to a separate medium bowl (that will hold the dressing).
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  4. 4
    Add olive oil, salt, and pepper to the garlic in the crouton bowl.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Cut the bun or roll into ¾-inch cubes; transfer to the bowl with the garlic-oil mixture and toss to coat.
    1 whole grain bun or roll
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add the croutons and cook, tossing frequently, until golden brown, 5 to 7 minutes. Once done, transfer back to the bowl and set aside.
  7. 7
    While the croutons cook, pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb salmon fillets
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the croutons have been transferred, wipe the skillet with a paper towel to remove any remaining crumbs. Add olive oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Place the salmon fillets, skin-side-down, in the skillet; cook until the flesh is opaque and flaky, 3 to 5 minutes per side (depending on thickness). Once done, transfer to a plate and cover with foil to keep warm.
  10. 10
    Finely grate about 1 cup of Parmesan and divide into thirds. Sprinkle ⅓ of the Parmesan over the croutons and toss to coat, add another ⅓ to the dressing bowl with the garlic, and transfer the remaining ⅓ to a small bowl (for garnishing).
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Wash and dry the lemon. Juice ½ of the lemon into the dressing bowl; cut the other ½ into 4 wedges.
    ½ lemon
  12. 12
    Mince the anchovy fillets and add to the dressing bowl.
    2 anchovy fillets, packed in oil
  13. 13
    To the dressing bowl, add mayonnaise, Dijon, Worcestershire, and pepper; whisk together (or blend with a hand blender for a smoother consistency).
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    Pour the dressing over the lettuce and toss with your hands (or salad spoons) until the lettuce is well coated.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide the dressed lettuce between plates, top with the croutons, remaining Parmesan, and salmon, and garnish with the lemon wedges. Enjoy!

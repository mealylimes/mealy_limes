Salmon Coconut Curry with Spinach, Tomatoes, Cilantro & Rice

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (25) $6.21/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • fish sauce
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lime
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  4. 4
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a medium bowl (reserving a small handful for garnish).
  5. 5
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic; add both to the bowl with the cilantro.
    2 cloves garlic
  6. 6
    If using salmon with skin, place the fillet, skin-side down, on the cutting board. Holding the tail end firmly, use a knife to cut between the flesh and skin; continue to cut through the length of the fillet (take care not to cut through the skin). Discard the skin.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  7. 7
    Cut salmon into 1-inch pieces, season with salt and pepper, and set aside.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add ginger, garlic, cilantro, curry powder, and turmeric; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp curry powder
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
  10. 10
    Add coconut milk, broth, chili-garlic sauce, fish sauce, sugar, salt, and pepper to the skillet; stir to combine.
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Juice lime into the skillet, add salmon, and stir to combine the curry. Bring to a simmer; once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until the salmon is cooked through and flakey, 7-8 minutes.
  12. 12
    While the salmon is cooking, using a clean cutting board, roughly chop spinach; halve tomatoes. Place both in the reserved bowl.
  13. 13
    Add spinach and tomatoes to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the veggies to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Continue to cook until heated through, about 3 minutes.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide rice and salmon curry between bowls. Top with reserved cilantro and enjoy!

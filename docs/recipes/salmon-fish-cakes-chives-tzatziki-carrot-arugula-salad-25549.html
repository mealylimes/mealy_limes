Salmon Fish Cakes with Chives, Tzatziki & Carrot-Arugula Salad

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 35% (5) $3.08/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1egg
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 2 (7.5 oz) canswild pink salmon, traditional style
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, and chop chives into small pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small pkg chives
  2. 2
    Drain salmon, add to the bowl with the chives, and flake with a fork.
    2 (7.5 oz) cans wild pink salmon, traditional style
  3. 3
    Add egg, flour, and spices to the bowl; stir until well combined.
    1 egg
    3 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, using a ¼-cup measure, form the salmon mixture into ½-inch thick patties; place on a plate.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add fish cakes and cook until golden brown and crispy, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    While the fish cakes are cooking, wash and dry arugula (skip if it came pre-washed).
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  8. 8
    To serve, divide fish cakes between plates, then top with tzatziki. Add arugula to the plate, top with carrots, and drizzle with vinaigrette. Enjoy!
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette

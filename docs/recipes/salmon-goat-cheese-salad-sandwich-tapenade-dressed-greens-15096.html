Salmon & Goat Cheese Salad Sandwich with Tapenade-Dressed Greens

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $6.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½English cucumber
  • 3sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½lemon
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1 (7.5 oz) canwild pink salmon, traditional style
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 1tomato
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    1 tomato
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Zest lemon into a medium bowl. (Set lemon aside for later use.)
  3. 3
    Drain salmon and place in the bowl with the lemon zest; flake with a fork.
    1 (7.5 oz) can wild pink salmon, traditional style
  4. 4
    Small dice tomato and add to the bowl along with spices.
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Crumble goat cheese into the bowl, stir to combine the salmon salad, and set aside.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
  6. 6
    Juice lemon into another medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Roughly chop olives, sun-dried tomatoes, and capers; add to the bowl with the lemon juice.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    3 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    1 tbsp capers
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl along with the oil. Stir to combine the tapenade dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Trim and halve the cucumber lengthwise; slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing and set aside.
  10. 10
    Toast bread if desired.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  11. 11
    Place bread slices on a flat surface. Divide salmon salad between half of the slices; cover with remaining slices.
  12. 12
    Add mixed greens to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide sandwiches and salad between plates. Sprinkle salad with pine nuts and enjoy!
    ⅛ cup pine nuts

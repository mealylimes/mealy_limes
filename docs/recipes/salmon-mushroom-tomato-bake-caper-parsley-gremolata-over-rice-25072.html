Salmon, Mushroom & Tomato Bake with Caper-Parsley Gremolata over Rice

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $4.44/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½shallot
  • ½lemon
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ lemon
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    While the rice is cooking, pat salmon dry with paper towels. Transfer to one half of a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season the top side with salt.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Add tomatoes and mushrooms to the other half of the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with additional oil, then season with Italian seasoning and salt; stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring veggies halfway through, until veggies are tender and salmon is cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
  9. 9
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the parsley.
  10. 10
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl.
    ½ shallot
  11. 11
    Mince capers. Add to the bowl along with oil, garlic powder, and salt. Stir to combine the gremolata.
    1 tbsp capers
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice, salmon, and veggies between plates; top salmon with gremolata. Enjoy!

Saucy Grilled Ham & Cheese "Croque Madame" with Pickled Veggies

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $2.05/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • Dijon mustard
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 2eggs
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • rice vinegar
  • brown sugar
  • 6 slicesprosciutto
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board mandoline slicer (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the marinade.
    ¼ cup rice vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, peel, trim, and thinly slice carrot. Peel and thinly slice shallot; add both to the bowl with the marinade.
    1 medium carrot
    1 shallot
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice cucumber; add to the bowl, toss to combine, and set the veggies aside to marinate.
    ½ English cucumber
  4. 4
    Thinly slice mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  5. 5
    Place the bread slices on a flat surface and spread with Dijon. Evenly top half the slices with the mozzarella and prosciutto. Cover the sandwiches with remaining bread slices.
    4 slices whole grain bread
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    6 slices prosciutto
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Place sandwiches in the skillet and cook until the bottoms are golden brown, 2-4 minutes.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Remove sandwiches to a plate and add more butter to the skillet. Return sandwiches to the skillet, browned-side up; cook until golden brown on the other side and the cheese is melted, 2-4 minutes. Once done, transfer to a cutting board.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  9. 9
    Wipe the skillet clean with a paper towel; return to medium heat, and add more butter. Gently crack eggs into the skillet and cook until whites are set but yolks are still runny, about 3 minutes. Remove from heat and season with salt and pepper.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    2 eggs
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, preheat a small saucepan over medium-high heat.
  11. 11
    Once the saucepan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add flour, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  12. 12
    Slowly whisk milk into the saucepan; bring to a boil and cook until sauce is thickened, about 2 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
  13. 13
    Divide sandwiches between plates; drizzle with sauce and top with a fried egg. Serve with pickled veggies on the side and enjoy!

Sausage Banh Mi Salad with Jalapeños, Herbs & Garlic Crostini

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $2.99/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • fish sauce
  • honey
  • 1whole grain bun or roll
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • mayonnaise
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 2pork sausages
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
    1 medium carrot
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ English cucumber
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Place sausages on a baking sheet pan and place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated); bake until sausages are cooked through, 12-15 minutes.
    2 pork sausages
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces and transfer to a salad bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel and thinly slice carrot into rounds; add to the bowl with the lettuce.
  6. 6
    Trim and thinly slice radishes; add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim and thinly slice cucumber into rounds; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and thinly slice jalapeño into rounds; add to the bowl. (Be careful, with jalapeños, do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  9. 9
    Pick mint leaves off the stems. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and add leaves to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad and set aside.
  10. 10
    When the sausages are done cooking, remove to a cutting board. Slice into ½-inch thick pieces, at an angle, and set aside.
  11. 11
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  12. 12
    Slice roll in half and place on the baking sheet, cut-side up. Drizzle with oil, then sprinkle with minced garlic. Place in the oven and toast until golden, 4-5 minutes. Remove from the oven and cut in half.
    1 whole grain bun or roll
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, juice the lime into a small bowl. Add mayo, honey, chili-garlic sauce, and fish sauce; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp honey
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp fish sauce
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad, sausage, and crostini between plates or bowls. Drizzle with dressing and enjoy!

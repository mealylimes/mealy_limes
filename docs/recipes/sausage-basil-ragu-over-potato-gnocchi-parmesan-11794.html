Sausage-Basil Ragù over Potato Gnocchi with Parmesan

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (21) $3.11/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • Italian seasoning
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • brown sugar
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater large pot measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Once the saucepan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring often, until it begins to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    While the onion is cooking, fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  5. 5
    Use the tip of a knife to slice down the length of each sausage. Peel off the casings and discard. Add sausage meat to the saucepan with the onions and, breaking apart with a spoon, cook until starting to brown, about 4 minutes.
    2 pork sausages
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Add garlic, tomato sauce, water, sugar, and spices to the saucepan and stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ⅓ cup water
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch cinnamon, ground
  8. 8
    Once the water in the pot is boiling, add gnocchi and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, about 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    ½ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan and set aside.
    ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  10. 10
    Wash and dry basil. Pick leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the ragú and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  11. 11
    To serve, divide gnocchi and ragú between plates or bowls. Garnish with Parmesan and enjoy!

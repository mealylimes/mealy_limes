Sausage with Honey-Chili Glazed Brussels Sprouts & Carrots over Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (16) $2.35/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbBrussels sprouts
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½lime
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • 2pork sausages
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove from the heat and let stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    ¾ lb Brussels sprouts
    ½ lime
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and cut carrots lengthwise into quarters, then crosswise into 2-inch long pieces; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  6. 6
    Trim and halve Brussels sprouts; add to the baking sheet with the carrots. Drizzle with oil, season with spices, and toss to combine; spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Add sausages to the baking sheet, nestling them into the veggies.
    2 pork sausages
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, tossing once, until veggies are fork-tender and sausages are cooked through, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, juice lime into a small bowl; add honey and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the honey-chili glaze.
    1 tbsp honey
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  10. 10
    When the sausages and veggies are done, transfer sausages to a cutting board; drizzle veggies with the glaze and toss to combine. Return to the oven and continue to roast until caramelized, 4-5 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    Cut sausages in half lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ½-inch thick half-moons.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice, veggies, and sausage between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Sausage, Mushroom & Ricotta Pita Bread "Calzones" with Dressed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (6) $2.90/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ lbwhite mushrooms
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • red wine vinegar
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 1pork sausage
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts parchment paper stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Use the tip of a knife to slice down the length of the sausage. Peel off the casing and discard. Add sausage meat to the skillet and break apart with a spoon until crumbly. Cook, stirring often, until browned and almost cooked through, 2-3 minutes.
    1 pork sausage
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and roughly chop mushrooms. Add to the skillet and cook, stirring often, until mushrooms are soft and sausage is cooked through, 2-3 minutes more.
    ¼ lb white mushrooms
  5. 5
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the skillet along with tomato sauce and spices; stir to combine and remove from heat.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Grate the cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  7. 7
    Cut pitas in half and carefully open them.
    2 whole grain pitas
  8. 8
    Spread a layer of ricotta cheese inside the bottom half of each pita. Layer with sausage mixture and grated cheese. Place filled pitas on the baking sheet, ricotta-side facing down.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  9. 9
    Place calzones in the oven and bake until the outside is crisp and the inside is hot and bubbly, 5-7 minutes.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, and spices in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
    1 pinch Italian seasoning
  11. 11
    Wash and dry the lettuce. Chop or tear into bite-sized pieces and transfer to the bowl with the dressing. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  12. 12
    To serve, divide calzones and salad between plates and enjoy!

Sausage Salad with Chickpeas, Pickled Shallots & Curry-Ginger Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (33) $3.23/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1tomato
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • brown sugar
  • 2pork sausages
  • tahini
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    2 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside.
    1 shallot
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lemon
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    1 tomato
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  6. 6
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the ginger and garlic. Add tahini, water, curry powder, sugar, and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp tahini
    1 tbsp water
    1 ½ tsp curry powder
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Add to the bowl with the dressing and stir to coat.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and cut bell pepper into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Halve tomato lengthwise, then cut into ½-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Slice the sausages into bite-sized rounds.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls, then top with sausage and pickled shallots. Enjoy!

Sausage, Shrimp & Cauliflower Rice Jambalaya Stew with Green Onions

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (5) $5.57/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • onion powder
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1green bell pepper
  • 2pork sausages
  • 1 tbsptomato paste
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater or food processor ladle large pot measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through, 6-8 minutes. Remove sausages to a plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 sticks celery
    1 green bell pepper
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  5. 5
    Combine spices in a medium bowl.
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp onion powder
    ¾ tsp thyme
    ½ tsp basil
    ½ tsp oregano
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  6. 6
    Peel and medium dice onion; add to the bowl with the spices.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Slice celery crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Seed and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Return the pot (with leftover oil from cooking) to medium-high heat. Add chopped veggies and spices from the bowl; cook, stirring often, until veggies begin to soften, about 5 minutes.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, cut cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Using a box grater or food processor, coarsely grate the cauliflower.
  11. 11
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the pot along with the cauliflower, broth, tomatoes, and tomato paste. Bring mixture to a boil over high heat.
    2 cloves garlic
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    1 tbsp tomato paste
  12. 12
    Slice sausages into ½-inch thick rounds; add back to the pot, along with the shrimp. Cook, stirring often, until shrimp are opaque and cauliflower is tender, about 5 minutes.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Stir half of the onions into the pot (reserve the rest for garnish).
  14. 14
    To serve, divide jambalaya between bowls and top with remaining green onions. Enjoy!

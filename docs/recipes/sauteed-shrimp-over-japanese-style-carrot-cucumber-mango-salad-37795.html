Sautéed Shrimp over Japanese-Style Carrot, Cucumber & Mango Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (17) $4.52/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1English cucumber
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • sesame seeds
  • ½avocado
  • 1mango
  • rice vinegar
  • mayonnaise
  • hot sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    1 English cucumber
    1 mango
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Place mayo, vinegar, hot sauce, honey, and salt in a medium bowl, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp hot sauce
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Peel and trim carrots; using a julienne (or regular) peeler, peel the carrots into noodle-like strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  4. 4
    Trim cucumber; using a julienne (or regular) peeler, peel the cucumber into noodle-like strips. Add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; cut lengthwise into ¼-inch thick pieces. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl, along with bread crumbs, and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
    3 tbsp panko bread crumbs
  7. 7
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until just opaque, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice lengthwise into thin pieces.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad, shrimp, and avocado between plates or bowls. Top with sesame seeds and enjoy!
    2 tsp sesame seeds

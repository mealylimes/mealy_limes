Savory Mushroom & Potato Tofu Scramble

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $2.52/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • turmeric, ground
  • ¼ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 mediumyellow potato
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut the tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich the tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium yellow potato
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ¼ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Small dice the potato (cut into ¼-inch cubes). Transfer to a medium saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer, covered, for 7 minutes. Once finished, drain the potatoes and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    Peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Add the onion to the skillet; cook, stirring frequently, until slightly softened, 3-4 minutes.
  8. 8
    Slice the mushrooms and add to the skillet along with spices.
    ¼ tsp thyme
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp turmeric
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Crumble the tofu with your fingers into the skillet. Add the cooked potatoes and stir to combine. Sauté until the mushrooms are softened, 5-7 minutes.
  10. 10
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  11. 11
    Divide the potatoes and veggies between plates and top with minced parsley. Enjoy!

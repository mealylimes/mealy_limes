Savory Oatmeal with Soft-boiled Egg, Avocado, Fresh Herbs & Sesame

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 95% (10) $1.56/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • sesame seeds
  • 1avocado
  • ¼ small bunchcilantro
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes. When eggs are done cooking, drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Add salt and broth to another small saucepan, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, stir in oats and reduce heat to medium-low. Cover and let simmer for 3-5 minutes or until oats reach desired consistency.
    1 pinch salt
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ¼ small bunch cilantro
    1 avocado
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the ends of the green onions; cut the onions crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle.
  5. 5
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  6. 6
    Halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out.
  7. 7
    Peel and halve the eggs.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide the oatmeal between bowls and top with soft-boiled eggs, avocado, green onions, and cilantro. Garnish with sesame oil, sesame seeds, salt, and pepper. Enjoy!
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp sesame seeds
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper

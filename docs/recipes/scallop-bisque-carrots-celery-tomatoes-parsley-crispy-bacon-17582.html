Scallop Bisque with Carrots, Celery, Tomatoes, Parsley & Crispy Bacon

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (9) $8.29/serving
Ingredients
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • all-purpose flour
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ¾ lbsea scallops
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board hand or regular blender ladle large pot measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrots; trim and small dice celery. Place both in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; add to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  4. 4
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half of the parsley to the bowl with the veggies (reserve remaining parsley for serving).
  5. 5
    Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the pot heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  7. 7
    Once the pot is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, pat the scallops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb sea scallops
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Using a slotted spoon, remove the bacon to a clean, paper towel-lined plate. (Reserve pot with drippings for the next step.)
  10. 10
    Return pot with drippings to medium-high heat. Add scallops in a single layer; cook until golden brown and opaque throughout, 1½-2 minutes per side. Once done, add to the plate with the bacon. (Reserve pot with drippings for the next step.)
  11. 11
    Return pot with drippings to medium heat. Add the veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 5-6 minutes.
  12. 12
    Add flour to the pot with the veggies and cook, stirring frequently, for 1 minute.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  13. 13
    Slowly add broth to the pot, while stirring constantly, and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 3-4 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, chop scallops into bite-sized pieces.
  15. 15
    Add tomatoes, cream, and spices to the pot; stir to combine the soup and remove from heat. Purée with an immersion or regular blender (return soup to the pot).
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp paprika
  16. 16
    To serve, divide soup between bowls; top with scallops, bacon, and reserved parsley. Enjoy!

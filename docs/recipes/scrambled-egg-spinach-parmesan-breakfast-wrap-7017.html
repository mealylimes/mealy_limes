Scrambled Egg, Spinach & Parmesan Breakfast Wrap

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (19) $1.60/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 4eggs
  • 2large flour tortillas
Cookware
colander grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the spinach (skip this step if the spinach came pre-washed).
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Break the eggs into a medium bowl and add spices; whisk together.
    4 eggs
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add the spinach and cook, stirring constantly, until wilted, 30-60 seconds.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Pour the egg mixture into the skillet. Scramble the eggs using a combination of stirring and flipping, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan. Cook until the eggs are no longer runny.
  6. 6
    Finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  7. 7
    Remove the skillet from the heat. Sprinkle the eggs with the Parmesan; stir to combine.
  8. 8
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and top with egg mixture. Tightly roll to close. Enjoy!
    2 large flour tortillas

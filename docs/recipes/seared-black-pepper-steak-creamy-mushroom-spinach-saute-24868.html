Seared Black Pepper Steak with Creamy Mushroom-Spinach Sauté

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (26) $4.40/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1shallot
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer steak to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place spinach in a colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain further.
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
  5. 5
    Peel and mince shallot.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    When the steak is done, wipe the skillet clean with paper towels. Return to medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add more butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shallot and mushrooms; cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms soften, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
  8. 8
    Add spinach, cream, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook until spinach is warmed through and sauce thickens slightly, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide steak and creamy veggie sauté between plates. Enjoy!

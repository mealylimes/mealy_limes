Seared Cod with Bacon-Balsamic Tomatoes, Asparagus & Sweet Potatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $6.06/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • ¾ lbcod fillet
  • ½lemon
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts slotted spoon spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch asparagus
    ½ lb sweet potato
    ½ lemon
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 4-5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving the drippings in the skillet. (Set skillet aside for later use.)
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, snap or cut off woody ends of the asparagus; peel and small dice sweet potato. Transfer both to a baking sheet pan.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic; sprinkle over the veggies. Squeeze lemon over top, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place veggies in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until crisp-tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from the oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, return skillet (with bacon drippings) to medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, pat fish dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb cod fillet
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add fish and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, about 5 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
  12. 12
    When the fish is done, remove all but 1 tablespoon of drippings from the skillet. Return to medium-high heat.
  13. 13
    Once the skillet is hot, add tomatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until they start to burst, 4-5 minutes.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  15. 15
    Add vinegar and half of the parsley (reserving the rest for garnish) to the skillet; continue to cook, crushing tomatoes with the back of a spoon to release their juices, until sauce is thickened, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  16. 16
    To serve, divide fish, sauce, and roasted veggies between plates. Top fish with bacon and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

Seared Lamb Chops with French-Dressed Asparagus, Chopped Egg & Lemon

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $7.77/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½lemon
  • 4lamb loin chops
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet slotted spoon stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus.
  4. 4
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, add asparagus and salt; cook until tender-crisp, 2-3 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer asparagus to the colander, and run under cold water to cool; transfer to a plate and set aside. (Reserve boiling water in pan for the next step.)
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Carefully add eggs to the reserved pan, reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    2 eggs
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ shallot
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the shallot. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  8. 8
    Add capers, oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper to the bowl. Stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp capers
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, pat the lamb chops dry with paper towels and place on another plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    4 lamb loin chops
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until lamb is golden brown and cooked through, about 3 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and set aside to rest.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, cut lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
  13. 13
    Peel and roughly chop the eggs. Season with salt.
    1 pinch salt
  14. 14
    Divide lamb and asparagus between plates. Top asparagus with dressing, chopped egg, and reserved parsley. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing and enjoy!

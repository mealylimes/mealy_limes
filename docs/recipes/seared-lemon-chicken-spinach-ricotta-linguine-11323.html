Seared Lemon Chicken with Spinach Ricotta Linguine

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (23) $4.11/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 4 ozlinguine pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 ½lemons
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve ½ cup of pasta cooking water before draining.)
    1 tsp salt
    4 oz linguine pasta
  2. 2
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Add chicken to the skillet and cook until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side (the chicken will not be cooked all the way through).
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Wash and dry lemons. Zest and juice 1 lemon into a small bowl. Slice remaining half a lemon into rounds and set aside for garnish.
    1 ½ lemons
  8. 8
    Reduce heat to low, then add garlic, lemon zest and juice, and water to the skillet with the chicken. Stir to combine.
    2 tbsp water
  9. 9
    Cover skillet and cook, turning chicken once and basting occasionally, until it's cooked through, about 10 minutes. Remove chicken to a plate and cover loosely with foil.
  10. 10
    Return skillet (with leftover cooking juices) to medium heat. Add ricotta and reserved pasta cooking water. Whisk until smooth.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  11. 11
    Wash and dry the spinach (skip if it came pre-washed). While stirring, add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  12. 12
    Add pasta to the skillet with spinach-ricotta sauce; toss to combine and cook until heated through, 1-2 minutes.
  13. 13
    Slice chicken into strips.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta and chicken between bowls and garnish with lemon rounds. Enjoy!

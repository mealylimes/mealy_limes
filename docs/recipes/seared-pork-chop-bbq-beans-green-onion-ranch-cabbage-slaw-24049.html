Seared Pork Chop with BBQ Beans & Green Onion-Ranch Cabbage Slaw

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (1) $2.41/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • cumin, ground
  • barbecue sauce
  • 30 mlranch dressing
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain and rinse beans; place in a medium saucepan.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  2. 2
    Add marinara sauce, barbecue sauce, and spices to the pan with the beans. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    2 tbsp barbecue sauce
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until beans are tender and sauce has thickened, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    While the chops are cooking, wash, dry, trim, and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  8. 8
    Add shredded cabbage and dressing to the bowl with the green onion. Stir to combine the slaw.
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
    2 tbsp ranch dressing
  9. 9
    To serve, divide pork chops, beans, and slaw between plates. Enjoy!

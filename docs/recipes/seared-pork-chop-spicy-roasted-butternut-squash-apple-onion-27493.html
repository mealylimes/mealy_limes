Seared Pork Chop with Spicy Roasted Butternut Squash, Apple & Onion

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $3.79/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2pork chops, bone-in
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ smallbutternut squash
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • brown sugar
  • 1Gala apple
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and peel squash; quarter lengthwise, scrape out the seeds, and cut crosswise into thin slices. Place in a medium bowl.
    ½ small butternut squash
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, quarter, core, and thinly slice apple. Add to the bowl with the butternut squash.
    1 Gala apple
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
    1 medium red onion
  5. 5
    Add oil, sugar, and spices to the bowl, then toss to combine. Transfer squash, apple, and onion to a baking sheet pan, then spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until squash and apples are tender, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    When the squash and apples have about 10 minutes left, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, bone-in
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    To serve, divide chops and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

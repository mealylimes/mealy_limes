Seared Pork Chop with Warm Ranch-Tossed Radishes & Snap Peas

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 12% (2) $3.04/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1 bunchradish
  • 45 mlranch dressing
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and halve radishes. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    1 bunch radish
  3. 3
    Wash and dry snap peas. Add to the baking sheet with the radishes.
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  4. 4
    Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until veggies are charred and fork-tender, 12-15 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    When the veggies are done, remove from the oven and let cool slightly, about 5 minutes. Drizzle with dressing and toss to coat.
    3 tbsp ranch dressing
  10. 10
    To serve, divide chops and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

Seared Pork Salad with Carrots, Blue Cheese, Pistachios & Balsamic

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $4.58/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork chops and pan-fry until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, peel, and trim carrots; run the peeler along each carrot to make long, noodle-like strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    2 medium carrots
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl.
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  7. 7
    Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the bowl, along with the blue cheese, and toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
  8. 8
    Thinly slice pork chops.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Top with pork and enjoy!

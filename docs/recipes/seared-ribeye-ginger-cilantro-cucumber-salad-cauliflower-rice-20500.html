Seared Ribeye with Ginger-Cilantro Cucumber Salad & Cauliflower Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $5.77/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • rice vinegar
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater or food processor measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ English cucumber
  2. 2
    Cut the cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Using a box grater or food processor, coarsely grate the cauliflower and place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces; add to the bowl with the cauliflower and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, pat steak dry with paper towels and cut into portions; season both sides with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Remove to a plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger and garlic; place both in a small bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Juice lime into the bowl with the ginger and garlic; add oil, vinegar, and salt. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Trim and small dice cucumber; add to the bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
  11. 11
    When the steak is done, return the skillet to medium-high heat; add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add cauliflower, green onion, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until cauliflower is soft, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    To serve, divide cauliflower rice, steak, and salad between plates. Enjoy!

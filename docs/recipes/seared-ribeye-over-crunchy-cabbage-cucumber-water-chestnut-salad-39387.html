Seared Ribeye over Crunchy Cabbage, Cucumber & Water Chestnut Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (19) $5.46/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 ozwater chestnuts
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • sesame seeds
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • onion powder
  • ½lime
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
Cookware
aluminum foil can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    2 medium carrots
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Zest and juice lime into a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the lime, along with mayo, soy sauce, honey, oil, chili-garlic sauce, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp honey
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Drain and roughly chop water chestnuts. Add to the bowl, toss to combine the salad, and set aside.
    2 oz water chestnuts
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad and steak between plates or bowls, then sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

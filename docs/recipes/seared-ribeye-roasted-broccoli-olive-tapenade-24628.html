Seared Ribeye with Roasted Broccoli & Olive Tapenade

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $6.11/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • red wine vinegar
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • 1anchovy fillet, packed in oil
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and separate broccoli into bite-sized florets; cut stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 crowns broccoli
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until broccoli is golden brown and tender, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  5. 5
    Mince olives, anchovy fillet, and capers. Add all three to the bowl with the parsley along with oil and vinegar; stir to combine the tapenade and set aside.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    1 anchovy fillet, packed in oil
    1 tbsp capers
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, about 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide steak between plates. Add tapenade to each plate, spreading it out in an even layer, then top with roasted broccoli. Enjoy!

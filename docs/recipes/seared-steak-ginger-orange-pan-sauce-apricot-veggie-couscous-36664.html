Seared Steak with Ginger-Orange Pan Sauce & Apricot Veggie Couscous

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $5.66/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ cupwhole wheat couscous
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1shallot
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4dried apricots
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1orange
  • 1tomato
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • brown sugar
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium carrot
    2 sticks celery
    1 orange
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 tomato
  2. 2
    Peel, trim, and small dice carrot. Trim and small dice celery. Place both in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Small dice apricots. Add to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
    4 dried apricots
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, peel and mince shallot and garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, apricot, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and cut into portions. Season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, about 5 minutes. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    When the veggies are tender, add broth to the pan and bring to a boil. Once boiling, remove from heat, add couscous, and stir to combine. Cover and let stand for 10 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ cup whole wheat couscous
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, zest and juice orange into the reserved bowl. Add Dijon and sugar, then stir to combine. Set aside.
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp brown sugar
  12. 12
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger and garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  13. 13
    When the steak is done, return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add ginger, garlic, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  14. 14
    Add orange mixture to the skillet and continue to cook, stirring frequently, until sauce is reduced slightly, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  15. 15
    Small dice tomato. Add to the pan with the couscous and gently stir to combine.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide couscous and steak between plates. Top steak with ginger-orange pan sauce and enjoy!

Seared Strip Steak with Cajun Corn, Tomato & Roasted Red Pepper Sauté

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (4) $5.28/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1 ½ cupsfrozen corn
  • Cajun seasoning
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise. Place in a small bowl.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
  5. 5
    Drain and roughly chop roasted peppers. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  6. 6
    Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium heat; add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tomatoes, peppers, corn, and spices; cook, stirring frequently, until veggies are tender and corn is warmed through, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ cups frozen corn
    2 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  7. 7
    When the corn sauté is done, remove skillet from heat, add vinaigrette, and stir to combine.
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette
  8. 8
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide steak and corn sauté between plates. Enjoy!

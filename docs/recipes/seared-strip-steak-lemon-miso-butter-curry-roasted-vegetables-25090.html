Seared Strip Steak with Lemon-Miso Butter & Curry Roasted Vegetables

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $4.54/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½lemon
  • ½ lbnew potatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 15 mlmiso paste, white
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and quarter potatoes. Transfer to a baking sheet pan along with cauliflower and mushrooms. Season with spices, toss to coat, and spread out in an even layer.
    ½ lb new potatoes
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    1 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Cut butter into small pieces. Sprinkle half of the butter over the veggies. (Place remaining butter in a small bowl and set aside to soften.)
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and zest lemon into the bowl with the butter. Cut lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
    ½ lemon
  6. 6
    Add miso paste to the bowl and mash with the back of a spoon until combined. Set miso butter aside.
    1 tbsp miso paste, white
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add additional butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak to the skillet and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to the cutting board.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    Just before serving, thinly slice steak against the grain.
  11. 11
    Divide steak and veggies between plates. Top steak with miso butter and serve with lemon wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

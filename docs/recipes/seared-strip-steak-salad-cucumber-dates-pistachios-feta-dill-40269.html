Seared Strip Steak Salad with Cucumber, Dates, Pistachios, Feta & Dill

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (30) $7.05/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½English cucumber
  • ⅛ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • ⅛ cupmedjool dates, pitted
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  5. 5
    Place oil, vinegar, honey, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Roughly chop dates and pistachios. Add both to the bowl, along with mixed greens and feta, then toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup medjool dates, pitted
    ⅛ cup pistachios, unsalted
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  10. 10
    Thinly slice steak into strips.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad and steak between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

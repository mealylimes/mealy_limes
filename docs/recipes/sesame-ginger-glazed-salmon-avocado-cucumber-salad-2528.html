Sesame-Ginger Glazed Salmon with Avocado-Cucumber Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (25) $4.67/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1avocado
  • 1English cucumber
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • rice vinegar
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • soy sauce
  • toasted sesame oil
  • mayonnaise
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince the garlic. Peel and mince or grate the ginger.
    1 clove garlic
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  2. 2
    In a shallow bowl, whisk together the minced garlic and ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, and sesame oil.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  3. 3
    Place the salmon flesh side down in the marinade and coat both sides; let it sit while you prepare the salad dressing.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  4. 4
    In a medium bowl, prepare the salad dressing by whisking together the mayo, vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, and salt.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    ½ tsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    Add the sesame seeds to the skillet and cook, stirring frequently, until lightly browned, 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl.
    1 tsp sesame seeds
  7. 7
    Once sesame seeds have been removed, increase heat to medium-high. Add oil to the skillet and swirl to coat bottom. Place salmon in the skillet skin-side down, reserving marinade in the bowl. Cook until skin is crisp, 3-4 minutes, then flip over and pour the reserved marinade over top. Continue cooking salmon in the marinade until opaque, 3-4 more minutes.
    ½ tbsp toasted sesame oil
  8. 8
    Wash the fresh produce.
    1 English cucumber
    1 avocado
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  9. 9
    Halve the cucumber crosswise, then quarter lengthwise and slice crosswise on an angle; add to the bowl with dressing. Halve and pit the avocado; scoop out the flesh with a spoon and medium dice; add to the bowl. Trim onion ends and thinly chop on an angle; add to the bowl. Gently toss until well combined.
  10. 10
    To serve, place salmon on a plate and sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

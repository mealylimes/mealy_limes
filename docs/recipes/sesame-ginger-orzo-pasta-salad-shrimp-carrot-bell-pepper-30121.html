Sesame-Ginger Orzo Pasta Salad with Shrimp, Carrot & Bell Pepper

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $4.00/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • 5 ozorzo pasta
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and orzo, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, about 8 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz orzo pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 medium carrot
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
  3. 3
    Peel and mince ginger. Place in a medium bowl along with soy sauce, vinegar, oil, sesame seeds, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ¼ cup soy sauce
    2 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tbsp sesame seeds
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  5. 5
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, if using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until just opaque, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, add cooled orzo to the bowl with the veggies and dressing. Stir to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide orzo pasta salad between plates or bowls. Top with shrimp and enjoy!

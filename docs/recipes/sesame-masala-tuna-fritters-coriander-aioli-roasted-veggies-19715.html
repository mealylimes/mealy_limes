Sesame-Masala Tuna Fritters with Coriander Aioli & Roasted Veggies

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $3.96/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 (6 oz) canschunk light tuna in water
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 1egg
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • sesame seeds
  • ½lemon
  • almond meal/flour
  • mayonnaise
  • garam masala
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 crown broccoli
    2 medium carrots
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel, trim, and thinly slice carrots; add to the baking sheet with the broccoli. Drizzle veggies with oil, then season with salt and pepper; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing halfway through, until veggies are fork-tender, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, drain tuna, place in a medium bowl, and flake with a fork.
    2 (6 oz) cans chunk light tuna in water
  7. 7
    Zest lemon into the bowl with the tuna. Add egg, almond flour, sesame seeds, mayo, and spices; stir well to combine. (Reserve lemon for later use.)
    1 egg
    2 tbsp almond meal/flour
    1 ½ tbsp sesame seeds
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp garam masala
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  8. 8
    Using a ¼-cup measure, form the tuna mixture into ½-inch thick fritters and place on a plate.
  9. 9
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add fritters and pan-fry until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, juice the reserved lemon into a small bowl; add mayo and spices. Stir to combine the aioli.
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  12. 12
    To serve, divide veggies, tuna fritters, and aioli between plates. Enjoy!

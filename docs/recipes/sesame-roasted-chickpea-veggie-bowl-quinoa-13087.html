Sesame Roasted Chickpea & Veggie Bowl with Quinoa

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (33) $3.29/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • sesame seeds
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ½ cupquinoa
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and broth; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup quinoa
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    1 crown broccoli
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  4. 4
    Drain and rinse the chickpeas; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  5. 5
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into half rounds. Transfer to a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
  7. 7
    Quarter mushrooms and add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince garlic and ginger; add to the bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper. Toss to combine, transfer to a baking sheet pan, and spread out in an even layer.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 ½ tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Place veggies in the oven and roast until starting to brown, about 10 minutes.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place chickpeas, oil, and soy sauce in the bowl used for the veggies; stir to coat.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
  11. 11
    Once the veggies are starting to brown, add seasoned chickpeas to the baking sheet; return to the oven and continue to bake until the veggies are fork-tender, 5 minutes more.
  12. 12
    Place additional soy sauce, maple syrup, and chili-garlic sauce in the bowl; whisk to combine the sauce.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  13. 13
    To serve, divide quinoa between bowls. Top with roasted chickpeas, veggies, and sesame seeds. Drizzle with sauce and enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

Sesame Salmon with Broccoli, Pickled Carrot, Bok Choy & Ginger Sauce

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (33) $4.42/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • mayonnaise
  • 2baby bok choy
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 crown broccoli
    2 baby bok choy
    1 medium carrot
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets; cut stems into smaller pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until broccoli is golden brown and tender, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, honey, and salt in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp honey
    1 tsp salt
  6. 6
    Trim and slice bok choy crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid.
  7. 7
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl, stir to combine the pickled veggies, and set aside.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince ginger. Place in a small bowl along with soy sauce, vinegar, honey, and chili-garlic sauce, then whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp honey
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  10. 10
    If using salmon with skin, place the fillet, skin-side down, on a cutting board. Holding the tail end (or corner) firmly, use a knife to cut between the flesh and skin; continue to cut through the length of the fillet (take care not to cut through the skin); discard the skin.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  11. 11
    Cut the salmon into 1-inch cubes. Place in another medium bowl, along with half of the marinade, and stir to coat. (Reserve remaining marinade for the sauce.)
  12. 12
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  13. 13
    While the skillet heats up, add sesame seeds to the bowl with the salmon, and stir once more to coat.
    ¼ cup sesame seeds
  14. 14
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add salmon (leaving any excess marinade in the bowl) and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  15. 15
    Meanwhile, add mayo to the bowl with the reserved marinade and whisk to combine the ginger sauce.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
  16. 16
    To serve, divide broccoli, pickled veggies, and salmon between bowls. Drizzle with sauce and enjoy!

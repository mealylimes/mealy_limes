Sesame Soba Noodle Soup with Mushrooms, Asparagus & Soft-Boiled Eggs

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $4.62/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • crushed red pepper
  • 2eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 32 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 5 ozsoba (buckwheat) noodles
  • 1 tbsptomato paste
  • tahini
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Peel and mince the garlic and ginger.
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Place garlic, ginger, broth, tahini, tomato paste, soy sauce, oil, and crushed red pepper in a medium saucepan.
    32 fl oz (4 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    3 tbsp tahini
    1 tbsp tomato paste
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Whisk to combine, cover, and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, thinly slice mushrooms and transfer to a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Snap or cut off woody ends of the asparagus and cut into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the mushrooms.
  8. 8
    Once the broth in the saucepan is simmering, add the noodles, mushrooms, and asparagus. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the noodles and vegetables are tender, about 5 minutes.
    5 oz soba (buckwheat) noodles
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Stir half of the onions into the soup (reserve the rest for garnish).
  10. 10
    Peel and halve the eggs.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide soup and eggs between bowls. Garnish with reserved green onions and crushed red pepper. Enjoy!
    1 pinch crushed red pepper

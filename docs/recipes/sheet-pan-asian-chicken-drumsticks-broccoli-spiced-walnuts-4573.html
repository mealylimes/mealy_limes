Sheet Pan Asian Chicken Drumsticks with Broccoli & Spiced Walnuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (25) $2.30/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • cayenne pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ⅓ cupwalnuts
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F, position a rack in the center position and another in the bottom position. Line two baking sheet pans with aluminum foil (optional).
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic and ginger; transfer to a large bowl.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Add soy sauce, oil, chili-garlic sauce, and sugar to the bowl and whisk to combine the marinade. Add the chicken and toss to coat.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
  4. 4
    Spread chicken out in a single layer on a prepared sheet pan. Place on the bottom rack of the oven and roast until the chicken is cooked through, 30-35 minutes. Reserve marinade.
  5. 5
    Wash broccoli and cut into small, bite-sized florets. Thinly slice the thicker stem. Transfer to a medium bowl.
    1 crown broccoli
  6. 6
    Toss broccoli with soy sauce and oil. Spread out in a single layer on the other baking sheet and place in the oven for 5-8 minutes while you prepare the nuts.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp soy sauce
  7. 7
    Remove chicken from the oven. Use tongs to flip each drumstick. Spoon on remaining marinade, and return to the oven.
  8. 8
    Roughly chop walnuts. In a small bowl, toss walnuts with water to moisten.
    ⅓ cup walnuts
    1 ½ tsp water
  9. 9
    In another small bowl, stir together sugar, cinnamon, and cayenne pepper. Toss moistened walnuts in the spice mixture.
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground
    1 pinch cayenne pepper
  10. 10
    Remove the broccoli pan from the oven and sprinkle the walnuts on top. Return the pan to the oven for an additional 5-8 minutes or until broccoli is tender-crisp, and walnuts are toasted.
  11. 11
    To serve, arrange chicken, broccoli, and walnuts on a plate. Enjoy!

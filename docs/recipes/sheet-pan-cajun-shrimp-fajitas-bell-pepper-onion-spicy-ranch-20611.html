Sheet Pan Cajun Shrimp Fajitas with Bell Pepper, Onion & Spicy Ranch

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (19) $4.41/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • ½lime
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ¼ cupranch dressing
  • Cajun seasoning
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 green bell pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips; transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into thin pieces; add to the baking sheet with the bell pepper.
    ½ medium red onion
  5. 5
    Drizzle veggies with oil, toss to combine, and spread out in an even layer. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until veggies have softened slightly, about 5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, if using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  7. 7
    Remove baking sheet from the oven. Arrange shrimp over the veggies, then sprinkle with Cajun seasoning and salt; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp Cajun seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to bake until veggies are tender and shrimp are cooked through, 5-7 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, cut lime into wedges.
  10. 10
    Place ranch dressing and additional Cajun seasoning in a small bowl; stir to combine the spicy ranch.
    ¼ cup ranch dressing
    ½ tsp Cajun seasoning
  11. 11
    Warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  12. 12
    Divide tortillas between plates and fill with shrimp and veggies; drizzle with spicy ranch and serve with a squeeze of lime. Enjoy!

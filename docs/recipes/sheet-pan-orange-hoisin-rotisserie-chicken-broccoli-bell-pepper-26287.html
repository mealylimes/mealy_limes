Sheet Pan Orange-Hoisin Rotisserie Chicken with Broccoli & Bell Pepper

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (26) $2.06/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • 1orange
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • hoisin sauce
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, trim, and seed bell peppers; slice lengthwise into ½-inch thick strips. Transfer to a baking sheet pan along with broccoli. Drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper; stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 yellow bell peppers
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    4 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until veggies soften, 10-12 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and juice orange into a medium bowl. Add hoisin sauce and stir to combine.
    1 orange
    ⅓ cup hoisin sauce
  5. 5
    Place chicken on the cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones; separate the meat into shred-like pieces. Add chicken to the bowl with the sauce and stir to coat.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  6. 6
    When the veggies are soft, remove from the oven. Add chicken and sauce to the baking sheet; stir to combine with the veggies, then spread out in an even layer.
  7. 7
    Return the baking sheet to the oven and continue to bake until veggies are tender and chicken is warmed through, 5-6 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide chicken-veggie bake between plates or bowls and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    1 tbsp sesame seeds

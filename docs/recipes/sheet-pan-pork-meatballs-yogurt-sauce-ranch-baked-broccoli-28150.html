Sheet Pan Pork Meatballs with Yogurt Sauce & Ranch-Baked Broccoli

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (11) $0.12/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
  • 80 mlranch dressing
  • 1 (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
baking sheet pan measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Coat a large baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Place ⅔ of the dressing in a medium bowl. Place remaining dressing in a small bowl and set aside for the sauce.
    ⅓ cup ranch dressing
  3. 3
    Add broccoli to the medium bowl with the dressing along with salt and pepper. Stir to coat, then transfer broccoli to one half of the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place pork, bread crumbs, and spices in the reserved bowl. Using your hands, mix well to combine.
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Using a tablespoon measure, form the pork mixture into rounded meatballs. Arrange on the other half of the baking sheet.
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing broccoli and turning meatballs halfway through, until meatballs are cooked and broccoli is fork-tender, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, add yogurt, salt, and pepper to the small bowl with the dressing. Stir to combine the sauce.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  8. 8
    To serve, divide sauce and broccoli between plates; top sauce with meatballs. Enjoy!

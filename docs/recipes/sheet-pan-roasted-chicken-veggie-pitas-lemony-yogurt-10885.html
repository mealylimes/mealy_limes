Sheet Pan Roasted Chicken & Veggie Pitas with Lemony Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 72% (8) $3.81/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • honey
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lemon
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts toaster (optional) tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    ½ lb sweet potato
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Zest lemon into a small bowl, add yogurt, and whisk to combine. Set aside.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic; place a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic. Add oil, honey, and spices; whisk to combine.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp thyme
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Cut the chicken into small bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and thinly slice the sweet potato into half rounds; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel, trim, and cut the onion into thick wedges; add to the bowl and toss to coat.
    ½ medium red onion
  9. 9
    Transfer chicken and veggies to a baking sheet pan and arrange in a single layer. Place in the oven and bake, tossing once, until the chicken is cooked through and veggies are fork-tender, 20-25 minutes.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, trim and thinly slice the cucumber; place in another medium bowl along with the salt and pepper.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  11. 11
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the cucumber and toss to combine; reserve the other half for garnish.
  12. 12
    Toast pitas if desired.
    2 whole grain pitas
  13. 13
    To serve, divide pitas between plates and spread with lemon yogurt. Top with chicken, veggies, cucumbers, and remaining cilantro. Enjoy!

Sheet Pan Sausage with Cauliflower, Peppers, Feta & Sun-Dried Tomatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (9) $4.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 4sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 3pork sausages
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Cut cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Cut into bite-sized florets and transfer to a baking sheet pan.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the baking sheet with the cauliflower.
  5. 5
    Drizzle oil over the veggies, season with spices, and toss to coat; spread the mixture out in an even layer.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Thinly slice sun-dried tomatoes. Sprinkle tomatoes and feta over the veggies.
    4 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  7. 7
    Place sausages on the baking sheet, nestling them between the veggies.
    3 pork sausages
  8. 8
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, tossing halfway through, until veggies are tender and sausages are cooked through, 20-25 minutes.
  9. 9
    When the veggies and sausages are done, remove from oven; transfer sausages to a cutting board and thinly slice crosswise. Add back to the baking sheet.
  10. 10
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Sprinkle over the veggies and sausage; toss to combine.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide roasted veggies and sausage between plates. Enjoy!

Sheet Pan Shrimp Fajitas with Tomato, Avocado, Cilantro & Lime

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (16) $5.73/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • ½lime
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 1tomato
  • 1yellow bell pepper
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 orange bell pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
    1 tomato
    ½ lime
    ½ avocado
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  4. 4
    In a small bowl, mix together the spices; set aside.
    ¾ tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Seed and slice bell peppers into thin strips; transfer to a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic. Peel and thinly slice onion into half rings; transfer both to the bowl with the peppers.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ medium red onion
  7. 7
    Add oil and approximately ⅔ of the spice mixture to the bowl with the veggies; toss to combine. Transfer veggies to a baking sheet pan, place in the oven and bake until slightly soft, about 8 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, transfer shrimp to the bowl used for the veggies. Add more oil and remaining spice mix; toss to coat and set aside.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Medium dice tomato and slice lime into wedges. Transfer both to a plate.
  10. 10
    Halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out; add to the plate.
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  12. 12
    Remove veggies from the oven and toss. Layer shrimp on top and return to the oven; bake until shrimp are opaque and cooked through, 7-8 more minutes. Remove from oven.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  14. 14
    To serve, divide shrimp and veggies between plates and garnish with cilantro. Serve with tortillas, tomato, avocado, yogurt, and lime wedges. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

Sheet Pan Turkey Meatballs with Roasted Apples, Plums & Basil Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (9) $3.68/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • mayonnaise
  • 2Gala apples
  • 2plums
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Coat one half of a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 plums
    2 Gala apples
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Halve plums lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Cut each half into ½-inch thick slices. Place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Quarter, core, and slice apples into ¼-inch thick wedges. Add to the bowl with the plums.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into ¼-inch thick strips. Add to the bowl along with oil, salt, and pepper, then stir to combine.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Transfer fruit and onions to the unoiled half of the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer. Set aside. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  7. 7
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Divide between the reserved medium bowl and a small bowl. (Set small bowl aside for later use.)
  8. 8
    Add turkey, bread crumbs, egg, and spices to the medium bowl with the basil. Using your hands, mix well to combine.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  9. 9
    Using a tablespoon measure, form the turkey mixture into rounded meatballs. Arrange on the oiled half of the baking sheet.
  10. 10
    Place baking sheet in the oven and bake, tossing fruit and turning meatballs halfway, until meatballs are cooked through and fruit is fork-tender, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, add yogurt, mayo, water, and spices to the small bowl with the basil. Stir to combine.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp water
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    To serve, divide yogurt and roasted fruit between plates; top yogurt with meatballs. Enjoy!

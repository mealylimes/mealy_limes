Shrimp, Avocado & Grapefruit Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $5.13/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • black pepper
  • honey
  • salt
  • 1avocado
  • 1grapefruit
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½lime
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) stainless steel skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using frozen shrimp, place in a medium bowl, cover with water, and set aside to thaw. If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the lettuce and then tear into bite-sized pieces.
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  3. 3
    Wash and quarter grapefruit. Peel and slice 3 of the quarters into wedges (save 1 for the dressing). Halve and pit avocado; thinly slice flesh while still in skin and scoop out (save a quarter of the avocado for the dressing).
    1 grapefruit
    1 avocado
  4. 4
    Wash and juice the lime and remaining grapefruit wedge into a small bowl; add saved avocado and mash thoroughly. Add honey, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine and set aside.
    ½ lime
    ¼ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Drain and rinse shrimp under cold water until there are no more ice chunks. Place on paper towels and pat dry. If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.
  8. 8
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds. Add shrimp and cook until opaque, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  9. 9
    To serve, arrange lettuce on a plate and top with avocado, grapefruit, and shrimp. Drizzle with dressing and enjoy!

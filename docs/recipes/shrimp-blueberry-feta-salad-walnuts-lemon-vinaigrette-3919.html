Shrimp, Blueberry & Feta Salad with Walnuts & Lemon Vinaigrette

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (5) $5.45/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • ½lemon
  • Dijon mustard
  • virgin coconut oil
  • honey
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
    ½ lemon
  2. 2
    Grate all of the zest from the lemon, then juice the lemon; transfer both to a small bowl.
  3. 3
    To the lemon zest and juice, add olive oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper; whisk together. Set aside.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place the feta in a small bowl.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  5. 5
    Roughly chop the walnuts and transfer to a small bowl.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water until there are no more ice chunks. Then, lay them out on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry. If using fresh shrimp, skip the first step.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add the walnuts; cook, stirring constantly, until they are lightly browned, 2 to 3 minutes. Once done, return back to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Once the walnuts have been transferred, add coconut oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Add the shrimp to the skillet; cook until they are opaque, 1 to 2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a bowl and set aside to cool.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the mixed greens between plates, top with the blueberries, shrimp, feta, and walnuts, and drizzle with the lemon vinaigrette. Enjoy!

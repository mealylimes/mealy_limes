Shrimp Coconut Curry with Spinach & Cilantro over Rice

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (19) $3.50/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    While the rice is cooking, place marinara sauce, coconut milk, and spices in a medium saucepan; stir to combine. Set over medium heat and bring the curry to a simmer.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    2 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, if using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  5. 5
    Once the curry is simmering, add shrimp and spinach to the pan; stir to combine. Return to a simmer and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until spinach is wilted and shrimp are cooked through, about 5 minutes.
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
  6. 6
    While the shrimp are cooking, wash, dry, and cut lime into wedges. Set aside for serving.
    ½ lime
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, and shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  8. 8
    When the shrimp are done, add cilantro to the pan and stir to combine. Remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
  9. 9
    Divide rice and curry between plates or bowls; serve with lime wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

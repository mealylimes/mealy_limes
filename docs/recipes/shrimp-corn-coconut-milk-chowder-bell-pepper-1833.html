Shrimp, Corn & Coconut Milk Chowder with Bell Pepper

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $5.75/serving
Ingredients
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 2 earscorn
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • black pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using frozen shrimp, place in a medium bowl, cover with water, and set aside to thaw. If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Wash potatoes and bell pepper. Peel and medium dice potatoes. Seed and medium dice pepper. Remove husks and silks from corn. Stand up in the middle of a medium bowl and slice kernels off the cob. Transfer everything to a medium bowl.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    1 red bell pepper
    2 ears corn
  3. 3
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  4. 4
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Coat bottom of saucepan with oil. Add onion and, stirring frequently, cook until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  6. 6
    Add garlic, potatoes, bell pepper, and corn to saucepan. Stir to combine.
  7. 7
    Add coconut milk, broth, cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer until potatoes are tender, about 10 minutes.
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp cumin
    ½ tsp coriander
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Drain shrimp and rinse under cold water until there are no more ice chunks. Place on paper towels and pat dry. Add to saucepan while the chowder is cooking.
  9. 9
    Toast bread if desired.
    2 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    To serve, pour soup into a bowl and enjoy with bread on the side!

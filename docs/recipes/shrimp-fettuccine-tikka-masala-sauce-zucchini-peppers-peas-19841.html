Shrimp Fettuccine with "Tikka Masala" Sauce, Zucchini, Peppers & Peas

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (5) $4.90/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • brown sugar
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • garam masala
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Reserve a ½-cup cooking water before draining; set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 yellow bell pepper
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion; trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  5. 5
    Trim and halve zucchini, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons; add to the bowl with the other veggies.
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place sugar and spices in the reserved bowl; stir to combine the spice mix.
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp garam masala
    1 tsp chili powder
    ¾ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
  9. 9
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic; add both to the bowl with the spice mix, along with the shrimp, and toss to coat.
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    When the veggies are soft, add seasoned shrimp to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until shrimp are just starting to turn opaque, 1-2 minutes.
  11. 11
    Add tomato sauce, peas, and cream to the skillet; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and continue to cook until shrimp are cooked through, 2-3 minutes more.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    ½ cup frozen peas
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy whipping cream
  12. 12
    Transfer drained pasta to the skillet and toss to combine; add reserved pasta water, a few tablespoons at a time, if necessary, to reach a saucy consistency. Remove from heat.
  13. 13
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Shrimp Penne with Swiss Chard, Tomatoes, Mint, Pistachios & Parmesan

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $4.82/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½lemon
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 bunchSwiss chard
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and set aside. (Reserve a ¼-cup pasta water before draining.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ lemon
    1 bunch Swiss chard
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  3. 3
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
  4. 4
    Zest and juice lemon into the bowl with the mint.
  5. 5
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add half to the bowl, stir to combine, and set aside. (Reserve remaining Parmesan for serving.)
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  6. 6
    Fold chard leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips and thinly slice the stems, keeping them separate. Place leaves in a medium bowl and stems in another small bowl. Set leaves aside for later use.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the chard stems.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chard stems, tomatoes, salt, and pepper to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, if using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  11. 11
    When the tomatoes have softened, add shrimp to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until shrimp are cooked through, 2-3 minutes.
  12. 12
    While the shrimp are cooking, roughly chop pistachios.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
  13. 13
    Add chard leaves to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for them to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Add drained pasta, reserved pasta water, pistachios, and the Parmesan mixture. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pasta is combined and coated in sauce, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and sprinkle with reserved Parmesan. Enjoy!

Shrimp Po'Boy with Spicy Mayo & Lemon-Dijon Garden Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (17) $4.87/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • honey
  • all-purpose flour
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1lemon
  • onion powder
  • 1tomato
  • mayonnaise
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
    1 medium carrot
    1 tomato
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic. Transfer to a small bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Juice half the lemon into the bowl with the garlic. Add mayonnaise, chili-garlic sauce, and salt; whisk to combine and set the spicy mayo aside.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    In a medium bowl, combine bread crumbs, flour, and spices. Add shrimp and toss to combine.
    2 tbsp panko bread crumbs
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Add shrimp to the skillet; cook until opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a paper-towel lined plate.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, juice the other half of the lemon into a large bowl. Add oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp thyme
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Separate the lettuce leaves. Set aside 2 large leaves per sandwich; chop or tear remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Trim carrot and peel into thin noodle-like sticks using a julienne (or regular) peeler. Add to the salad bowl.
  11. 11
    Slice half of the tomato and set aside for the sandwiches. Small dice the other half and add to the salad bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  13. 13
    Place the bottom half of a bun on each plate. Layer with lettuce, shrimp, tomato, and spicy mayo; close with top half of the bun. Serve salad on the side and enjoy!

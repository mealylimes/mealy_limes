Shrimp and Sausage Boil with Corn, Baby Potatoes, and Fresh Thyme

Dinner Meat
⏱ 45 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 42% (3) $4.63/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 earcorn
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • paprika
  • salt
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • butter, unsalted
  • ¾ lbnew potatoes
  • ½ small pkgfresh thyme
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board large pot measuring spoons tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); and place over high heat.
  2. 2
    Add seasoning to the pot, cover with a lid, and bring to a boil.
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb green beans
    ¾ lb new potatoes
  4. 4
    Halve the potatoes and add to the pot. Prick the sausages with a fork and add to the pot.
    2 pork sausages
  5. 5
    Once water is boiling reduce the heat to medium-high. Make sure the water continues to bubble, but not over boil.
  6. 6
    Trim the ends off of the beans, halve them, and set them aside.
  7. 7
    Remove the husks and silks from the corn. Cut the corn into 1 inch long rounds - use a large knife to cut right through the cob. Add the corn to the pot.
    1 ear corn
  8. 8
    Drain shrimp in a colander and rinse under cold water until there are no more ice chunks. Spread out on a clean towel and pat dry.
    ½ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  9. 9
    Shrimp and beans cook for a very short time, be sure the potatoes are almost tender and the sausage is cooked through before adding them (8-10 minutes). Add the beans to the pot, followed by the shrimp and cook for another 2-4 minutes.
  10. 10
    Using a large colander strain out the water. Using tongs, remove the sausage and slice diagonally into ¼ inch rounds.
  11. 11
    Return all ingredients to the pot and toss with butter, thyme, salt, and pepper (save some thyme for garnishing).
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ½ small pkg fresh thyme
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    To serve, arrange on a plate, sprinkle with thyme, and enjoy!

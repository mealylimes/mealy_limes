Shrimp Sauté with Capers, Green Pea-Rice Pilaf & Creamy Broccoli Slaw

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $4.70/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½lemon
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • brown sugar
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, if using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, place peas in a clean colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    1 cup frozen peas
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, and zest lemon into a medium bowl (set lemon aside for later use). Add mayo, vinegar, sugar, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ lemon
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    1 tbsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Add broccoli slaw to the bowl with the dressing, stir to combine, and set aside.
    1 (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  7. 7
    Juice reserved lemon into a small bowl.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic.
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic, capers, and thyme; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp capers
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
  11. 11
    Add shrimp and lemon juice to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until the shrimp are cooked through, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    While the shrimp are cooking, wash and dry the dill. Using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the rice, along with the peas, and gently stir to combine the pilaf.
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
  13. 13
    To serve, divide rice pilaf, broccoli slaw, and shrimp between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

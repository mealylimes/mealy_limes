"Shrimp Scampi" Pasta with Swiss Chard, Lemon & Parmesan Bread Crumbs

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (11) $2.96/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 5 ozspaghetti pasta
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½lemon
  • 1 bunchSwiss chard
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, then stir for a few seconds. Cook until pasta is desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. (Reserve a ¼-cup cooking water before draining and set aside.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz spaghetti pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in a small bowl and set aside.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bread crumbs and toast, stirring frequently, until golden brown, 3-4 minutes. Add to the bowl with the Parmesan, along with spices, and stir to combine; set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Wash and dry chard; fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Cut leaves and stems into bite-sized pieces, keeping them separate. Place leaves in a medium bowl (reserving stems for later use).
    1 bunch Swiss chard
  6. 6
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  7. 7
    Return skillet to medium-high heat, add more butter, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chard stems, shrimp, and spices to the skillet; cook, stirring frequently, until shrimp are just opaque, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Add cooked pasta and reserved pasta water to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until water has evaporated, 1-2 minutes.
  9. 9
    Add chard leaves to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the leaves to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the chard is tender, about 1 minute.
  10. 10
    Wash, dry, and juice lemon into the skillet. Stir to combine and remove from heat.
    ½ lemon
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and top with cheesy bread crumbs. Enjoy!

"Shrimp Scampi" Pita Pizza with Cucumber, Bell Pepper & Romaine Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $5.48/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ lemon
    ½ head romaine lettuce
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in the colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Shave parsley leaves off stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Place half in a medium bowl. (Set remaining parsley aside for the pizzas.)
  5. 5
    Add mayo, vinegar, oregano, and salt to the bowl with the parsley. Stir to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp white wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince shallot and garlic.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shrimp, shallot, garlic, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until shrimp are opaque, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    When the shrimp are done, zest and juice lemon into the skillet. Stir to combine and remove from heat.
  12. 12
    Coarsely grate cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  13. 13
    Arrange pitas on a baking sheet pan. Top with shrimp and sauce from the skillet, then sprinkle with cheese. Place in the oven and bake until the cheese is melted, 4-5 minutes. Remove from oven and cut into wedges.
    2 whole grain pitas
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing and veggies; toss to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pizzas and salad between plates. Sprinkle pizzas with reserved parsley and enjoy!

Shrimp "Summer Roll" Bowl with Rice Noodles, Veggies & Peanut Sauce

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (26) $4.50/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • rice vinegar
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls slotted spoon stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 medium carrot
    ½ English cucumber
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.)
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Once the water is boiling, add shrimp to the pan. Cook, stirring occasionally, until shrimp are cooked through, 2-3 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer shrimp to a plate. (Reserve boiling water in pan for the next step.)
  5. 5
    Add rice noodles to the pan with the boiling water, remove from heat, and soak for 10 minutes (or prepare according to package instructions). Drain in a clean colander, rinse under cold water, and set aside.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Place on a plate.
  7. 7
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot. Add to the plate with the green onions.
  8. 8
    Trim and cut cucumber into thin matchsticks. Add to the plate.
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and cut bell pepper into thin matchsticks. Add to the plate.
  10. 10
    Trim and thinly slice radishes. Add to the plate.
  11. 11
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the plate and set aside.
  12. 12
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a small bowl along with peanut butter, honey, soy sauce, fish sauce, and vinegar. Whisk to combine the peanut sauce.
    1 clove garlic
    3 tbsp natural peanut butter
    1 tbsp honey
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp rice vinegar
  13. 13
    To serve, divide rice noodles, veggies, and shrimp between bowls. Drizzle with peanut sauce and enjoy!

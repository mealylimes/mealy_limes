Shrimp Tacos with Mango Salsa, Cabbage, Avocado & Spicy Yogurt

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 96% (7) $5.15/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • 1avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 1mango
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ¼ small headred cabbage
  • 6small corn tortillas
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    1 mango
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    ¼ small head red cabbage
    1 avocado
  3. 3
    Zest and juice lime into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel mango and slice the flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides. Medium dice and add to the bowl with the lime.
  5. 5
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Finely dice and add to the bowl. (Be careful, with jalapeños, do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Peel and mince onion; add to the bowl along with salt and pepper. Stir to combine the salsa and set aside.
    ½ medium red onion
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place yogurt, chili-garlic sauce, and salt in a small bowl; whisk to combine and set aside.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    1 tsp virgin coconut oil
  12. 12
    Add shrimp to the skillet and season with salt and pepper. Cook until opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Once done, chop into small pieces and set aside.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; quarter cabbage lengthwise and remove the core. Slice crosswise into thin strips.
  14. 14
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon.
  15. 15
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small corn tortillas
  16. 16
    To serve, divide tortillas between plates, top with cabbage, avocado, shrimp, salsa, and spicy yogurt. Enjoy!

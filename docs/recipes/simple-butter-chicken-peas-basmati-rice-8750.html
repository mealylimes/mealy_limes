Simple Butter Chicken with Peas & Basmati Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (78) $2.65/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • coriander, ground
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • cumin, ground
  • ¼ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ⅔ cupbasmati rice
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Season the rice with salt and add water; bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ⅔ cup basmati rice
    ½ tsp salt
    1 ⅓ cup water
  2. 2
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towel, cut into bite-sized (1-inch) pieces, and set aside.
    ¾ lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  3. 3
    Once the water for the rice is boiling, reduce the heat to low, stir, and cover. Cook until the liquid is absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Remove from the heat and let the rice stand, covered, 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, mix spices in a small bowl.
    1 tsp cumin
    1 tsp coriander
    1 tsp salt
    ¾ tsp cinnamon
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Heat a large skillet over medium heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the onions and cook, stirring often, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Wash, peel, and mince or grate ginger. Peel and mince garlic.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Add ginger, garlic, and spices to the onions and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, 30 seconds.
  10. 10
    Using a strainer or colander, drain the diced tomatoes, pressing with the back of a spoon to extract as much liquid as possible; discard liquid.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  11. 11
    Add additional butter, chicken, diced tomatoes, and cream to the skillet. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium; cover and cook until chicken is cooked through, 6-8 minutes.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy cream
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, wash and dry cilantro. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
    ¼ small bunch cilantro
  13. 13
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the frozen peas under running water. Add peas to the butter chicken, stir, and remove from heat.
    ½ cup frozen peas
  14. 14
    To serve, divide rice and butter chicken between bowls; garnish with cilantro and enjoy!

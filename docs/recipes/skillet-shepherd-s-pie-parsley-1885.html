Skillet Shepherd's Pie with Parsley

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (15) $3.34/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupfrozen peas
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • all-purpose flour
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • Worcestershire sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons potato masher small saucepan stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, peel, and large dice potatoes. Place in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook until potatoes can be easily pierced with a fork, 10-12 minutes. Drain and return to saucepan.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
  2. 2
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Peel and small dice onion. Wash, peel, and small dice carrots.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 medium carrots
  4. 4
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add onion, carrots, and ground beef. Break apart the beef with a spoon until crumbly. Stirring occasionally, cook until beef is browned and vegetables are tender, 5-7 minutes.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  5. 5
    Wash and dry parsley. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  6. 6
    Transfer beef and vegetables to a colander and set aside.
  7. 7
    Return skillet to stove. Add butter and flour; whisk until smooth. Add broth and Worcestershire. Bring to a simmer.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  8. 8
    Return beef and vegetables to skillet. Add peas, salt, and pepper. Mix well and cook until sauce thickens, 4-5 minutes.
    ½ cup frozen peas
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Add butter, broth, salt, and pepper to potatoes. Mash until smooth.
    ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    To serve, place beef and veggie mixture in a bowl or on a plate, top with mashed potatoes, and garnish with parsley. Enjoy!

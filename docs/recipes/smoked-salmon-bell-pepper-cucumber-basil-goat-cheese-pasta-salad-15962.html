Smoked Salmon, Bell Pepper, Cucumber, Basil & Goat Cheese Pasta Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (6) $5.07/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 small pkgfresh basil
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside.
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    1 small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic and shallot; add both to the bowl with the lemon juice.
    1 clove garlic
    1 shallot
  5. 5
    Add yogurt, mayo, vinegar, and spices to the bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Trim and medium dice cucumber; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Add pasta to the bowl with the veggies and stir to combine the pasta salad.
  10. 10
    Chop or tear salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pasta salad and salmon between plates or bowls. Crumble goat cheese over top and enjoy!
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese

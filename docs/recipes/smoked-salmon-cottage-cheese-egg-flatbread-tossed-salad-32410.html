Smoked Salmon, Cottage Cheese & Egg Flatbread with Tossed Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $1.64/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 ½ tbspcapers
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ bunchradish
  • 1 cupcottage cheese
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts strainer tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 475°F.
  2. 2
    Arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place cottage cheese in a strainer and press gently with the back of a spoon to remove excess liquid. Set aside to drain further.
    1 cup cottage cheese
  4. 4
    When the flatbreads are done, spread the drained cottage cheese over top, creating a large well in the center of each. Crack an egg into each well.
    2 eggs
  5. 5
    Return baking sheet to the oven and bake until eggs are just set, about 12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, chop or tear smoked salmon into bite-sized pieces. Place on a plate.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  7. 7
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the plate with the salmon, along with capers, and set aside for serving.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp capers
  8. 8
    About 5 minutes before the flatbreads are done, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Wash, dry, trim, and slice radishes crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    ½ bunch radish
  10. 10
    Wash and dry the mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing and radish, then toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  11. 11
    When the flatbreads are done, top with smoked salmon, minced shallot, and capers, then cut into wedges.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide flatbreads and salad between plates. Enjoy!

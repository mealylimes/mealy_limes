Smoked Salmon, Cream Cheese & Cucumber Roll-Ups with Side Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $5.93/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • white wine vinegar
  • 1English cucumber
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ½lemon
  • ¼ cuppine nuts
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ lemon
    1 English cucumber
    1 yellow bell pepper
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  2. 2
    Cut cream cheese into small pieces and place in a small bowl.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
  3. 3
    Using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the cream cheese.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic; add to the bowl. Use the back of a spoon to soften and combine the cream cheese mixture.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into the bowl. Add yogurt, salt, and pepper; stir to combine and set aside.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into (thin) half-moons.
  7. 7
    Lay tortillas on a flat surface. Using a spatula, spread cream cheese mixture evenly over each tortilla.
    2 large flour tortillas
  8. 8
    Top tortillas with half of the cucumbers (reserve remaining cucumbers for the salad) and the salmon; roll tightly to close and cut crosswise into pieces. Set aside.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  9. 9
    Place oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Add reserved cucumbers to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Trim, seed, and thinly slice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear lettuce leaves into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl, along with the pine nuts, and toss to combine the salad.
    ¼ cup pine nuts
  13. 13
    To serve, divide roll-ups and salad between plates. Enjoy!

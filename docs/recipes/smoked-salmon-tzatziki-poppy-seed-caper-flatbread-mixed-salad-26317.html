Smoked Salmon, Tzatziki, Poppy Seed & Caper Flatbread with Mixed Salad

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $1.42/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • ½shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • poppy seeds
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
  • ½ bunchradish
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 45 mlFrench vinaigrette
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool slightly, about 3 minutes.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with the vinaigrette.
    ½ shallot
    3 tbsp French vinaigrette
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, trim, and thinly slice radishes. Add to the bowl with the vinaigrette and set aside.
    ½ bunch radish
  5. 5
    Chop or tear salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  6. 6
    Once the flatbreads have cooled slightly, spread tzatziki over the surface. Top with smoked salmon, capers, and poppy seeds. Slice flatbreads into wedges.
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce
    1 tbsp capers
    ½ tsp poppy seeds
  7. 7
    Wash and dry mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the vinaigrette and radishes, then toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  8. 8
    To serve, divide flatbread and salad between plates. Enjoy!

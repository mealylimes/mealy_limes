Smoky Pork Chop with Roasted Acorn Squash, Walnut Butter Sauce & Feta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (4) $3.32/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • rosemary, dried
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • ¼ cupwalnuts
  • 1acorn squash
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil for easier cleanup (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and halve acorn squash lengthwise; trim ends, scrape out the seeds, and cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Transfer to the baking sheet and spread out in an even layer.
    1 acorn squash
  3. 3
    Small dice butter and sprinkle over the squash. Season with salt and pepper.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until squash is tender, 18-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with spices on both sides.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ½ tsp rosemary, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork chops and cook until golden brown and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, roughly chop walnuts.
    ¼ cup walnuts
  9. 9
    Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels, then return to medium-low heat. Add more butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add walnuts and cook, stirring occasionally, until toasted, about 3 minutes.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  10. 10
    While the walnuts are cooking, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    When the walnuts are done, add garlic, water, and soy sauce to the skillet. Stir to combine the sauce and remove from heat.
    1 tbsp water
    1 tbsp soy sauce
  12. 12
    To serve, divide squash and pork chops between plates. Sprinkle squash with feta and drizzle with sauce. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

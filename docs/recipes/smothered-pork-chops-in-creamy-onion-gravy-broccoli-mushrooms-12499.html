Smothered Pork Chops in Creamy Onion Gravy with Broccoli & Mushrooms

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (23) $4.31/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • all-purpose flour
  • 1 mediumyellow onion
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 6 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a medium bowl, stir together flour and spices; remove 1 tablespoon seasoned flour and set aside for later use.
    ¼ cup all-purpose flour
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Pat pork chops dry with paper towels, add to the bowl with the flour, and toss to coat.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add chops to the skillet; cook until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Once golden, transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil (the pork will not be cooked all the way through).
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel and thinly slice onion into half rings.
    1 medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Return skillet to medium heat. Once hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Add onions, salt, and pepper to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until onions are soft and slightly caramelized, 10-15 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash and dry broccoli. Separate into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces; place in another medium bowl.
    1 crown broccoli
  9. 9
    Wash, dry, and thinly slice mushrooms. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the broccoli.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Add reserved flour to the skillet and stir to coat onions. Cook, stirring frequently, about 1 minute.
  11. 11
    Add broth and cream to the skillet, stir to combine, and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce begins to thicken, 4-5 minutes.
    6 fl oz (¾ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy cream
  12. 12
    Return pork chops to the skillet and turn to coat in sauce. Cover and simmer until chops are cooked through, 5 minutes more.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, heat another skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  14. 14
    Add veggies, salt, and pepper to the skillet; stir-fry until broccoli is bright green and tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pork chops, sauce, and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

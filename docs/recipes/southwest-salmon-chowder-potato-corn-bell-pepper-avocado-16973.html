Southwest Salmon Chowder with Potato, Corn, Bell Pepper & Avocado

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 58% (3) $3.90/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • 2 mediumyellow potatoes
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½avocado
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (7.5 oz) canwild pink salmon, traditional style
  • ½lime
  • ⅔ cupfrozen corn
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium yellow potatoes
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ lime
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice potatoes; place in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the potato and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add potato, bell pepper, and onion; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Sprinkle flour and spices over the veggies; continue to cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    1 tbsp all-purpose flour
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Add broth and milk to the pan, stir to combine, and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium, and cook until potatoes are tender, 10-12 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, slice lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  10. 10
    Halve and pit avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon.
  11. 11
    When the soup is done, drain and break salmon into chunks; add to the pan, along with the corn, and stir to combine. Cook until heated through, 3-4 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    1 (7.5 oz) can wild pink salmon, traditional style
    ⅔ cup frozen corn
  12. 12
    Divide soup between bowls, top with avocado, and sprinkle with additional chili powder. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing and enjoy!
    ⅛ tsp chili powder

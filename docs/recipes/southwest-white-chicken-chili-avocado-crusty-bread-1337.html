Southwest White Chicken Chili with Avocado & Crusty Bread

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $2.91/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • 1avocado
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • cumin, ground
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan salad spinner (optional) stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Wash, seed, and mince jalapeño.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
    1 jalapeño pepper
  3. 3
    Coat bottom of saucepan with oil. Add onion, garlic, and jalapeño. Stirring frequently, cook until onion is softened, 3-4 minutes.
    about 1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  4. 4
    Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces.
    ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  5. 5
    Drain and rinse beans.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  6. 6
    Add chicken to saucepan and, stirring occasionally, cook until no longer pink, 2-3 minutes.
  7. 7
    Wash and dry cilantro. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  8. 8
    Add beans, broth, ½ of the cilantro (save the rest for garnishing), cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, for about 10 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp cumin
    ½ tsp oregano
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Toast bread.
    2 slices whole grain bread
  10. 10
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out and medium dice.
    1 avocado
  11. 11
    To serve, add chili to a bowl and garnish with avocado and remaining cilantro. Enjoy!

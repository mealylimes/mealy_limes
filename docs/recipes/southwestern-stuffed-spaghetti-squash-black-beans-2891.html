Southwestern Stuffed Spaghetti Squash with Black Beans

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $3.78/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 mediumspaghetti squash
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • chili powder
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 500°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash squash, halve lengthwise, and scoop out the seeds. Rub each half with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Place halves, cut side down, on a baking sheet.
    1 medium spaghetti squash
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place the squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender so a knife can easily pierce though the flesh, 20-25 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the squash cooks, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Seed and small dice the bell pepper; transfer to a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Seed and mince the jalapeño pepper (leave the seeds in for more heat if desired); add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel and small dice the onion; add to the bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  8. 8
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Transfer to a small bowl.
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, drain and rinse the beans.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  11. 11
    Add olive oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the onion and peppers; cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables begin to soften, 3 to 4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Add the beans, chili powder, salt, and black pepper to the skillet and squeeze lime juice over top; stir to combine and cook for 1 to 2 minutes. Reduce heat to low and stir occasionally while you wait for the squash to cook.
    1 tbsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ½ lime
  13. 13
    Coarsely grate ½ cup of cheddar.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  14. 14
    Remove the squash from the oven and change the oven setting to broil. Allow the squash to cool slightly, about 5 minutes. Using a fork, scrape out the squash flesh to make spaghetti-like strings (use an oven mitt or towel to hold it with your other hand).
  15. 15
    Add the strands of spaghetti squash along with half of the minced cilantro (save the rest for garnishing) to the skillet; toss to combine.
  16. 16
    Fill the squash halves with the squash-vegetable mixture and sprinkle with the grated cheddar.
  17. 17
    Place the squash back in the oven and broil until the cheese is melted, 1-3 minutes.
  18. 18
    Divide the squash halves between plates and sprinkle with the remaining cilantro. Enjoy!

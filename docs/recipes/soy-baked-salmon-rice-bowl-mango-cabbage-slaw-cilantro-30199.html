Soy-Baked Salmon Rice Bowl with Mango Cabbage Slaw & Cilantro

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (18) $4.69/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • ½mango
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    While the water is heating, place soy sauce, oil, honey, and spices in a small baking dish. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp honey
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, if using salmon with skin, place the fillet, skin-side down, on a cutting board. Holding the tail end (or corner) firmly, use a knife to cut between the flesh and skin; continue to cut through the length of the fillet (take care not to cut through the skin); discard the skin.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
  6. 6
    Cut the salmon into 1-inch cubes. Add to the baking dish with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside.
  7. 7
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ mango
  8. 8
    Using a clean cutting board, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in a medium bowl. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  9. 9
    Juice lime into the bowl with the cilantro. Add mayo, soy sauce, honey, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl, stir combine the slaw, and set aside.
    ½ medium red onion
  13. 13
    When the rice has about 10 minutes left, place salmon in the oven and bake, flipping once, until golden and cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide rice, salmon, and slaw between bowls. Top with reserved cilantro and enjoy!

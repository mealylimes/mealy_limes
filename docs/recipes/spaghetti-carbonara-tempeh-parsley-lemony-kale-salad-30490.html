Spaghetti "Carbonara" with Tempeh, Parsley & Lemony Kale Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 56% (8) $1.06/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • 5 ozspaghetti pasta
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • 1egg
  • ½lemon
  • ½ bunchkale
  • 1 (8 oz) packagetempeh
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and set aside. (Reserve a ½-cup pasta water before draining.)
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz spaghetti pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, small dice tempeh. Place in a small bowl, drizzle with soy sauce, then season with smoked paprika and salt. Stir to coat.
    1 (8 oz) package tempeh
    2 tsp soy sauce
    ¼ tsp smoked paprika
    1 pinch salt
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tempeh and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, 4-5 minutes. Transfer tempeh to a plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Divide between two medium bowls.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ lemon
    ½ bunch kale
  7. 7
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to one of the bowls with the Parmesan. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  8. 8
    Add egg, pepper, and salt to the bowl with the Parmesan and parsley. Whisk to combine the egg mixture and set aside.
    1 egg
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Juice lemon into the remaining bowl with the Parmesan. Add oil, crushed red pepper, and salt. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
  10. 10
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing and, using your hands, massage kale until wilted, 1-2 minutes. Set aside.
  11. 11
    When the pasta is done, return skillet to medium-high heat. Add drained pasta and cook, tossing frequently, until warmed through, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    Carefully transfer warmed pasta to the bowl with the egg mixture. Tossing continuously, combine pasta with the egg mixture until pasta is glossy and evenly coated, 1-2 minutes. (The heat of the pasta will cook the egg.)
  13. 13
    Add tempeh to the bowl with the pasta and toss once more to combine. Add reserved pasta water, 1 tablespoon at a time, if necessary, to loosen the pasta.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta and kale salad between plates or bowls. Top pasta with reserved parsley and enjoy!

Spaghetti Marinara with Chicken, Kale & Parmesan

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $1.96/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozspaghetti pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ (10 oz) bagkale, cut and ready to use
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes, then drain and set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz spaghetti pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, pat chicken dry with paper towels and medium dice.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  6. 6
    When the onion is soft, increase heat to medium-high. Add chicken to the skillet and sauté until just cooked through, 3-4 minutes.
  7. 7
    Add kale to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the kale to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 1-2 minutes more.
    ½ (10 oz) bag kale, cut and ready to use
  8. 8
    Add marinara to the skillet, stir to combine, and cook until warmed through, 1-2 minutes.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
  9. 9
    Add pasta to the skillet and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and sprinkle with Parmesan. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

Spaghetti with Roasted Bacon, Brussels Sprouts, Mushrooms & Parmesan

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (6) $2.83/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozspaghetti pasta
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater large pot measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz spaghetti pasta
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, trim, and halve Brussels sprouts lengthwise. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, and quarter mushrooms. Add to the baking sheet with the Brussels sprouts.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
  5. 5
    Small dice bacon and add to the baking sheet. Drizzle with oil and maple syrup, then season with spices. Stir to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    4 slices bacon
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    1 ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until veggies are tender and bacon is cooked through, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
  8. 8
    When the pasta is done, reserve a ⅓-cup of cooking water, then drain. Return pasta and reserved cooking water to the pot; set aside until the veggies are done.
  9. 9
    Add roasted veggies, bacon, Parmesan, and Dijon to the pot, then set over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring often, until the liquid has evaporated and Parmesan has melted, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

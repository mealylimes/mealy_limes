Spaghetti Squash "Carbonara" with Bacon, Leeks, Parmesan & Fried Egg

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $3.34/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2eggs
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • 1leek
  • 1 mediumspaghetti squash
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts slotted spoon spatula stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F and place rack in the center position. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium spaghetti squash
    1 leek
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Trim spaghetti squash, then halve lengthwise, and scrape out the seeds. Rub the inside of the squash with oil and season with salt. Arrange halves, cut-side down, on the baking sheet.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender, so a knife can easily pierce through the flesh, 20-25 minutes.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, small dice bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until crispy, 3-4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, remove crispy bacon to a paper towel-lined plate; set aside. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, trim off the root end and the dark green top of the leek, leaving only the white and pale green parts. Cut the leek in half lengthwise and rinse under running water, separating the layers to remove any dirt and grit. Thinly slice crosswise.
  9. 9
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat. Add leek and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender but not browned, 2-3 minutes. Transfer leek to a medium bowl. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
    1 pinch salt
  10. 10
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add to the bowl with the leek.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl, along with pepper, and set aside.
    ½ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Remove squash from the oven and allow to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  13. 13
    While the squash is cooling, wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat.
  14. 14
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Gently crack eggs into the skillet and cook until the tops of the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat and season with salt and pepper.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 eggs
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  15. 15
    Meanwhile, scrape the inside of the squash with a fork to separate into spaghetti-like strands (use an oven mitt or towel to hold it with your other hand). Add to the bowl with the leek and Parmesan, then toss to combine.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide spaghetti squash between plates or bowls. Top with crispy bacon and fried eggs. Enjoy!

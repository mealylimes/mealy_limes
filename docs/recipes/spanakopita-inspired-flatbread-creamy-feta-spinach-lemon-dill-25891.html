Spanakopita-Inspired Flatbread with Creamy Feta, Spinach, Lemon & Dill

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $4.19/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½lemon
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Zest lemon into a small bowl. Add ⅔ of the feta, yogurt, oil, pepper, and salt. Mash with a fork until semi-smooth and combined, then set aside. (Reserve lemon and remaining feta for later use.)
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch salt
  4. 4
    Arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until flatbreads are slightly crispy, about 8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, juice reserved lemon into another small bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and thinly slice green onions crosswise. Add to the bowl with the lemon juice.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with oil and salt, then stir to combine.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add lemon-oil mixture. Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Cook, stirring frequently, until wilted, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    Spread the creamy feta over each flatbread, then top with sautéed greens and reserved feta.
  11. 11
    To serve, cut flatbreads into wedges and divide between plates. Enjoy!

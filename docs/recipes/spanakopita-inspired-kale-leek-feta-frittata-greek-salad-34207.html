Spanakopita-Inspired Kale, Leek & Feta Frittata with Greek Salad

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (27) $3.95/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ bunchkale
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1leek
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ bunch kale
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    1 leek
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
  2. 2
    Fold kale leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems; cut leaves crosswise into thin (shred-like) pieces. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl with the kale and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, trim off root end and dark green top of the leek, leaving only the white and pale green parts. Cut leek in half lengthwise and rinse under running water, separating the layers to remove any dirt and grit. Slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add leek and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender but not browned, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, crack eggs into another medium bowl. Add salt and pepper, then whisk to combine. Add half of the feta, whisk once more to combine, and set aside. (Reserve remaining feta for later use.)
    6 eggs
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  8. 8
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  9. 9
    When the leeks are tender, add kale and dill to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the greens to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
  10. 10
    When the kale is wilted, pour the egg mixture into the skillet. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but top is still runny, about 5 minutes.
  11. 11
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until top of the frittata is just set, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, and spices in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Halve grape tomatoes and olives. Add both to the bowl with the dressing.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  14. 14
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl, along with reserved feta, and gently stir to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    Cut frittata into wedges and divide between plates. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

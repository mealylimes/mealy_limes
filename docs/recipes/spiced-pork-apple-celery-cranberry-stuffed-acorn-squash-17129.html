Spiced Pork, Apple, Celery & Cranberry Stuffed Acorn Squash

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (7) $1.79/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ⅛ cupdried cranberries
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • brown sugar
  • 1Gala apple
  • 1acorn squash
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
  • nutmeg, ground
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F and place rack in the center position. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 acorn squash
    1 Gala apple
    2 sticks celery
  3. 3
    Halve acorn squash lengthwise, trim ends, then scoop out the seeds. Rub the inside of the squash with oil and season with salt and pepper; arrange halves, cut-side down, on the baking sheet.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender, so a knife can easily pierce through the flesh, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Quarter and core apple, then medium dice; add to the bowl with the onion and garlic.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, trim and small dice celery; add to the bowl with the veggies and apple.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, apple, pork, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 5-6 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ¼ tsp nutmeg, ground
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  10. 10
    When the pork is done, add broth, cranberries, and sugar; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, 2-3 minutes more. Cover and remove from heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ⅛ cup dried cranberries
    2 tsp brown sugar
  11. 11
    To serve, divide squash halves between plates and fill with the pork and veggie stuffing. Enjoy!

Spiced Pumpkin Parfaits with Cinnamon-Coconut Granola

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (12) $1.50/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • ½ (15 oz) canpure pumpkin purée
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1 cupplain Greek yogurt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ¼ cuppumpkin seeds
  • ¼ cuppecan halves
  • oats, quick cooking
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Roughly chop nuts and place in a medium bowl.
    ¼ cup pecans, dry roasted
  3. 3
    Add oats and pumpkin seeds to the bowl with the nuts.
    ½ cup oats, quick cooking
    ¼ cup pumpkin seeds
  4. 4
    Melt oil in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add maple syrup, cinnamon, and salt; whisk to combine.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp cinnamon
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Pour oil mixture over oats and nuts; stir to coat.
  6. 6
    Spread mixture out on the baking sheet and bake for 6-8 minutes, stirring halfway through cooking. Watch carefully as the granola can burn easily. Reserve bowl for the next step.
  7. 7
    Add pumpkin, yogurt, maple syrup, and cinnamon to the bowl and whisk to combine.
    ½ (15 oz) can pure pumpkin purée
    ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp cinnamon
  8. 8
    To serve, divide pumpkin-yogurt, plain yogurt, and granola between jars or bowls, alternating ingredients to create layers. Enjoy!
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt

Spicy Asian Pork Stir-Fry with Eggplant, Green Beans & Sesame Seeds

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $1.75/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1eggplant
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 eggplant
    ½ lb green beans
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork, salt, and pepper; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Transfer to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, trim and small dice eggplant. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim and halve green beans crosswise. Add to the bowl with the eggplant.
  6. 6
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and softened, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, place soy sauce, vinegar, chili-garlic sauce, and additional oil in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the stir-fry sauce.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    When the veggies are done, return pork to the skillet, along with sauce, and stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until pork and veggies are coated in sauce, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide stir-fry between plates or bowls. Top with sesame seeds and enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

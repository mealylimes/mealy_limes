Spicy Asian Turkey Meatballs with Veggie Fried Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (25) $3.18/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • salt
  • ½ crownbroccoli
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 3eggs
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • sesame seeds
  • onion powder
  • ½ (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ crown broccoli
    1 medium carrot
    ½ (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  6. 6
    In a large bowl, combine the minced garlic, ground turkey, egg, bread crumbs, soy sauce, chili-garlic sauce, onion powder, and salt.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    1 egg
    ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 ½ tsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Coat a glass baking dish with a thin layer of olive oil. Using a tablespoon measure, form the turkey mixture into rounded meatballs and place in the glass dish. Place the dish in the oven and bake for 18 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Peel and grate the ginger.
  9. 9
    Place a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, cornstarch, sugar, sesame oil, chili-garlic sauce, and water. Whisk until well combined. Continue to cook, whisking occasionally, until sauce becomes thick enough to coat a spoon, 3-4 minutes. Once done, remove from heat and set aside.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 ½ tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp cornstarch
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    1 ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tbsp water
  10. 10
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces.
  11. 11
    Trim the ends and peel the carrot; discard the trimmings and coarsely grate or julienne.
  12. 12
    Heat sesame oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the broccoli, carrots and snap peas. Stir fry until soft, 4-5 minutes. Add the cooked rice to the skillet and toss to combine.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  13. 13
    Push ingredients to the sides of the skillet and crack eggs into the middle. Let sit for a few seconds and then scramble. Add the soy sauce and stir to combine everything together.
    2 eggs
    1 tsp soy sauce
  14. 14
    When the meatballs are done baking, remove from the oven and toss with the sauce in a large bowl.
  15. 15
    Divide the rice and veggies between bowls and top with the meatballs with sauce. Sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

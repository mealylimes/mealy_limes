Spicy Baked Chicken Wings with Italian Mushroom & Celery Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $4.49/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • white wine vinegar
  • 4 stickscelery
  • 1lemon
  • onion powder
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ½ small pkgchives
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 475°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    4 sticks celery
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ small pkg chives
  3. 3
    Juice half the lemon into a medium bowl (reserve remaining lemon for the salad). Add oil and spices; whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp thyme, dried
  4. 4
    Add chicken wings to the bowl with the marinade and toss to coat; transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in a single layer.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
  5. 5
    Place wings in the oven and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, juice remaining lemon into another medium bowl. Add oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp white wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Trim celery and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Thinly slice mushrooms and add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Chop chives into small pieces and add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Use a vegetable peeler to shave Parmesan; add to the bowl and gently toss to combine the salad.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  12. 12
    To serve, divide chicken wings and salad between plates. Enjoy!

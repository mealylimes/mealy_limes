Spicy Beef, Bok Choy & Mushroom Stir-Fry with Jasmine Rice

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $4.79/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 3baby bok choy
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • stir-fry sauce
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board; slice crosswise into thin strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and stir-fry until almost cooked through, about 3 minutes. Transfer steak to a plate and set aside. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry green onions and bok choy. Using a clean cutting board, trim and cut the onions and bok choy crosswise into 1-inch pieces.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    3 baby bok choy
  7. 7
    Return the skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat. Add green onions, bok choy, and mushrooms; stir-fry until veggies are fork-tender, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
  8. 8
    When the rice is almost done, return steak strips (and any accumulated juices) to the skillet. Add stir-fry and chili-garlic sauces, then set over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally until combined and warmed through, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ¼ cup stir-fry sauce
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  9. 9
    To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Spicy Black Bean-Avocado Tacos with Lime Crema & Cilantro-Cabbage Slaw

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (23) $2.31/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • salt
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½avocado
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • 1lime
  • ½ small headred cabbage
  • hot sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small head red cabbage
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Juice half of the lime into a small bowl. Add yogurt and salt, then whisk to combine. Add water, 1 teaspoon at a time, if needed, to reach a drizzling consistency. Set crema aside. (Reserve remaining lime for the next step.)
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Juice reserved lime into a medium bowl. Add oil and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the dressing. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  5. 5
    Halve jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Slice crosswise into thin half-moons and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  6. 6
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Quarter cabbage lengthwise, then cut out and discard the core; slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw. Set aside.
  8. 8
    Drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and cumin; cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
  12. 12
    Add beans, hot sauce, and salt to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
    1 tbsp hot sauce
    ¼ tsp salt
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Season with salt.
    1 pinch salt
  14. 14
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small flour tortillas
  15. 15
    Divide tortillas between plates, then top with beans, avocado, crema, and reserved cilantro. Serve with slaw on the side and enjoy!

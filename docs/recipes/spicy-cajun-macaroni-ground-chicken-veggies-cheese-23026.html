Spicy Cajun Macaroni with Ground Chicken, Veggies & Cheese

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (12) $1.21/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 1green bell pepper
  • Cajun seasoning
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ lbground chicken
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 green bell pepper
    1 stick celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Place in a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add to the bowl with the other veggies along with the carrots.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, chicken, Cajun seasoning, and salt. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    1 ½ tbsp Cajun seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Add water, tomato sauce, and pasta to the pan. Stir to combine, cover, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer (while covered) until pasta is tender and the liquid is mostly absorbed, 8-10 minutes.
    1 cup water
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Set aside.
  9. 9
    When the pasta is done, add parsley, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls and sprinkle with cheese. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

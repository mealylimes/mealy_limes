Spicy Cajun Shrimp Skillet with Zucchini, Corn & Bell Peppers

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (7) $4.89/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1 stickcelery
  • onion powder
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • 1 tbsptomato paste
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 stick celery
    1 medium zucchini squash
    1 red bell pepper
  2. 2
    In a medium bowl, combine and mix together the spices.
    ½ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp onion powder
    ⅛ tsp oregano
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp thyme
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Small dice the celery. Peel and mince the shallot.
    1 shallot
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Once the saucepan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Add the shallot and celery to the saucepan and cook, stirring occasionally, 2-3 minutes.
  6. 6
    Add the tomato paste and half of the spice mixture to the saucepan. Cook, stirring continuously, for 1 minute.
    1 tbsp tomato paste
  7. 7
    Add the rice and broth to the saucepan and bring to a boil. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. 8
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  9. 9
    Add the shrimp and oil to the medium bowl with the rest of the spice mixture. Stir to combine and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Halve zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into half moons. Medium dice the bell pepper.
  11. 11
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Add the zucchini, bell pepper, and frozen corn to the skillet and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    ½ cup frozen corn
  13. 13
    Add the shrimp to the skillet; cook until opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Once done, remove skillet from heat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide the rice between bowls and top with the shrimp and vegetables. Enjoy!

Spicy Chicken & Scrambled Egg Wrap with Mango-Spinach Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (4) $2.68/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 3eggs
  • ½shallot
  • ½red bell pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lime
    1 mango
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Peel and mince shallot and garlic; place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ shallot
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Juice lime into the bowl with the shallot and garlic, then add oil and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice and add to the bowl with the dressing. Set aside.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add bell pepper, chicken, and spices. Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, crack eggs into a small bowl and whisk until smooth.
    3 eggs
  9. 9
    When the chicken is done, pour eggs into the skillet. Scramble the eggs using a combination of stirring and folding, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan. Cook until the eggs are no longer runny; remove from heat.
  10. 10
    While the eggs are cooking, add spinach to the bowl with the dressing and mango; toss to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and divide chicken-egg scramble between them; roll tightly to close.
    2 large flour tortillas
  12. 12
    Cut wraps in half and divide between plates. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

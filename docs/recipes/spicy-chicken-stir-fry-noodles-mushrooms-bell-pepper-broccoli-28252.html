Spicy Chicken Stir-Fry with Noodles, Mushrooms, Bell Pepper & Broccoli

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (17) $3.82/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • cornstarch
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap) and bring to a boil. Remove from heat and add rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes or according to package directions. Drain in a colander and rinse.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and slice crosswise into thin strips. Sprinkle with cornstarch, salt, and pepper.
    ¾ lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    1 tsp cornstarch
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and stir-fry until cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Transfer chicken to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  5. 5
    While the chicken is cooking, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ red bell pepper
    1 crown broccoli
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  6. 6
    Using a clean cutting board, thinly slice mushrooms. Place in a medium bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the mushrooms.
  8. 8
    Separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Add both to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add veggies, salt, and pepper; stir-fry until veggies are fork-tender, 5-6 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    While the veggies are cooking, place soy sauce, water, fish sauce, chili-garlic sauce, and cornstarch in the reserved bowl. Whisk to combine the stir-fry sauce.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp water
    1 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp cornstarch
  12. 12
    When the veggies are done, add sauce to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce has thickened, 2-3 minutes more.
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, trim green onions and cut crosswise into thin pieces.
  14. 14
    Once the sauce has thickened, add drained noodles, chicken, and green onions to the skillet. Toss to combine and continue to cook until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide stir-fry between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Spicy Chicken Thighs with Spinach, Tomato, Coconut & Turmeric Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 77% (26) $4.27/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1shallot
  • 1 lbchicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, pat the chicken dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    1 lb chicken thighs, boneless skinless
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and cook, flipping once, until starting to brown, about 4 minutes (the chicken will not be cooked all the way through). Transfer chicken to a clean plate.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic and shallot.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 shallot
  5. 5
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water. Set aside to drain.
    ½ cup basmati rice
  6. 6
    Return skillet (with cooking juices) to medium heat. Add garlic and shallot; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
  7. 7
    Add rice, coconut milk, water, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine and bring to a boil over high heat.
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ⅓ cup water
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  8. 8
    Once the mixture is boiling, reduce heat to medium-low. Return chicken to the skillet, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and chicken is cooked through, 15-18 minutes.
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and halve tomatoes.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
  10. 10
    Wash and dry spinach. (Skip this step if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  11. 11
    When the chicken and rice are done, remove skillet from the heat; transfer chicken to a clean plate and set aside.
  12. 12
    Stir handfuls of spinach into the coconut rice, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  13. 13
    Gently stir tomatoes into the coconut rice.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide the coconut-vegetable rice and chicken between plates. Enjoy!

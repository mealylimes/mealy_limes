Spicy Chickpea Stew (Chana Masala) with Rice & Cilantro-Lime Raita

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (21) $3.00/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • chili powder
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1lime
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, peel and mince garlic and ginger; place in a small bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl with the garlic and ginger; set aside. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  6. 6
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  7. 7
    While the pan heats up, peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  8. 8
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 3 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse chickpeas in a colander. Set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  10. 10
    Add garlic, ginger, jalapeño, and spices to the pan with the onion. Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    1 tsp coriander, ground
    1 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    1 pinch turmeric, ground
    1 pinch chili powder
  11. 11
    Add chickpeas, broth, and diced tomatoes to the pan; stir to combine and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until chickpeas are soft and stew has thickened, about 15 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  12. 12
    While the stew cooks, juice half the lime into the reserved bowl. Cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  13. 13
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the lime juice along with yogurt, salt, and pepper; whisk to combine the raita.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    When the stew is done, add spinach to the pan in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  15. 15
    Divide rice and chickpea stew between bowls; serve with lime wedges and raita on the side. Enjoy!

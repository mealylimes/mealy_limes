Spicy Cottage Cheese "Nacho" Dip with Tortilla Chips

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 5 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (15) $0.08/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • chili powder
  • ½lime
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • ⅓ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • 2 oztortilla chips
  • ¾ cupcottage cheese
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons silicone spatula
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel garlic and place in a blender.
    1 clove garlic
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and juice lime into the blender.
    ½ lime
  3. 3
    Add cottage cheese, shredded cheese, salsa, and chili powder to the blender. Blend on high for several minutes until smooth, scraping down the sides of the blender as needed. Add water, 1 tablespoon at a time, if needed, to reach desired consistency.
    ¾ cup cottage cheese
    ⅓ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
    ½ cup salsa
    ¾ tsp chili powder
  4. 4
    Divide dip between bowls and serve with tortilla chips on the side. Enjoy!
    2 oz tortilla chips

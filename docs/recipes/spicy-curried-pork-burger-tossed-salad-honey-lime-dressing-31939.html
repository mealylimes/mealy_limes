Spicy Curried Pork Burger & Tossed Salad with Honey-Lime Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (76) $1.74/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½lime
  • ketchup
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls spatula stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place pork, bread crumbs, Worcestershire sauce, and spices in a medium bowl, then mix with your hands until well combined. Form the pork mixture into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    3 tbsp panko bread crumbs
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    1 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add burger patties and cook, undisturbed, for 5 minutes; flip patties and cook an additional 5-7 minutes, until cooked through. Once done, transfer to a clean plate.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    While the burgers are cooking, place ketchup and crushed red pepper in a small bowl. Stir to combine the spicy ketchup and set aside.
    3 tbsp ketchup
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    1 medium carrot
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  6. 6
    Juice lime into a clean medium bowl. Add oil, honey, and spices, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Peel and trim carrot; using a julienne (or regular) peeler, peel the carrot into noodle-like strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl.
    ½ shallot
  10. 10
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  11. 11
    Toast the buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  12. 12
    Spread spicy ketchup over the bottom half of each bun; top with burger patties and a small handful of salad. Close with the top half of each bun. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!

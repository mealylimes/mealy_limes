Spicy Mexican-Style Pasta Salad with Shrimp, Corn, Pepper & Cilantro

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (19) $4.59/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1 ozParmesan cheese
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • mayonnaise
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ English cucumber
  3. 3
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Small dice and add to the bowl with the bell pepper. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper, jalapeño, garlic, corn, and spices; cook, stirring frequently, until veggies are soft, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 cup frozen corn
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Add shrimp to the skillet and continue to cook, stirring often, until opaque, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
  10. 10
    While the shrimp are cooking, juice lime into the reserved bowl.
  11. 11
    Finely grate Parmesan. Add to the bowl with the lime juice, along with yogurt and mayo, then stir to combine the dressing.
    1 oz Parmesan cheese
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
  12. 12
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  13. 13
    Trim and small dice cucumber. Add to the bowl.
  14. 14
    When the shrimp are done, add to the bowl along with the veggies and cooled pasta. Stir to combine the pasta salad.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide the pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

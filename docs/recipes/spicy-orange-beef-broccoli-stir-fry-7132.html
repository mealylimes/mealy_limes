Spicy Orange Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry

⏱ 40 min 🍽 4 servings
Ingredients
  • 4 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • 2 crownsbroccoli
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 2 (1 inch) piecesginger root
  • 2oranges
  • rice vinegar
  • 1 cupbasmati rice
  • 1 ½ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    1 cup basmati rice
    2 cup water
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 (1 inch) pieces ginger root
    2 oranges
    2 crowns broccoli
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince the garlic. Peel and mince or grate the ginger. Set both aside in a small bowl.
    4 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Use a grater to zest the oranges and transfer to the bowl with the garlic and ginger. Juice the orange into a separate small bowl.
  6. 6
    Heat the oil in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Once the oil is hot, add the garlic, ginger, orange zest, and chili-garlic sauce. Sauté until ingredients soften, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  7. 7
    Add orange juice, brown sugar, rice vinegar, and soy sauce to the saucepan. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, uncovered and stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens and reduces by half, about 10 minutes.
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    ½ cup rice vinegar
    ½ cup soy sauce
  8. 8
    Preheat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Slice the steak against the grain into ½-inch strips.
    1 ½ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Add the beef to the skillet and cook, without stirring, for 1 minute. Then turn the beef pieces over and cook, stirring occasionally, 2-3 minutes for medium-rare. Transfer beef to a plate and set aside.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets. Return the skillet to medium heat and add the broccoli. Stir-fry until tender-crisp, about 7 minutes.
  13. 13
    Trim off and discard the ends of the green onions; cut the onions crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle.
  14. 14
    Add the beef back into the skillet with the broccoli. Add green onions and orange sauce to the skillet and stir well.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide the rice into bowls and top with the beef and veggie mixture. Enjoy!

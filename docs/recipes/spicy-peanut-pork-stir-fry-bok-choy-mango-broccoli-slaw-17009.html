Spicy Peanut Pork Stir-Fry with Bok Choy, Mango & Broccoli Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (7) $3.69/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • curry powder
  • coriander, ground
  • crushed red pepper
  • turmeric, ground
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • rice vinegar
  • 3baby bok choy
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  2. 2
    Add soy sauce, oil, and crushed red pepper to the bowl with the garlic; whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  3. 3
    Slice pork chops crosswise into ½-inch thick strips, add to the bowl with the marinade, toss to coat, and set aside.
    2 pork chops, boneless
  4. 4
    While the pork marinates, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lime
    3 baby bok choy
    1 mango
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger; place in a small bowl.
  6. 6
    Juice lime into the bowl with the ginger; add peanut butter, fish sauce, water, honey, and spices. Whisk to combine the peanut sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp natural peanut butter
    1 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp water
    2 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp turmeric, ground
    ⅛ tsp curry powder
    ⅛ tsp coriander, ground
  7. 7
    Place oil, soy sauce, vinegar, honey, and chili-garlic sauce in another medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pork strips and marinade; stir-fry until browned and cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, trim and thinly slice bok choy crosswise into shred-like strips; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; large dice and add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Add broccoli slaw to the bowl and toss to combine.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  13. 13
    When the pork is done, add peanut sauce, stir to combine, and cook until sauce is warmed through and pork is coated, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pork stir-fry and slaw between plates. Sprinkle pork with additional crushed red pepper and enjoy!
    1 pinch crushed red pepper

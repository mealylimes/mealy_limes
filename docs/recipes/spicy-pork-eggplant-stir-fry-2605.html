Spicy Pork & Eggplant Stir Fry

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (9) $3.30/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1eggplant
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½lime
  • 2pork chops, boneless
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • cornstarch
  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • rice vinegar
  • toasted sesame oil
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small bowl, create a slurry by whisking together water with cornstarch. Then add chili-garlic sauce, soy sauce, vinegar, and sesame oil and whisk together. Set aside.
    2 tbsp cold water
    1 tsp cornstarch
    1 tbsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    ½ tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the eggplant and green onions. Medium dice the eggplant; transfer to a bowl. Trim ends and cut the onions on an angle into ½ pieces; transfer to another bowl.
    1 medium eggplant
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Peel and mince the garlic. Peel and mince or grate the ginger. Transfer both to a small bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  4. 4
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Thinly slice the pork chops into bite-sized strips and season with salt.
    2 pork chops, boneless
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Add oil to the skillet and swirl to coat bottom. Add the pork and stir-fry until browned, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  7. 7
    Once pork has been removed, add more oil to the skillet. Add the eggplant and stir-fry until lightly browned and softened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Add the garlic and ginger to the skillet. Stir fry with the eggplant for 1-2 minutes.
  9. 9
    Return pork to the skillet and add the green onions and sauce. Toss until the ingredients are well combined and sauce is thickened, about 1 minute.
  10. 10
    Cut the lime into wedges for serving.
    ½ lime
  11. 11
    Serve stir fry in a bowl, squeeze lime juice over top, and enjoy!

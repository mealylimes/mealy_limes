Spicy Pork Tacos with Apple Salsa & Bell Pepper-Mixed Green Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (16) $2.75/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½jalapeño pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • 2Gala apples
  • 6small corn tortillas
  • ¾ lblean ground pork
  • hot sauce
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 lime
    ½ jalapeño pepper
    2 Gala apples
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  2. 2
    Juice half of the lime into a small bowl. (Cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.)
  3. 3
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level, then mince. Add to the bowl with the lime juice. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  4. 4
    Quarter, core, and small dice apples. Add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl along with salt and pepper. Stir to combine the salsa and set aside.
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add garlic, pork, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground pork
    2 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and cut bell pepper into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing, along with the mixed greens, and toss to combine the salad. Set aside.
  11. 11
    When the pork is done, add hot sauce to the skillet, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    2 tbsp hot sauce
  12. 12
    Warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).
    6 small corn tortillas
  13. 13
    Divide tortillas between plates, then fill with spicy pork and apple salsa. Serve with salad on the side and lime wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

Spicy Salmon Cakes with Yogurt & Crunchy Veggie-Cilantro Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (6) $4.26/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • all-purpose flour
  • 2eggs
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 2 (7.5 oz) canswild pink salmon, traditional style
  • 1lime
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain salmon, place in a medium bowl, and flake with a fork.
    2 (7.5 oz) cans wild pink salmon, traditional style
  2. 2
    Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl with the salmon.
    1 shallot
  3. 3
    Add eggs, flour, and spices to the bowl; stir until well combined.
    2 eggs
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, using a ¼-cup measure, form the salmon mixture into ½-inch thick patties; place on a plate.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add fritters and cook until golden brown and crispy, 3-4 minutes per side; transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    While the patties are cooking, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ avocado
  8. 8
    Zest and juice lime into another medium bowl.
  9. 9
    Add oil and spices to the bowl with the lime; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper into thin strips; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Trim cucumber, cut crosswise into 3-inch portions, then cut each portion lengthwise into thin sticks. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Halve and pit avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out into the bowl. Gently toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salmon cakes and salad between plates; top salmon cakes with yogurt. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt

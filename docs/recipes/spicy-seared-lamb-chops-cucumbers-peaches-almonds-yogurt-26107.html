Spicy Seared Lamb Chops with Cucumbers, Peaches, Almonds & Yogurt

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (12) $8.24/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupalmonds
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • paprika
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ¾ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1English cucumber
  • cumin, ground
  • 1lemon
  • 4lamb loin chops
  • 2peaches
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, roughly chop almonds.
    ¼ cup almonds
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add the almonds; cook, stirring constantly, until lightly browned, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to a medium bowl and set aside to cool. (Reserve skillet for later use.)
  4. 4
    Place spices in a small bowl. Stir to combine the spice rub.
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  5. 5
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat the lamb chops dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with the spice rub on both sides. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    4 lamb loin chops
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chops and sear, flipping once, until cooked through, 5-6 minutes. Remove to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    1 English cucumber
    2 peaches
  9. 9
    Zest and juice half of the lemon into the reserved bowl. Cut remaining lemon into wedges and set aside for serving.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, pick parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems. Mince half of the leaves and add to the bowl with the lemon. Roughly chop remaining leaves and set aside for serving.
  11. 11
    Add yogurt and salt to the bowl, then whisk to combine the lemony yogurt. Set aside.
    ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ¼ tsp salt
  12. 12
    Trim and medium dice cucumber. Add to the bowl with the almonds.
  13. 13
    Quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Medium dice the flesh and add to the bowl. Season the salad with salt, then stir to combine.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  14. 14
    Divide chops and lemony yogurt between plates. Top yogurt with the salad and reserved parsley, then drizzle with honey. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing and enjoy!
    2 tsp honey

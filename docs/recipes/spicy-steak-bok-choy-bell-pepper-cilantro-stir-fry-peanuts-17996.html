Spicy Steak, Bok Choy, Bell Pepper & Cilantro Stir-Fry with Peanuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $7.04/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ⅛ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 1lime
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 3baby bok choy
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    3 baby bok choy
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Peel, halve, and mince shallot; peel and mince garlic. Place both in a small bowl.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  3. 3
    Juice lime into the bowl with the shallot and garlic; add soy sauce, peanut butter, chili-garlic sauce, and brown sugar. Whisk to combine the sauce and set aside.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp natural peanut butter
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
  4. 4
    Trim bok choy and cut crosswise into 1-inch strips; place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and halve bell pepper crosswise, then slice into thin strips; add to the bowl with the bok choy.
  6. 6
    Trim and thinly slice mushrooms; add to the bowl and set aside.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, pat steak dry with paper towels, then slice crosswise into thin strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak strips and stir-fry until just cooked through, 3-4 minutes. Using tongs, transfer the steak to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil. (Reserve skillet with cooking juices for the next step.)
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies to the skillet and stir-fry until fork-tender, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. 12
    Roughly chop peanuts.
    ⅛ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  13. 13
    When the veggies are done, add sauce, steak (and any accumulated juices), and cilantro to the skillet; stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens and steak is warmed through, 1-2 minutes.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide stir-fry and sauce between plates or bowls; top with peanuts and enjoy!

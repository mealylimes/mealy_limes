Spicy Sweet Potato & Black Bean Burrito

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (38) $2.65/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ (15 oz) canblack beans
  • cumin, ground
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • onion powder
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater large pot measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ lb sweet potato
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice onion. Small dice bell pepper.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Heat a large pot over medium heat. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Add onion and bell pepper to the pot; cook, stirring frequently, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
  5. 5
    Peel and small dice sweet potato.
  6. 6
    Add sweet potato and broth to the pot and bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer, 10 minutes, until potatoes are fork-tender.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  7. 7
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    ½ (15 oz) can black beans
  8. 8
    Grate cheddar. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  9. 9
    Once potatoes are tender, drain vegetables; return them to the pot and place on low heat.
  10. 10
    Add beans, cheddar, cilantro, yogurt, and spices to the pot. Stir to combine and remove from the heat.
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 ½ tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
    ¼ tsp cumin
  11. 11
    Lay tortillas on a flat surface. Divide vegetable mixture evenly and place in the middle of each tortilla. Fold in sides of tortilla and roll tightly.
    2 large flour tortillas
  12. 12
    Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  13. 13
    Lay burritos seam side down and cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, until golden brown and crispy.
  14. 14
    Remove burritos from heat, cut in half, and enjoy!

Spicy Tex-Mex Pasta Salad with Beans, Corn, Peppers, Tomato & Cheddar

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (73) $2.80/serving
Ingredients
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozpenne pasta
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½lime
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 2tomatoes
  • ⅔ cupfrozen corn
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap). Add salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, uncover, add pasta, and stir for a few seconds; cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz penne pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    2 tomatoes
    1 orange bell pepper
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  3. 3
    Juice lime into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince shallot. Add to the bowl with the lime juice, along with oil and spices, then stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Medium dice tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Mince and add to the bowl. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  8. 8
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Drain and rinse beans. Add to the bowl.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  10. 10
    Rinse corn under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Add to the bowl.
    ⅔ cup frozen corn
  11. 11
    Coarsely grate cheese. Add to the bowl, along with cooled pasta, and stir to combine.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

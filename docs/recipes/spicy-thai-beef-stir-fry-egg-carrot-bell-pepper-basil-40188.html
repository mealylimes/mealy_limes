Spicy Thai Beef Stir-Fry with Egg, Carrot, Bell Pepper & Basil

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (61) $1.77/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • 1red chili pepper
  • brown sugar
  • ¾ lbflank steak
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place soy sauce, oil, fish sauce, cornstarch, and crushed red pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp cornstarch
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  2. 2
    Pat the steak dry with paper towels, place on a cutting board, and slice crosswise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside.
    ¾ lb flank steak
  3. 3
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; set aside.
    2 eggs
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    2 medium carrots
    1 red bell pepper
    1 red chili pepper
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ lime
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in another medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl with the ginger and garlic.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Thinly slice chili pepper into rings; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. Add to the bowl and set aside. (Be careful with chilis; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  9. 9
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak (leaving excess marinade in bowl) and cook, undisturbed, until browned, about 3 minutes. Turn and continue to cook until desired doneness, 1-2 minutes more. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Place in a small bowl along with soy sauce, water, sugar, and vinegar. Stir to combine the stir-fry sauce and set aside.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tbsp water
    2 tsp brown sugar
    1 tsp rice vinegar
  12. 12
    Return the skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Juice lime into the skillet, then add the stir-fry sauce and steak (along with any accumulated juices). Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until steak is warmed through and sauce is thickened slightly, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  14. 14
    Meanwhile, peel and halve eggs. Season with salt and crushed red pepper.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  15. 15
    To serve, divide stir-fry and eggs between plates or bowls. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and enjoy!
    2 tsp sesame seeds

Spicy Thai-Style Chicken Caesar Salad with Cucumber, Radish & Croutons

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $0.98/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½ bunchradish
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 10 mlThai curry paste, red
  • ¼ cupCaesar dressing
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  2. 2
    Cut bread into 1-inch cubes and transfer to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil and season with spices. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 slices whole grain bread
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp coriander, ground
    1 pinch salt
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, tossing halfway through, until the croutons are golden and toasted, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place dressing and curry paste in a medium bowl; whisk to combine.
    ¼ cup Caesar dressing
    2 tsp Thai curry paste, red
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice shallot crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ bunch radish
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  7. 7
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and thinly slice radishes. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Place chicken on the cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces, add to the bowl, and toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  11. 11
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and top with croutons. Enjoy!

Spicy "Turkey Club" Wrap with Prosciutto & French Dressed Greens

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $2.14/serving
Ingredients
  • ½shallot
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½avocado
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • 1tomato
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • mayonnaise
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
  • 8 ozoven roasted turkey breast, sliced
  • 30 mlFrench vinaigrette
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and slice shallot crosswise into thin rings. Place in a medium bowl, drizzle with vinaigrette, and set aside to soften.
    ½ shallot
    2 tbsp French vinaigrette
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ avocado
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    1 tomato
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Halve and pit avocado; cut crosswise while still in the skin, then scoop out with a spoon. Place on a plate.
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper into thin strips. Add to the plate with the avocado.
  5. 5
    Slice tomato into thin rounds and add to the plate; set aside.
  6. 6
    Place mayo and chili-garlic sauce in a small bowl. Stir to combine the spicy mayo.
    1 ½ tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  7. 7
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and spread with a layer of spicy mayo. Divide avocado, bell pepper, tomato, turkey, and prosciutto between wraps. Roll tightly to close, then cut in half.
    2 large flour tortillas
    8 oz oven roasted turkey breast, sliced
    4 slices prosciutto
  8. 8
    Add mixed greens to the bowl with the shallot and dressing; toss to combine the salad.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide wraps and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Spicy Turkey Meatballs with Lime Yogurt & Green Bean Veggie Sauté

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (9) $3.08/serving
Ingredients
  • 5 ozwater chestnuts
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ lbgreen beans
  • panko bread crumbs
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • cumin, ground
  • ½lime
  • 1 cupfrozen corn
  • hot sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
baking dish can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking dish with a thin layer of oil.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  2. 2
    Place turkey, bread crumbs, hot sauce, oil, and spices in a medium bowl. Using your hands, mix well to combine. Using a tablespoon measure, form the turkey mixture into mini rounded meatballs. Transfer to the baking dish.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ⅓ cup panko bread crumbs
    2 tbsp hot sauce
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place meatballs in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until golden and cooked through, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ lb green beans
    ½ lime
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Place in a clean medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and cut green beans crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, drain and roughly chop water chestnuts. Add to the bowl along with corn.
    5 oz water chestnuts
    1 cup frozen corn
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; sauté until tender and starting to brown, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, juice lime into the reserved bowl. Add yogurt, water, garlic powder, and salt, then stir to combine the sauce.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    2 tsp water
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
  11. 11
    To serve, divide meatballs and veggies between plates or bowls. Drizzle meatballs with sauce and enjoy!

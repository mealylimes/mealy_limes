Spicy Turkey Meatballs with Peanut-Broccoli Slaw & Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (22) $2.45/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • honey
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • sesame seeds
  • onion powder
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • rice vinegar
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    In a medium bowl, combine the ground turkey, bread crumbs, egg, and spices; mix with your hands until well combined.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. 5
    Coat the bottom of a baking dish with oil. Using a tablespoon measure, form the turkey mixture into rounded meatballs and place in the dish. Place in the oven and bake until golden and cooked through 15-18 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel and mince the garlic; wash, peel, and mince or grate the ginger. Place in another small saucepan.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  7. 7
    Add soy sauce, vinegar, oil, chili-garlic sauce, and honey to the saucepan with the ginger and garlic; whisk to combine the sauce for the meatballs.
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    2 tsp honey
  8. 8
    Bring the sauce to boil over medium-high heat, stirring frequently; reduce heat to low and continue to cook, stirring occasionally, 2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince garlic; add to a medium bowl along with water, peanut butter, vinegar, and soy sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tbsp water
    1 tbsp natural peanut butter
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp soy sauce
  10. 10
    Add broccoli slaw to bowl with dressing and toss to combine.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  11. 11
    Remove meatballs from oven, drizzle with sauce, and toss to coat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide rice and peanut slaw between plates. Top rice with meatballs and sauce. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and enjoy!
    1 tbsp sesame seeds

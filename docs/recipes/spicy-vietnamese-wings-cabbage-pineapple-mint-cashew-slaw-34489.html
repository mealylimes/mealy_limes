Spicy Vietnamese Wings with Cabbage, Pineapple, Mint & Cashew Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (8) $4.56/serving
Ingredients
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • salt
  • ¼ cupcashews, roasted unsalted
  • 1shallot
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1lime
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • 1red chili pepper
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F.
  2. 2
    Pat chicken wings dry with paper towels and place in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with cornstarch and salt, then toss to coat. Drizzle with oil and toss once more to coat. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
    1 tbsp cornstarch
    ½ tsp salt
    1 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
  3. 3
    Place wings in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, turning once, until cooked through, 20-25 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 lime
    1 red chili pepper
    ½ small pineapple
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  5. 5
    Peel and mince ginger and shallot. Place both in a clean medium bowl.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Juice half of the lime into the bowl with the ginger and shallot (reserve remaining lime for later use).
  7. 7
    Add soy sauce, sugar, fish sauce, vinegar, and salt to the bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    1 ½ tsp fish sauce
    1 ½ tsp rice vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Thinly slice chili pepper into rings; seed and remove ribs with a spoon to reduce spice level. (Be careful with chilis; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.) Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and small dice the flesh. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard root end of cabbage; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Shave mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop leaves. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Roughly chop cashews. Add to the bowl and toss to combine the slaw. Set aside.
    ¼ cup cashews, roasted unsalted
  13. 13
    Juice reserved lime into a small saucepan. Add soy sauce, water, chili-garlic sauce, sugar, fish sauce, and cornstarch to the pan. Cook over medium heat, whisking frequently, until sauce is slightly thickened, 2-3 minutes. Transfer sauce to another medium bowl and set aside.
    1 ½ tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp water
    1 tbsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    1 ½ tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp cornstarch
  14. 14
    When the wings are done, remove from the oven. Add to the bowl with the sauce and toss to coat.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide wings and slaw between plates. Enjoy!

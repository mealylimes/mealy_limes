"Spinach & Artichoke Dip" Gnocchi Bake with Cauliflower

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 70% (16) $1.25/serving
Ingredients
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • onion powder
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • ½ (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • ⅓ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • 1 (10 oz) bagcauliflower florets
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
Cookware
baking dish chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. 3
    While the water heats up, drain and roughly chop artichoke hearts. Place in a baking dish.
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  4. 4
    Add Alfredo sauce and spices to the baking dish with the artichokes. Stir to combine and set aside.
    ½ (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, add gnocchi and cauliflower. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, 3-4 minutes.
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
    1 (10 oz) bag cauliflower florets
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place frozen spinach in a colander.
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
  7. 7
    When the gnocchi and cauliflower are done, drain in the colander over the spinach.
  8. 8
    Add gnocchi, cauliflower, and spinach to the baking dish. Stir to combine with the sauce, then top with cheese.
    ⅓ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
  9. 9
    Place baking dish in the oven and bake until cheese is golden, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide gnocchi bake between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

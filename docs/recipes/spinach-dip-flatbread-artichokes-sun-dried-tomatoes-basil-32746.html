"Spinach Dip" Flatbread with Artichokes, Sun-Dried Tomatoes & Basil

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 89% (33) $4.26/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½shallot
  • 6sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • 2flatbreads or naan
  • 1 (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
  • 1 cupcottage cheese
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the basil along with cottage cheese and spices. Set aside.
    1 clove garlic
    1 cup cottage cheese
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Wash and dry spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed).
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  6. 6
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, uncover, then add salt and spinach. Stir until wilted, 30-60 seconds, then drain in a colander and rinse with cold water to cool.
    ½ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Using your hands, squeeze as much water from the spinach as you can, then transfer to a cutting board. Roughly chop spinach and add to the bowl with the cottage cheese mixture. Stir to combine the spinach dip and set aside.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince shallot. Place on a plate.
    ½ shallot
  9. 9
    Drain and thinly slice sun-dried tomatoes and artichoke hearts. Add both to the plate with the shallot.
    6 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
    1 (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
  10. 10
    Coarsely grate cheese.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  11. 11
    Arrange flatbreads on a baking sheet pan. Spread with spinach dip, then top with cheese, sun-dried tomatoes, artichoke hearts, and shallots.
    2 flatbreads or naan
  12. 12
    Place baking sheet in the oven and bake until flatbreads are lightly crispy and cheese is melted, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
  13. 13
    To serve, cut flatbreads into wedges and divide between plates. Enjoy!

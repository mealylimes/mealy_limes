Spinach-Egg Tacos with Mexican Pickled Veggies (Escabeche) & Avocado

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 90% (5) $2.57/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 5eggs
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • 6small flour tortillas
  • brown sugar
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 jalapeño pepper
    1 medium carrot
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Peel, halve, and slice onion lengthwise into ½-inch wide strips. Place in a small bowl.
    ½ medium red onion
  3. 3
    Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Cut lengthwise into ½-inch wide strips and add to the bowl with the onion. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  4. 4
    Peel, trim, and slice carrot crosswise into ¼-inch thick rounds; add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, peel and thinly slice garlic. Transfer to a small saucepan and set aside.
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, jalapeño, carrot, and spices to the skillet; cook, stirring often, until veggies are crisp-tender, about 3 minutes. Return veggies to the bowl, reserving the skillet for later use.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
  8. 8
    While the veggies are cooking, add vinegar, water, sugar, and salt to the saucepan with the garlic; stir to combine. Bring the pickling liquid to a boil over high heat.
    ¼ cup red wine vinegar
    ¼ cup water
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Pour the pickling liquid into the bowl over the cooked veggies. Stir to combine and set aside to cool.
  10. 10
    Coarsely grate cheese and place in a medium bowl. Add eggs and spices, then whisk until combined.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
    5 eggs
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cumin, ground
  11. 11
    Roughly chop the spinach leaves and add to the bowl with the egg; stir to combine.
  12. 12
    Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat.
  13. 13
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Pour egg mixture into the skillet; scramble the eggs using a combination of stirring and folding, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan. Cook until eggs are no longer runny, about 3 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  14. 14
    While the eggs are cooking, warm tortillas in another skillet, oven, or microwave (optional), 15-30 seconds.
    6 small flour tortillas
  15. 15
    Halve and pit the avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide tortillas between plates and fill with spinach-scrambled eggs. Top with pickled veggies and avocado. Enjoy!

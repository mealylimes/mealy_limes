Spinach & Ricotta Stuffed Zucchini Boats with Blistered Tomato Toasts

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (18) $3.49/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make clean-up easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise. Scrape out the soft flesh from the middle of each half, place in a medium bowl, and set aside for later use.
  4. 4
    Arrange zucchini halves, cut-side up, on one half of the baking sheet; drizzle with oil and season with spices.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Add grape tomatoes to the other half of the baking sheet. Drizzle with oil, then season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until zucchini are starting to soften, 5-7 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, roughly chop reserved zucchini flesh and return to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Roughly chop spinach. Add to the bowl with the zucchini.
  8. 8
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  9. 9
    While the pan heats up, peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and garlic to the pan in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are softened, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  12. 12
    When the veggies are done, add ricotta, half of the Parmesan, salt, and pepper to the pan. Stir to combine the filling. (Reserve remaining Parmesan for the next step.)
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    When the zucchini boats have started to soften, divide filling between them, then top with reserved Parmesan. Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to bake until zucchini is tender, cheese is bubbly, and tomatoes have started to burst, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  14. 14
    When the zucchini boats are done, change oven to broil on high and position the rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  15. 15
    Arrange bread on another baking sheet. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes. Remove from oven and cut toast in half. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    2 slices sourdough bread
  16. 16
    To serve, divide zucchini boats and toast between plates. Top toast with blistered tomatoes and enjoy!

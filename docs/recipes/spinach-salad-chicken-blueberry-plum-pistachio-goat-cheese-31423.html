Spinach Salad with Chicken, Blueberry, Plum, Pistachio & Goat Cheese

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (8) $5.76/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 1shallot
  • 1lime
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • 2plums
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lime
    2 plums
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  2. 2
    Zest lime into a medium bowl. Juice half of the lime into the bowl with the lime zest, then add oil and spices. Whisk to combine the marinade. (Reserve remaining lime for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp coriander, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and place on a cutting board. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin cutlets. Add to the bowl with the marinade and turn to coat. Set aside.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  4. 4
    While the chicken is marinating, juice remaining lime into another medium bowl. Add oil, honey, Dijon, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel, halve, and slice shallot lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing and stir to coat.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Halve plums lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Cut each half into ¼-inch thick slices and add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Roughly chop pistachios. Add to the bowl and set aside.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
  8. 8
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add chicken (leaving any excess marinade in the bowl) and grill, flipping once, until cooked through and golden, 8-10 minutes. Remove to a cutting board.
  10. 10
    When the chicken is almost done, crumble goat cheese into the bowl with the dressing, then add spinach and blueberries. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  11. 11
    Thinly slice chicken into strips.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls and top with chicken. Enjoy!

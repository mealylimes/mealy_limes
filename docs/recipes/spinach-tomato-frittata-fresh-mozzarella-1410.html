Spinach & Tomato Frittata with Fresh Mozzarella

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (6) $2.40/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1tomato
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts salad spinner (optional) spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil and position rack under the broiler.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry spinach and tomato. Slice the tomato into rounds. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    1 tomato
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Thinly slice mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  4. 4
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    In a medium bowl, whisk together eggs, water, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
    6 eggs
    2 tbsp water
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add garlic and spinach. Cook until spinach begins to wilt, 1-2 minutes.
    about 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Pour in egg mixture. As eggs set around the edge of the skillet, gently lift cooked portions with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath. Cook until bottom and sides are firm but the top is still runny, 8-10 minutes.
  8. 8
    Add tomato and mozzarella.
  9. 9
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until the top is set, 2-3 minutes.
  10. 10
    To serve, cut frittata into wedges and place on a plate. Enjoy!

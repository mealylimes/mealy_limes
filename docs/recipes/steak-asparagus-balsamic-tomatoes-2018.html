Steak with Asparagus & Balsamic Tomatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (11) $6.21/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • balsamic vinegar
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, add salt, and bring to a boil.
    1 tsp salt
  2. 2
    Wash asparagus and snap off woody ends. Add to boiling water and cook until bright green and tender-crisp, 2-3 minutes. Drain in a colander.
    1 small bunch asparagus
  3. 3
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Wash and halve tomatoes. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Season steak with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and cover with foil.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  7. 7
    Once steak has been transferred, lower heat to medium. Add garlic and tomatoes to skillet and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  8. 8
    Add balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper to tomatoes. Stir and cook for another 1-2 minutes.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    To serve, arrange asparagus on a plate and top with tomato mixture. Add steak on the side and enjoy!

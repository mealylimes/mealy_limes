Steak, Asparagus & Strawberry Salad with Balsamic-Poppy Seed Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $7.82/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • brown sugar
  • poppy seeds
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 small bunch asparagus
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Snap or cut off woody ends of the asparagus; cut into bite-sized pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and sprinkle with salt and pepper; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until asparagus is tender-crisp, 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, pat steak dry with paper towels and season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, sugar, poppy seeds, Dijon, and spices in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp poppy seeds
    ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Trim and slice strawberries crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Trim off and discard tops and root ends of radishes; thinly slice and add to the bowl.
  11. 11
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Add roasted asparagus and mixed greens to the bowl; toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates. Top with steak and enjoy!

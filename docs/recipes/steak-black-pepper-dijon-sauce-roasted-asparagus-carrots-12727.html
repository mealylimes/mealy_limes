Steak with Black Pepper-Dijon Sauce, Roasted Asparagus & Carrots

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 87% (17) $5.91/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 1shallot
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the asparagus. Snap or cut off woody ends, then cut into bite-sized pieces. Transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    1 small bunch asparagus
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, peel, and slice carrot into thin rounds. Add to the baking sheet with the asparagus.
    1 medium carrot
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic and shallot; sprinkle both over the veggies. Drizzle with oil, season with salt, and toss to combine.
    1 clove garlic
    1 shallot
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast veggies, tossing once, until fork-tender, 12-15 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and loosely cover with foil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat; add cream, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the sauce and bring to a simmer. Cook until heated through and slightly thickened, 1-2 minutes. Remove from the heat.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy cream
    2 tbsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, thinly slice steak into strips.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide steak and veggies between plates. Top steak with sauce and enjoy!

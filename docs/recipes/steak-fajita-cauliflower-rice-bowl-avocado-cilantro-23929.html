Steak Fajita Cauliflower Rice Bowl with Avocado & Cilantro

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $7.40/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • ½avocado
  • 1 mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater or food processor measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lime
    1 red bell pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Juice half of the lime into a medium bowl. Cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  4. 4
    Add oil, soy sauce, and spices to the bowl with the lime juice. Stir to combine the spice paste and set aside.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp chili powder
    1 ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Pat steak dry with paper towels and place in the center of a baking sheet pan; drizzle with half of the spice paste, turning the steak to coat on both sides (reserve remaining spice paste for the veggies).
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the remaining spice paste.
  7. 7
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into ½-inch thick half-moons. Add to the bowl, then toss veggies in the spice paste to coat.
    1 medium red onion
  8. 8
    Add veggies to the baking sheet, arranging them in an even layer around the steak.
  9. 9
    Place baking sheet in the oven and roast, stirring veggies and flipping steak halfway through, until veggies are tender and steak is cooked to desired doneness, 13-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, cut cauliflower head into quarters; using a box grater, hold the stem end of the cauliflower and coarsely grate. (Alternatively, transfer cauliflower to the bowl of a food processor; pulse until rice-like in consistency.)
  11. 11
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  12. 12
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add cauliflower and salt; cook, stirring often, until cauliflower is lightly golden and cooked through, 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  13. 13
    While the cauliflower rice is cooking, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add half to the cauliflower rice and stir to combine. (Set remaining cilantro aside for serving.)
  14. 14
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Set aside.
  15. 15
    Transfer steak to the cutting board and thinly slice against the grain.
  16. 16
    Divide cauliflower rice, steak, veggies, and avocado between bowls; sprinkle with reserved cilantro. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing and enjoy!

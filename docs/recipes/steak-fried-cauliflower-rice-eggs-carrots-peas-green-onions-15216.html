Steak "Fried Cauliflower Rice" with Eggs, Carrots, Peas & Green Onions

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $5.15/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • 2eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • ⅓ cupfrozen peas
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ⅓ cupfrozen corn
  • chili-garlic sauce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater or food processor measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place soy sauce, vinegar, chili-garlic sauce, and maple syrup in a small bowl; whisk to combine the marinade.
    4 tsp soy sauce
    4 tsp rice vinegar
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
  2. 2
    Thinly slice steak against the grain into strips and transfer to the bowl with the marinade; toss to coat and set aside to marinate.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    1 medium carrot
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, in another small bowl, whisk together the eggs and salt.
    2 eggs
    1 pinch salt
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Pour eggs into the skillet; scramble using a combination of stirring and folding. Cook until eggs are no longer runny, 1-2 minutes. Transfer eggs to a plate and set aside.
    ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  7. 7
    Return skillet to medium-high heat; add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak strips and marinade to the skillet; stir-fry until just cooked through, 3-5 minutes. Transfer to the plate with the scrambled eggs.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  8. 8
    While the steak is cooking, place peas and corn in a colander and rinse under hot, running water (from the tap) to defrost; set aside to drain.
    ⅓ cup frozen peas
    ⅓ cup frozen corn
  9. 9
    Cut the cauliflower head into quarters, then trim off and discard the leaves and thick stem. Using a box grater or food processor, coarsely grate the cauliflower and place in a medium bowl. Set aside.
  10. 10
    Peel and small dice carrot.
  11. 11
    Wipe the skillet clean with paper towels and return to medium-high heat; add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the carrot and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 2 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic and ginger.
    1 clove garlic
  13. 13
    Add cauliflower, garlic, ginger, and salt to the skillet with the carrot; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until cauliflower begins to soften, about 3 minutes.
    ¼ tsp salt
  14. 14
    While the cauliflower is cooking, trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces.
  15. 15
    Add peas, corn, green onion, scrambled eggs, steak, and any accumulated juices to the skillet; stir to combine and continue to cook until fragrant and heated through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide fried cauliflower rice between bowls. Drizzle with additional oil and soy sauce, then sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp soy sauce
    2 tsp sesame seeds

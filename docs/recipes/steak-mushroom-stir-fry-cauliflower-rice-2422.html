Steak & Mushroom Stir Fry with Cauliflower Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (9) $7.34/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1 medium headcauliflower
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • black pepper
  • cornstarch
  • rice vinegar
  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) stainless steel skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Peel and mince or grate ginger. In a medium bowl, prepare marinade by whisking together the minced ginger, water, soy sauce, vinegar, cornstarch, and pepper.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ¼ cup water
    3 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    ½ tbsp cornstarch
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  2. 2
    Thinly slice steak and place in marinade; toss to coat and set aside.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  3. 3
    Wash and remove leaves from cauliflower. Using a food processor or grater, grate to rice-like texture.
    1 medium head cauliflower
  4. 4
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Add oil to skillet and swirl to coat bottom. Add cauliflower and cook, stirring frequently, until warmed through and slightly tender, 5-6 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Transfer to a bowl and cover with foil to keep warm.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Wash mushrooms and green onions. Thinly slice mushrooms. Trim ends and thinly slice onions on the diagonal. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    2 cloves garlic
  7. 7
    Once cauliflower rice has been transferred, add more oil to skillet. Remove steak slices from marinade, reserving the marinade in the bowl, and add to skillet. Cook until browned, 1-2 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Add garlic, mushrooms, ½ of the green onions (save the rest for garnishing), and reserved marinade to skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until mushrooms are tender and sauce has thickened.
  9. 9
    Wash and dry spinach.
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  10. 10
    Return steak and add spinach to skillet. Stir until spinach is wilted and ingredients are well combined.
  11. 11
    Serve stir fry over cauliflower rice and garnish with remaining green onions. Enjoy!

Steak Salad with Egg, Bell Pepper, Avocado & Blue Cheese Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $7.99/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled blue cheese
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2eggs
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½avocado
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ small pkgchives
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain eggs and transfer to a small bowl; add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ small pkg chives
    1 red bell pepper
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil; add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer steak to a clean plate, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    While the steak cooks, peel and mince garlic; place in a medium bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Juice lemon into the bowl with the garlic.
  8. 8
    Chop chives into small pieces; add to the bowl along with blue cheese, mayo, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled blue cheese
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-size pieces, add to the bowl, and set aside.
  11. 11
    Peel and roughly chop eggs.
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    Just before serving, halve and pit avocado; scoop the flesh out with a spoon and medium dice. Add to the bowl with the lettuce and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad, steak, and eggs between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

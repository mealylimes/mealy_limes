Steak Salad with Roasted Asparagus, Brussels Sprouts & Dijon Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $6.34/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ lbBrussels sprouts
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ¾ lbribeye steak
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts spatula tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb Brussels sprouts
    ½ small bunch asparagus
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
  3. 3
    Trim and quarter Brussels sprouts; transfer to a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Snap or cut off woody ends of the asparagus; cut into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl with the sprouts. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle with spices, and toss to combine.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    Transfer veggies to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. Place in the oven and roast until fork-tender, 12-15 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the salad.)
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the grill pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb ribeye steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Add steak and grill until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate, cover loosely with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, peel and mince garlic; transfer to the bowl used for the veggies.
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Add oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper to the bowl with the garlic; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    When the veggies are done, transfer to the bowl with the dressing and toss to combine. Set aside to cool, about 5 minutes.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces; add to the bowl with the veggies and toss to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide roasted vegetable salad between plates or bowls and top with steak. Enjoy!

Steak Zucchini Rolls with Creamy Chili-Garlic Dip & Arugula Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $5.27/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½yellow bell pepper
  • mayonnaise
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ bunch radish
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ yellow bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and let rest, 5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place mayo, chili-garlic sauce, and garlic powder in a small bowl; whisk to combine the dipping sauce and set aside.
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
  6. 6
    Trim, halve, and slice radishes into thin half-moons; place on another plate.
  7. 7
    Trim zucchini and use a vegetable peeler to make thin ribbons; add to the plate with the radish and set aside for the zucchini rolls.
  8. 8
    Place oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the dressing, along with the arugula, and toss to combine the salad; set aside.
  10. 10
    After the steak has rested, thinly slice against the grain and return to the plate.
  11. 11
    To assemble the zucchini rolls, arrange zucchini ribbons on a flat surface; place a few pieces of steak and radish at one end and roll tightly to secure. (Assemble in batches if necessary.)
  12. 12
    Divide zucchini rolls and salad between plates; serve with dipping sauce on the side and enjoy!

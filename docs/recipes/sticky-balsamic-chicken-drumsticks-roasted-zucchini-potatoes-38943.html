Sticky Balsamic Chicken Drumsticks with Roasted Zucchini & Potatoes

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (36) $2.38/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • balsamic vinegar
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 mediumyellow potato
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • onion powder
  • ketchup
  • brown sugar
  • smoked paprika
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Place oven racks in the upper and middle positions. Line two baking sheet pans with aluminum foil to make clean-up easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Pat chicken dry with paper towels and transfer to one of the baking sheets. Drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, flipping chicken halfway, until cooked through, 25-30 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and large dice potato. Transfer to the other baking sheet, drizzle with oil, and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer. Add baking sheet to the oven and roast until starting to soften, 10-12 minutes.
    1 medium yellow potato
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  5. 5
    While the potatoes are roasting, place vinegar, ketchup, Dijon, sugar, and spices in a small saucepan. Set pan over medium-high heat and cook, whisking occasionally, until sauce is reduced by half, 2-3 minutes. Transfer sauce to a medium bowl and set aside for the chicken.
    2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
    1 tbsp ketchup
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp smoked paprika
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, trim, and large dice zucchini. Place in another medium bowl, drizzle with oil, and season with spices. Toss to coat.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  7. 7
    When the potatoes are starting to soften, remove baking sheet from the oven. Add zucchini, toss to combine, and spread out in an even layer. Return to the oven and continue to roast until veggies are tender, 15 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    When the chicken and veggies are done, change oven to broil on high.
  9. 9
    Add roasted chicken to the bowl with the sauce, toss to coat, then return to the baking sheet. Place baking sheet on the top rack of the oven and broil until sauce is caramelized, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide chicken and veggies between plates. Enjoy!

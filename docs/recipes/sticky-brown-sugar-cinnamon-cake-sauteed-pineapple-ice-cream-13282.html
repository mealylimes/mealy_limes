Sticky Brown Sugar Cinnamon Cake with Sautéed Pineapple & Ice Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (3) $0.67/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • brown sugar
  • baking powder
  • nutmeg, ground
  • vanilla extract
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls muffin pan oven mitts silicone spatula small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Place butter in a small saucepan and melt over medium heat.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  3. 3
    Divide melted butter between 2 muffin cups and sprinkle with sugar. (Reserve pan for later use.)
    1 tbsp brown sugar
  4. 4
    Place flour, water, sugar, baking powder, vanilla, and spices in a small bowl; whisk to combine the batter.
    ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
    ¼ cup water
    3 tbsp brown sugar
    ½ tsp baking powder
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cinnamon
    1 pinch nutmeg, ground
  5. 5
    Divide batter between muffin cups. Place in the oven and bake until a knife or skewer inserted in the center comes out clean, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and cut off skin from pineapple. Core and small dice.
    ½ small pineapple
  7. 7
    Return saucepan to medium heat.
  8. 8
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add pineapple, sugar, vanilla, and cinnamon. Cook, stirring occasionally, until pineapple has softened, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
    1 pinch cinnamon
  9. 9
    Slide a knife down and around the side of each cake to loosen. Holding the muffin pan over a baking sheet pan, carefully invert, and tap the pan to loosen the cakes.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide cakes, pineapple, and ice cream between bowls. Enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

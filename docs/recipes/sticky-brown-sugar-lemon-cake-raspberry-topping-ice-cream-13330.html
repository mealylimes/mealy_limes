Sticky Brown Sugar Lemon Cake with Raspberry Topping & Ice Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (6) $1.38/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • ½lemon
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • brown sugar
  • baking powder
  • vanilla extract
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board grater (optional) ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons metal or wooden skewers (optional) mixing bowls muffin pan oven mitts silicone spatula small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Place butter in a small saucepan and melt over medium heat.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  3. 3
    Divide melted butter between 2 muffin cups, sprinkle with sugar, and set aside. (Reserve saucepan for later use.)
    1 tbsp brown sugar
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, zest, and juice lemon into a small bowl.
    ½ lemon
  5. 5
    Add flour, sugar, water, baking powder, vanilla, and salt to the bowl with the lemon; whisk to combine the batter.
    ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
    3 tbsp brown sugar
    2 tbsp water
    ½ tsp baking powder
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Divide batter between muffin cups. Place in the oven and bake until a knife or skewer inserted in the center comes out clean, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the raspberries.
    1 (6 oz) pkg raspberries
  8. 8
    Return saucepan to medium heat.
  9. 9
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add raspberries, sugar, and vanilla. Cook, stirring occasionally, until raspberries have softened, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
    1 ½ tbsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  10. 10
    Slide a knife down and around the side of each cake to loosen. Holding the muffin pan over a baking sheet pan, carefully invert, and tap the pan to loosen the cakes.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide cakes, raspberry sauce, and ice cream between bowls. Enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

Sticky Salted Caramel Apple Wedges with Peanuts

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $1.37/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 2Gala apples
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • granulated sugar
  • water
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place water in a small saucepan. Add sugar, gently stir to combine, and place over medium-high heat. Cook, without stirring, until sugar begins to turn light golden in places, 5-7 minutes.
    1 fl oz (⅛ cup) water
    ½ cup granulated sugar
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, place cream, butter, and salt in another small saucepan; place over medium heat. Cook just until butter is melted and cream is heated through (do not boil), 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy cream
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ⅛ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Roughly chop peanuts and set aside.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  4. 4
    When the sugar begins to turn golden, gently swirl the saucepan in a circular motion to distribute the caramelization. Return to the heat and continue to cook, swirling occasionally, until the entire mixture is bright amber in color, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  5. 5
    Slowly add the warm cream mixture, a few tablespoons at a time, to the saucepan with the caramelized sugar, whisking after each addition (be careful, the sugar mixture will bubble vigorously). Once all of the cream is added, continue to whisk until smooth; set aside.
  6. 6
    Wash, dry, quarter, and core apples; slice each quarter into wedges.
    2 Gala apples
  7. 7
    To serve, divide apple wedges between plates. Drizzle with warm caramel sauce and sprinkle with peanuts. Enjoy!

Strawberry, Bacon & Spinach Salad with Feta & Poppy Seed Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 91% (16) $3.79/serving
Ingredients
  • ⅛ cupalmonds, sliced
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 6 slicesbacon
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • apple cider vinegar
  • black pepper
  • honey
  • mayonnaise
  • poppy seeds
  • salt
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Add the bacon to the skillet and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side.
    6 slices bacon
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the spinach. Wash the strawberries, trim the tops, and thinly slice.
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
  4. 4
    In a small bowl, prepare the dressing by whisking together mayo, vinegar, honey, poppy seeds, salt, and pepper.
    ¼ cup mayonnaise
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    ½ tsp poppy seeds
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Measure out the almonds.
    ⅛ cup almonds, sliced
  6. 6
    Once the bacon is cooked, crumble or chop into small pieces.
  7. 7
    Arrange a bed of spinach on a plate, top with the strawberries, bacon, feta, and almonds, and drizzle with the dressing. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

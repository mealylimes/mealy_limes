Strawberry, Banana & Lemon Oatmeal Protein Bites

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 76% (18) $0.65/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1egg
  • ½lemon
  • ¼ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • almond meal/flour
  • 1banana
  • oats, quick cooking
  • vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts parchment paper potato masher spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet pan with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Peel banana, break into chunks, and place in a medium bowl. Mash with a fork or potato masher until smooth.
    1 banana
  3. 3
    Wash, dry, zest, and juice lemon into the bowl with the banana. Add egg and maple syrup, then stir to combine.
    ½ lemon
    1 egg
    1 tbsp pure maple syrup
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, trim, and roughly chop strawberries. Add to the bowl along with oats, protein powder, and almond flour. Stir once more to combine the batter.
    ¼ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    1 cup oats, quick cooking
    1 serving vanilla whey-based protein powder (containing 20 grams protein/serving)
    2 tbsp almond meal/flour
  5. 5
    Using a ¼-cup measure, scoop batter onto the baking sheet, leaving some space between. Use the back of a spoon to spread each "bite" into a 3-inch circle. Place in the oven and bake until just firm to the touch, 12-15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Allow to cool for a few minutes before transferring to plates. Enjoy!

Strawberry Cheesecake Bites

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $0.61/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • powdered sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • ½ (8 oz) blockcream cheese
Cookware
colander grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls silicone spatula strainer
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry strawberries; set aside.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
  2. 2
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate cream cheese into a medium bowl; add vanilla. Use a silicone spatula or fork to soften and combine the cream cheese mixture, creating a smooth cream.
    ½ (8 oz) block cream cheese
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  3. 3
    Using a strainer, sift powdered sugar into the bowl with the cream cheese. Use the spatula or fork to mash and combine the mixture until smooth and no lumps remain.
    ¼ cup powdered sugar
  4. 4
    To serve, divide strawberries and dip between plates. Enjoy!

Strawberry Kiwi Greek Yogurt Bowl with Pecans

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 10 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 94% (8) $2.79/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • honey
  • 2 cupsplain Greek yogurt
  • 2kiwifruit
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ½ cuppecan halves
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons
Instructions
  1. 1
    Divide yogurt between bowls.
    2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fruit.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    2 kiwifruit
  3. 3
    Remove stems from the strawberries; chop into small pieces.
  4. 4
    Trim ends from kiwis and peel; chop into small pieces.
  5. 5
    Coarsely chop the pecans.
    ½ cup pecans, dry roasted
  6. 6
    To serve, top yogurt with fruit, nuts, and honey. Enjoy!
    2 tbsp honey

Strawberry Panzanella (Italian Bread Salad) with Chicken, Feta & Mint

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $4.65/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the chicken in half horizontally to form thin fillets. Season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and pan-fry until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  5. 5
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and mince shallot. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    1 ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp white wine vinegar
    1 ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Trim and quarter strawberries. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl and set aside. (Reserve remaining mint for serving.)
  10. 10
    Cut or tear bread into bite-sized pieces.
    2 slices whole grain bread
  11. 11
    Return skillet (with drippings) to low heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bread and cook, stirring occasionally, until croutons are golden and toasted, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  12. 12
    Add croutons to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  13. 13
    Slice chicken into thin strips.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide panzanella salad between plates; top with chicken, feta, and reserved mint. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

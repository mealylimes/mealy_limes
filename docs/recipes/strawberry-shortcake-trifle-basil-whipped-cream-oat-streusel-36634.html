"Strawberry Shortcake" Trifle with Basil, Whipped Cream & Oat Streusel

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $1.55/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • ½lemon
  • ½ (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • cornstarch
  • brown sugar
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • oats, quick cooking
  • vanilla extract
  • water
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Place a medium bowl (preferably metal) in the freezer to chill. (If space is limited, place in the refrigerator.)
  3. 3
    Place flour, oats, sugar, and salt in another medium bowl. Whisk to combine.
    ½ cup all-purpose flour
    ¼ cup oats, quick cooking
    ¼ cup brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Cut butter into small pieces. Place in a medium saucepan and melt over medium heat, stirring occasionally. Add melted butter to the bowl with flour mixture and stir to combine; transfer to a baking sheet pan and spread out in an even layer. (Wipe pan clean and reserve for later use.)
    3 tbsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Place baking sheet in oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until the streusel is golden brown and crisp, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  7. 7
    Juice lemon into the reserved pan. Trim and roughly chop strawberries, then add to the pan along with sugar. Stir to combine.
    1 tbsp brown sugar
  8. 8
    Set pan over medium heat and cook, stirring frequently, until strawberries are very soft and release their juices, 6-8 minutes.
  9. 9
    While the strawberries are cooking, place cornstarch and water in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the slurry.
    1 tsp cornstarch
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) water
  10. 10
    When the strawberries are very soft, add slurry to the pan. Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until the compote thickens, 1-2 minutes more. Transfer to a clean small bowl and set aside to cool, 5 minutes.
  11. 11
    Pick basil leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. 12
    When the compote is cool, add basil to the bowl and stir to combine. Set aside.
  13. 13
    Remove bowl from the freezer. Pour cold cream and vanilla into the bowl; use a whisk to beat the cream vigorously, until medium-soft peaks form, 1-2 minutes. (For medium-soft peaks, when you pull the whisk out of the bowl, the cream should hold its shape, with a peak that curls over slightly.)
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
  14. 14
    To serve, divide streusel, compote, and whipped cream between small bowls, cups, or jars, alternating ingredients to create layers. Enjoy!

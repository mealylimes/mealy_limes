Strawberry Shrimp Arugula Salad with Feta, Almonds & Herb Vinaigrette

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $6.56/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1lemon
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgstrawberries
  • ¼ cupalmonds, sliced
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Drain shrimp in a colander and rinse under cold water until there are no more ice chunks. Spread out on a clean towel and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce (skip the arugula if it came pre-washed).
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
    1 (16 oz) pkg strawberries
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 lemon
  3. 3
    Trim off the stem ends and slice the strawberries; add to a medium salad bowl along with the arugula.
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard the ends of the green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle; add to the bowl with the strawberries and toss to combine.
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into a small bowl; add oil, Dijon, vinegar, maple syrup, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp dill
    ¼ tsp basil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Add the shrimp and garlic to the skillet; cook until shrimp are opaque, 1-2 minutes per side. Once done, transfer to a plate.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide the salad between the plates, top with feta, sliced almonds, and shrimp. Drizzle with dressing and enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
    ¼ cup almonds, sliced

Strip Steak & Bell Pepper Fajitas with Guacamole & Butter Lettuce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (1) $8.17/serving
Ingredients
  • 1avocado
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1lime
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • cumin, ground
  • virgin coconut oil
  • salt
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry bell peppers. Seed, slice into thin strips, and transfer to a bowl.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 yellow bell pepper
  2. 2
    Peel, thinly slice into half rings, and add onion to bowl with peppers. Peel and mince garlic; add ½ to bowl with vegetables and place the other ½ into a medium bowl for preparing guacamole.
    ½ medium red onion
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Halve avocado and remove pit; scoop out the flesh into bowl with garlic.
    1 avocado
  4. 4
    Wash and dry cilantro. Shave leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add ½ to bowl with avocado and save the rest for garnishing.
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Season steak generously with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and loosely cover with foil.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  8. 8
    Add lime juice, salt, and pepper to avocado. Mash with a fork until smooth. Set aside.
    juice of ½ lime
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    After steak has been transferred add more oil to skillet. Add peppers, onion, and garlic from bowl. Stirring frequently, cook until peppers are tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Wash and dry lettuce.
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  11. 11
    Cut remaining ½ lime into wedges.
  12. 12
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  13. 13
    Return steak slices and any juices to skillet. Season with chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Stir and cook for 1-2 minutes.
    ½ tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  14. 14
    To serve, fill lettuce leaves with fajita mix. Garnish with guacamole, remaining cilantro, and lime wedges. Enjoy!

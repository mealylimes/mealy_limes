Strip Steak & Brussels Sprouts Stir Fry with Jalapeño Pepper

Dinner Meat
⏱ 45 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (10) $5.58/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 lbBrussels sprouts
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1jalapeño pepper
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • cornstarch
  • soy sauce
  • rice vinegar
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash brussels sprouts, carrot, jalapeño, and green onions.
    1 lb brussels sprouts
    1 medium carrot
    1 jalapeño pepper
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  2. 2
    Trim, peel, and slice carrot on the diagonal into ¼ inch rounds. Transfer to a bowl.
  3. 3
    Halve, seed, and thinly slice jalapeño crosswise. Peel and mince garlic. Peel and mince or grate ginger. Transfer all to a small bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger
  4. 4
    Trim green onion root ends and slice on the diagonal. Add whites to bowl with jalapeño, garlic, and ginger; transfer the greens to a separate bowl and save for garnishing.
  5. 5
    Trim rough ends of brussels sprouts and halve lengthwise. Transfer to a separate bowl.
  6. 6
    In a small bowl, prepare sauce by whisking together soy sauce, vinegar, water, and cornstarch.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tbsp water
    ¼ tsp cornstarch
  7. 7
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Thinly slice steak into strips.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
  9. 9
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add steak strips in a single layer and cook until browned, 2-3 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  10. 10
    Add more oil to skillet, followed by brussels sprouts. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown, 2-3 minutes. Transfer to plate with steak.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  11. 11
    Add more oil to skillet, followed by green onion whites, jalapeño, garlic, and ginger. Stir and cook until fragrant, 15-30 seconds. Add carrot and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 2-3 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  12. 12
    Return steak and sprouts to skillet and add sauce. Cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce is thickened, 2-3 minutes.
  13. 13
    To serve, place stir fry on a plate and garnish with remaining green onions. Enjoy!

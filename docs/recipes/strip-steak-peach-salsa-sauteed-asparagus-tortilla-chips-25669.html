Strip Steak with Peach Salsa, Sautéed Asparagus & Tortilla Chips

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $6.06/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 small bunchasparagus
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2peaches
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • 2 oztortilla chips
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or regular skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the pan heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the pan is hot, coat with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and snap (or cut) off woody ends of the asparagus; cut spears into bite-sized pieces.
    1 small bunch asparagus
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add asparagus, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat, cover with a lid to keep warm, and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pit. Medium dice the flesh. Place in a small bowl, along with the salsa, and stir to combine.
    2 peaches
    ½ cup salsa
  8. 8
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  9. 9
    Divide steak, asparagus, and chips between plates; top steak with half of the salsa. Serve remaining salsa on the side for dipping. Enjoy!
    2 oz tortilla chips

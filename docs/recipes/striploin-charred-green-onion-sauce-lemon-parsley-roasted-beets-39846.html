Striploin with Charred Green Onion Sauce & Lemon-Parsley Roasted Beets

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 85% (45) $6.43/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 4 mediumbeets
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • honey
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½lemon
  • mayonnaise
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    4 medium beets
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ lemon
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Peel and trim beets; halve lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick half-moons. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until beets are fork-tender, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the pan heats up, trim green onions. Place on a plate, drizzle with oil, and season with salt.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add green onions. Grill until tender and charred, 2-3 minutes per side; return to the plate. (Reserve grill pan for later use.)
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on another plate; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Return the pan to medium-high heat and coat with oil. Add steak and cook until desired doneness, 2-5 minutes per side. Add to the plate with the green onions, loosely cover with aluminum foil, and set aside to rest, 5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, place yogurt, mayo, water, and spices in a small bowl, then whisk to combine the sauce.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp water
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Mince grilled green onions. Add to the bowl with the yogurt sauce and whisk once more to combine. Set aside.
  12. 12
    Juice lemon into another small bowl. Add oil, honey, Dijon, and salt, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ⅛ tsp salt
  13. 13
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the dressing and stir to combine.
  14. 14
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide green onion yogurt and beets between plates; top yogurt with steak and drizzle beets with dressing. Enjoy!

Stuffed Tomatoes with Cheesy Taco Chicken, Lettuce, Avocado & Yogurt

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $2.19/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • ¼ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½avocado
  • 4tomatoes
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 22 ½ mltaco seasoning
  • ⅔ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken, taco seasoning, and salt to the skillet; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    1 ½ tbsp taco seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
    ½ avocado
    4 tomatoes
  4. 4
    Halve lettuce lengthwise, then chop crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Place on a plate.
  5. 5
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the plate with the lettuce.
  6. 6
    Trim tomatoes. Slicing from the top down, cut tomatoes into wedges, being careful to not cut completely through to the bottom. Season with salt.
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Add cheese to the skillet with the chicken and stir to combine.
    ⅔ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend
  8. 8
    To serve, divide tomatoes between plates. Stuff with cheesy chicken, then top with lettuce, avocado, and yogurt. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt

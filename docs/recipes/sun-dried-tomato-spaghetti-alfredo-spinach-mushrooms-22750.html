Sun-Dried Tomato Spaghetti Alfredo with Spinach & Mushrooms

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (134) $0.15/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 ozspaghetti pasta
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • 80 mlsun-dried tomato pesto
  • ½ (16oz) bagfrozen chopped spinach
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsliced white mushrooms
Cookware
colander measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz spaghetti pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add mushrooms, salt, and pepper; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 (8 oz) pkg sliced white mushrooms
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Add spinach, Alfredo sauce, pesto, and water to the skillet; cook, stirring often, until heated through, about 5 minutes. Reduce heat to low and allow to simmer gently until the pasta is finished cooking.
    ½ (16oz) bag frozen chopped spinach
    ½ (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
    ⅓ cup sun-dried tomato pesto
    2 tbsp water
  5. 5
    When the pasta is done, drain in a colander and add to the skillet with the sauce. Toss to combine and remove from heat.
  6. 6
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

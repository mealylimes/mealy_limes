Sweet Chili Salmon Sandwich with Avocado & Ginger-Tahini Dressed Salad

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (6) $5.56/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 2whole grain buns or rolls
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • tahini
  • ½ bunchradish
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula toaster (optional) tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place vinegar, sugar, water, chili-garlic sauce, and salt in a small saucepan; whisk to combine. Set over medium-high heat and bring to a boil, whisking occasionally.
    1 ½ tbsp rice vinegar
    1 ½ tbsp brown sugar
    1 tbsp water
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, in a small bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth.
    1 tbsp water
    1 ½ tsp cornstarch
  3. 3
    Once the sauce is boiling, add cornstarch slurry to the pan in a steady stream while whisking quickly. Cook, whisking constantly, until the sweet chili sauce thickens, about 30 seconds. Remove from heat, cover, and set aside.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, pat salmon dry with paper towels and place skin-side down on a plate; season the top side with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add salmon, skin-side down, and cook, flipping once, until skin is crispy and flesh is opaque, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a clean plate and set aside.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ bunch radish
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ avocado
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
  8. 8
    Peel and mince ginger; place in a medium bowl. Add tahini, vinegar, soy sauce, oil, and chili-garlic sauce. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp tahini
    1 ½ tbsp rice vinegar
    1 ½ tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
  9. 9
    Trim and thinly slice radishes; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  11. 11
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and thinly slice. Add to the plate with the salmon.
  12. 12
    Toast buns if desired.
    2 whole grain buns or rolls
  13. 13
    Separate lettuce leaves; set aside two leaves per sandwich. Chop or tear remaining leaves into bite-sized pieces, then add to the bowl with the dressing and veggies. Toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    Place the bottom half of a bun on each plate. Top with salmon, sweet chili sauce, avocado, and reserved lettuce leaves; close with the top half of each bun. Serve with salad on the side and enjoy!

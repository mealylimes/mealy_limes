Sweet Potato, Apple & Sausage Breakfast Skillet

Meat Breakfast
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (15) $2.32/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • salt
  • 1Granny Smith apple
  • butter, unsalted
  • ½ lbsweet potato
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½lemon
  • 1pork sausage
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons medium saucepan nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lb sweet potato
    1 Granny Smith apple
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
    ½ lemon
  2. 2
    Peel and small dice the potato (cut into ¼-inch cubes). Transfer to a medium saucepan and cover with hot water (from the tap). Bring to a boil over high heat; reduce heat and simmer, covered, for 7 minutes. Once finished, drain the potatoes and set aside.
  3. 3
    Slice the sausage into ½-inch thick rounds.
    1 pork sausage
  4. 4
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 ½ tsp butter, unsalted
  5. 5
    Add the sausage to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned on all sides and cooked through, 10-12 minutes.
  6. 6
    Peel and small dice onion.
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Turn the heat to medium and add the potatoes and onion. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion has softened, 3-4 minutes.
  8. 8
    Core and small dice the apple.
  9. 9
    Turn the heat to low. Stir in the apples and spinach and cook, stirring occasionally until the spinach has wilted, 2 minutes.
  10. 10
    Remove the skillet from the heat. Juice the lemon into the skillet and stir to combine; season with salt.
    ½ tsp salt
  11. 11
    Divide the veggies and sausage between plates and enjoy!

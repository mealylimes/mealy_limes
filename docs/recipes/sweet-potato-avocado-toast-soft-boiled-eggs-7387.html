Sweet Potato "Avocado Toast" with Soft-boiled Eggs

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $1.71/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • 4eggs
  • 1 lbsweet potato
  • 1avocado
  • ½lemon
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts potato masher small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil and position rack in the middle of the oven.
  2. 2
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    4 eggs
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 lb sweet potato
    1 avocado
    ½ lemon
  4. 4
    Trim off and discard ends of sweet potatoes; peel and slice lengthwise into ½-inch thick pieces. Discard the two small, outer slices, then place remaining slices on a baking sheet lined with aluminum foil.
  5. 5
    Rub the oil over both sides of the sweet potato slices, then season with salt.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Place sweet potatoes in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, 8-10 minutes, or until tender. (Keep a close watch on the sweet potatoes to avoid burning.)
  7. 7
    Halve and pit the avocado, then scoop out into a small bowl.
  8. 8
    Grate zest from the lemon and set aside, then juice lemon into the bowl with the avocado. Add salt and garlic powder and mash with a potato masher or fork; set aside.
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp garlic powder
  9. 9
    Peel and halve the eggs.
  10. 10
    To serve, spread a spoonful of avocado mash over each slice of sweet potato; top with eggs, lemon zest, chili powder, and salt. Enjoy!
    ¼ tsp chili powder
    ⅛ tsp salt

Sweet Potato Burrito Bowl with Kidney Bean Chili, Cheddar & Avocado

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (43) $2.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • cayenne pepper
  • chili powder
  • paprika
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cankidney beans
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½avocado
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil and set aside.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Peel and cut sweet potato lengthwise into 1-inch thick wedges (4-6 pieces per potato, depending on size), then transfer to the baking sheet. Drizzle with oil and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp chili powder
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place sweet potato wedges in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until fork-tender, about 25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can kidney beans
  6. 6
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and corn; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ cup frozen corn
  9. 9
    While the veggies are cooking, trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper.
  10. 10
    Add beans, bell pepper, tomatoes, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine the chili and bring to a simmer.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    2 tsp chili powder
    1 tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  11. 11
    Once the chili is simmering, reduce heat to medium and cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender and sauce has thickened, 5-6 minutes. Remove from heat.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, coarsely grate cheese.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  13. 13
    Halve and pit avocado. Slice thinly while still in skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide sweet potato wedges between plates or bowls; top with chili, cheese, and avocado. Enjoy!

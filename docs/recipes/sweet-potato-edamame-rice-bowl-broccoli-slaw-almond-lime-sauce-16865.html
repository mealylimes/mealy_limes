Sweet Potato-Edamame Rice Bowl with Broccoli Slaw & Almond-Lime Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (4) $2.09/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • natural almond butter
  • ¾ lbsweet potato
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1lime
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • ¾ cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ¾ lb sweet potato
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 lime
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  5. 5
    Peel and medium dice sweet potato; transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with spices. Toss to combine, then spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp coriander, ground
    ⅛ tsp cumin, ground
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, tossing once, until sweet potatoes are fork-tender, about 20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    While the sweet potatoes are roasting, place edamame in a colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Set aside to drain.
    ¾ cup edamame, shelled
  8. 8
    Peel and mince ginger and garlic; place both in a small bowl.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Juice lime into the bowl with the ginger and garlic; add almond butter, water, and soy sauce. Whisk to combine the sauce, adding additional water, 1 teaspoon at a time, if necessary, to reach a drizzling consistency. Set sauce aside.
    3 tbsp natural almond butter
    2 tbsp water
    1 ½ tsp soy sauce
  10. 10
    Trim, seed, and cut bell pepper into thin strips; transfer to a plate.
  11. 11
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into ¼-inch pieces; add to the plate with the bell pepper.
  12. 12
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  13. 13
    When the rice is done, add cilantro and stir to combine.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide rice, roasted sweet potatoes, broccoli slaw, edamame, bell pepper, and green onions between bowls. Drizzle with sauce and enjoy!
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw

Sweet & Sour Thai Chicken Vegetable Soup

Dinner Meat
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (20) $3.81/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • fish sauce
  • crushed red pepper
  • turmeric, ground
  • honey
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 1lime
  • 1 capportobello mushroom
  • rice vinegar
  • ½orange bell pepper
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater large pot measuring cups measuring spoons stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 medium carrot
    ½ orange bell pepper
    1 cap portobello mushroom
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Cut chicken into 1-inch pieces, set aside.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  3. 3
    Heat coconut oil in a large pot over medium heat.
    1 ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  4. 4
    Peel and mince the garlic. Peel and mince or grate the ginger. Add both to the pot.
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Add to the pot turmeric and crushed red pepper, stirring until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
    1 tsp turmeric
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  6. 6
    Add chicken pieces to pot, stirring occasionally.
  7. 7
    Peel and thinly slice carrot on the bias. Cut the bell pepper lengthwise into quarters; remove and discard the seeds and white membrane then slice thinly. Add veggies to the pot.
  8. 8
    Remove the stems from the mushroom and carefully scrape out the gills with a spoon, making sure not to damage the rim of the mushrooms. Cut cap in half, thinly slice and add to the pot.
  9. 9
    Cook chicken and vegetables, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are tender, about 5-7 minutes.
  10. 10
    Add broth, coconut milk, fish sauce, honey, and vinegar and stir to combine.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    1 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp honey
    1 ½ tsp rice vinegar
  11. 11
    Bring soup to boil and then reduce heat to low. Simmer for 8-10 minutes, allowing the chicken to cook fully and flavors to blend.
  12. 12
    Shave the cilantro leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  13. 13
    Juice the lime and add to the pot right before serving, stir to combine.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and garnish with cilantro. Enjoy!

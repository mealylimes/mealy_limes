Sweet Soy Beef Stir-Fry with Peaches, Tomatoes, Basil & Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 93% (21) $6.12/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2peaches
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • brown sugar
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 peaches
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    While the rice is cooking, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels, place on a cutting board, and slice crosswise into thin strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add beef and stir-fry until just cooked through, 3-4 minutes. Remove to a plate and loosely cover with aluminum foil to keep warm. (Reserve skillet with drippings for later use.)
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, using a clean cutting board, quarter peaches lengthwise (around the pit); twist quarters to separate, then remove and discard pits. Large dice and place in a medium bowl.
  8. 8
    Halve grape tomatoes. Add to the bowl with the peaches and set aside.
  9. 9
    Peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    1 ½ tsp neutral vegetable oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  11. 11
    Add peaches and tomatoes to the skillet; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 5-6 minutes more. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Place half in the reserved bowl, along with soy sauce and sugar, then whisk to combine the stir-fry sauce. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp brown sugar
  13. 13
    When the peaches and tomatoes are softened, return steak strips (and any accumulated juices) to the skillet, then add stir-fry sauce. Continue to cook, stirring frequently, until the sauce has thickened and steak is warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between plates or bowls. Top with reserved basil and enjoy!

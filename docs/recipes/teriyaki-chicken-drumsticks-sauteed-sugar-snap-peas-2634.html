Teriyaki Chicken Drumsticks with Sautéed Sugar Snap Peas

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (14) $3.75/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 ¼ lbchicken drumsticks
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 2 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • black pepper
  • cornstarch
  • honey
  • rice vinegar
  • salt
  • sesame seeds
  • soy sauce
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 500°F, position rack in the centre, and place a baking sheet inside to warm up.
  2. 2
    Peel and mince the garlic. Peel and mince or grate the ginger.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  3. 3
    In a medium bowl, whisk together water and cornstarch. Add the minced garlic and ginger, honey, rice vinegar, and soy sauce; whisk again.
    2 tbsp cold water
    ½ tsp cornstarch
    1 tbsp honey
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    2 tbsp soy sauce
  4. 4
    Add the chicken to the sauce and toss to coat.
    1 ¼ lb chicken drumsticks
  5. 5
    Remove baking sheet from the oven and line with foil. Add the chicken, reserving the sauce in the bowl.
  6. 6
    Place in the oven and bake until chicken is golden brown and cooked through, 25-30 minutes. Spoon teriyaki sauce over the chicken a couple times during cooking until there is no more sauce left.
  7. 7
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Wash the snap peas.
    2 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
  9. 9
    Add oil to the skillet and swirl to coat bottom. Add the peas and season with salt and pepper. Cook, tossing occasionally, until bright green and tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Serve chicken drumsticks with sugar snap peas on the side. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and enjoy!
    1 tsp sesame seeds

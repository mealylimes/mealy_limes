Teriyaki Chicken Wings with Veggie Sticks & Ranch Dip

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $2.69/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 3 mediumcarrots
  • 3 stickscelery
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 lbchicken wings, split with tips removed
  • black pepper
  • dill, dried
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • garlic powder
  • honey
  • mayonnaise
  • onion powder
  • salt
  • soy sauce
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lemon
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 500°F and position rack in the centre. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Place the chicken wings on the baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper; toss to coat. Spread out in a single layer.
    1 lb chicken wings, split with tips removed
    ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place in the oven and bake, flipping halfway through cooking time, until cooked through, 15-20 minutes.
  4. 4
    In a small bowl, prepare the ranch dip by mixing together lemon juice, mayo, onion powder, garlic powder, dill, salt, and pepper. Place in the fridge to chill.
    ½ lemon
    ⅓ cup mayonnaise
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp dill
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Wash and dry the carrots, celery, and cucumber. Peel carrots, trim ends, and cut into sticks. Trim ends and cut celery into sticks. Cut cucumber into sticks.
    3 medium carrots
    3 sticks celery
    ½ English cucumber
  6. 6
    Peel and mince or grate the ginger.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  7. 7
    In a small saucepan, prepare the teriyaki sauce by combining water, soy sauce, honey, ginger, and garlic powder. Bring to a boil and allow to boil until the mixture has reduced and thickened, 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ½ cup water
    6 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tbsp honey
    1 tsp garlic powder
  8. 8
    Once the wings are cooked, transfer them to a bowl and pour the teriyaki sauce over top; toss to coat.
  9. 9
    Serve wings with veggie sticks and ranch dip. Enjoy!

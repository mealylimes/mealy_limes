Tex-Mex Scrambled Egg-Black Bean Bowl with Avocado, Cheese & Salsa

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (20) $2.13/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 4eggs
  • ½avocado
  • 1 (15 oz) canblack beans
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ cupsalsa
  • 22 ½ mltaco seasoning
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Mexican blend
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ avocado
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper, taco seasoning, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tbsp taco seasoning
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans.
    1 (15 oz) can black beans
  6. 6
    When the bell pepper is softened, add beans to the skillet. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 1-2 minutes more. Transfer to a medium bowl and set aside. (Wipe skillet clean with paper towels and reserve for later use.)
  7. 7
    While the beans are cooking, place eggs, salt and pepper in another medium bowl. Whisk to combine.
    4 eggs
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Return the skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet and scramble using a combination of stirring and flipping, making sure to bring in all the mixture from the edges of the pan. Cook until the eggs are set, then remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the beans, stir to combine, and set aside.
  10. 10
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice crosswise into thin half-moons.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide beans, eggs, salsa, avocado, and cheese between bowls. Enjoy!
    ½ cup salsa
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Mexican blend

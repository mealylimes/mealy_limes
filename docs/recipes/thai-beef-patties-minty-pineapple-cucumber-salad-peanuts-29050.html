Thai Beef Patties with Minty Pineapple-Cucumber Salad & Peanuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (14) $4.21/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • fish sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1English cucumber
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • ½ smallpineapple
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • rice vinegar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small pineapple
    1 English cucumber
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces. Divide between two medium bowls, then set one bowl aside for the salad.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the remaining bowl with the green onions along with beef, bread crumbs, egg, fish sauce, chili-garlic sauce, and spices. Use your hands to combine the beef mixture. Using a ¼-cup measure, form the mixture into ½-inch thick patties and place on a plate.
    1 clove garlic
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    1 tbsp fish sauce
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ½ tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add patties and cook, flipping once, until golden and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side. Remove to a clean plate.
    2 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, add fish sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper to the remaining bowl with the green onions. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 ½ tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Cut off skin from pineapple; quarter, core, and cut the flesh into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Pick mint leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl and stir to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide patties and salad between plates or bowls. Top salad with peanuts and enjoy!
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted

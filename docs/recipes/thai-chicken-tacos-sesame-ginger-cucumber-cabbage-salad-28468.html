Thai Chicken Tacos with Sesame-Ginger Cucumber & Cabbage Salad

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (14) $2.04/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbchicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lime
  • 10 mlThai curry paste, red
  • 15 mlmiso paste, white
  • ½ (14 oz) bagshredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  • 6hard taco shells
  • ¼ cupsesame ginger dressing
Cookware
chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place curry paste, oil, and fish sauce in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the marinade.
    2 tsp Thai curry paste, red
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp fish sauce
  2. 2
    Slice chicken into ½-inch thick strips. Add to the bowl with the marinade, stir to coat, and set aside for 10 minutes.
    ¾ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  3. 3
    When the chicken is done marinating, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken strips and marinade; cook, stirring occasionally, until cooked through, 5-6 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place dressing and miso paste in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine.
    ¼ cup sesame ginger dressing
    1 tbsp miso paste, white
  6. 6
    Wash and dry cucumber; using a clean cutting board, trim and small dice. Add to the bowl with the dressing along with the shredded cabbage. Stir to combine the salad and set aside.
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ (14 oz) bag shredded cabbage (coleslaw mix)
  7. 7
    Wash, dry, and cut lime into wedges; set aside for serving.
    ½ lime
  8. 8
    Warm taco shells in the microwave if desired.
    6 hard taco shells
  9. 9
    Divide taco shells between plates, then fill with a small portion of the salad and chicken. Serve with remaining salad and lime wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

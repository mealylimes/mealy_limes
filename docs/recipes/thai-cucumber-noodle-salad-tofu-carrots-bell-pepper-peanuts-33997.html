Thai Cucumber "Noodle" Salad with Tofu, Carrots, Bell Pepper & Peanuts

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 55% (11) $3.56/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • ½shallot
  • ½red bell pepper
  • 2English cucumbers
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • ½ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½orange
  • rice vinegar
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Crumble the tofu with your fingers into the skillet, then add sesame seeds, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tofu is golden and warmed through, 5-7 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
    2 tsp sesame seeds
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ orange
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    2 English cucumbers
    1 medium carrot
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  4. 4
    Juice orange into a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince ginger and shallot. Add both to the bowl with the orange juice along with oil, vinegar, soy sauce, peanut butter, and salt. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    ½ shallot
    2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp natural peanut butter
    ¼ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Trim and halve cucumbers crosswise, then run a vegetable peeler along the length of each cucumber to make ribbons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl, along with the tofu, and toss to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    Roughly chop peanuts.
    ½ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  11. 11
    To serve, divide the salad between plates or bowls and sprinkle with peanuts. Enjoy!

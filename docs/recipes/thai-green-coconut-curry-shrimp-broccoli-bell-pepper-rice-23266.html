Thai Green Coconut Curry with Shrimp, Broccoli, Bell Pepper & Rice

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (6) $4.20/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½lime
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • 30 mlThai curry paste, green
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ lime
  4. 4
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, trim and seed bell pepper; slice lengthwise into thin strips.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add curry paste and salt; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute.
    2 tsp virgin coconut oil
    2 tbsp Thai curry paste, green
    ¼ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Add bell pepper, broccoli, coconut milk, and broth to the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. Once the curry is simmering, reduce heat to medium and cook until veggies start to soften, about 5 minutes.
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, cut lime into wedges. Set aside for serving.
  10. 10
    When the veggies are softened, add shrimp to the skillet. Continue to cook until veggies are tender and shrimp are cooked through, 3-4 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  11. 11
    Divide rice and curry between plates or bowls; serve with lime wedges for squeezing. Enjoy!

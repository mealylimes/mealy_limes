Thai Ground Beef & Carrot Lettuce Wraps

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (17) $4.28/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lblean ground beef
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 1 headbutter (Boston) lettuce
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • toasted sesame oil
  • crushed red pepper
  • 1lime
  • fish sauce
  • honey
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • virgin coconut oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater grater (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 head butter (Boston) lettuce
    2 medium carrots
    1 lime
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  2. 2
    Juice the lime into a medium bowl.
  3. 3
    Add fish sauce, honey, sesame oil, and crushed red pepper to the lime juice; whisk together.
    2 tbsp fish sauce
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  4. 4
    Trim the ends and peel the carrots; discard the trimmings and coarsely grate the carrots. Add to the bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim and discard the root ends of the green onions; chop the onions on an angle into ¼-inch pieces. Add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Pick the basil leaves off the stems; discard the stems and thinly slice the leaves crosswise into ribbons. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince or grate the ginger; transfer to a small bowl.
  8. 8
    Peel and mince the garlic; add to the ginger.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  10. 10
    Separate the lettuce head into leaves and place on a plate.
  11. 11
    Add coconut oil to the skillet and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  12. 12
    Add the garlic and ginger to the skillet; cook until fragrant, 15 to 30 seconds.
  13. 13
    Add ground beef to the skillet; cook, breaking apart with a spoon, until crumbly and browned, 3 to 4 minutes.
    ¾ lb lean ground beef
  14. 14
    To serve, spoon the ground beef into lettuce leaves and top with the carrot slaw. Enjoy!

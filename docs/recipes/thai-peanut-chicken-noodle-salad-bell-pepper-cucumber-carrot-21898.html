Thai Peanut Chicken Noodle Salad with Bell Pepper, Cucumber & Carrot

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (4) $1.29/serving
Ingredients
  • soy sauce
  • crushed red pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½lime
  • flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • brown sugar
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • 80 mlpeanut sauce
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    1 yellow bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
  3. 3
    Once the water is boiling, remove from heat and add the rice noodles. Soak for 10 minutes, or according to package directions, then drain in a colander and rinse under cold water; set aside.
    5 oz flat rice (pad thai) noodles
  4. 4
    While the noodles are soaking, juice lime into a medium bowl. Add peanut sauce, soy sauce, sugar, and crushed red pepper; whisk to combine the dressing.
    ⅓ cup peanut sauce
    1 tsp soy sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin pieces. Add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Place chicken on a cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones. Separate the meat into shred-like pieces and add to the bowl.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  8. 8
    Add drained noodles and carrots to the bowl. Toss to combine the noodle salad.
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  9. 9
    To serve, divide noodle salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Thai Peanut-Coconut Soup with Parsnip, Zucchini & Butternut Squash

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (2) $2.31/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½lime
  • 2 mediumparsnips
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cuppeanut sauce
  • 2 cupsfrozen butternut squash, pieces
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium parsnips
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
  2. 2
    Place broth, coconut milk, peanut sauce, chili-garlic sauce, salt, and pepper in a medium saucepan. Stir to combine the broth and bring to a boil over high heat.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ½ cup peanut sauce
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, peel, trim, and small dice parsnips. Add to the pan with the broth.
  4. 4
    Trim and medium dice zucchini. Add to the pan.
  5. 5
    Once the soup is boiling, reduce heat to low and add butternut squash. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are fork-tender, 8-10 minutes.
    2 cup frozen butternut squash, pieces
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  7. 7
    Cut lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  8. 8
    When the soup is done, remove from heat. Add cilantro and stir to combine.
  9. 9
    Divide soup between bowls and serve with lime wedges for squeezing over. Enjoy!

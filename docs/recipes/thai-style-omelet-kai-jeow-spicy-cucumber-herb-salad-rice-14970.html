Thai-Style Omelet (Kai Jeow) with Spicy Cucumber-Herb Salad & Rice

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 67% (3) $2.75/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • salt
  • 6eggs
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1English cucumber
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • rice vinegar
  • 1red chili pepper
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
Cookware
aluminum foil colander measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add broth and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 English cucumber
    1 red chili pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Place oil, vinegar, fish sauce, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp fish sauce
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and halve cucumber lengthwise; cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  6. 6
    Thinly slice the chili pepper into rounds; add to the bowl with the cucumber. (Be careful with chili peppers; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  7. 7
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; pick mint leaves off the stems. Discard stems, roughly chop the leaves, and add to the bowl. Stir to combine the salad and set aside.
  8. 8
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces. Place in another medium bowl.
  9. 9
    Crack eggs into the bowl with the green onions. Add fish sauce and whisk to combine the egg batter.
    6 eggs
    2 tsp fish sauce
  10. 10
    Preheat a skillet over medium high heat.
  11. 11
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    1 ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  12. 12
    Add half the egg batter (a scant 1 cup per omelet) to the skillet and swirl to cover the bottom. As eggs begin to set, use a spatula to pull the cooked edges towards the center of the skillet. Then tilt the skillet, allowing the uncooked egg to move to the edges. Cook until set, about 3 minutes.
  13. 13
    Fold the omelet in half and remove to a plate; cut in half. Loosely cover with aluminum foil.
  14. 14
    Return skillet to medium heat and add more oil. Repeat with remaining egg batter.
    1 ½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  15. 15
    To serve, divide rice, salad, and omelets between plates. Spoon chili-garlic sauce over the omelets and enjoy!
    2 tsp chili-garlic sauce

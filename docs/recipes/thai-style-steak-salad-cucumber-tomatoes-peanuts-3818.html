Thai-Style Steak Salad with Cucumber, Tomatoes & Peanuts

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (3) $6.83/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • 1lime
  • crushed red pepper
  • fish sauce
  • ½ small pkgfresh mint
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¼ cuppeanuts, roasted unsalted
  • honey
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • salt
  • black pepper
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls stainless steel or cast iron skillet tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ small pkg fresh mint
  2. 2
    Transfer the mixed greens to a large salad bowl.
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the ends of the cucumber; halve the cucumber lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch-thick half-rounds at an angle. Add to the salad bowl.
  4. 4
    Halve the grape tomatoes crosswise and add to the salad bowl.
  5. 5
    Trim off and discard the ends of the shallot; peel and halve the shallot lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise into half-rounds. Add to the salad bowl.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Using a knife, shave the cilantro leaves off the stems at a downward angle, working away from your body; discard the stems and finely chop the leaves. Add to the salad bowl.
  7. 7
    Pick the mint leaves off the stems; discard the stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the salad bowl.
  8. 8
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the skillet heats up, pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    ½ tbsp virgin coconut oil
  11. 11
    Carefully place the steak in the skillet; flipping every 15 to 30 seconds, cook the steak until desired doneness (5 to 7 minutes for medium-rare). (Use a thermometer or cut into the steak to check the doneness.) Once done, transfer the steak to a plate and let it rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
  12. 12
    Roughly chop the peanuts and transfer to a small bowl.
    ¼ cup peanuts, roasted unsalted
  13. 13
    Juice the lime into a small bowl (that will be used to make the dressing).
  14. 14
    To the lime juice, add olive oil, fish sauce, honey, and crushed red pepper; whisk together.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tbsp fish sauce
    1 tsp honey
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  15. 15
    Pour the dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
  16. 16
    Transfer the steak to a cutting board and thinly slice against the grain.
  17. 17
    To serve, divide the salad between plates, top with the steak slices, and garnish with the chopped peanuts. Enjoy!

Tikka Masala Eggs with Spinach & Garlic Naan

Vegetarian Breakfast
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (14) $3.07/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • curry powder
  • chili powder
  • coriander, ground
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • 4eggs
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1shallot
  • 1red bell pepper
  • cumin, ground
  • 1flatbread or naan
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons small saucepan stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon toaster (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool; peel and halve.
    4 eggs
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 red bell pepper
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Seed and small dice the bell pepper.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic, then set aside ⅓ of the garlic for the naan. Peel and small dice shallot.
    2 cloves garlic
    1 shallot
  5. 5
    Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  6. 6
    Add bell pepper, garlic, and shallot. Season with spices and cook, stirring frequently, until vegetables have softened, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tsp cumin
    1 tsp curry powder
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp coriander
    ¼ tsp chili powder
  7. 7
    Add tomato sauce to the skillet and bring to a simmer. Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful; simmer 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently. Remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  8. 8
    Let the skillet sit for 5 minutes, then stir in yogurt.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, quarter and toast the naan. Drizzle with oil and spread with reserved garlic.
    1 flatbread or naan
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  10. 10
    To serve, divide masala, eggs, and naan between bowls. Enjoy!

Tofu Fried Rice with Carrots & Peas

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 69% (31) $1.80/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • salt
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • 1egg
  • 2 mediumcarrots
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan stainless steel skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) water
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15 to 18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Wash carrots and green onions. Peel and small dice carrots. Trim and discard root ends and chop onions. Transfer both to a plate. Measure out peas.
    2 carrots
    ½ small bunch green onions
    1 cup frozen peas
  4. 4
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Drain and medium dice tofu. Transfer to paper towels and pat dry.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  6. 6
    Coat bottom of skillet with oil. Add tofu and season with salt. Stirring occasionally, cook until lightly browned, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  7. 7
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Transfer tofu to a plate and return skillet to stove. Add more oil to skillet, followed by garlic, yellow onion, ½ of the green onions (save the rest for garnishing), carrots, and peas. Stirring often, cook until carrots are tender, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  9. 9
    In a small bowl, whisk egg.
    1 egg
  10. 10
    Move vegetables to one side of skillet and pour egg on the other side. Scramble egg until cooked and mix with vegetables.
  11. 11
    Uncover the rice and fluff with a fork.
  12. 12
    Add rice, tofu, and soy sauce to vegetables. Stir until well combined and heated through, 1-2 minutes.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
  13. 13
    To serve, place fried rice in a bowl and garnish with remaining green onions. Enjoy!

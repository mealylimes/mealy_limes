Tofu Parmesan over Linguini with Tomato & Mushroom Sauce

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (2) $2.56/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • 5 ozlinguine pasta
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 ½ (8 oz) canstomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1 ½ ozParmesan cheese
  • onion powder
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut the tofu into approximately ¼-inch thick planks along the long side. Sandwich the tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    1 tsp salt
    5 oz linguine pasta
  3. 3
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lb white mushrooms
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  4. 4
    Peel and mince the garlic. Slice the mushrooms. Transfer both to a medium bowl and set aside.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Combine spices in a small bowl; stir to combine the spice mix and set aside.
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Grate Parmesan and set aside.
    1 ½ oz Parmesan cheese
  7. 7
    When pasta is done, drain in a colander, and rinse under cold water; set aside to drain further.
  8. 8
    Return the saucepan to medium heat; add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the mushrooms and garlic; cook, stirring often, until mushrooms soften, 1-2 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Add tomato sauce and spices to the saucepan and stir to combine. Cover and let simmer over medium-low for 5 minutes; turn off the heat.
    1 ½ (8 oz) cans tomato sauce
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    1 pinch crushed red pepper
  10. 10
    Cut tofu planks into approximately 2-inch wide rectangles along the short side. Arrange tofu on a baking sheet in a single layer. Season with half of the spice mix, followed by half of the bread crumbs. Press down lightly with your hands to help the breadcrumbs adhere.
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
  11. 11
    Carefully turn the tofu planks over. Season with remaining spice mix and bread crumbs. Press down lightly with your hands to help the breadcrumbs adhere.
  12. 12
    Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
  13. 13
    When the skillet is hot, add olive oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the tofu and cook until golden brown on one side, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  14. 14
    Using a spatula, carefully flip the tofu over and top each piece with Parmesan cheese. Cook until the other side has turned golden brown and the cheese has melted, 2-3 minutes.
  15. 15
    Add the pasta back to the saucepan with the sauce and toss until well coated. Cook until heated through, 2-3 minutes.
  16. 16
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  17. 17
    Divide pasta and sauce between bowls or dishes. Top with tofu and parsley. Enjoy!

Tomato-Basil Soup with Cheddar Grilled Cheese

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 40 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (16) $2.63/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1 stickcelery
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockcheddar cheese
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 2tomatoes
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 sliceswhole grain bread
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • black pepper
  • butter, unsalted
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
Cookware
can opener chef's knife cutting board garlic press (optional) hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Remove butter from fridge and allow to soften in the microwave or on the counter.
    2 tbsp butter, unsalted
  2. 2
    Heat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Coat bottom of saucepan with oil. Add onion and garlic; begin cooking while you chop the carrot and celery.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Wash and thinly slice carrot and celery; add to saucepan. Stirring occasionally, cook until carrots and celery have softened, 6-7 minutes.
    1 medium carrot
    1 stick celery
  6. 6
    Wash and medium dice tomatoes.
    2 tomatoes
  7. 7
    Wash basil. Pick leaves off the stems; discard the stems and thinly slice the leaves into ribbons.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  8. 8
    Add fresh and canned tomatoes, broth, salt, and pepper to saucepan. Stir and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for about 10 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Heat a skillet over medium-low heat.
  10. 10
    Grate ½ cup of cheddar.
    ¼ (8 oz) block cheddar cheese
  11. 11
    Spread butter onto 1 side of each bread slice and place buttered-side down on a flat surface.
    4 slices whole grain bread
  12. 12
    Divide cheddar between 2 of the bread slices and top with the other slices. Place in the skillet and gently press down with a spatula. Cook until cheese is melted and bread is golden brown, 2-4 minutes per side.
  13. 13
    Add basil to the soup and stir. Purée soup until smooth using a hand or regular blender.
  14. 14
    Pour soup into a bowl and service with grilled cheese on the side. Enjoy!

Tomato, Kale & Cannellini Bean Skillet with Parmesan Polenta

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (12) $2.17/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½ bunchkale
  • ½ cuppolenta (cornmeal)
  • 1green bell pepper
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    In a small saucepan, bring broth and water to a boil. Reduce heat to low and gradually add polenta in a thin steady stream, while whisking, until the polenta is incorporated into the liquid. Cook, stirring occasionally, until polenta is soft and the texture is creamy, 15-25 minutes.
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 cup water
    ½ cup polenta (cornmeal)
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  3. 3
    Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic; place both in a medium bowl.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the onion and garlic.
    1 green bell pepper
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Add beans, tomatoes, and spices to the skillet; stir to combine, and cook until heated through, about 5 minutes.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp paprika
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash and dry kale. Fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces.
    ½ bunch kale
  9. 9
    Add kale to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the kale to wilt slightly before adding the next handful. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, 3-5 minutes.
  10. 10
    While the kale cooks, finely grate Parmesan.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  11. 11
    Add half the Parmesan, salt, and pepper to the polenta and stir to combine. If the polenta is too firm, add hot water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until you reach a desired consistency.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    To serve, divide polenta between bowls; top with veggie-bean skillet and remaining Parmesan. Enjoy!

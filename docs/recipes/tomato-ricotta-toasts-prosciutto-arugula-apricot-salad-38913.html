Tomato-Ricotta Toasts with Prosciutto & Arugula-Apricot Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (16) $2.34/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½lemon
  • 2tomatoes
  • 4 slicesprosciutto
  • 2apricots
  • 4 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon toaster (optional) tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add prosciutto and cook until crispy, about 1 minute per side. Remove to a plate and set aside.
    4 slices prosciutto
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven. (Skip this step if you are using a toaster for the bread.)
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lemon
    2 apricots
    2 tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  5. 5
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl. Add oil, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper, then whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  6. 6
    Halve apricots lengthwise (around the pit); twist halves to separate, then remove and discard pits. Cut each half into ¼-inch thick slices and add to the bowl with the dressing. Set aside.
  7. 7
    Slice tomatoes crosswise into thin rounds. Place on a plate, season with spices, and set aside.
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Arrange bread on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven. (Alternatively, you can use a toaster.)
    4 slices sourdough bread
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  10. 10
    Spread toasted bread with ricotta, then top with tomatoes, basil, and crispy prosciutto.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  11. 11
    Add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and apricots, then toss to combine the salad.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide toasts and salad between plates. Enjoy!

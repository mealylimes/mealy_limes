Tortellini Pasta Salad with Roasted Red Peppers, Arugula & Parmesan

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $2.31/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ⅓ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ¼ cupItalian salad dressing
  • 9 ozcheese tortellini, fresh or frozen
  • ⅓ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
  • ½ (12 oz) jarroasted red bell peppers
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add tortellini, and stir for a few seconds. Cook, stirring occasionally, until just tender, about 5 minutes (or cook according to package instructions).
    9 oz cheese tortellini, fresh or frozen
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Halve tomatoes and place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons; add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  5. 5
    Roughly chop peppers and add to the bowl.
    ½ (12 oz) jar roasted red bell peppers
  6. 6
    Roughly chop olives; add to the bowl along with the arugula and cheese.
    ⅓ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    ⅓ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
  7. 7
    When the tortellini is done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Add to the bowl along with dressing, salt, and pepper; stir to combine the pasta salad.
    ¼ cup Italian salad dressing
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls and enjoy!

Tossed Skillet Lasagna with Zucchini, Carrot, Ricotta & Basil

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 84% (17) $2.84/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 4no-boil lasagna noodles
  • 1tomato
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium zucchini squash
    1 medium carrot
    1 tomato
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  4. 4
    Break lasagna noodles into thirds. Once the water in the pan is boiling, uncover, add salt and broken noodles, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Once done, drain in a colander and set aside.
    4 no-boil lasagna noodles
    ½ tsp salt
  5. 5
    While the noodles are cooking, trim the zucchini and carrot. Run the peeler along the zucchini and carrot to make long, noodle-like strips. Place both in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Medium dice tomato; add to the bowl with the zucchini and carrot.
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, garlic, and spices to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender, 3-4 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  11. 11
    Coarsely grate mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  12. 12
    When the veggies are done, add lasagna noodles, half of the basil, and tomato sauce to the skillet; stir to combine and continue to cook until heated through, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat. (Reserve remaining basil for serving.)
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  13. 13
    Top lasagna with dollops of ricotta and sprinkle with mozzarella.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
  14. 14
    Place skillet in the oven and bake until the mozzarella is melted and golden, 7-8 minutes. Remove from oven.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide lasagna between plates or bowls and top with reserved basil. Enjoy!

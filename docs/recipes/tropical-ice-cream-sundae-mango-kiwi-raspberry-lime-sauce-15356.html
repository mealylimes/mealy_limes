Tropical Ice Cream Sundae with Mango, Kiwi & Raspberry-Lime Sauce

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $1.63/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1kiwifruit
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • ½ (6 oz) pkgraspberries
  • granulated sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • 12 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board hand or regular blender ice cream scoop (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls silicone spatula strainer
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lime
    ½ (6 oz) pkg raspberries
    1 mango
    1 kiwifruit
  2. 2
    Juice lime into a blender. Add raspberries, sugar, and vanilla; blend until smooth.
    1 ½ tbsp granulated sugar
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  3. 3
    Pour raspberry-lime pureé into a strainer placed over a medium bowl. Using a silicone spatula, press pureé through the strainer to remove seeds; discard seeds and set sauce aside.
  4. 4
    Peel and slice mango flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice.
  5. 5
    Trim, peel, and small dice kiwi.
  6. 6
    To serve, place one scoop of ice cream in the bottom of each bowl; top with half the raspberry-lime sauce and the diced fruit. Place a second scoop of ice cream on top, drizzle with remaining sauce, and enjoy!
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) vanilla ice cream

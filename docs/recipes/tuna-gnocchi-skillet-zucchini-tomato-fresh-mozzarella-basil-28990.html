Tuna-Gnocchi Skillet with Zucchini, Tomato, Fresh Mozzarella & Basil

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $5.33/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 tbspcapers
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) pkgfresh mozzarella cheese
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½lemon
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1 (16 oz) pkgpotato gnocchi
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ lemon
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Once the water in the pan is boiling, add salt and gnocchi; cook, stirring frequently, until cooked through, about 3 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    1 (16 oz) pkg potato gnocchi
  4. 4
    While the gnocchi are cooking, trim and medium dice zucchini. Place in a small bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the zucchini.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, capers, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until zucchini and onion soften, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for the next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp capers
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, drain tuna, place in the reserved bowl, and flake with a fork.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  9. 9
    Halve grape tomatoes and add to the bowl with the tuna.
  10. 10
    When the veggies are soft, juice lemon into the skillet. Add cooked gnocchi, tuna, and tomatoes, then stir to combine. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until warmed through, 2-3 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, medium dice mozzarella.
    ½ (8 oz) pkg fresh mozzarella cheese
  12. 12
    Pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  13. 13
    Add mozzarella and basil to the skillet with the gnocchi, then stir to combine.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide tuna-gnocchi skillet between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

"Tuna Melt" Quesadillas & Tomato-Carrot Soup with Parsley

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 92% (10) $3.16/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ tbspcapers
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • butter, unsalted
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 1 stickcelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • cumin, ground
  • 12 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4small flour tortillas
  • mayonnaise
  • brown sugar
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater hand or regular blender ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    1 stick celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  2. 2
    Peel and mince garlic; peel and small dice onion. Place both in a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
    ½ medium yellow onion
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl with the garlic and onion.
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic, onion, carrot, and spices to the pan; cook, stirring often, until veggies begin to soften, 3-4 minutes. (Reserve bowl for next step.)
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, mince capers. Place in the reserved bowl along with mayo, vinegar, cumin, and pepper; stir to combine.
    ½ tbsp capers
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Add tomatoes, broth, and sugar to the pan; stir to combine and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and let simmer until veggies are completely soft, 6-8 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    12 fl oz (1 ½ cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ tsp brown sugar
  8. 8
    While the soup is cooking, drain tuna; flake into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl with the mayo mixture (do not mash).
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  9. 9
    Trim, and small dice celery. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl (reserving the rest for the soup).
  11. 11
    Grate cheese and add to the bowl; gently stir to combine the tuna filling.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  12. 12
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and divide the tuna filling between them. Move the filling onto one half of each tortilla; fold in half to close.
    4 small flour tortillas
  13. 13
    Preheat a large skillet over medium heat.
  14. 14
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add quesadillas and cook, flipping once, until cheese is melted and tortillas are golden, 1½-2 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  15. 15
    Meanwhile, using a hand (or regular) blender, purée the soup until smooth. Alternatively, use a potato masher to crush the tomatoes (the soup will be chunkier).
  16. 16
    Divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Serve with quesadillas on the side and enjoy!

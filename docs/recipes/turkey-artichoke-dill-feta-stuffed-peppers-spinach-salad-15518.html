Turkey, Artichoke, Dill & Feta Stuffed Peppers with Spinach Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $5.50/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • ⅛ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 2yellow bell peppers
  • ⅛ cupwalnuts
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • ½ (6 oz) jarartichoke hearts, marinated
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack about 6-inches from the top.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    2 yellow bell peppers
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and halve bell peppers lengthwise. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, season with salt and pepper, then arrange cut-side down.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven and broil, flipping once, until peppers are tender and charred, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince garlic. Peel and small dice onion.
    1 clove garlic
    ½ medium yellow onion
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add garlic and onion; cook, stirring often, until starting to soften, 2-3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Add ground turkey and spices to the skillet; continue to cook, breaking the turkey apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    While the turkey is cooking, using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the skillet with the cooked turkey.
  10. 10
    Mince olives and artichokes; add both to the skillet along with half the feta (reserving the rest for topping the peppers). Stir to combine the filling.
    ⅛ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
    ½ (6 oz) jar artichoke hearts, marinated
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  11. 11
    Divide turkey filling between pepper halves and top with reserved feta. Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until cheese is golden, 2-3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, peel and mince additional garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, and spices; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 clove garlic
    2 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 ½ tsp red wine vinegar
    ⅛ tsp salt
    1 pinch black pepper
    1 pinch oregano, dried
  13. 13
    Mince walnuts. Add to the bowl with the dressing along with the spinach; toss to combine the salad.
    ⅛ cup walnuts
  14. 14
    To serve, divide peppers and salad between plates. Enjoy!

Turkey Bacon Burgers with Napa Cabbage & Carrot Sesame Slaw

Dinner Meat
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.59/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • paprika
  • honey
  • virgin coconut oil
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½ lbground turkey
  • sesame seeds
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • 1tomato
  • ½ medium headnapa cabbage
  • 4 slicesbacon
  • ½ headbutter (Boston) lettuce
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls salad spinner (optional) spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 tomato
    ½ head butter (Boston) lettuce
    ½ medium head napa cabbage
    1 medium carrot
  2. 2
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Peel and thinly slice onion into half rings. Once the skillet is hot, add coconut oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and, stirring frequently, cook until softened, 4-5 minutes. Once done, transfer onions to a bowl and return skillet to medium heat.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
  4. 4
    Finely chop bacon.
    4 slices bacon
  5. 5
    In a medium bowl, combine turkey, bacon, paprika, garlic powder, ground cumin, and black pepper. Mix well and form into 4 patties.
    ½ lb ground turkey
    1 tsp paprika
    1 tsp garlic powder
    1 tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place the patties in the skillet; cook until they are cooked through and browned, 3 to 5 minutes per side. Once done, transfer patties to a plate.
  7. 7
    In a salad bowl, whisk together soya sauce, apple cider vinegar, honey, sesame oil, and sesame seeds.
    2 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp honey
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tbsp sesame seeds
  8. 8
    Trim off and discard the root end of the cabbage; halve the cabbage lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise and add to the salad bowl.
  9. 9
    Trim the ends and peel the carrot; discard the trimmings and coarsely grate the carrots. Add to the salad bowl and toss to combine.
  10. 10
    Separate the lettuce head into leaves. Slice the tomato crosswise into ¼-inch-thick rounds.
  11. 11
    To serve, place each patty on a lettuce leaf and top with a slice of tomato and grilled onions, arrange salad on the side. Enjoy!

Turkey-Basil Fettuccine with Bell Peppers & Broccoli

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (16) $3.78/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 crownbroccoli
  • butter, unsalted
  • all-purpose flour
  • 4 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • 5 ozfettuccine pasta
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes; drain and set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz fettuccine pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 crown broccoli
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and slice bell pepper into thin strips; place in a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom; add turkey and cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
    ¾ lb ground turkey
  6. 6
    While the turkey is cooking, separate broccoli into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  7. 7
    When the turkey is done, add veggies to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, 4-5 minutes.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  9. 9
    When the veggies are done, sprinkle with flour and continue to cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.
    1 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
  10. 10
    Add milk, salt, and pepper to the skillet; cook, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens, 4-5 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) whole milk
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Add drained pasta and basil to the skillet; toss to combine and remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide pasta between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

Turkey Bolognese Stuffed Acorn Squash with Parmesan & Basil

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $2.92/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 2 stickscelery
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • 1acorn squash
  • ½ (24 oz) jarmarinara sauce
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 500°F and place rack in the center position. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil to make cleanup easier (optional).
  2. 2
    Wash and dry acorn squash; halve lengthwise, trim ends, then scoop out the seeds. Rub the inside of the squash with oil, then season with salt and pepper; arrange halves, cut-side down, on the baking sheet.
    1 acorn squash
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place squash in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast until tender, so a knife can easily pierce through the flesh, 20-25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, wash, dry, trim, and small dice celery.
    2 sticks celery
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add celery, turkey, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    While the turkey is cooking, wash, dry, and pick basil leaves off the stems; roll up crosswise and thinly slice into ribbons.
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  8. 8
    Add half of the basil (reserving the rest for garnish) and marinara sauce to the skillet; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to low and cook until the bolognese sauce thickens slightly, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat and cover with a lid to keep warm.
    ½ (24 oz) jar marinara sauce
  9. 9
    To serve, divide squash halves between plates and fill with the turkey bolognese; top with cheese and reserved basil. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded

Turkey Burger Salad with Spinach, Mango, Avocado & Spicy Lime Aioli

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (7) $4.65/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • basil, dried
  • cayenne pepper
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • ½avocado
  • ½lime
  • 1mango
  • mayonnaise
  • neutral vegetable oil
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lime
    ½ red bell pepper
    1 mango
    ½ avocado
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Place turkey, bread crumbs, egg, and spices in a medium bowl; mix with your hands until well combined. Form the turkey mixture into 2 equal-sized patties and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ¼ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, zest and juice lime into a small bowl. Add mayo and spices, then whisk to combine the aioli. Set aside.
    3 tbsp mayonnaise
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook, undisturbed, until golden underneath, about 5 minutes; flip patties and cook an additional 5-6 minutes, until cooked through. Remove from heat.
    1 tsp neutral vegetable oil
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, trim, seed, and slice bell pepper lengthwise into thin strips. Place on a clean plate.
  7. 7
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; thinly slice the flesh. Add to the plate with the bell pepper.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and slice crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the plate.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide spinach between plates or bowls. Top with bell pepper, mango, avocado, and burger patties. Drizzle with aioli and enjoy!

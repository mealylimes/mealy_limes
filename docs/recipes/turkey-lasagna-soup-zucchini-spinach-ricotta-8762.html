Turkey Lasagna Soup with Zucchini, Spinach, and Ricotta

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 81% (12) $4.38/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • 3no-boil lasagna noodles
  • onion powder
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) large pot measuring cups measuring spoons stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Heat a large pot over medium heat.
  3. 3
    Peel and mince the garlic.
    1 clove garlic
  4. 4
    Add ground turkey, garlic, onion powder, and crushed red pepper to the pot. Cook, breaking apart the meat with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 3 to 4 minutes.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    1 ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  5. 5
    Small dice the zucchini.
  6. 6
    Add the zucchini, tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, broth, and spices to the pot. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to medium-low.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    Break the lasagna noodles into bite-sized pieces. Add to the pot and stir; cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
    3 no-boil lasagna noodles
  8. 8
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves.
  9. 9
    Add spinach to the pot in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful; cook until fully wilted, 2-3 minutes.
  10. 10
    To serve, divide the soup between bowls and top with a large spoonful of ricotta and parsley. Enjoy!
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese

Turkey "Meatball" Pita Pizza with Creamy Oregano & Olive Romaine Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (6) $4.32/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • oregano, dried
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • red wine vinegar
  • 1shallot
  • ½red bell pepper
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • ⅛ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • mayonnaise
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ head romaine lettuce
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Place mayo, vinegar, and spices in a medium bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp mayonnaise
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper; add to the bowl with the dressing.
  5. 5
    Trim, halve lengthwise, and cut cucumber crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the bowl.
  6. 6
    Halve olives lengthwise and add to the bowl; set aside.
    ⅛ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  7. 7
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  8. 8
    While the skillet heats up, peel and mince shallot and garlic.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
  9. 9
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add shallot, garlic, turkey, and spices; cook, breaking apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, coarsely grate mozzarella and set aside.
    ¼ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  11. 11
    Add tomato sauce to the skillet with the turkey and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
    1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  12. 12
    Arrange pitas on a baking sheet pan. Divide turkey mixture between the pitas and top with cheese. Place in the oven and bake until the cheese is melted, 4-5 minutes. Remove from oven and cut into wedges.
    2 whole grain pitas
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, chop or tear lettuce into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl with the veggies.
  14. 14
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard the stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl (reserving the rest for the pizzas) and toss to combine the salad.
  15. 15
    To serve, divide pizzas and salad between plates. Sprinkle pizzas with reserved parsley and enjoy!

Turkey Meatballs with Baked Zucchini Noodles, Cream Sauce & Parsley

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 79% (7) $3.40/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • Dijon mustard
  • panko bread crumbs
  • 1egg
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 mediumzucchini squash
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • onion powder
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • nutmeg, ground
  • cloves, ground
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and trim zucchini; run a vegetable peeler along the length of the zucchini to make ribbons. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt and pepper; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    3 medium zucchini squash
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated), and bake, tossing halfway through, until zucchini is tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Place half in a medium bowl (reserve remaining parsley for serving).
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  5. 5
    Add turkey, bread crumbs, egg, and spices to the bowl with the parsley; use your hands to combine. Using a tablespoon measure, form the mixture into rounded meatballs and place on a plate.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ½ cup panko bread crumbs
    1 egg
    ½ tsp salt
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp onion powder
    ¼ tsp oregano, dried
    ⅛ tsp nutmeg, ground
    ⅛ tsp cloves, ground
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Preheat an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. 7
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add meatballs and cook, turning often, until browned on all sides, 3-4 minutes (the meatballs will not be cooked all the way through). Transfer meatballs to a clean plate. (Reserve skillet with drippings for next step.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  8. 8
    Return skillet (with drippings) to medium heat. Add broth, cream, Worcestershire, Dijon, and salt. Whisk to combine the sauce and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until sauce has thickened, 4-5 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy whipping cream
    1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp salt
  9. 9
    Return meatballs to the skillet along with any juices from the plate. Gently stir to cover with sauce.
  10. 10
    Place skillet in the oven and bake until meatballs are cooked through, 5-7 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide zucchini noodles, meatballs, and sauce between plates. Top with remaining parsley and enjoy!

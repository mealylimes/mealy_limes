Turkey Smash Tacos with Guacamole & Pickled Onion-Cucumber Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 86% (29) $3.61/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • apple cider vinegar
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • 1avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1lime
  • 1tomato
  • brown sugar
  • 6small corn tortillas
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet potato masher spatula stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ English cucumber
    1 lime
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    1 avocado
    1 tomato
  2. 2
    Place vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl. Whisk to combine the pickling liquid.
    2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 tsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Peel, halve, and small dice onion. Add half to the bowl with the pickling liquid. Place remaining onion in a medium bowl and set aside.
    ½ medium red onion
  4. 4
    Trim and small dice cucumber. Add to the small bowl, stir to combine the salad, and set aside.
  5. 5
    Place turkey and spices in another medium bowl. Mix with your hands until well combined.
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    1 tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Arrange tortillas on a flat surface, then divide the turkey mixture between them (about 3 tablespoons per tortilla). Using a knife (or your hands), spread the turkey mixture over the surface of each tortilla in an even layer.
    6 small corn tortillas
  7. 7
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  8. 8
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches, add tacos, meat-side down. Cook, pressing down once with the bottom of a spatula halfway through cooking, until the turkey is cooked through, about 3 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Flip tacos, turkey-side up, and continue to cook until tortillas are golden underneath, 1 minute more. Transfer to a plate and carefully fold in half to form tacos.
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, juice half of the lime into the medium bowl with the onion (cut remaining lime into wedges and set aside for serving).
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh. Add to the bowl along with salt and pepper. Mash with a fork or potato masher until guacamole is smooth.
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Small dice tomato. Add to the bowl with the guacamole and stir to combine.
  14. 14
    Divide tacos between plates; fill with guacamole and a small amount of salad. Serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!

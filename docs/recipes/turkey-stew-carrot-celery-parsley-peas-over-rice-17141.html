Turkey Stew with Carrot, Celery, Parsley & Peas over Rice

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 66% (8) $3.13/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • paprika
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • 1 (14.5 oz) candiced tomatoes
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • 8 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • cornstarch
  • ½ cupbasmati rice
  • ⅔ cupfrozen peas
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls small saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup basmati rice
    1 cup water
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  3. 3
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, peel, trim, and small dice carrots; trim and small dice celery. Place both in a small bowl.
  5. 5
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  6. 6
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion; peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the carrot and celery.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add turkey, veggies, and spices; cook, breaking the turkey apart with a spoon, until the veggies are softened and turkey is browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb ground turkey
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ½ tsp paprika
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  9. 9
    When the turkey is done, add tomatoes, broth, peas, and soy sauce to the pan; stir to combine and bring the stew to a boil.
    1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
    8 fl oz (1 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    ⅔ cup frozen peas
    1 tbsp soy sauce
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, in the reserved bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth.
    2 tbsp water
    1 ½ tbsp cornstarch
  11. 11
    Once the stew is boiling, reduce heat to medium, and add the slurry in a steady stream while stirring. Continue to cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 2 minutes more.
  12. 12
    Add half of the parsley to the stew, stir to combine, and remove from heat. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  13. 13
    To serve, divide rice and stew between bowls; sprinkle with reserved parsley and enjoy!

Turkey Taco Pita Pizza with Lime Crema & Mango-Tomato Mixed Greens

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 62% (4) $4.67/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • garlic powder
  • salt
  • ½ (8 oz) cantomato sauce
  • ½ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ¾ lbground turkey
  • cumin, ground
  • 1lime
  • ½mango
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 lime
    ½ mango
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  3. 3
    Place spices in a small bowl and stir to combine; set aside.
    2 tsp chili powder
    ¾ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, trim and cut green onions crosswise into thin pieces.
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add the turkey, spice mix, and half of the green onions (reserving the rest for garnish). Cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¾ lb ground turkey
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, arrange pitas on a baking sheet pan. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, flipping halfway through, until slightly crispy, about 5 minutes.
    2 whole grain pitas
  8. 8
    Remove pitas from oven and evenly spread with tomato sauce; top with turkey. Return baking sheet to the oven and bake until pizzas are warmed through, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven.
    ½ (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  9. 9
    While the pizzas are baking, juice half of the lime into the reserved bowl (set remaining lime aside for the dressing). Add yogurt and additional spices, then whisk to combine the lime crema; set aside.
    ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
    ½ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    1 pinch salt
  10. 10
    Juice remaining lime into a medium bowl; add oil and additional spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ¼ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Peel mango and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; small dice and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  12. 12
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Add mixed greens to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pizzas and salad between plates; drizzle pizzas with lime crema and top with reserved green onion. Enjoy!

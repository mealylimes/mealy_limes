Turkish Steak Wrap with Arugula, Tomato, Cucumber & Olive Salad

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $5.93/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • coriander, ground
  • dill, dried
  • salt
  • ⅛ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • cumin, ground
  • 2large flour tortillas
  • ⅛ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1tomato
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Pat the steak dry with paper towels and place on a plate; season with spices on both sides.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ½ tsp cumin
    ¼ tsp coriander
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Once the grill pan is hot, coat with oil. Add steak and cook, flipping once, until desired doneness, 4-5 minutes for medium-rare. Transfer to a clean plate and cover loosely with aluminum foil.
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 tomato
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  5. 5
    Peel and slice shallot into thin rings. Peel and mince garlic. Place both in a medium bowl along with oil, vinegar, dill, and salt.
    1 shallot
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    ¼ tsp dill
    1 pinch salt
  6. 6
    Small dice tomato and add to the bowl.
  7. 7
    Trim cucumber, quarter lengthwise, then slice crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Halve olives and add to bowl.
    ⅛ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  9. 9
    Add arugula to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  10. 10
    Return the grill pan to medium-high heat. Working in batches if necessary, place tortillas in the pan and warm, flipping occasionally, until lightly charred, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
    2 large flour tortillas
  11. 11
    Thinly slice steak against the grain.
  12. 12
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and spread evenly with yogurt. Divide steak and ⅓ of the salad between them; roll tightly to close and cut in half.
    ⅛ cup plain Greek yogurt
  13. 13
    Divide wraps between plates; serve with remaining salad on the side and enjoy!

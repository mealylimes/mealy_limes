Tzatziki-Dressed Salad with Chicken, Pita Chips, Olives & Feta

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (4) $2.76/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • salt
  • red wine vinegar
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 2whole grain pitas
  • ½ (30 oz) whole chickenrotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 2 heartsromaine hearts
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Place pitas on a flat surface. Holding a knife parallel to the cutting board, cut the pita in half horizontally to separate the two sides; cut or tear the bread into 2-inch pieces and transfer to a baking sheet pan.
    2 whole grain pitas
  3. 3
    Drizzle pita with oil and season with salt; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  4. 4
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until the pita chips are crisp, 6-8 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place tzatziki, vinegar, oil, and salt in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce
    2 tsp red wine vinegar
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    1 yellow bell pepper
    2 hearts romaine hearts
  7. 7
    Halve tomatoes lengthwise. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  8. 8
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Chop (or tear) lettuce into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  10. 10
    Roughly chop olives. Add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  11. 11
    Place chicken on the cutting board and, using your hands, remove meat from the skin and bones; separate the meat into shred-like pieces. Add to the bowl.
    ½ (30 oz) whole chicken rotisserie chicken, white and dark meat
  12. 12
    Add pita chips and feta to the bowl. Toss to combine the salad.
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese
  13. 13
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

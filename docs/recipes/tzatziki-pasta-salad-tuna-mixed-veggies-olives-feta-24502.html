Tzatziki Pasta Salad with Tuna, Mixed Veggies, Olives & Feta

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (9) $3.50/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ (4 oz) pkgcrumbled feta cheese
  • 1 (6 oz) canchunk light tuna in water
  • 5 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • oregano, dried
  • salt
  • ½English cucumber
  • ¼ cupKalamata olives, pitted
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • ½ cuptzatziki sauce
  • 30 mlbalsamic vinaigrette
  • ½ (10 oz) bagshredded carrots
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, place tzatziki, vinaigrette, and spices in a medium bowl. Stir to combine the dressing.
    ½ cup tzatziki sauce
    2 tbsp balsamic vinaigrette
    ½ tsp oregano, dried
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Drain tuna. Add to the bowl with the dressing and flake with a fork.
    1 (6 oz) can chunk light tuna in water
  4. 4
    Wash, dry, trim, and small dice cucumber. Add to the bowl.
    ½ English cucumber
  5. 5
    Wash, dry, and halve tomatoes lengthwise. Add to the bowl along with the carrots.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ (10 oz) bag shredded carrots
  6. 6
    Thinly slice olives. Add to the bowl.
    ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  7. 7
    When the pasta is done, drain and rinse under cold water to cool. Add to the bowl with the tuna and veggies, then stir to combine the pasta salad.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls and top with feta. Enjoy!
    ½ (4 oz) pkg crumbled feta cheese

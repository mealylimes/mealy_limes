Vegan "BLT" Salad with Tofu Bacon, Avocado & Garlic Croutons

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $3.39/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 1 headromaine lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • soy sauce
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • paprika
  • salt
  • pure maple syrup
  • Dijon mustard
  • white wine vinegar
  • ½avocado
  • 3 sliceswhole grain bread
  • onion powder
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • vegan mayonnaise
  • cardamom, ground
Cookware
baking dish baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Place soy sauce, maple syrup, oil, and spices in a baking dish; whisk to combine the marinade.
    3 tbsp soy sauce
    2 tbsp pure maple syrup
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp paprika
    1 tsp onion powder
    ½ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp cardamom, ground
  3. 3
    Cut tofu into ¼-inch thick planks along the short side; sandwich the slices between clean towels or paper towels and gently press to remove excess water. Add to the marinade in a single layer, turn to coat, and set aside.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  4. 4
    While the tofu bacon marinates, peel and mince garlic. Place in a medium bowl along with oil and spices; whisk to combine the garlic oil.
    1 clove garlic
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp basil, dried
    ⅛ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    Cut bread into cubes and add to the bowl with the garlic oil. Toss to combine the croutons, transfer to a baking sheet pan, and spread out in an even layer. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    3 slices whole grain bread
  6. 6
    Place baking sheet in the oven and bake, tossing halfway through, until the croutons are golden and crispy, about 8 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. 7
    Add the baking dish to the oven and bake until tofu bacon is firm and marinade has been absorbed, 15-20 minutes. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ avocado
    1 head romaine lettuce
  9. 9
    Place oil, mayo, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
    2 tsp white wine vinegar
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  11. 11
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  12. 12
    Chop or tear the romaine into bite-sized pieces and add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Right before serving, add croutons to the bowl and toss to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls, top with tofu bacon, and enjoy!

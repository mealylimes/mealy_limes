Vegan Tofu Tacos with Bell Pepper, Lettuce, Avocado & Salsa

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $2.49/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (12 oz) pkgextra firm tofu
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1red bell pepper
  • 1avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ¼ cupsalsa
  • 22 ½ mltaco seasoning
  • 1 heartromaine hearts
  • 6hard taco shells
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons nonstick skillet stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Cut the tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.
    1 (12 oz) pkg extra firm tofu
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 red bell pepper
    1 heart romaine hearts
    1 avocado
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper.
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium heat.
  5. 5
    While the skillet heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium red onion
    1 clove garlic
  6. 6
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper, onion, and garlic. Cook, stirring often, until softened, 4-5 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, halve lettuce lengthwise, then slice crosswise into thin (shred-like) strips. Transfer to a plate.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and small dice. Add to the plate with the lettuce.
  9. 9
    Crumble tofu into small pieces with your fingers and add to the skillet along with spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tofu is golden brown, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    1 ½ tbsp taco seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Warm taco shells in the microwave if desired.
    6 hard taco shells
  11. 11
    To serve, divide taco shells between plates. Fill with tofu mixture, salsa, lettuce, and avocado. Enjoy!
    ¼ cup salsa

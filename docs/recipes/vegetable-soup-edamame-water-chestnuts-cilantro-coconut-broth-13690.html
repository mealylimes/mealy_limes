Vegetable Soup with Edamame, Water Chestnuts & Cilantro-Coconut Broth

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 58% (3) $2.62/serving
Ingredients
  • 5 ozwater chestnuts
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • virgin coconut oil
  • 1shallot
  • 1 mediumyellow potato
  • ½red bell pepper
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • brown sugar
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • ¾ cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    1 medium carrot
    1 medium yellow potato
    ½ red bell pepper
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  2. 2
    Peel and mince the ginger and garlic; transfer both to a medium bowl.
    2 cloves garlic
  3. 3
    Peel and medium dice carrot and potato; add both to the bowl.
  4. 4
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    While the pan heats up, peel and small dice shallot; add to the bowl.
    1 shallot
  6. 6
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies, salt, and pepper to the pan; cook, stirring often, until they begin to soften, 3-4 minutes.
    1 tbsp virgin coconut oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  7. 7
    While the veggies cook, seed and slice bell pepper into thin, bite-sized strips.
  8. 8
    Add the bell pepper, broth, coconut milk, soy sauce, and sugar to the pan; stir to combine and bring to a boil.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    2 tsp soy sauce
    ½ tsp brown sugar
  9. 9
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse the water chestnuts in a colander.
    5 oz water chestnuts
  10. 10
    Once the soup comes to a boil, add water chestnuts, edamame, and corn. Return to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer until edamame is heated through and veggies are tender, 5 minutes more.
    ¾ cup edamame, shelled
    ½ cup frozen corn
  11. 11
    Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems. Add cilantro to the pan, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and enjoy!

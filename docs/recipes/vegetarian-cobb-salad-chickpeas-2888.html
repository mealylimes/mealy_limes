Vegetarian Cobb Salad with Chickpeas

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 73% (13) $2.32/serving
Ingredients
  • ½ headromaine lettuce
  • 3eggs
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 1avocado
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • red wine vinegar
  • Dijon mustard
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • honey
Cookware
can opener chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls small saucepan whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place the eggs in a small saucepan and cover with cold water by about 1 inch. Cover with a lid and bring to a rolling boil over high heat.
    3 eggs
  2. 2
    While the water comes to a boil, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ head romaine lettuce
    1 avocado
  3. 3
    Trim off and discard the root end of the lettuce and remove any blemished leaves; halve the lettuce lengthwise, then chop crosswise into 1-inch pieces and transfer to a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Once the water is boiling, remove the pan from the heat and let the eggs sit, covered, for 6 minutes. Once done, drain and run the eggs under cold water for 1-2 minutes to stop the cooking process.
  5. 5
    Halve the tomatoes and transfer to a small bowl.
  6. 6
    Halve the avocado lengthwise and twist the halves to separate, then remove the pit with a knife or spoon; scoop out and medium dice the flesh. Transfer the avocado to a small bowl.
  7. 7
    Drain and rinse the chickpeas; set aside in the colander.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  8. 8
    In a small bowl, combine and whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, honey, salt, and pepper.
    3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Peel and roughly chop the eggs.
  10. 10
    To serve, arrange the lettuce on a plate, top with the tomatoes, avocado, chickpeas, and eggs, and drizzle with the dressing. Enjoy!

Vegetarian Mushroom & Bell Pepper "Cheesesteak" Toasts with Arugula

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $3.97/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • ⅓ (8 oz) blockmozzarella cheese
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • red wine vinegar
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 capsportobello mushrooms
  • 1green bell pepper
  • brown sugar
  • 4 slicessourdough bread
Cookware
aluminum foil baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil and set aside.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 red bell pepper
    1 green bell pepper
    2 caps portobello mushrooms
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  3. 3
    Trim, seed, and slice bell peppers lengthwise into thin strips. Transfer to the baking sheet.
  4. 4
    Remove stems from portobello mushrooms; thinly slice. Add to the baking sheet with the peppers.
  5. 5
    Peel, halve, and slice onion crosswise into thin half-moons; add to the baking sheet. Drizzle veggies with oil and season with spices; toss to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Place veggies in the oven and bake, stirring halfway through, until softened and starting to color in spots, 12-15 minutes.
  7. 7
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp red wine vinegar
    ½ tsp Dijon mustard
    ½ tsp brown sugar
    ⅛ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Coarsely grate mozzarella.
    ⅓ (8 oz) block mozzarella cheese
  9. 9
    When the veggies are done, remove from the oven. Change oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  10. 10
    Sprinkle veggies with cheese. Return baking sheet to the oven and broil until cheese is melted, 1-2 minutes. Remove from oven.
  11. 11
    Carefully remove cooked veggies from the baking sheet by lifting the corners of the foil and transferring the whole sheet to a flat surface. (Reserve baking sheet for the next step.)
  12. 12
    Arrange bread on the reserved baking sheet. Place bread in the oven and broil, flipping halfway through, until toasted, about 2 minutes; remove from oven.
    4 slices sourdough bread
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide dressed arugula and toast between plates; top the toast with cheesy mushrooms and peppers. Enjoy!

Veggie Burger Salad with Cheese, Tortilla Chips & Honey Mustard

Vegetarian Simple & Quick
⏱ 20 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (2) $2.87/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
  • 1green bell pepper
  • ¼ cuphoney mustard dressing
  • ½ cupshredded cheese, Italian blend
  • 2veggie burger patties, plant-based
  • 2 oztortilla chips
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet spatula tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add burger patties and cook until golden brown on one side, 4-5 minutes.
    1 ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 veggie burger patties, plant-based
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the mixed greens if they came pre-washed.)
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    1 green bell pepper
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  4. 4
    Flip the burger patties, top with cheese, and continue to cook until cheese is melted and patties are golden brown and cooked through, 4-5 minutes more (or cook patties according to package instructions). Remove to a plate.
    ½ cup shredded cheese, Italian blend
  5. 5
    While the burgers are cooking, halve tomatoes. Place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl with the tomatoes.
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and small dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl, along with the mixed greens, and toss to combine the salad.
  8. 8
    When the burgers are done, slice into strips.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls. Crumble tortilla chips over the salad, then top with veggie burger slices and dressing. Enjoy!
    2 oz tortilla chips
    ¼ cup honey mustard dressing

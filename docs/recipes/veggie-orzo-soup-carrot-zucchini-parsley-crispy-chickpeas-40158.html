Veggie Orzo Soup with Carrot, Zucchini, Parsley & Crispy Chickpeas

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (45) $1.92/serving
Ingredients
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cangarbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 3 ozorzo pasta
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon vegetable peeler
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse chickpeas. Transfer to a baking sheet pan and pat dry with paper towels. Drizzle with oil and season with spices. Stir to coat and spread out in an even layer.
    1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until chickpeas are golden and crispy, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 medium carrots
    2 sticks celery
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  5. 5
    Peel, trim, and medium dice carrots. Place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim and medium dice celery. Add to the bowl with the carrots.
  7. 7
    Trim and quarter zucchini lengthwise, then slice crosswise into bite-sized pieces. Add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
  9. 9
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl with the other veggies.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  10. 10
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add veggies and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 4-5 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. 12
    When the veggies are softened, add parsley, broth, and orzo to the pan. Stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat and simmer until the orzo is tender, 8-10 minutes. Remove from heat.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
    3 oz orzo pasta
  13. 13
    To serve, divide soup between bowls and top with crispy chickpeas. Enjoy!

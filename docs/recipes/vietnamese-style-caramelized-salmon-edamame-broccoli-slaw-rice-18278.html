Vietnamese-Style Caramelized Salmon with Edamame, Broccoli Slaw & Rice

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 80% (5) $4.69/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbsalmon fillet
  • toasted sesame oil
  • fish sauce
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • ½English cucumber
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½lime
  • ½ (12 oz) pkgbroccoli slaw
  • rice vinegar
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • ½ cupedamame, shelled
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts small saucepan spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    1 cup water
    ⅛ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ lime
    1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
    ½ English cucumber
    1 medium carrot
  4. 4
    Peel and mince ginger; place in a medium bowl.
  5. 5
    Zest and juice lime into the bowl with the ginger. Add sugar, fish sauce, soy sauce, and water; whisk to combine the sauce.
    1 ½ tbsp brown sugar
    1 ½ tbsp fish sauce
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp water
  6. 6
    Preheat oven to broil on high and position rack in the upper third of the oven.
  7. 7
    Cut salmon into equal portions, pat dry with paper towels, and place on a plate; drizzle skin-side with oil, then sprinkle with salt and pepper on both sides. Pour sauce into an ovenproof skillet, then place salmon, skin-side down, in the sauce. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
    ¾ lb salmon fillet
    1 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  8. 8
    Place skillet in the oven and broil until salmon is just opaque, about 5 minutes. Flip salmon and continue to broil until the skin is crispy, about 3 minutes. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    While the salmon cooks, place edamame in a colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw. Set aside to drain.
    ½ cup edamame, shelled
  10. 10
    Place oil, vinegar, soy sauce, and chili-garlic sauce in the reserved bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tsp chili-garlic sauce
  11. 11
    Using a clean cutting board, trim green onions and cut crosswise into thin pieces; add half to the bowl with the dressing. (Reserve remaining green onions for serving.)
  12. 12
    Trim and quarter cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into ¼-inch thick pieces; add to the bowl.
  13. 13
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrot; add to the bowl along with the broccoli slaw and edamame. Toss to combine the slaw.
    ½ (12 oz) pkg broccoli slaw
  14. 14
    When the salmon is done, transfer to a clean plate.
  15. 15
    Place skillet over medium-high heat and cook, stirring frequently, until sauce begins to thicken and caramelize, 1-2 minutes. Remove from heat.
  16. 16
    To serve, divide rice, salmon, and slaw between bowls; spoon sauce over salmon and top with reserved green onion. Enjoy!

Warm Bread Pudding with Blueberry Compote & Vanilla Ice Cream

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $1.76/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • pure maple syrup
  • 1 (6 oz) pkgblueberries
  • butter, unsalted
  • 2 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1egg
  • 1whole grain bun or roll
  • 2 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • granulated sugar
  • nutmeg, ground
  • vanilla extract
  • 6 fl ozvanilla ice cream
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board ice cream scoop (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls muffin pan oven mitts silicone spatula small saucepan whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Cut bun into 1-inch cubes, transfer to a baking sheet pan, and spread out in a single layer. Place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, tossing halfway through, until bread is dry but not toasted, about 5 minutes. Remove from oven and set aside to cool.
    1 whole grain bun or roll
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, grease 2 muffin cups with a thick layer of butter; set aside.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
  4. 4
    Place egg, milk, cream, sugar, vanilla, nutmeg, and cinnamon in a medium bowl; whisk until smooth.
    1 egg
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) whole milk
    2 fl oz (¼ cup) heavy cream
    2 tbsp granulated sugar
    ¼ tsp vanilla extract
    ⅛ tsp nutmeg, ground
    1 pinch cinnamon
  5. 5
    Once the bread cubes have cooled, transfer to the bowl with the egg mixture. Using a silicone spatula, gently stir to combine, making sure all of the bread is submerged. Set aside for 10 minutes to soak.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the blueberries.
    1 (6 oz) pkg blueberries
  7. 7
    Combine blueberries, maple syrup, and butter in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until blueberries soften and release their juices, 3-5 minutes. Remove from heat, cover, and set aside while you finish the bread pudding.
    1 ½ tbsp pure maple syrup
    2 tsp butter, unsalted
  8. 8
    Spoon soaked bread into the prepared muffin cups, creating a mound of bread in the center of each cup. Divide remaining egg mixture between the cups, filling each to the rim.
  9. 9
    Carefully place the muffin pan in the oven. Cook, rotating the pan halfway through baking, until bread puddings puff up to double their size, and custard is set, about 30 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  10. 10
    Slide a knife down and around the side of each pudding to loosen. Holding the muffin pan over a baking sheet pan, carefully invert, and tap the pan to loosen the puddings.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide blueberry sauce, bread pudding, and ice cream between bowls. Enjoy!
    6 fl oz (¾ cup) vanilla ice cream

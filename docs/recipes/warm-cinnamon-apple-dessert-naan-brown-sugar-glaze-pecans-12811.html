Warm Cinnamon-Apple Dessert Naan with Brown Sugar Glaze & Pecans

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $1.54/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • honey
  • butter, unsalted
  • 1flatbread or naan
  • brown sugar
  • 2Gala apples
  • 1 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ⅛ cuppecan halves
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts small saucepan spatula stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F and position racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven.
  2. 2
    Transfer naan to a baking sheet pan. Place on the upper rack of the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until slightly crispy, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
    1 flatbread or naan
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry apples. Peel, quarter, core, and small dice; transfer to a second baking sheet and spread out in an even layer.
    2 Gala apples
  4. 4
    Using a box grater, coarsely grate cold butter. Sprinkle butter, sugar, and cinnamon over the apples.
    1 tbsp butter, unsalted
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
  5. 5
    Place apples on the lower rack of the oven and bake until fork-tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, melt additional butter in a small saucepan over low heat.
    1 ½ tbsp butter, unsalted
  7. 7
    Once butter is melted, whisk in cream, sugar, honey, and salt. Increase heat to medium and simmer, whisking frequently, until glaze is slightly thickened and glossy, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat, and let cool slightly to further thicken the sauce.
    1 fl oz (⅛ cup) heavy cream
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    1 tbsp honey
    1 pinch salt
  8. 8
    Roughly chop pecans.
    ⅛ cup pecans, dry roasted
  9. 9
    To assemble, spoon baked apples over the surface of the crispy naan. Drizzle with brown sugar glaze and sprinkle with pecans.
  10. 10
    Cut naan into pieces.
  11. 11
    To serve, divide naan pieces between plates and enjoy!

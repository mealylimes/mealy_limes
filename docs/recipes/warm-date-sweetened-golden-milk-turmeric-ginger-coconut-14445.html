Warm Date-Sweetened Golden Milk with Turmeric, Ginger & Coconut

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (1) $0.54/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (13.5 fl oz) cancoconut milk
  • black pepper
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • turmeric, ground
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • 16 fl ozalmond milk, unsweetened
  • cardamom, ground
  • vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cupmedjool dates, pitted
Cookware
can opener chef's knife cutting board grater (optional) hand or regular blender measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan stirring spoon strainer
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, peel, and mince ginger; transfer to a medium saucepan.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
  2. 2
    Add both milks and dates to the saucepan with the ginger; bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. (Watch the milk carefully, so it does not boil over.) Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) almond milk, unsweetened
    ½ (13.5 fl oz) can coconut milk
    ⅓ cup medjool dates, pitted
  3. 3
    Transfer cooled date-milk to a blender. Add vanilla and spices; blend on high until smooth, about 1 minute. (Reserve saucepan for next step.)
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
    1 tsp turmeric, ground
    ¼ tsp cinnamon, ground
    ⅛ tsp cardamom, ground
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  4. 4
    Pour the golden milk through a strainer set over the saucepan; discard solids. Reheat over medium-high heat to preferred temperature.
  5. 5
    To serve, divide golden milk between mugs and enjoy!

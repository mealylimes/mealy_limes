Warm Goat Cheese, Parmesan & Sun-Dried Tomato Stuffed Mushrooms

Vegetarian Snacks
⏱ 35 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 91% (11) $2.49/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • ½ (4 oz) loggoat cheese
  • 2sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  • ½ lbcremini mushrooms
  • ¼ cupParmesan cheese, shredded
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) measuring spoons mixing bowls oven mitts tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash and dry mushrooms; remove stems and set aside for the next step. Transfer mushroom caps to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and turn to coat. Arrange mushrooms, top-side down, and set aside.
    ½ lb cremini mushrooms
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  3. 3
    Mince mushroom stems. Place in a small bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince garlic. Add to the bowl with the minced mushroom stems.
    1 clove garlic
  5. 5
    Mince sun-dried tomatoes. Add to the bowl.
    2 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed
  6. 6
    Crumble goat cheese into the bowl.
    ½ (4 oz) log goat cheese
  7. 7
    Add Parmesan and spices to the bowl. Mash with a fork or the back of a spoon to combine. Divide the filling between mushroom caps, gently pressing it into each cavity.
    ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, shredded
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    ⅛ tsp crushed red pepper
    1 pinch salt
  8. 8
    Place mushrooms in the oven; bake until tender and cheese filling is warm and golden on top, 15-20 minutes depending on size of the mushrooms. Remove from oven.
  9. 9
    To serve, divide warm stuffed mushrooms between plates and enjoy!

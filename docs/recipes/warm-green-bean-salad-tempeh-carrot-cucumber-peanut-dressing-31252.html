Warm Green Bean Salad with Tempeh, Carrot, Cucumber & Peanut Dressing

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (16) $1.52/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • crushed red pepper
  • salt
  • natural peanut butter
  • pure maple syrup
  • ¾ lbgreen beans
  • 1shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • 2 mediumcarrots
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • rice vinegar
  • 1 (8 oz) packagetempeh
Cookware
aluminum foil chef's knife colander cutting board grater measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place vinegar, maple syrup, and salt in a small bowl; stir to combine the pickling liquid.
    3 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tsp pure maple syrup
    ¼ tsp salt
  2. 2
    Peel and slice shallot crosswise into thin rounds. Add to the bowl with the pickling liquid, stir to combine, and set aside.
    1 shallot
  3. 3
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. 4
    While the skillet heats up, medium dice tempeh. Place in a medium bowl, drizzle with soy sauce, and stir to coat.
    1 (8 oz) package tempeh
    1 tbsp soy sauce
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add tempeh and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden, 5-7 minutes. Transfer tempeh to a plate, season with salt, and cover with foil; set aside. (Reserve skillet and bowl for later use.)
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    1 pinch salt
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    2 medium carrots
    ½ English cucumber
    ¾ lb green beans
    ½ small bunch cilantro
  7. 7
    Peel and mince ginger. Place in the reserved bowl along with peanut butter, vinegar, soy sauce, water, maple syrup, and spices. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp natural peanut butter
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tbsp water
    2 tsp pure maple syrup
    ½ tsp garlic powder
    ¼ tsp black pepper
    ⅛ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Peel, trim, and coarsely grate carrots. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  9. 9
    Trim and halve cucumber lengthwise, then cut crosswise into thin half-moons. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  10. 10
    Trim and halve green beans crosswise.
  11. 11
    Return the skillet to high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add green beans and spices; cook, stirring occasionally, until blistered and tender, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool, about 5 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  12. 12
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the other veggies. (Reserve remaining cilantro for serving.)
  13. 13
    When the green beans have cooled slightly, add to the bowl along with pickled shallots. Stir to combine the salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide salad between plates or bowls; top with crispy tempeh and reserved cilantro. Enjoy!

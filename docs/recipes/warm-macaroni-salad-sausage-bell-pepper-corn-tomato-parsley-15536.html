Warm Macaroni Salad with Sausage, Bell Pepper, Corn, Tomato & Parsley

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (5) $3.86/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 ozmacaroni (elbow) pasta
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • ½lemon
  • 1orange bell pepper
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
  • ½ cupfrozen corn
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.
    4 oz macaroni (elbow) pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 orange bell pepper
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ lemon
  3. 3
    Place corn in a colander and rinse under hot water (from the tap) to thaw; transfer to a medium bowl and set aside.
    ½ cup frozen corn
  4. 4
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add sausages and cook, turning occasionally until golden brown and almost cooked through, 5-6 minutes. Remove to a plate, leaving the cooking juices in the skillet.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    2 pork sausages
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, peel, halve, and thinly slice onion into half-moons; add to the bowl with the corn.
    ½ medium red onion
  7. 7
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Halve tomatoes and add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Return skillet (with cooking juices) to medium heat; add veggies and Italian seasoning. Stir to combine, then nestle sausages into the veggies; cover and cook until the veggies are charred and sausages are cooked through, 6-8 minutes. Remove from the heat.
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
  10. 10
    Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  11. 11
    When the pasta is done, drain in the colander and return to the pan.
  12. 12
    Zest and juice lemon into the pan with the pasta. Add the parsley (reserving a small handful for serving), oil, salt, and pepper; stir to combine.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  13. 13
    Cut sausages crosswise into rounds; add to the pan along with charred veggies and any cooking juices. Stir to combine the pasta salad.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!

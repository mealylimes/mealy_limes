Warm Rice Pudding with Apple, Cranberry & Ginger Compote

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (3) $1.52/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupdried cranberries
  • cinnamon, ground
  • salt
  • butter, unsalted
  • 8 fl ozwhole milk
  • 1 (1 inch) pieceginger root
  • ½orange
  • brown sugar
  • 1Gala apple
  • 4 fl ozheavy whipping cream
  • ½ cupjasmine rice
  • vanilla extract
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons small saucepan stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add milk, sugar, and vanilla; stir to combine and bring the mixture to a simmer over medium heat.
    ½ cup jasmine rice
    8 fl oz (1 cup) whole milk
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    1 tsp vanilla extract
  2. 2
    Once the liquid comes to a simmer, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    1 Gala apple
    1 (1 inch) piece ginger root
    ½ orange
  4. 4
    Quarter, core, and medium dice apple.
  5. 5
    Peel and mince ginger.
  6. 6
    Preheat another small saucepan over medium heat.
  7. 7
    Once the pan is hot, add butter and swirl to coat the bottom. Add apple, ginger, cranberries, sugar, cinnamon, and salt to the saucepan; stir to combine.
    1 tsp butter, unsalted
    ¼ cup dried cranberries
    2 tbsp brown sugar
    ¼ tsp cinnamon
    1 pinch salt
  8. 8
    Juice orange into the pan with the fruit and stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until apples are tender and the sauce has thickened, 5-6 minutes. Remove from the heat.
  9. 9
    Once the rice is done, remove from the heat. Add cream, stir to combine, and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
    4 fl oz (½ cup) heavy cream
  10. 10
    To serve, divide rice pudding between bowls and top with fruit compote. Enjoy!

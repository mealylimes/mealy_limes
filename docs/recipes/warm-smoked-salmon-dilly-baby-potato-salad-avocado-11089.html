Warm Smoked Salmon & Dilly Baby Potato Salad with Avocado

Dinner Fish & Seafood
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 75% (3) $4.36/serving
Ingredients
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • honey
  • Dijon mustard
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby arugula
  • ½ small bunchgreen onions (scallions)
  • 1avocado
  • 1 lbnew potatoes
  • 1 cupfrozen peas
  • ½ small bunchfresh dill
  • 1 (4 oz) pkgsmoked salmon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lb new potatoes
    ½ small bunch fresh dill
    1 avocado
    ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby arugula
  2. 2
    Cut potatoes in half and place in a medium saucepan. Cover with hot water (from the tap) by 1-inch and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and cook until fork-tender, 10-15 minutes.
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, place oil, vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper in a small bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    2 tsp Dijon mustard
    1 tsp honey
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Using a knife, shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add half to the bowl with the dressing, stir to combine, and set aside. Reserve the other half for the potatoes.
  5. 5
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice thinly while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon.
  6. 6
    Trim green onions and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces at an angle.
  7. 7
    Add peas to the potatoes during the last 2 minutes of cooking.
    1 cup frozen peas
  8. 8
    Drain potatoes and peas in a colander, then return to the pan. Add oil and reserved dill; toss to combine.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  9. 9
    Chop or tear salmon into bite-sized pieces.
    1 (4 oz) pkg smoked salmon
  10. 10
    To serve, divide arugula between plates and top with dilly potatoes, avocado, green onions, and salmon. Drizzle with dressing and enjoy!

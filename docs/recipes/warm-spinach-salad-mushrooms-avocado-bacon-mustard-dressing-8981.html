Warm Spinach Salad with Mushrooms, Avocado & Bacon Mustard Dressing

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $4.25/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ lbwhite mushrooms
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • Dijon mustard
  • 2eggs
  • apple cider vinegar
  • 1avocado
  • ½ mediumred onion
  • brown sugar
  • 4 slicesbacon
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls slotted spoon small saucepan spatula stainless steel or cast iron skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.
    2 eggs
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    Add the bacon to the skillet and cook until crispy, 3-5 minutes per side.
    4 slices bacon
  4. 4
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    ½ lb white mushrooms
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  5. 5
    Peel and thinly slice the onion. Slice the mushrooms.
    ½ medium red onion
  6. 6
    Once the bacon is cooked, use a slotted spoon to transfer to a plate lined with paper towels; pat to remove excess oil. Crumble or chop into small pieces.
  7. 7
    Return skillet (with bacon fat) to the stove over medium heat. Add the onions, mushrooms, and salt; cook, stirring occasionally, until vegetables soften, 4-5 minutes. Leaving bacon fat in the skillet, transfer the vegetables to a plate; set aside.
    ¼ tsp salt
  8. 8
    Return the skillet to medium heat. Add the vinegar, brown sugar, oil, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Whisk vigorously, scraping the bottom of the skillet to incorporate any browned bits. Bring to a simmer and reduce heat, whisking occasionally, until dressing thickens, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.
    3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
    1 ½ tsp brown sugar
    2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Dijon mustard
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Halve and pit the avocado; slice crosswise while still in the skin and scoop out with a spoon.
    1 avocado
  10. 10
    Peel and slice the eggs.
  11. 11
    In a large bowl, combine the spinach, bacon, onion, mushrooms, avocado, and dressing from the skillet. Toss to combine.
  12. 12
    Divide the salad between plates or bowls. Top with the sliced egg and enjoy!

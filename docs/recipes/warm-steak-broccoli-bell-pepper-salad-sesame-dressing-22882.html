Warm Steak, Broccoli & Bell Pepper Salad with Sesame Dressing

Meat Simple & Quick
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 88% (2) $6.06/serving
Ingredients
  • toasted sesame oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgspring mix (mixed greens)
  • sesame seeds
  • 1yellow bell pepper
  • ¾ lbstriploin (New York strip) steak
  • stir-fry sauce
  • ½ (18 oz) bagbroccoli florets
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet stirring spoon tongs whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. 2
    While the skillet heats up, wash, dry, trim, and seed bell pepper; slice lengthwise into thin strips.
    1 yellow bell pepper
  3. 3
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add bell pepper and broccoli; cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, about 4 minutes.
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    ½ (18 oz) bag broccoli florets
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, pat the steak dry with paper towels and slice crosswise into thin strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb striploin (New York strip) steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  5. 5
    When the veggies have softened, add steak and stir-fry sauce to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until veggies are tender and steak is cooked through, 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tbsp stir-fry sauce
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, place water, additional stir-fry sauce, oil, and sesame seeds in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp water
    2 tbsp stir-fry sauce
    2 tsp toasted sesame oil
    2 tsp sesame seeds
  7. 7
    Wash and dry mixed greens (skip this step if they came pre-washed). Add to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.
    1 (5 oz) pkg spring mix (mixed greens)
  8. 8
    To serve, divide dressed greens between plates or bowls; top with warm steak and veggies. Enjoy!

Warm Sweet Chili Steak Salad with Snap Peas, Spinach & Sesame Seeds

Dinner Meat
⏱ 25 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (10) $2.75/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • 1 (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • 1 (8 oz) pkgsugar snap peas
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 mediumcarrot
  • sesame seeds
  • rice vinegar
  • cornstarch
  • brown sugar
  • chili-garlic sauce
  • ¾ lbflank steak
Cookware
chef's knife colander cutting board measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet small saucepan stirring spoon tongs vegetable peeler whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the spinach if it came pre-washed.)
    1 (8 oz) pkg sugar snap peas
    1 medium carrot
    1 (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  2. 2
    Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. 3
    While the skillet heats up, pat steak dry with paper towels and slice crosswise into thin strips. Season with salt and pepper.
    ¾ lb flank steak
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  4. 4
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add steak and snap peas; cook, stirring occasionally, until snap peas are soft and steak is cooked through, 4-5 minutes. Remove from heat.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  5. 5
    Meanwhile, place vinegar, sugar, water, chili-garlic sauce, and salt in a small saucepan; whisk to combine. Set over medium-high heat and bring to a boil, whisking occasionally.
    1 ½ tbsp rice vinegar
    1 tbsp brown sugar
    1 tbsp water
    ½ tsp chili-garlic sauce
    ⅛ tsp salt
  6. 6
    While the sauce comes to a boil, in a small bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth.
    1 tbsp water
    1 ½ tsp cornstarch
  7. 7
    Once the sauce is boiling, add cornstarch slurry to the pan in a steady stream while whisking quickly. Cook, whisking constantly, until the sweet chili sauce thickens, about 30 seconds. Remove from heat and set aside to cool slightly.
  8. 8
    Place oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Whisk to combine the dressing.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tbsp rice vinegar
    ¼ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  9. 9
    Using a clean cutting board, peel and trim carrot; run a vegetable peeler along the length of the carrot to make ribbons. Add to the bowl with the dressing.
  10. 10
    Add spinach to the bowl. Using your hands, massage spinach and carrot until wilted, about 30 seconds.
  11. 11
    Add sweet chili sauce to the skillet with the steak and snap peas. Stir to coat.
  12. 12
    To serve, divide spinach salad between plates or bowls. Top with steak and snap peas, then sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!
    2 tsp sesame seeds

Whipped Honey-Orange Ricotta with Mango & Pistachios

Vegetarian Dessert
⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 100% (2) $2.54/serving Pro
Ingredients
  • ½ (15 oz) pkgricotta cheese
  • honey
  • 2mangoes
  • ½orange
  • ¼ cuppistachios, unsalted
  • vanilla extract
Cookware
chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) cutting board grater (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash, dry, zest, and juice orange into a medium bowl.
    ½ orange
  2. 2
    Add ricotta, honey, and vanilla to the bowl with the orange. Whisk until light, fluffy, and combined.
    ½ (15 oz) pkg ricotta cheese
    1 tbsp honey
    ½ tsp vanilla extract
  3. 3
    Roughly chop pistachios and place in a small bowl.
    ¼ cup pistachios, unsalted
  4. 4
    Peel mangoes and slice flesh away from the pit, starting with the flat sides; medium dice.
    2 mangoes
  5. 5
    To serve, divide whipped ricotta between bowls; top with mango and nuts. Enjoy!

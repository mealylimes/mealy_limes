White Bean-Cauliflower Soup with Tomato, Fresh Basil & Garlic Sticks

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 50% (1) $3.85/serving
Ingredients
  • 3 clovesgarlic
  • ½ small pkgfresh basil
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • balsamic vinegar
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • ½ medium headcauliflower
  • 24 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • 1flatbread or naan
  • 1 pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife colander cutting board garlic press (optional) ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ medium head cauliflower
    1 pint grape tomatoes
    ½ small pkg fresh basil
  2. 2
    Drain and rinse beans in a colander; set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  3. 3
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  4. 4
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and medium dice onion; peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    2 cloves garlic
  5. 5
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom; add onion, garlic, and spices. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 4 minutes.
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Meanwhile, separate cauliflower into bite-sized florets and cut stems into smaller pieces. Add cauliflower and water to the pan with the onion; continue to cook, stirring occasionally, while preparing the tomatoes.
    2 tbsp water
  7. 7
    Halve tomatoes; add to the pan along with the beans and broth. Stir to combine the soup and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring occasionally, until veggies are fork-tender, about 10 minutes.
    24 fl oz (3 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, preheat oven to broil on high and position rack under the broiler.
  9. 9
    Peel and mince additional garlic; place in a small bowl along with oil and spices. Stir to combine the garlic oil.
    1 clove garlic
    1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp Italian seasoning
    1 pinch salt
    1 pinch black pepper
  10. 10
    Place flatbread on a baking sheet pan and spread garlic oil over top; place in the oven and broil until golden and crisp, 3-4 minutes. Remove from oven and slice crosswise into sticks.
    1 flatbread or naan
  11. 11
    While the garlic bread is toasting, pick basil leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons.
  12. 12
    When the soup is done, add vinegar, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  13. 13
    Divide soup between bowls and top with basil. Serve with breadsticks on the side and enjoy!

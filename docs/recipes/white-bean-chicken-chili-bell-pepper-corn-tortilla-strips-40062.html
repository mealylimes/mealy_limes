White Bean-Chicken Chili with Bell Pepper, Corn & Tortilla Strips

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 78% (56) $2.86/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • chili powder
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • all-purpose flour
  • ⅓ cupplain Greek yogurt
  • 1 mediumzucchini squash
  • 1red bell pepper
  • ½ mediumyellow onion
  • 2 stickscelery
  • cumin, ground
  • ½ small bunchcilantro
  • 16 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ½lime
  • ⅓ cupfrozen corn
  • 2small corn tortillas
  • ½ lbground chicken
Cookware
baking sheet pan can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater ladle measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls oven mitts stirring spoon
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Slice tortillas in half, then crosswise into ½-inch strips. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with salt; toss to coat and spread out in a single layer.
    2 small corn tortillas
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ¼ tsp salt
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake, stirring halfway through, until tortilla strips are golden and crispy, 10-12 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    2 sticks celery
    1 red bell pepper
    1 medium zucchini squash
    ½ small bunch cilantro
    ½ lime
  5. 5
    Trim and medium dice celery. Place in a medium bowl.
  6. 6
    Trim, seed, and medium dice bell pepper. Add to the bowl with the celery.
  7. 7
    Trim and coarsely grate zucchini. Add to the bowl and set aside.
  8. 8
    Preheat a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  9. 9
    While the pan heats up, peel, halve, and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic.
    ½ medium yellow onion
    1 clove garlic
  10. 10
    Once the pan is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, garlic, chicken, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 5-6 minutes.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ lb ground chicken
    2 tsp chili powder
    ¼ tsp cumin, ground
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  11. 11
    Meanwhile, drain and rinse beans. Set aside to drain further.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  12. 12
    When the chicken is done, add veggies, corn, and flour to the pan. Stir to combine and continue to cook until veggies are tender, 3-4 minutes more.
    ⅓ cup frozen corn
    2 tsp all-purpose flour
  13. 13
    Meanwhile, shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Set aside.
  14. 14
    When the veggies are done, add beans and broth to the pan; stir to combine the chili and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until beans are tender and chili thickens slightly, 10-12 minutes.
    16 fl oz (2 cups) chicken or vegetable broth
  15. 15
    When the chili is done, juice lime into the pan. Add cilantro and yogurt, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
  16. 16
    To serve, divide chili between bowls and top with crispy tortilla strips. Enjoy!

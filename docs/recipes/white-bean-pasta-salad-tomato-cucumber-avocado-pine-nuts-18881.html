White Bean Pasta Salad with Tomato, Cucumber, Avocado & Pine Nuts

Dinner Vegetarian
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 82% (7) $3.09/serving
Ingredients
  • 5 ozrotini pasta
  • basil, dried
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • 1 (15 oz) cancannellini (white kidney) beans
  • ½shallot
  • ½English cucumber
  • ½avocado
  • ½lemon
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • mayonnaise
  • ½ pintgrape tomatoes
Cookware
can opener chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board measuring cups measuring spoons medium saucepan mixing bowls stirring spoon whisk or fork
Instructions
  1. 1
    Fill a medium saucepan halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside.
    ½ tsp salt
    5 oz rotini pasta
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ lemon
    ½ pint grape tomatoes
    ½ English cucumber
    ½ avocado
  3. 3
    Juice lemon into a medium bowl.
  4. 4
    Peel and mince shallot; add to the bowl with the lemon juice.
    ½ shallot
  5. 5
    Add mayo and spices to the bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.
    2 tbsp mayonnaise
    ½ tsp basil, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ⅛ tsp black pepper
  6. 6
    Quarter tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. 7
    Trim and small dice cucumber; add to the bowl.
  8. 8
    Halve and pit avocado; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  9. 9
    Drain and rinse beans, then add to the bowl.
    1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans
  10. 10
    Add cooked pasta and pine nuts to the bowl, then stir to combine the pasta salad.
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
  11. 11
    To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!

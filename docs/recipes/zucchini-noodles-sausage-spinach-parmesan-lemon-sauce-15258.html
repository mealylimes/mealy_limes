Zucchini Noodles with Sausage, Spinach & Parmesan-Lemon Sauce

Dinner Meat
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 71% (7) $4.16/serving
Ingredients
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • ½ (5 oz) pkgbaby spinach
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • thyme, dried
  • ½ small bunchItalian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 2 ozParmesan cheese
  • 2 mediumzucchini squash
  • ½lemon
  • 4 fl ozchicken or vegetable broth
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • 2pork sausages
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife citrus juicer (optional) colander cutting board garlic press (optional) grater julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons mixing bowls nonstick skillet oven mitts stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F. Coat a baking sheet pan with a thin layer of oil.
    ½ tsp extra virgin olive oil
  2. 2
    Arrange sausages on the baking sheet and place in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated); roast, flipping once, until sausages are golden and cooked through, 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven.
    2 pork sausages
  3. 3
    Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
    ½ small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
    ½ lemon
    2 medium zucchini squash
    ½ (5 oz) pkg baby spinach
  4. 4
    Shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Transfer to a plate.
  5. 5
    Finely grate Parmesan and add to the plate with the parsley (keeping them separate); set aside.
    2 oz Parmesan cheese
  6. 6
    Trim zucchini and use a julienne (or regular) peeler to make noodles; place in a medium bowl and set aside.
  7. 7
    Peel and mince garlic; place in a skillet.
    1 clove garlic
  8. 8
    Juice lemon into the skillet with the garlic.
  9. 9
    Mince pine nuts and add to the skillet along with broth, oil, and spices; set over medium-high heat and bring to a simmer.
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
    4 fl oz (½ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
    ½ tsp thyme, dried
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  10. 10
    Once the liquid in the skillet comes to a simmer, add zucchini noodles and cook, stirring often, until just tender, 2-3 minutes.
  11. 11
    Add spinach to the skillet in handfuls, waiting for the spinach to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  12. 12
    Add parsley and half of the Parmesan (reserve remaining cheese for serving) to the skillet; stir to combine and remove from heat.
  13. 13
    When the sausages are done, transfer to a cutting board and slice crosswise into rounds.
  14. 14
    To serve, divide saucy zucchini noodles and sausage between bowls; top with remaining Parmesan and enjoy!

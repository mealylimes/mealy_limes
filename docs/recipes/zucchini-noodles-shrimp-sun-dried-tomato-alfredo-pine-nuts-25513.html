Zucchini Noodles with Shrimp, Sun-Dried Tomato Alfredo & Pine Nuts

Simple & Quick Fish & Seafood
⏱ 30 min 🍽 2 servings 👍 83% (3) $4.93/serving
Ingredients
  • ¾ lbraw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • 4 mediumzucchini squash
  • ⅛ cuppine nuts
  • ½ (16 oz) jarAlfredo sauce
  • ¼ cupsun-dried tomato pesto
Cookware
baking sheet pan chef's knife colander cutting board julienne peeler (optional) measuring cups measuring spoons oven mitts small saucepan stirring spoon tongs
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2
    Wash, dry, and trim zucchini; use a julienne (or regular) peeler to make noodles. Transfer to a baking sheet pan, drizzle with oil, and season with spices; toss to combine and spread out in an even layer.
    4 medium zucchini squash
    1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    1 tsp Italian seasoning
    ½ tsp salt
    ¼ tsp black pepper
  3. 3
    Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until zucchini noodles start to soften, about 8 minutes.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, place Alfredo sauce and pesto in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring occasionally. Once simmering, remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
    ½ (16 oz) jar Alfredo sauce
    ¼ cup sun-dried tomato pesto
  5. 5
    If using frozen shrimp, place them in a colander and run under cold water to thaw them slightly and remove any ice crystals. (If using fresh shrimp, skip this step.) Place the shrimp on a clean towel or paper towels and pat dry.
    ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp, fresh or frozen
  6. 6
    When the zucchini is soft, remove from the oven and toss. Top with shrimp and drizzle with additional oil.
    2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  7. 7
    Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to bake until zucchini noodles are tender and shrimp are cooked through, 6-7 minutes more. Remove from oven.
  8. 8
    To serve, divide zucchini noodles and shrimp between plates or bowls; top with sauce and pine nuts. Enjoy!
    ⅛ cup pine nuts
